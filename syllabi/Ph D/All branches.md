Course Outcomes: After successful completion of this course, researchers should be able to -
1. Carry out literature survey in a chosen area of research effectively and identify the
gaps in the body of knowledge.
2. Plan and conduct research in the respective domains.
3. Present results of a research study effectively.
4. Write research proposals and papers for different purposes.
Detailed Syllabus:
| Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Definition of Research, Importance of carrying out Research, Types of   3
research and characteristics of each type, Process of Research, Features
of a good research study.
Literature review - Significance and source of literature, approach   5
towards literature review, modern tools of reviewing research,
identifying gaps in the literature, Planning the research to fill in the gaps
observed in the literature review.
3.   Research Problem Definition/Hypotheses formulation, Research   6
Strategy, Outlining the approach for research- experimental, theoretical,
descriptive, numerical, analytical etc., Tools& Techniques of Data
Collection-Scaling, Validity & Reliability of Tools, Scale Construction,
Quantitative tools and Qualitative Techniques, Primary and Secondary
Sources of Data.
4.   Basic Statistics- Measures of Central Tendency- Mean. Median, Mode,   6
Other averages, Measures of Dispersion-Range, Mean and Standard
Deviation, Measures of Skewness, Kurtosis, Measures of Relationship-|
Covariance, Pearson's Coefficient of Co-relation, Spearman's Rank
Correlation.
.   Sampling and Statistical Inference   - Sampling and Non-Sampling Errors,   10
Sampling Distribution of Mean, Central Limit Theorem, Sampling|
distribution Proportion, Studene's t-distribution, chi-square distribution,
f-distribution. Statistical Inference-Point Estimation, Interval Estimation,
Sample size and its determination, Tests of Significance (Large Sample
and Small Sample).
