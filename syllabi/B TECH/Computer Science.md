# Syllabus common for B Tech/ MechaniMBA Tech (alll programs), can use common question paper.

Essential Electronics Practices - This course empowers students to gather practical knowledge and skills using Internal Continues Assessment (ICA) throughout the semester.

Engineering Ethics - This course focuses on instilling Engineering ethics, which are taught and assessed throughout the semester.

Indian Knowledge System - This course enables students to integrate traditional indian wisdom with modem concepts.

Constitution of India - This course focuses on making the students aware of the Constitution of India, which is taught and assessed throughout the semester.
