Pre-requisite: Knowledge of security, risks
Course Objective
The course provides students with an understanding of cybersecurity principles in data science,
covering secure data handling, risk analysis, and threat detection. It also explores advanced topics like
adversarial machine learning, privacy-preserving techniques, and ethical considerations in security.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understand the fundamentals of cyber security and its intersection with data science, including
core concepts, threats, and the impact of cyber-attacks on data science processes.
2. Apply foundational principles of cyber security such as encryption, secure data handling, and
privacy regulations to enhance data protection and ensure compliance.
3. Implement data privacy and protection strategies, including secure data sharing, cloud
security, and data retention policies, to maintain secure data management practices.
4. Analyze and develop threat detection and incident response strategies, including real-time
detection, forensic analysis, and machine learning methods for cyber resilience.
