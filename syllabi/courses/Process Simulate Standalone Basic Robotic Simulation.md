Prerequisite: CAD Advanced Processes
Course Objective
The CAD Advanced Processes course aims to equip students with expertise in simulation
environments for replicating actual manufacturing processes. It introduces robotics and
automation in production lines while providing exposure to standard engineering
manufacturing simulations. The course emphasizes practical applications across multiple
domains, including automotive, aerospace, product manufacturing, and FMCG industries.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to –
1. Understand Simulation Environment
2. Replicate Actual Manufacturing Process.
3. Understand Robotics and Automation in Production Line.
4. Learn the standard engineering manufacturing process in Simulation Environment.
5. Understand multiple domains like Automotive, Aerospace, Product Manufacturing,
FMCG Industries and more.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to NX Simulation and Visualization:   08
Basic environment, study creation, creating sequences of simulative
operations, collision detection, section cutting, video and picture output.
2.   Introduction to Kinematic Devices in NX:   05
Defining kinematic devices, navigating the Motion Simulation
environment, setting up kinematic constraints and joints.
3.   Advanced Spot-Welding Techniques: Pneumatic and Servo Guns:   06
Defining and simulating robotic spot welding, pneumatic and servo gun
definition and usage, pedestrial welding and gun on robot path
development.
4.   Simulating Robotic Grinding and Welding Processes:   07
Defining and simulating robotic continuous applications, arc welding
and grinding path development.
5.   Simulating Robotic Material Handling Processes:   04
Defining and simulating robotic material handling, gripper definition
and usage, pick and place path development, Multi-robot simulation (i.e.
interference zones), swept volumes, 7th axis, etc.
Total   30
