Pre-requisite: Design of Reinforced Concrete Structures, Design of Steel Structures
Objectives:
1. To design RCC Structural elements
2. To design steel trusses for industrial sheds
3. To design structural steel flooring system
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Analyse and design RCC Structural elements
2. Draw detailed drawings of RCC Structural elements
3. Analyse and design steel trusses for industrial sheds subjected to live load and wind load
4. Analyse and design structural steel flooring system
5. Prepare CAD drawings for detailing of structural steel
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1   RCC Detailing:   30
Mini projects on the following topics with detailed drawings drawn in pencil only on
minimum of A2 size drawing sheets.
1. Design and structural detailing of RCC open well staircase
2. Design and structural detailing of continuous one-way slab
3. Design and structural detailing of continuous two-way slab
4. Design and structural detailing of continuous beams
5. Design and structural detailing of column subjected to biaxial bending
6. Design and structural detailing of combined footing
2   Structural Steel Detailing:   30
Mini projects on the following topics with detailed design report and CAD drawings
on minimum of A2 size drawing sheets.
1. Design and structural detailing of roofing system for an industrial shed
2. Design and structural detailing of flooring system with column and their
bases
Total   60
Text Books:

1. S. K. Duggal (2014), “Design of Steel Structure - 3rd Edition”, Tata McGraw- Hill Education
