Pre-requisite: Engineering mathematics

Objectives:

1. To introduce the concept particles and their characteristics.

2. To introduce basic concepts of solid - fluid operations of size reduction,
enlargement, separations, mixing, and conveying.

3. To familiarize the students with the working, construction and diagrams of
the equipments used for solid - fluid operations.

4. To choose the equipment for desired solid ~ fluid operation of chemical
industries

Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1: Understand and reproduce the fundamentals of particles size
measurements and its characteristics
2: Identify and select appropriate heat exchange equipment for various
applications
3: Understand, and reproduce the concepts of various solid - fluid
operations like size reduction, enlargement, separations, mixing and
conveying operations
