Pre-requisite: Heat Transfer Operation, Mass Transfer Operation I & II, Chemical Reaction Engineering.
Course Objective
The course stresses on the development of programming skills to solve the chemical engineering cases based
on transport phenomenon reaction kinetics etc.   It further provides an insight on various aspects of
computational logic helpful in solving advance chemical operations.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Select the appropriate method to solve the chemical engineering problem.
2. Develop the programming algorithm for solving the linear and differential equation.
3. Apply appropriate logic to develop the computational program for chemical engineering
problems.
