Retaining Structures)

3   0   1   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Design of Concrete Structures
Objectives:
1. To impart knowledge on design concepts of structures for storage of liquids
2. To understand and design of on-ground circular and rectangular tanks
3. To understand and design of underground circular and rectangular tanks
4. To understand and design of elevated circular and rectangular tanks
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand the design concepts of structures for storage of liquids
2. Analyse and design on-ground circular and rectangular tanks
3. Analyse and design underground circular and rectangular tanks
4. Analyse and design elevated circular and rectangular tanks
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Introduction: Types of tanks, materials, types of joints, types of methods,   05
fundamentals of RCC design of water tanks, special considerations, special
requirements, codal provisions.
2   Design (un-cracked and cracked design) of on-ground circular water tanks:   10
Various boundary conditions at base slab, variation of hoop tension, moment and
deflection of wall with various H2/DT ratios, deep and shallow tanks.
3   Design (un-cracked and cracked design) of on-ground rectangular water tanks:   10
Various boundary conditions at base slab, variation of moments with respect to
height/span ratio.
4   Design of underground storage circular and rectangular tanks: Ring beams, side   10
walls, side wall and column footing, cover and base slab.
5   Design of elevated storage circular and rectangular Tanks: Ring beams, staging-   10
columns and bracings, foundation.
Total   45
Text Books:
1. Vaziani, V.N. (2008), “Design of Reinforced Concrete Structures”, Khanna Publishers
2. Syal, I. C. & Goel, A.K. (2008), “Reinforced Concrete Structures”, S. Chand Publishing
3. Ghali, A. (2014), “Circular Storage Tanks and Silos”, CRC Press
4. H. J. Shah (2008), “Reinforced Concrete I & II”, Charotar Publishing House Pvt. Ltd
