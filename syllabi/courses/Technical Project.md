Pre-requisite: Knowledge of courses completed until 3rd year.
Course Objective
The minor project is designed to bridge the gap between theory and practice.   It provides an
opportunity to students to explore various societal problems and provide innovative solutions for
the same. The course encourage students to develop technical and soft skills. The students may
also work in interdisciplinary areas which will help broaden their scope and learning in different
domains.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify, analyze and solve problems creatively using critical and design thinking principles.
2. Implement the prototype/proof of concept using fundamental and disciplinary concepts and
methods.
3. Manage a team project.
4. Use effectively oral, written and visual communication
Syllabus
A student group is required to identify some real world problem and develop a solution using
design and critical thinking principles. They may develop a working model or a proof of concept
of the solution. At the end of the semester, students will submit a report detailing the problem
analysis process and designed solution. Each group will present their work before examiners.
