Course Objective
The objective of this course is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of industrial design
principles and their application in modern manufacturing and engineering systems. It aims to develop
knowledge of ergonomics, man–machine interaction, workstation design, and control–display systems to
enhance safety, comfort, and operational efficiency. The course also emphasizes aesthetic concepts,
including form, style, and colour, and their influence on product appeal and usability.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Explain basic ergonomics concepts with man–machine relationships and workstation design in
industrial applications
2. Apply anthropometric data for product and system design
3. Analyze posture, manual handling, workstation layout, and control–display design to improve
workplace safety and efficiency
4. Evaluate aesthetic principles, style, and colour application in engineering products and industrial
equipment
5. Design ergonomically safe work environments considering occupational health, environmental control,
and personal protective equipment.
