Pre-requisite: Fluid Mechanics, Hydraulic Engineering
Objectives:
1. To understand design various units of water and Wastewater conventional treatment
system.
2. To understand the laboratory analysis of Water and Wastewater
3. To get acquainted the working principle of hydraulic machines
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Design various units of water and Wastewater conventional treatment system.
2. Discuss the laboratory analysis of Water and Wastewater
3. Develop the models for hydraulic machines
Detailed Syllabus: (per section plan)
Unit   Description   Duration

Mini projects based on following topics:

 Mini Project – Design of Conventional Water Supply Scheme for an
Area
 Wastewater Flow Rates Calculation and Design of Conveyance
 Design of Conventional Wastewater Treatment Units
 Mini Project – Design of Conventional Wastewater Management
Scheme for an Area
2   Section B   30
Mini projects consists of animated/simulated or physical working models
and their presentation based on following topics:
 Pipe network model for a residential area (hypothetical or real)
 Hydraulic structure
 Impact of jets
 Turbines
 Pumps
 Miscellaneous hydraulic machines
