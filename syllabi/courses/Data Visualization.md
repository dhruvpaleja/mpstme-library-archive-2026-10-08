Prerequisite: Nil
Course Objective
This course aims to provide students with a thorough understanding of the importance of data
visualization and its useful applications. In order to ensure that they can convert complex data into
understandable, insightful visuals, students will become proficient in common data visualization tools
and methodologies. Along with learning how to successfully present statistics, they will also
concentrate on ethical issues and narrative. The course equips students with critical thinking and
teamwork abilities, which are crucial for data science and engineering projects. By the end, students
will be well-equipped to communicate insights and make data-driven decisions using data
visualization as a potent tool.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to
1. Demonstrate fundamental knowledge of data visualization concepts and the tools used to create
them. (L3 – Applying)
2. Apply data visualization skills to produce interactive dashboards. (L3 – Applying)
3. Analyse intricate datasets, prepare and integrate data using visualization tools, and utilize
advanced visualization techniques to generate insights.
4. Evaluate data visualizations for effectiveness and apply best practices in data storytelling (L3 –
Applying)
