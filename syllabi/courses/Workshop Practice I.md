Course Objectives:
1. To impart hand on safety precaution of different workshop practices on
various trades.
2. To impart knowledge of basic tools used for different workshop jobs.
3. To familiarize students with assembling and troubleshooting of PC.

Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Differentiate various tools used in workshop for fitting, welding and sheet
metal fabrication.
2. Follow the appropriate safety methods for handling of tools in workshop.
3. Assemble all components to build the Personal Computer functional.
4. Troubleshoot problems of computer system.
5. Maintain Personal Computer system.
