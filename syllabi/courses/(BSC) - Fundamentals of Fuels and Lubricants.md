Course Objectives
To introduce basic theory of Fuels & Lubrication also familiarizing with the concepts and
applications of Fuels , Combustion & Lubricants
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. discuss theory of Fuels & Lubrication,
2. apply principles of combustion in various real time problems,
3. examine the different properties & testing of Fuels & Lubricants.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction & Theory of Fuels: Definition, Classification, Characteristics,   09
Calorific value & numerical based on calorific value, Different Types of fuels,
Structure of petroleum, refining process, fuels, thermal cracking, catalytic
cracking, polymerization, alkylation, isomerisation, blending, products of
refining process.
2.   Properties & Testing of Fuel: Properties and testing of fuels, relative density,   09
distillation, vapour pressure, flash point, spontaneous ignition temperature,
viscosity, pour point, flammability, ignitability, diesel index, API gravity,
aniline point etc.
3.   Combustion & Fuel Rating: Combustion of fuels & numerical based on   09
combustion, Mechanism of combustion, normal combustion, knocking, octane
rating, fuel requirements. CI Engine, mechanism of combustion, diesel knock,
cetane rating, fuel requirements.
4.   Theory of Lubrication: Introduction, Classification, Properties Mechanism of   09
Lubrication, hydrodynamic lubrication, elasto hydrodynamic lubrication,
boundary lubrication, bearing lubrication, functions of the lubrication system,
introduction to design of a lubricating system. Current trends in tribology
5.   Lubricants: Specific requirements for automotive lubricants, oxidation   09
deterioration and degradation of lubricants, additives and additive
mechanism, synthetic lubricants, classification of lubricating oils, properties of
lubricating oils, tests on lubricants. Grease, classification, properties, test used
in grease.
