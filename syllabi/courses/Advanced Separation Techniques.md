Pre-requisite: Mass Transfer Operation-I & II, Engineering and Solid Mechanics.
Objectives
This subject delves into recent developments in chemical separation methods.
This subject will help students to understand, analyse the advantage and
disadvantage of new separation techniques and design criteria for its
application.
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. understand the application of advanced separation methods.
2. analyse the performance recent technique with respect to conventional
methods.
3. design modified process system with application of recent separation
techniques.
