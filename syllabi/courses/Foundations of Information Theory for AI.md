Prerequisite: Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability
Course Objective
This course introduces students to the basic ideas of information, uncertainty, and entropy that are
essential for understanding data and decision-making in Artificial Intelligence. Students learn how
information measures like mutual information and KL divergence are used in learning, prediction, and
evaluation. The course emphasizes simple statistical reasoning to understand how information flows
through learning systems and how uncertainty limits performance. By the end, students will appreciate
why information theory forms a fundamental mathematical basis for modern AI methods.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to
1. Explain the basic concepts of information, uncertainty, and entropy.
2. Apply mutual information and KL divergence to compare probability distributions in learning
problems.
3. Analyze information flow in simple learning systems using Markov assumptions and the Data
Processing Inequality.
4. Describe how information flow and error bounds influence performance in basic classification
tasks.
