3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics, Strength of Materials
Course Objective
The course deals with the structural aspects of airworthiness that depend chiefly on the
strength and stiffness of the aircraft. The course investigates the stresses and displacements
in thin-walled open and single-cell closed section beams produced by bending loads for an
aircraft.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Categorize the structural components of aircraft
2. Estimate the airworthiness of an aircraft and its airframe loads
3. Analyze aircraft components and its structural idealization
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1.   Structural Components of Aircraft   07
Loads on Structural Components, Function of Structural Components,
Fabrication of Structural Components, Connections
2.   Airframe Loads   07
Aircraft Inertia Loads, Symmetric Maneuver Loads, Normal Accelerations
Associated with Various Types of Maneuver, Gust Loads
3.   Structural Idealization   06
Principle, Idealization of a Panel, Analysis of Open and Closed Section
Beams, Deflection of Open and Closed Section Beams
4.   Wing Spars and Box Beams   07
Tapered Wing Spar, Open and Closed Section Beams, Beams Having
Variable Stringer Areas
5.   Fuselages   05
Bending, Shear, Torsion, Cutouts in Fuselages
6.   Wings   08
Three-Boom Shell, Bending, Torsion, Shear, Shear Center, Tapered Wings,
Deflections, Cutouts in Wings
7.   Fuselage Frames and Wing Ribs   05
Principles of Stiffener/Web Construction, Fuselage Frames, Wing Ribs
Total   45
