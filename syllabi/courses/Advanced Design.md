Program: B. Tech. (Civil Engineering)   Semester: VIII
Course/Module: Professional Elective Course - V (Advanced Design   Module Code: BTCI08021
of Steel Structures)
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Term End

(Hours   (Hours   Examinations (TEE)

week)   week)   in Question Paper)
3   0   1   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Design of Steel Structures
Objectives:
1. To understand the concept of tubular sections in design of steel trusses
2. To understand the design concept of different type of steel water tank
3. To understand the design concept of lattice tower and steel chimney
4. To understand the design concept of gantry girders and plate girders
5. Use of various relevant IS codes for designing such special steel structures
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Perform the analysis and design of steel girders
2. Perform analysis and design of steel trusses using tubular sections
3. Analyse and design steel chimney and lattice tower
4. Design elevated water tanks
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Design of Tubular Sections: Properties of steel tubes, design of tension member and   04
compression member, design of welded connections, design of flexural members,
analysis and design of tubular trusses including purlins and supports.
2   Design of Plate Girders: Design of welded plate girder: proportioning of web and   08
flanges, flange plate curtailment, stiffeners and connections.
3   Design of Chimney: Forces acting on chimney, design of self-supporting welded   08
and bolted chimney and components including design of foundation. (consider the
effect of wind and earthquake).
4   Design of Lattice tower: Different configuration of lattice towers, loads acting on   06
lattice towers, Analysis of lattice tower, design of lattice tower including welded
or bolted connections for members by limit state method. (consider the effect of
wind and earthquake)
5   Design of Gantry Girders: Loads acting on gantry girder, Analysis of gantry   06
girder, design of gantry girder by limit state method.
6   Design of Moment Resisting Connections: Design of moment resistant bolted   04
and welded beam end connections by limit state method.
7   Elevated Steel Tanks and Staging: Loads acting on tanks including wind and   09
earthquake, design of circular tanks with conical bottom, supporting ring beam,
staging for circular tanks including design of columns and foundation, design of
rectangular steel tanks including design of staging, columns and foundation.
(consider the effect of wind and earthquake)
