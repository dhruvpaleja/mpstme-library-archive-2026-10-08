1. To impart knowledge of various characteristics of industrial wastewater on basis of type of
industry
2. To provide ability to analyse Quality and calculate Quantity of Industrial Wastewater
3. To understand and apply knowledge of wastewater treatment process for specific characteristics
of industrial Wastewater
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Differentiate various characteristics of industrial wastewater on basis of type of industry
2. Evaluate Quality and Quantity of Industrial Wastewater
3. Recommend wastewater treatment process for specific characteristics of industrial Wastewater
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Necessity for Industrial Waste Treatment: Introduction to Industrial Standards   09
for disposal of treated industrial wastewaters into water bodies, municipal
sewer and land, Standards for Disposal of industrial solid wastes and gaseous
emission from various industries and treatment systems such as
Incinerator etc.
2.   Characteristics of Industrial Waste: Industrial waste generation (solid & liquid   09
waste and gaseous emission) and their characteristics, variation in its Quality
and quantity, Estimation of capacity of equalisation tank. Industry specific
physico-chemical and biological treatment requirements, alternatives and their
evaluation in respect of treatment.
3.   Industrial Waste Treatment Typical Case Studies: Waste streams (solid, liquid   09
and gaseous), their characteristics and manufacturing processes of integrated steel
plant, sponge iron unit, alumina/aluminum manufacturing unit, copper smelter,
fertilizer plant, thermal power plant, distillery/brewery, paper/pulp industry,
tannery, textile unit and oil refinery
4.   Industrial Waste Treatment Methods: Methods of waste reduction such as   09
process modification, volume and strength reduction, segregation, reuse,
recycle, material conservation, good housekeeping. Neutralization, equalization,
Engineering precipitation and solidification.
Economic feasibility of joint treatment of raw industrial effluent with municipal
sewage. Need assessment and design of common effluent treatment plant for
industrial estates.
