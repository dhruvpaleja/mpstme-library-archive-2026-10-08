Objectives:
e To impart knowledge about engineering design and its place in society.
e To introduce the visual aspects of engineering design.
e To familiarize the aspects of engineering graphics standards.
e To be able to create solid models.
e Toapply computer-aided geometric design concepts and creation of working drawings.

Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
e Interpret and communicate drawings effectively using different types of curves, lines, planes.
e Analyse the concepts of projection and section of right regular solids with their development.
e Apply the techniques, skills, and modern tools to create projections of machine components
with the help of software.
