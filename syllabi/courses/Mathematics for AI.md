Course Objective
 Understanding and applications of Matrices, system of linear equations and vector spaces,
Understanding and application of Eigenvalue and eigenvector
 Gain familiarity with key ideas of precalculus, including the manipulation of equations
and elementary functions. (Self-study)
 Develop fluency with the preliminary methodology of tangents and limits, and the
definition of a derivative. (Self-study)
 Develop and practice methods of differential calculus with applications.
 Develop and practice methods of the integral calculus.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
 Representation and operation on different types of matrices. Representation of system of
linear equation in form of matrices to find solutions by different methods. Understanding
of vector space including orthogonal projection and its usage and application to solve
business problem. Understand eigenvalue, eigenvector, and their application to solve
business problem.
