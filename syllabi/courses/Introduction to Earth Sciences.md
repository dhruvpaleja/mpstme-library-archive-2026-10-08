Course Objectives
This course fosters a holistic understanding of different minerals, rock types and landscape types
with reference to composition and working of the Earth. It aims to apply knowledge of plate tectonic
theory to differentiate structural features in rocks, decipher seismicity probabilities and predict
geological hazards. It also aims to apply Earth Science theories to the Fundamentals of Geo-
Economics and use geological principles in civil engineering
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. Differentiate between various Earth materials, Earth processes and landscapes,
2. Relate Earth movements and Earth resources with respect to basic Structural Geology and
Tectonics,
3. Identify natural disasters based on geological parameters,
4. Utilize the geological knowledge to other core civil subjects.
