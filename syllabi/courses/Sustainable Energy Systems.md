Pre-requisite: Engineering Thermodynamics (BTME03012)
Objectives:
 To acquire basic knowledge on relevant energy technology.
 To have an overall understanding of energy systems.
 To understand the complex relationship between energy and society.
 To have a basic understanding of energy and electricity markets.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
 Understand the principles of operation of renewable energy technologies.
 Explain and demonstrate design and operating principles for solar, wind,
hybrid and emerging systems.
 Explore renewable energy storage methods and emerging renewable
technologies.
