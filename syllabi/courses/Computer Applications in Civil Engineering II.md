Pre-requisite: Computer Applications in Civil Engineering - I
Course Objective
The course aims to provide the knowledge, understanding and skills necessary to use relevant
software for Civil Engineering projects. It also aims at interpretation of the results obtained using
software and preparation of report.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Prepare a model for a part of infrastructure project in Civil Engineering
2. Analyse and design the Civil Engineering infrastructure project
3. Prepare a technical report of the project
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Importance and need of software for modeling, analysis and design in Civil
1   Engineering field, Advantages and limitations of software, causes for errors,   03
validation of software results. Failures due to errors in modeling, data entry and
interpretation of software results.

2   18
tools like STADD. Pro / Revit Architectural software / Water Gems.
3   Post Processing of the results obtained by the software   09
Total   30
Reference Books
1. Relevant Software manuals.
2. Relevant Indian standard codes.
Laboratory Work
Students will be engaged in a variety of in class assignments, group projects, and presentations
throughout the semester.
