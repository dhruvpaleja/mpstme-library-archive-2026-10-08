Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to :

e Understand and construct various types of Cam profile and analyze the
standard motion of the follower.

e Compare different type of gear and its applications.

e Design gear trains for different applications.

e Apply gyroscopic couple on stability of an automobile, airplane and ship.
