Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to give an introduction to the concept of Metaverse. It will also provide
information on Block chain Protocols and other diverse platforms and technologies involved in the
building of Metaverse.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Explain the concept, history, components, and benefits of the Metaverse, and identify the key
technologies enabling immersive digital environments.
2. Describe the role of Extended Reality (XR), including AR, VR, MR, 3D reconstruction, edge
computing, and interoperability protocols in building the Metaverse ecosystem.
3. Illustrate the integration of blockchain technologies, including smart contracts and tokenization,
and assess the need for decentralization within the Metaverse.
4. Analyze the use of Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) and cryptocurrencies in the Metaverse, and
demonstrate how NFTs are created, traded, and utilized across different MV applications.
5. Design and simulate personalized user experiences (e.g., avatars, virtual learning, gaming, e-
commerce) in the Metaverse using real-world use cases across industries.
