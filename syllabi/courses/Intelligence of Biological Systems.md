Course Objectives
This course offers an insight into the basic understanding of biological intelligence needed to
implement artificial intelligence. It also aims to enhance the knowledge of the basics of biology
for bio-inspired engineering and designs while focusing on the principles of bioinspired
algorithm implementation.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. recall basics of biological systems,
2. summarize the intelligence at the cellular level,
3. identify and discuss the intelligence of different biological systems,
4. summarize and categorize different forms of intelligence in different animals.
Detailed Syllabus: ( per session plan )
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to intelligence in plants and animals. Need for intelligence for   3
survival. Difference in animal intelligence and plant intelligence.
2.   Introduction to animal cell. Different functions of cell. Different cell
components and their functions. Different functions of cell like cell   5
biochemistry, cell metabolism, cell communication, and cell composition.
3.   Different types of intelligent body systems like-
Immune system, Hormonal system, Cardiovascular system, Muscular   5
systems etc.
4.   Different defence mechanisms of body, Sympathetic body systems that   3
understand different emotions, trauma, and external attacks. Intelligence of
instincts, gut intelligence.
