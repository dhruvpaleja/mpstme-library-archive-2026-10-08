Course Objective
The objective of this course is to introduce students to the foundational concepts and practices of data
wrangling and enable them to understand how raw data is systematically represented, transformed,
cleaned, explored, and validated for analytical use. The course aims to develop strong data-handling
skills by combining structured data manipulation with visual understanding and ethical, reproducible
data practices.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to-
1. Explain fundamental data structures and data operations used for representing and
manipulating structured data (K1)
2. Describe data transformation, cleaning, and reshaping techniques used in data wrangling
workflows (K2)
3. Apply exploratory and applied data wrangling techniques, including categorical handling and
visual data understanding, to analyze datasets (K3)
4. Analyze datasets to ensure data quality, ethical handling, and reproducibility in data
preparation processes (K4)
