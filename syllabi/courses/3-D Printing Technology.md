Pre-requisite: Nil
Objectives:
● Acquire introductory knowledge of 3D Printing (3DP) and its capabilities in the
modern digital manufacturing industry.
● To get acquainted with the operating principles and applications of most
prominent technologies in the field of 3D Printing
● Explore the Design for 3D Printing capabilities of each 3D Printing process and to
identify and create complex and novel designs.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
● Understand the fundamental principles of various 3D Printing technologies, material
requirements and cost considerations with regards to each 3DP process.
● Identify critical areas where 3DP can be applied to create value for the entire
product life-cycle.
● Apply the necessary skills in selecting the right 3DP process for a given application.
● Develop an enterprising perspective concerning the application of 3DP in the
design of a product, either entirely or partly.

reaction mechanism, scan patterns, applications, benefits and
