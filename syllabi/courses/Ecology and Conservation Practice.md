Pre-requisite: Introductory course in Environmental Studies, physics, chemistry, geography, biology
Objectives:
1. To investigate and describe the diversity of the living world
2. To understand the effects of human activities on species, communities, and ecosystems
3. To develop practical interdisciplinary approaches to protecting and restoring biological diversity
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Use contemporary tools and techniques for studying wildlife, habitat, and ecosystem processes;
2. Be familiar with a variety of laws and regulations that influence how natural resources are used
and protected
3. Understand the impacts of land use and environmental management decisions on ecosystems and
society
