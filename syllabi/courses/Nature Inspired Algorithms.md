Prerequisite: Programming for Problem Solving
Course Objective
This course provides knowledge of evolutionary computation techniques such as mechanisms of
natural evolution--including genetic recombination, mutation, and natural selection--to automatically
produce solutions to a wide range of problems. The students will explore several evolutionary
computation techniques and apply them to practical problems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the concepts Evolutionary computations and algorithms
2. Use the concepts of variation and selection techniques for particular classes of problems
3. Implement various evolutionary algorithms including ant colony optimization and swarm
optimization algorithms
4. Apply the principles of evolutionary and genetic programming for real life problems
