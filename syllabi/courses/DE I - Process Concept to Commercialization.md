Course Objective
The course focuses on the development of the entrepreneur skills in the chemical engineering students in
order to take up the lab scale concept to the commercial level. It will also emphasize on the incorporation of
sustainable production technology along with the safety and recyclable consideration.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Analyze the factors in scaling up of a chemical plant.
2. Develop the commercial process flow diagram based on a lab process.
3. Design the unit operation for large commercial plant.
