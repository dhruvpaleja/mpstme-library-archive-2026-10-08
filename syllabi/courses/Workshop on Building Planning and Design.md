Pre-requisite: Building Design and Drawing
Course Objective
This aims to acquire the skill sets to prepare computer aided architectural / Civil Engineering
drawings and knowledge on architectural planning aspects and building regulations. It also aims to
examine a drawing critically and with understanding of CAD.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Outline an architectural design idea
2. Plan and Design the buildings with the sketches
3. Create drawings using software

Unit   Description   Duration
