1. Demonstrate effective verbal communication by neutralizing accents, addressing Mother

etiquette.

and implement creative and effective solutions in a professional setting.
4. Apply writing skills to craft well-structured business emails and chats, following proper
etiquette and formatting to ensure clarity and professionalism in communication.
