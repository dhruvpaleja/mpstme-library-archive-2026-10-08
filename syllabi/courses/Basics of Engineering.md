Prerequisite: 10th Standard Science, Chemistry
Objectives:
The objective of this course is to introduce the basic principles of chemistry
including periodicity of elements, mole concept, and basic organic chemistry as well
as familiarizing with the concepts and applications of solution stoichiometry,
chemical bonding &organic reactions.
Outcomes: After completion of the course, students will be able to:
1. Compare the elements and their periodic properties,
2. Apply basic concepts of chemistry in various real time problems,
3. Analyse the fundamental of chemical bonding, hydrocarbon ,alcohols,
aldehydes, ketones and carboxylic acids,
4. Discuss the different effects of dilutions in aqueous solutions and solve
numerical based on stoichiometry.
