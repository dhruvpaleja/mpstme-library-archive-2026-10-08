Course Objectives:
1. To describe scientific facts, principals and technique of drawing in order to
visualize and express the ideas.
2. To know different curves used in engineering
3. To acquire the concepts of projections of an object
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Draw the different curves used in engineering.
2. Draw the projections of points and lines.
3. Draw the projections of solids and section of solid of machine component.
