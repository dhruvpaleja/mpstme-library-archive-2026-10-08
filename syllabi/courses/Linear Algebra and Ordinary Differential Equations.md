Pre-requisite: Knowledge of fundamental concepts in Algebra, Differential and
Integral Calculus.
Course Objectives
This course aims to intsill in prospective engineers knowledge of concepts and techniques in
Linear Algebra and Ordinary Differential Equations which enable students to deal with advanced
level of Mathematics and applications that would be essential for their disciplines.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. demonstrate understanding of the fundamental concepts of Linear Algebra and carry out
related computational skills,
2. use effective mathematical methods for solving ordinary differential equations,
3. analyse functions, matrices and equations,
4. apply Calculus techniques and Algebraic skills to solve real life problems.
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Linear Equations and Vector Spaces   10
Rank of Matrix, System of linear equations, Vector space, Subspace, Linear span,
Linear independence and dependence, Basis, Dimension, Extension of a basis of
a subspace.
2.   Linear Transformation and Eigenvalues   12
Linear transformation, Matrix associated with linear transformation, Kernel
and Range of a linear map, Rank-Nullity Theorem, Cayley- Hamilton
Theorem, Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, Eigenvalues of Symmetric, Hermitian
and unitary matrices, Diagonalization, Diagonalization of a real symmetric
matrix, Singular Value Decomposition.
