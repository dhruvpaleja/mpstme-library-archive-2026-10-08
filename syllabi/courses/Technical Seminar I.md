Pre-requisite: NIL
Course Objective
The Seminar course is designed to enhance the students' presentation and research skills. It provides
students an opportunity to explore contemporary topics in data science, conduct an in-depth literature
review, and present their findings to an academic audience. The seminar aims to develop critical
thinking, oral communication, and the ability to engage with technical discussions.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify and evaluate recent advancements or research trends in a chosen domain of data science
through comprehensive literature review.
2. Develop and deliver clear, concise technical presentations with effective oral communication
skills.
3. Synthesize research findings into a structured, well-documented technical report.
4. Demonstrate the ability to address technical questions and engage in meaningful discussions
during presentations.
Detailed Syllabus
The students of a group would contact the concerned faculty to find a mutually convenient 2 hour/
week schedule for the seminar course. The group would be required to meet their faculty instructor
regularly and seek advice on the topic of the seminar and guidance in various aspects of technical
presentation (Example: Composition of presentation, Length of presentation, Graphical Vs textual
aspects, Content density, etc.) The organization of the course can be divided into two parts. The initial
phase includes the students deciding seminar topics and receiving guidance on technical
communication aspects. This could also include mock presentations which could facilitate faculty and
peer feedback. The second part of the seminar course would include student presentations, spread over
several sessions; based on which grade is awarded.
