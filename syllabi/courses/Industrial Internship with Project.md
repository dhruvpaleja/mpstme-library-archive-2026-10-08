Pre-requisite: All subject of M Tech DS and M Tech AI
Course Objective
The course aims to immerse students in a full-time industrial setting where they can apply their
knowledge of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence to solve practical problems. The internship serves
as a platform for learning through experience, interaction with professionals, and exposure to real-world
datasets and business environments. Students will gain domain-specific knowledge, technical
proficiency, and insights into project management, communication, and collaboration.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Apply data science and AI tools, models, and techniques to solve real industry challenges.
2. Gain firsthand experience working within an industrial framework under professional guidance.
3. Understand the importance of project planning, time management, and ethical responsibility in
the workplace.
4. Collaborate effectively with teams, understand client requirements, and contribute to innovative
solutions.
5. Compile their learnings into a professionally written report as per institutional and industry
standards.
6. Demonstrate technical and communication skills through presentations and technical
documentation suitable for publication.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Initial Information Report
Students must submit this report within the first week of joining their internship.
1.   1 Month
It should include company details, mentor contact information, internship title, and
brief project description. It serves as a formal record of internship engagement.
Project Proposal
This document outlines the goals, scope, timeline, and expected outcomes of the
2.   internship project. It should be submitted after initial discussions with both   1 Month
industry and faculty mentors. The proposal must demonstrate clarity in objectives
and feasibility
Interim Report
Midway through the internship, students must submit a progress report that
3.   includes completed tasks, key observations, encountered challenges, and updated   2 Month
plans. This report is reviewed jointly by both mentors and includes feedback
sessions to steer project direction.

A visit by the faculty mentor is scheduled during the internship to assess student
4.   performance. If a physical visit is not feasible, an online meeting will be arranged.   1 Month
The student will present their progress in an internal seminar attended by industry
mentors, HR representatives, and academic supervisors.
Final Project Report, Presentation & Viva
Upon internship completion, students must submit a comprehensive final report
5.   (both printed and digital formats). The report should cover methodology, findings,   1 Month
challenges, and outcomes. It must follow the university's standard format. Each
student will also present their project and defend it in a formal viva voce.
Total   6 Months
Evaluation Components
 Initial Information Report (10%)
 Project Proposal (10%)
 Interim Report (20%)
 Final Report (30%)
 Presentation and Viva Voce (30%)
General Guidelines for Students
   Internship duration must be a full semester (minimum 6 months).
 Students should adhere to the rules and working hours of the host organization.
 Regular attendance and performance updates are mandatory. Monthly summaries should be
shared with faculty mentors.
 Maintain a professional demeanor and respect confidentiality clauses where applicable.
 Seek timely feedback and maintain open communication with both mentors.
 Ensure that all documents (proposal, reports) are submitted by the specified deadlines.
