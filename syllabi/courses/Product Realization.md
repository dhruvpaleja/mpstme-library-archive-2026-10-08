Course Objective
Students will get acquainted with the digital fabrication tools and methods. The course is aimed at
familiarizing students with various principles of 3D printing along with solid modelling, part slicing
and fabrication using fused deposition modelling (FDM) process.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to –
1. Understand Digital manufacturing concepts and various manufacturing methods.
2. Prepare CAD model and select suitable materials for manufacturing
3. Select different materials for product realization.
4. Apply principles of digital manufacturing to develop products of high technological value.
