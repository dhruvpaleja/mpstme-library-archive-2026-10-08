Pre-requisite: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics

Objectives:
1. To foster a holistic understanding of the composition and working of the Earth and be able to

differentiate between minerals, rock types and landscape types with reference to their genesis.
2. Application of plate tectonic theory to differentiate structural features in rocks, decipher
seismicity probabilities and predict geological hazards
3. Using Earth Science theories to understand the Fundamentals of Geo-Economics and the
history of the planet
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Differentiate between and understand Earth materials, Earth processes and landscapes.
2. Understand Earth movements and Earth resources in terms of basic Rock Mechanics,
Structural Geology and Tectonics.
3. Understand Disaster Management protocol based on geological parameters
4. Appreciate the 4.8 + billion-year history of the planet and get sensitized towards the planet
they live on and live off.
