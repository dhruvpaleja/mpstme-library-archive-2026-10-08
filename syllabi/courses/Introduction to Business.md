Objective:

1. Students will be familiar with basic rules of probability and will be able to use them
in modeling uncertainty in obtaining and recording data. They will be able to utilize
graphical and numerical summaries of data in understanding data generating
processes. To enable the students to analyse data more effectively using MS Excel.

2. To increase the student's ability in problem solving

Outcomes:

After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Describe basic concepts of probability and statistics
2. Analyse real data sets from business environments.
