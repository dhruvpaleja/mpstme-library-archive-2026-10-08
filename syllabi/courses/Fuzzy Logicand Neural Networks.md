Objectives:

1, To study the basic concepts of artificial neural networks, fuzzy logic systems

and their applications.
2. To introduce basic theory, algorithm formulation and ways to apply these
techniques to solve real world problems.

Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
1. Explain the basic concepts of artificial neural networks
2. Explain the basic theory of neural networks.
3. Formulate algorithms and apply techniques to solve various problems.
