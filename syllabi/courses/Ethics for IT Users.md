privacy and freedom of speech.

Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students will be able to :

e Describe various approaches to ethical decision making

e Manage ethical issues that arise at workplace

e Understand various cybercrimes

e Recognize various privacy issues

e Understand freedom of expression issues related to the use of IT
