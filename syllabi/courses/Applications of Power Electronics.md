Objectives:
1. To understand the effect of power electronic converters and their application in
power systems
2. To gain knowledge on the power quality problems and understand the
harmonics mitigation techniques.
Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, the students will be able to
1. Explore the role of power electronics as an enabling technology in various
applications such as flexible transmission systems, energy conservation,
renewable energy, power quality improvement etc.
2. analyze working of various static power converters for applications in power
quality improvement
3. learn basic design aspects for various compensation techniques supporting
power quality standards
