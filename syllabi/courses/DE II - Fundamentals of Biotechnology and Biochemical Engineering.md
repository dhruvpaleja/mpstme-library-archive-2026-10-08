Course Objective
This course aims to aquatint students with various fields of Biotechnology and their applications. This course
will also create a general understanding of enzymatic processes in chemical industry. It will also introduce
the concept of manufacturing and recovery of bio products.
Course Outcomes
After the successful completion of this course, the student will be able to –
1. Understand different application of Biotechnology and Fermentation Techniques.
2. Apply the knowledge of basic biochemical processes in chemical industry.
3. Design bioreactors.
