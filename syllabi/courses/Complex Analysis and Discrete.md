Pre-requisite: Knowledge of Basic concepts in Complex numbers, Number theory and
Algebra.
Objectives:
1. To train the students in the construction and understanding of mathematical proofs,
common mathematical arguments, complex functions and conformal mapping.
2. To prepare the students to use the basic concepts of discrete mathematics and complex
analysis in the subsequent courses of the program.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. define and relate basic notions of discrete mathematics and complex functions.
2. demonstrate the ability to understand concepts in abstract algebra , complex variables
and conformal mapping.
3. solve problems based on combinatorics, graph theory, group theory differentiation
and integration of functions of complex variables.
4. Apply various techniques of algebra, combinatorics, graph theory and complex
analysis in solving engineering problems.
