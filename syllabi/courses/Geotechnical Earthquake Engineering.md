Pre-requisite: Soil Mechanics; Foundation Engineering
Objectives:
1. To explain the mechanism of earthquake and its related causes to build structures and in-situ soils
2. To explain how seismic site investigation will be done and seismic soil design parameters are
estimated
3. To Understand the behaviour of soil slopes under seismic loading
4. To identify the soil improvement techniques for mitigation of seismic hazards
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Evaluate the significance of earthquake source, path, and site effects on the characteristics of strong
ground motions
2. Determine the influence of site-specific soil deposits on the characteristics of strong ground motions
3. Synthesize dynamic soil behaviour for application to seismic stability analyses
4. Evaluate ground improvement techniques for mitigating seismic hazards; and
