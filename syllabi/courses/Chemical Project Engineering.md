Objectives
This subject is providing basic knowledge of project engineering and stages
involved and the principal objective of this course to provide the understanding
of selection criteria and economic analysis of projects and to know cost
estimation, profitability evaluation, and financing of projects
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Classify, analyze, and design the chemical projects
2. Recognize the optimum design of the plant and its layout
3. Estimate the capital investment, operating cost
4. Evaluate the project profitability and product pricing
