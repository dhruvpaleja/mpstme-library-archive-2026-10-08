Objectives:
To provide the understanding of basics of links, mechanisms and machines.
To provide knowledge of motion analysis of mechanisms.
To impart basic knowledge of basic machine components like flywheel, belt,
ropes and chains.
Outcomes :
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
1   Understand classification of kinetics and kinematics based on relative motion
and inversion of chains.
Calculate the velocity and acceleration of given mechanisms graphically and
analytically.
Analyze the static and dynamic forces of flywheel for reciprocating prime
movers and machines.
Understand the importance of basic machine components like belt, ropes and
chains.
