1. Describe the working principle of electronic communication system and
discuss noise and its parameters.
2. Explain, Analyze and Compare the performance of various AM, FM, SSB and
ISB systems.
3. Define and explain various AM receiver parameters.
4, Explain, analyze and compare different pulse modulation techniques.
5. Differentiate the time division and frequency division multiplexing

techniques.
