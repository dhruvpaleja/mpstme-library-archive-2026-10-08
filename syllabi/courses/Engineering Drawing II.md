Course Objectives:
1. To get acquainted with the use of drafting software (CAD) in engineering
drawing.
2. To acquire the concepts of orthographic projections.
3. To know the concepts of isometric projections.

Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Generate orthographic projections of machine components with the help of
computer aided drawing (CAD) software.
2. Generate isometric projections of machine components with the help of
computer aided drawing (CAD) software.
3. Generate free hand drawings of machine elements.

Detailed Syllabus: ( per session plan )

Unit | Description Duration

1. | Orthographic projections: 08
Projections of various objects having flat and curved surfaces using
1st angle projection method only, Concept of Missing views.

2. | Sectional views of Orthographic Projections: 08
Conversion of pictorial view with sectional orthographic
projection (Full Section Only)

3. | Isometric Projections: 08
Isometric view and projection of linear and curvilinear features.
4. | Introduction to machine parts: 06
Types of nuts, bolts, screws, studs and riveted joints.
Total 30
