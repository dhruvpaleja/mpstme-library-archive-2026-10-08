Pre-requisite: NIL
Course Objective
The course aims to extend students’ ability to critically analyze state-of-the-art research in data science or AI,
encourage the integration of interdisciplinary knowledge, and promote innovative thinking. It also
emphasizes professional-level presentation, critical analysis, and technical writing skills required for
academic or industrial research settings.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Critically analyze and interpret advanced topics or real-world case studies in data science or AI
through extended literature review or preliminary experimentation.
2. Present complex technical content in a professional format, incorporating audience feedback and data
visualization tools effectively.
3. Create a structured seminar report and research paper that could evolve into a thesis proposal or
publication draft.
4. Participate in peer discussions with deeper insights and handle Q&A sessions reflecting domain
expertise.
Detailed Syllabus
Initial Phase:
1. Topic selection should focus on niche, advanced, or interdisciplinary areas.
2. Optionally include review of datasets, tools, or short proof-of-concept code (for applied relevance).
3. Workshops or tutorials on LaTeX, referencing tools, and advanced presentation tool (e.g., Canva,
Tableau, etc.).
Mid Phase:
1. Peer Review Sessions for mutual learning.
2. Fortnightly progress updates (as already practiced in Seminar-I).
Final Phase:
1. Final seminar presentation (assessed on depth, clarity, originality, and response to queries).
2. Submission of a seminar report and research paper preferably in IEEE or Scopus conference paper
format.
