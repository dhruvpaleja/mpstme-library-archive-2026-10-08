Pre-requisite: Basic Electrical Engineering and Basic Electronics
Objectives:

1. To understand the operation of power semiconductor devices.

2. To study converter circuits for AC-DC, DC-DC and DC-AC power conversion.
3, To understand the working of industrial drives.

4. To study different industrial electronic applications.

Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course students will be able to
1. Know power semiconductor devices for different applications.
2. Analyze and design inverter, controlled rectifier and chopper circuits.
3. Select and analyze drives for an industrial application.
4. Analyze and design power electronic circuits for industrial applications.
