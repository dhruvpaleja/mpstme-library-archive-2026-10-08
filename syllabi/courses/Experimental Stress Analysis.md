Program: B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: IX
Course: Experimental Stress Analysis   Code:
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Materials Engineering, Strength of Materials
Course Objective
The course covers the basic needs of engineers working in the area of stress analysis, important
concepts of theoretical and experimental techniques in stress-analysis. It also aims to impart a
comprehensive understanding of the stress and strain measurements with mathematical and
experimental perspectives.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Apply the theoretical concepts of stress and strain in measurement procedures
2.   Articulate principal stress measurement by the photoelastic technique on 2-Dimensional and
3-Dimensional objects
3. Apply strain gauges for strain measurement with understanding of different mounting
approaches
4. Analyze Moire fringe techniques and interferometry concepts to carryout stress analysis
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Elementary Elasticity   05
Review of Principal stresses and stress-strain relations, Generalized Hook’s
law, Equilibrium equations, Stress and strain measurements
2.   Strain Measurement   08
Requirements of strain measuring devices, Mechanical, Optical, Electrical and
Semiconductor strain gauges, Gauge Factor, Gauge materials, Backing
Materials, Adhesives, Protective Coatings, Bonding of Strain Gauges, Lead
wires and connections, Performance of Strain Gauges, Temperature
compensation, Transverse sensitivity, Gauge Length, Response, Excitation
level, Stability, Strain Gauge Circuits and recording instruments, Strain
Gauge Rosettes analysis, Stress Gauges.
3.   Photoelasticity Methods   10
Behaviour of light, Plane polarized and circular polariscope, Isochromatic
and isoclinic fringe patterns for two-dimensional photoelasticity, three-
dimensional photoelasticity, Model slicing and shear difference method,
Birefringent coating method.
4.   Moire Fringe Technique   08
Moire Method of Strain Analysis, Grid Method of Strain Analysis.
Holographic interferometry.

5.   Brittle Coating Method   10
Coating stresses, Failure theories, Brittle coating Crack patterns produced by
direct loading ,brittle-coating crack patterns produced by refrigeration
techniques, brittle coating crack, Pattern produced by releasing the load,
double crack pattern, crack detection, Load-Time relation and its influence
on the threshold strain, Effects of a biaxial stress field.
6.   Digital Image Correlation   04
Operating principle, Computation area (Facet), Speckle pattern, 2D and 3D
image correlation.
Total   45

2013.
