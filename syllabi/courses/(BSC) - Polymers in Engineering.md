Course Objectives
The objective of this course is to introduce students to basic and advanced concepts about
polymers, different techniques of polymerization used in industry, and familiarize them with
the analytical tools used to evaluate polymers.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. Explain basic concepts and classify different polymers,
2. Develop the relation between structure of polymers and their properties,
3. Evaluate the polymer structure using different analytical tools,
4. Discuss various polymerization techniques and specialty polymers used in industry.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction: Basic concepts & definitions: monomer & functionality,   06
oligomer, polymer, degree of polymerization, molecular weight & molecular
weight distribution, Classification of polymers, thermoplastics, thermoset,
tacticity, Glass transition temperature
2.   Thermoplastic & Thermosets: Structure, properties and applications of -   08
Polyolefins, Styrenic polymers, polyamides, acrylic polymers, polyesters,
epoxy resin, polyurethanes and silicones
3.   Polymerization techniques: Techniques of polymerization- bulk, solution,   08
suspension, emulsion, plasma etc. Different inititators and catalysts for
cationic, anionic, free radical polymerization and control over polymer
structure. Condensation polymerization, Copolymerization, Metathesis
polymerization. Polymer processing techniques like extrusion and molding.
4.   Structure Property Relationship: Effects of type of bonds, bond energy and   08
functional groups on properties of polymers. Molecular mass heterogeneity
and structure properties, polymer chain flexibility, crystallinity of polymers.
Thermal properties of polymers.
5.   Evaluation of Polymers:   Structural evaluation-   IR, NMR, Mass   08
spectrometry.   Molecular   weight   determination-   Gel   Permeation
Chromatography. Thermal properties- Differential Scanning Calorimetry &
TGA. Rheological properties- Scanning electron and transmission electron
microscopy, XRD.
6.   Advanced Polymers: Specialty polymers such as Conductive polymers,   07
liquid crystal polymers, smart polymers, IPN and biodegradable polymers
used in key industries including automobiles, smart devices, healthcare,
energy production and storage.
