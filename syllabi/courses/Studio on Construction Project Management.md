Pre-requisite: Quantity Surveying, Estimation and Valuation
Objectives:
1. To explore various aspects of Construction Management such as preparation of construction site
layout, preparation of time, material procurement, and manpower deployment schedule etc.
2. To understand project monitoring and controlling techniques
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand construction site layouts and prepare time, material and manpower schedules
2. Discuss project monitoring and controlling techniques
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Students to be given one or two Mini Projects involving some or all activities   40
like :
1. Preparation of site layout for a large construction project clearly defining the
project construction area and project services areas, area requirements, area
allocation, proper placement of each unit.
2. Study of tender documents and interpretation of terms, conditions and
specifications, BOQ etc.
3. Quantity estimation and rate analysis
4. Preparation of WBS - Identification of items to be included, preceding,
parallel and succeeding activities
5. Time Schedule of work: Liquidation of quantities, time estimation etc.
6. Material Procurement Schedule: Calculation of material quantities on a time
frame
7. Labour/ Manpower schedule: Requirement and smoothening for optimal
cost
8. Preparation of project cash flows, and monitoring with the help of S- Curve
Students may use Project Management soft-wares like MS Project etc.
2   Time estimation and rate analysis   08
3   Material Procurement: Lead time and quantity to be procured, inventory   08
4   Report and presentation   04
Total   60
