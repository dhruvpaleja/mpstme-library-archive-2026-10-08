Course Rationale:

Workshop Practice is a basic practical engineering course. Students are able to perform various operations
using hand tool equipment and machineries in various shops. Working in workshop develops the attitude of
group working and safety awareness. This course provides miniature industrial environment in the educational
institute

Course Objectives:
1. To impart hands on experience of different workshop practices on various trades.
2. To impart knowledge of basic tools used for different workshop jobs.

3. To introduce basic concepts of electrical and electronic instruments and its applications

Course Outcomes:

After completion of the course, students would be able to:

1. comprehend the safety measures required to be taken while using the tools. Fabricate components with
their own hands.

2. decide suitable manufacturing process required for the jobs.

3. assembling Electronics & Electrical components, they will be able to produce small devices of their
interest.

Pedagogy:
Lectures, Experiential learning activities, Work based learning
