After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Identify different types of conics to solve related problems.

2. Distinguish between permutations and combinations and apply the knowledge
to solve simple problems.

3. Demonstrate understanding of the fundamental concepts of vector algebra.

4. Solve basic application problems described by first order differential equations.

5. Apply calculus techniques and algebraic skills to solve real life problems.
