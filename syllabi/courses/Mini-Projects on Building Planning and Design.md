Pre-requisite: Building Design and Drawing
Objectives:

1. To achieve the skill sets to prepare computer aided civil engineering drawings.
2. To impart knowledge on architectural planning aspects and building regulations.
3. To impart knowledge on how to examine a drawing critically and with understanding of
CAD.
Outcomes:

After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Communicate a design idea/concept graphically/ visually

2. Gain a broad understanding of planning and designing of buildings.
3. Interpret and produce Building drawings using software.

Detailed Syllabus:
Unit | Description Duration
1 | Introduction: 12

Overview of any one of the CAD software’s prevailing in the market
(AutoCAD, Revit, 3D Max etc.). Introduction to computer aided drawing,
coordinate systems, and reference planes. Commands: Initial settings,
drawing aids, drawing basic entities, Modify commands, Layers, Text
and Dimensioning, Blocks. Drawing, presentation norms and standards.

2 | Project 1: 16
Building for Education: schools, colleges, institutions, libraries etc.

sectional drawing, Foundation plan, roof drainage plans ete.

Building for commercial complexes: office building, bank, library etc.
Details of building drawing required: Methods of making line drawing
and detailed drawing, Site plan, location plan, floor plan, Elevation and

Building for Industrial shed: Details of building drawing required:
