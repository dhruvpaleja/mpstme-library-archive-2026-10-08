Pre-requisite: Business Statistics
Course Objectives
 Understand the fundamental concepts and importance of forecasting in business contexts.
 Apply various quantitative and qualitative forecasting methods to real-world data.
 Evaluate and select appropriate forecasting models based on data characteristics and business
needs.
   Utilize software tools for data analysis and model implementation.
   Interpret and communicate forecasting results to inform strategic decisions.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the fundamental concepts and importance of forecasting in business contexts
2. Apply various quantitative and qualitative forecasting methods to real-world data
3. Evaluate and select appropriate forecasting models based on data characteristics and business
needs
4. Interpret and communicate forecasting results to inform strategic decisions.
