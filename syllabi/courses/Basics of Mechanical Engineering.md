Course Objective
The objective of the Basics of Mechanical Engineering course is to provide first-year engineering
students with a comprehensive foundation in the fundamental principles and applications of
mechanical engineering. The course aims to familiarize students with core concepts of
engineering materials, thermodynamics, manufacturing processes, energy systems, and
automobile engineering, while developing an understanding of the role of mechanical
engineering in modern industry and society. Emphasis is also placed on emerging technologies,
sustainability, renewable energy systems, and Industry 4.0 concepts to prepare students for future
technological challenges and multidisciplinary learning.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1.   Explain fundamentals, scope, and applications of mechanical engineering.
2.   Understand basic thermodynamic and heat transfer.
3.   Identify manufacturing processes, materials, and machine elements
4.   Describe conventional and renewable energy systems
5.   Recognize emerging mechanical technologies and sustainability challenges
Detailed Syllabus

Definition, scope, and importance of Mechanical Engineering; Branches of
Mechanical Engineering; Role of mechanical engineers in modern industries

Ferrous and non-ferrous metals, Polymers, ceramics, and composites; Smart
materials and shape memory alloys; Bio-materials and nano-materials;
Introduction to sustainable materials.

Thermodynamic system, surroundings, properties, state, and process; Zeroth
and First Laws of Thermodynamics (conceptual); Energy forms and energy
conversion.
Basics of Heat Transfer   04
Modes of heat transfer: conduction, convection, radiation. Waste heat recovery
systems; Energy efficiency and thermal management; Basics of hydrogen
energy and fuel cells; Applications in engineering systems
