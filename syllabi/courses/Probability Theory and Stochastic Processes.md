Objectives
The objective of this course is to develop the foundation of probability and improve the skills
required to analyze stochastic processes needed in different applications of engineering and
science such as disease modeling, climate prediction, computer networks etc.
Course Outcomes
After the successful completion of this course, the student will be able to-
1. Explain the concept of probability and random variables,
2. Compare different probability density functions and their applications,
3. Define random process and spectral density and analyse the response of linear, time-
invariant systems to random input signals or noise,
4. Estimate parameters of a probability distribution using maximum likelihood and mean
square error estimations.
