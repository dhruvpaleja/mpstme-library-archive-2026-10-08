Course Objective
The course introduces students to interrelationship between global and Indian energy economics and
politics and the policies developed to optimize energy production, utilization, minimization of energy
waste, and impact of energy on climate. The course familiarizes students to the principles of energy
management and audit.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Interpret the energy economics, politics and policies related to energy.
2. Relate the global and national sustainable actions to energy optimization
3. Understand the concept of energy management and audit, and learn its application as per industry
type and need.
