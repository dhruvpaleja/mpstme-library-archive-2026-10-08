Program: B. Tech (Civil Engineering)   Semester: VII
Course/Module: Professional Elective Course - III (Matrix   Module Code: BTCI07019
Method of Analysis of Indeterminate Structures)
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Term End
Tutorial   Internal Continuous
(Hours   (Hours   Examinations (TEE)
(Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)

week)   week)   in Question Paper)
3   0   1   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Theory of Structures
Objectives:
1. Evaluate the structural systems to application of concepts of flexibility and stiffness matrices for
simple problems.
2. Identify, formulate and solve engineering problems with respect to flexibility and stiffness matrices as
applied to continuous beams, rigid frames and trusses.
3. Identify, formulate and solve engineering problems by application of concepts of direct stiffness
method as applied to continuous beams and trusses.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Apply concepts of flexibility and stiffness matrices for simple problems.
2. Solve engineering problems with respect to flexibility and stiffness matrices as applied to continuous
beams, rigid frames and trusses.
3. Apply solution techniques of direct stiffness method to solve continuous beams and trusses.
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Element Flexibility Method:   15
Force transformation matrix, global flexibility matrix, analysis of continuous beams,
rigid frames and trusses.
2   Element Stiffness Method:   30
Displacement transformation matrix, global stiffness matrix, analysis of continuous
beams, rigid frames and trusses. grid
Total   45
Text Books:
1. G. S. Pandit, S. P. Gupta (2011), “Structural Analysis – A Matrix Approach”, Tata McGraw Hill
2. S. S. Bhavikatti (2013), “Structural Analysis – Volume II”, Vikas Publishing
Reference Books:
1. R. C. Hibbeler (2016), “Structural Analysis in SI Units”, Pearson
2. Reddy C. S. (2011), “Basic Structural Analysis – Third Edition”, Tata McGraw Hill
