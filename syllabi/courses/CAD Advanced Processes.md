Prerequisite: CAD Fundamental Processes
Course Objective
The CAD Advanced Processes course aims to enhance students' proficiency in the NX platform by
introducing advanced commands and modeling techniques. It focuses on transitioning from sketches
to 3D models, including raster image development. Students will gain expertise in face and edge
operations, along with fundamental freeform modeling. Additionally, the course covers assembly
modeling and the effective use of reference sets for complex designs.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to –
1. Gain the experience about the NX platform on advance commands.
2. Sketch to model and raster image development.
3. Understand face and edge operations and basics of freeform.
4. Learn assembly in models and using reference set.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Advanced Curve and Freeform Modeling Techniques in NX:   10
Documenting design intent (feature groups, product interfaces), editing
parametric models (replace features, suppression by expression, model
updates), associative curve operations (project, join, intersect, wrap/unwrap,
text), create curves and derived curves used in creating freeform features,
analyze curves and faces, create freeform shapes through curves, create freeform
shapes by sweeping sections along curves.
2   Advanced Embossing and Blending Techniques in NX:   10
Emboss geometry (emboss body, emboss, offset emboss), blending techniques
(advanced edge blend options, face blends), interpart references (interpart
expressions, overriding expressions), capturing part shape variations
(deformable parts), design optimization (optimization study, local and global
algorithms), create thicken and draft faces.
3   Modifying Detail Features Using Synchronous Modeling:   10
Introduction to synchronous modeling (move, pull, replace, delete face),
modifying detail features using synchronous (resize blend, chamfer, replace
blend), create transition and blend shapes, reusing and relating faces using
synchronous (pattern face, copy/paste face, dimensions), optional: history-free
synchronous (optimize face, adaptive, extend and offset faces.
