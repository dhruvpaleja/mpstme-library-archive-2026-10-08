Pre-requisite: Knowledge of Materials Engineering and Engineering Mechanics

Objectives:
1. To understand the impact of different types of stresses on components
2. To introduce the fundamental concepts of design of simple machine parts.

Outcome:

After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
1. Select appropriate materials for safe design.
2. Understand the principle and basic procedure of machine component design.
3. Analyze the different types of loads and stresses acting on components
