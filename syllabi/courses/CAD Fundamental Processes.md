Prerequisite: Nil
Course Objective
The CAD Fundamental Processes course aims to introduce students to the NX platform, providing hands-
on experience with its interface and essential commands. It focuses on developing an understanding of
design intent, model creation, and geometry evaluation. Students will learn to generate accurate 2D
drawings and explore drafting techniques. Additionally, the course covers 3D modeling, visualization,
and engineering graphics concepts to enhance their CAD proficiency.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the students will be able to –
1. Gain the experience about the NX platform.
2. Understand NX software its interface and command use.
3. Understand design intent, model development and geometry evaluation.
4. Learn about how to produce 2D drawings and drafting methods.
5. Learn about how to produce 3D views and Engineering Graphics concepts and drafting methods.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1   Introduction to NX and User Interface:   06
Work with NX part files and NX template files, understand how to effectively use
the NX user interface and work space, create sketches to capture design intent, create
reference geometry for model development such as datum planes, axes, and
coordinate systems.
2   Creating Basic Features and Geometry in NX:   06
Create basic features by sweeping and extruding geometry, analyze feature
geometry, add detail to features such as blends and drafts, produce assemblies of
component parts, produce annotated 2D drawings of models.
3   Advanced Part Modeling and Feature Editing in NX:   06
Working with existing parts, datum features, swept features, trim Body, hole
features, expressions.
4   Coordinate Systems and Part Navigation in NX:   06
Coordinate systems, part navigator, associative copies, face and edge operations,
basic freeform.
5 NX Assembly Design: Constraints, References, and Modifications   06
Creating and modifying assemblies, assembly constraints, assembly arrangements,
reference sets, interpart geometry and references, component arrays, reuse library,
revise and replace components.
Total   30
