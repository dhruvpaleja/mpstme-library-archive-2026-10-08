Pre-requisite: Building Design and Drawing
Objectives:

1. To achieve the skill sets to prepare computer aided civil engineering drawings.

2. To impart knowledge on architectural planning aspects and building regulations.

3. To impart knowledge on how to examine a drawing critically and with understanding of
CAD.

Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Communicate a design idea/concept graphically/ visually

2. Gain a broad understanding of planning and designing of buildings.
3. Interpret and produce Building drawings using software.
