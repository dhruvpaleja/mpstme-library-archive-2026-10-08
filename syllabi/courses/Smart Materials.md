Pre-requisite: Physics (BTME01003), Chemistry (BTME02010), Materials Engineering
(BTME03010)
Objectives:
 To introduce the concept smart materials.
 To provide a detailed overview of smart materials, structures, characteristics and
their applications.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
 Understand structure and characteristics of smart materials.
 Select smart materials as sensors and use them in smart structures.
 Design complex systems using smart materials.
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Introduction: Introduction to Smart Materials, Historical Overview   6
of Piezoelectric Materials, Shape Memory Alloys, and Electroactive
Polymers, Recent Applications of Smart Materials and Smart Material
Systems, Smart Material Properties.
2   Shape Memory Alloys (SMA): Introduction, One-Way Shape   8
Memory Effect, Two-Way Shape Memory Effect, SMA Systems-Fe-
based Alloys; Cu-based Alloys; Composite Materials; Hybrid
Composites, Material Processing Techniques, SMA Applications,
Future Trends and Prospects.
3   Nanotubes and Nano Structured Materials (NSM): Growth and   10
Fabrication of nanotubes, Properties: Mechanical, Composite-
Reinforced, Electrical, Thermal and Dialing-Up Properties, Welding of
Nanotubes.
Structure and Properties of NSM, Nanocrystals and Nanocrystalline
Materials, Nanocrystalline Processing and Nanocomposites.
4   Functionally Gradient Materials (FGMs): Introduction, Definition
and Design of FGMs, Material Combinations, Fabrication/Processing
6
Methods and Properties. Applications - Dental Implant; Building
Materials; Ballistic Protection; Temperature Sensors.
5   Smart polymers: Thermally responsive polymers, Electroactive   8
polymers microgels, Synthesis, Properties and Applications, Protein-
based smart polymers, pH-responsive and photo-responsive
polymers, Self-assembly, Molecular imprinting using smart polymers,
Approaches to molecular imprinting, Drug delivery using smart
polymers
