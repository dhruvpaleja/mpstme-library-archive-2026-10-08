Course Objective
The principal objective of this course is to introduce basics of backend of drafting packages and
to give hands on experience on the 3D modeling software. Introduction of Solid Modeling course
aims to provide hands-on experience with its interface and essential commands. It focuses on
developing an understanding of design intent, model creation, and geometry evaluation.
Students will learn to generate accurate 3D drawings and explore drafting techniques.
Additionally, the course covers assembly modeling and the effective use of reference sets for
complex designs.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Analyze CAD system architecture and its engineering approaches
2. Develop geometric models of complex engineering systems and apply these models in
various engineering applications
3. Apply transformation techniques to solve problems related to 2D and 3D objects
4. Design engineering models using parametric geometry and CAD modeling approaches,
including curves, solid and surface modeling
5. Utilize assembly modeling techniques in SOLIDWORKS and design complex engineering
assemblies.
