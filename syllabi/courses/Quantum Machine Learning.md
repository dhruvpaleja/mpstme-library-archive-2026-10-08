Pre-requisite: Programming, Managing Uncertainty, Foundation of Machine Learning
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to introduce the students with fundamentals of Quantum Machine Learning.
It will enable students to relate elements from classical Machine Learning techniques with Quantum
computing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understand key concepts of quantum computing including qubits, gates, measurement, and
quantum parallelism. (L2 – Understand)
2. Apply data encoding methods like amplitude encoding and Hamiltonian simulation on quantum
systems. (L3 – Apply)
3. Analyze quantum algorithms such as Deutsch-Jozsa, Bernstein-Vazirani, and Simon using Qiskit
and Cirq. (L4 – Analyze)
4. Apply and evaluate variational circuits for quantum machine learning, including gradient-based
optimization. (L3 – Apply, L5 – Evaluate)
5. Analyze and implement quantum ML models using kernel methods and algorithms like HHL,
SVM, and k-means. (L4 – Analyze, L5 – Evaluate)
