Course Objective
To sensitize students about the importance of operations and supply chain management in
a business organization. To impart students with the knowledge on basic operations
function, operations management and productivity related issues and equip them with
necessary skills to analyze them with effective corrective action.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify, appreciate and effectively deal with operational issues in business
2. Comprehend different production systems along with a learning on Product-Process
Matrix and job sequencing and scheduling methods
3. Distinguish the processes, their types, layouts, process analysis and their applications
and carry out a detailed study on selection of suitable locations for manufacturing
plants, facilities and service centers
4. Undertake a detailed study and learning of Inventory management systems and
different inventory control models with their applications
5. Describe Quality Management and Statistical Process Control concepts and its
application in an organization and expand on the Theory of Constraints
