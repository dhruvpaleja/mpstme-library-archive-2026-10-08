Course Objective
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to Line Designer in NX, covering fundamental
modeling techniques and the use of connectors for seamless integration. Learners will develop
skills in factory layout creation, including planning space conception, positioning components,
and adding resources & stations. Additionally, the course explores Bill of Equipment creation,
Plant Navigator functionalities, and validation & reporting in both standalone and managed
modes.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to –
1. Understand Line Designer, NX Modelling Basics and Connectors
2. Create Bill of Equipment and Factory Layouts
3. Plan Space Conception and Position Components
4. Add Resources, Stations and Plant Navigator
5. Validate and make a Report
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Basics of Line Designer: Features and Workflow:   05
Basics of line designer, introduction to line designer, basics of CAD,
import resources.
2.   Connector Usage and Asset Management in Line Designer:   05
Layout planning, connectors in line designer, working with assets.
3.   Factory Explorer and Library Management:   07
Creating factory layouts, using the factory explorer and factory libraries,
importing tooling and product geometry.
4.   Positioning Components in a Kinematic System:   07
Robotics and kinematics, basics of kinematics in CAD, positioning
components, add resources, building custom parametric objects.
5.   Overview of Plant Navigation and Management:   06
Plant navigations, overview of plant navigations, validation & reporting,
managed mode.
