Course Objective
The paramount objective of this course is to cultivate a profound comprehension of applications
that integrate a diverse array of multimedia elements including textual content, graphical
illustrations, imagery illustration such as AR , auditory /video components and animations.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1.   Understand basics of Multimedia technology
2. Apply various tools for Image editing
3. Apply various tools for Audio/ Video
4.   Develop Animation with Action script

• Motion Blocks: Move sprites, rotate them, or glide to a position.
