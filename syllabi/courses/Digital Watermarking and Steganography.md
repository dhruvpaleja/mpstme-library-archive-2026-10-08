Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objective
The main objective of this course is to provide introduction to various techniques used for digital
watermarking and steganography. Another goal is to introduce various digital image processing
techniques to students. Students will learn about different attacks to digital watermarking and
steganography systems and their countermeasures.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Describe various digital image processing techniques
2. Implement various digital watermarking and steganography techniques
3. Discuss various attacks on digital watermarking and steganography and suggest suitable
countermeasures
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   04
Information Hiding, Steganography, and Watermarking, History of
Watermarking and Steganography, Importance of Digital Watermarking,
Importance of Steganography. Applications and Properties: Applications of
Watermarking, Applications of Steganography.
2.   Introduction to Digital Image Processing Systems   02
Introduction, Image acquisition, grey scale and colour adoption and
discrimination, Image sampling and quantization, basic relationship between
pixels.
3.   Image Processing in the Spatial domain   03
Spatial domain point operation and Neighbourhood Operation, Grey-Level
Transformation, Median Filter, Bit plane slicing, Histogram.
4.   Image Processing in the frequency domain   03
Frequency-domain filters: smoothing and sharpening filters, homomorphic
filtering.
5.   Steganography   08
Techniques: Substitution systems, Transforms domain Techniques, Statistical
methods, Distortion techniques, Steganographic tools.
Attacks
Stego-only, known cover, known message, chosen stego, chosen message,
known stego, method of to extract hidden information.
6.   Watermarking Techniques   05
Spatial, Simple spectral, Spread spectrum.

7.   Watermark security and authentication   05
Security requirements – Watermark security and cryptography – Attacks –
Exact authentication – Selective authentication – Localization – Restoration.
Total   30

Edition, CRC Press, 2020.

Watermarking, Artech House, 2022.
2. Abid yahya, Steganography Techniques for Digital Images, Springer, 2019.
