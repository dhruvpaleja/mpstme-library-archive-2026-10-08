Prerequisite: Domain Knowledge of relevant stream
Course Objectives
To equip students with industry exposure, enabling them to understand various industrial aspects,
identify key challenges, analyze situations, and effectively solve engineering problems relevant to the
industry.
Course Outcomes
After completing the course, students will be able to:
1. Describe industrial practices and work culture to bridge the gap between theory and practice with
insights from industry professionals.
2. Demonstrate effective communication skills to comprehend and engage in industrial practices.
3. Identify and follow discipline, safety norms, and environmental regulations in an industrial setting.
4. Interpret and analyse industrial problems to suggest appropriate solutions.
5. Collaborate effectively within a team to achieve common industrial objectives.
6. Recognize the importance of lifelong learning and adaptability in a dynamic industrial environment.
Guidelines
Internship Trainee’s Code of Conduct:
Trainee is required to:
a.   Join on the stated date and complete the training as specified by the industry.
b.   Fill the Joining Report, get it endorsed by the concerned Industry Official and email the scanned copy
within three days of joining to the Placement Department and Faculty Mentor.
c. Adhere to all the rules and regulations, safety norms of the industry and thereby ensure professional
conduct.
d. Take instructions from the Industry Mentor on a daily basis and complete the same with due diligence
and quality.
e.   Fill the Weekly logbook, get it endorsed by the concerned Industry Official and then email the scanned
copy to the Faculty Mentor on regular basis.
Mentoring process:
a.   Every trainee shall be allocated two Mentors: An Industry Mentor and a Faculty from the
Department (appointed by Head of the Department).
b.   Faculty Mentor shall connect with the Industry Mentor and the Trainee on a Periodic basis.
