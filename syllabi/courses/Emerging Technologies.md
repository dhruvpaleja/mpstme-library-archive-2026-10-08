Course Objective
The primary objective of this course is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding
of the emerging technologies that are shaping modern mechanical engineering. It aims to develop
the learner’s ability to appreciate, analyze, and apply contemporary advancements - such as
Industry 4.0 systems, IoT integration, renewable energy solutions, smart materials, sustainable
engineering practices, nanotechnology, and 3D Printing - in diverse mechanical applications. The
course intends to cultivate technological awareness, interdisciplinary thinking, and readiness for
future industrial transitions.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the role and impact of emerging technologies in modern Mechanical Engineering
applications
2. Describe the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and
Internet of Things (IoT), and their applications in mechanical systems
3. Analyze renewable energy technologies and sustainable waste management practices with
respect to environmental and industrial requirements
4. Explain green technologies, sustainable materials, nanotechnology, and smart materials,
and their applications in sustainable engineering systems
5. Demonstrate conceptual understanding of advanced manufacturing and aerospace-related
technologies including drones, nanomaterials, smart systems, and 3D printing technologies.
