Objectives:
This is an introductory course in Information Technologies. This course is designed to allow
students to understand the importance of Information and communication enable services
and sectors. Information and communication technologies (ICTs) thus represent the fastest,
broadest and deepest technical change experienced in international development. The
objective is to encourage the development of Technologies so as to enable students to
know about every development sector like farming, healthcare , political , teaching and
also environment.
Outcomes:
After completion of this course, students will be able to
1. Discuss the core relation between ICTs and development.
2. Compare the underlying components needed for ICT4D to work.
3. Analyse the various practice in implementing ICT4D.
