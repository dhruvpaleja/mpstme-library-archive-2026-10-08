Objectives
To make aware the environmental and social impact due to various types of pollutants
and to asses environmental and social impact due to new projects. It will also include
the concept of Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) for evaluation of the environmental
footprint of various systems and technologies.
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Assess the environmental footprint of projects, products and systems.
2. Understand LCA and EIA process for new projects, products and systems
3. Apply the knowledge of LCA and EIA
