Pre-requisite: Knowledge of Basic Electronics and Electromagnetic Wave Theory.
Objectives:
1. To introduce to the students the fundamentals of active & passive components and
circuits used at Radio Frequencies
2. To teach the basic concepts of Transmission lines, Microstrip lines.
3. To use smith chart to various design problems in RF.
4. To be able to design RF filters.
Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
1. Describe the active and passive components and circuits at Radio Frequencies.
2. Compare the parameters of different types of transmission lines.
3. Use Smith chart for analysis and design of RF networks.
4. Design different types of filters for RF range.
