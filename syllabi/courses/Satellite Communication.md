Pre-requisite: Communication Theory and Systems, Electromagnetic Field Theory
Course Objective
The aim of this course is to familiarize students with the fundamentals of satellite communication and
Radar systems and their current applications. Students will be exposed with examples of applications
and trade-offs that typically occur in engineering system design with respect to spacecraft launching
and detection of targets using Radar systems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the structure, techniques, technology and key organizations involved in satellite
communication and Radar systems.
2. Design and plan satellite communication links for a prescribed Quality of Service.
3. Classify the various satellite subsystems and their functionality and able to understand
effects of scintillation.
4. Apply knowledge of existing satellite systems and state of the art technology to the design of
future systems.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Satellite orbital mechanics and launchers
Overview of satellite communication, frequency allocations, types of satellite,
orbits and their applications, orbital mechanics- developing the equations of the   07
orbit, Kepler’s three laws of planetary motion, describing the orbit of a satellite,
locating the satellite in the orbit, locating the satellite with respect to the Earth,
orbital elements, look angles, orbital perturbations, launching vehicles.
2.   Satellite link design and propagation effects
Introduction, transmission theory, system noise temperature and G/T ratio,   07
calculation of system noise temperature, noise figure, G/T for earth stations,
design of downlinks, link budgets, uplink design, combining uplink and
downlink carrier to noise ratios.
3.   Satellite subsystems
Attitude and orbit control system (AOCS), telemetry, tracking, command and   06
monitoring system (TTC&M), power systems, communication subsystems,
transponders- Single conversion and double conversion, satellite antennas.
4.   Satellite services and navigation
Introduction to Satellite Mobile Services, Mobile Satellite (MSAT), Very small   06
aperture terminal (VSAT) systems, Direct broadcast satellite (DBS) TV, Global
positioning systems (GPS)- position location principles, GPS segments, GPS
signal structure, GPS receiver, Indian Regional Navigation Satellite System
(IRNSS).
5.
