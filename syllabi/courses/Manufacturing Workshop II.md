Pre-requisite: Manufacturing Workshop-I
Course Objective
This course is useful for gaining practical knowledge of manufacturing processes taking care of
safety and standard operating procedures to fabricate jobs. The students would apply the
theoretical principles to understand the design requirements of the job.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to –
1. Interpret the requirements for machining jobs for different types of operations
2. Create a machine part using different operations
3. Apply safety practices for the manufacturing processes
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Safety of machines and job preparation employing turning, facing, drilling   08

2.   Gear cutting on milling machine and square key cutting on shaping machine.   12
