Course Objectives

This course is aimed to teach the drawbacks of classical physics in explaining several experimental
observations and old quantum theory; and to discuss the necessity of new mechanics and the laws
related to it.

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. define and illustrate the basic laws related to quantum and statistical mechanics,
2. interpret the concepts related to quantum and statistical mechanics to explain observed
phenomena in nature,
3. apply the concepts of quantum and statistical mechanics to solve different engineering
problems.
