Objectives:
1. Demonstrate applications of AI in chemometrics and chemical systems.
2. Appreciate chemical reaction patterns using predictive modelling and chemometrics.
3. Analyze the chemical data and cybersecurity concerns in vaccine delivery systems.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. understand the scope of AI in chemometrics and chemical data processing.
2. evaluate patterns in chemical reactions using expert systems and neural networks.
3. interpret spectroscopic data obtained from various analytical techniques.
4. apply AI in vaccine development and delivery systems during the pandemic.
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1. AI and Chemometrics   8
Definition, scope, significance and applications of chemometrics, modern
analytical techniques (chromatography, UV-Visible, IR, NMR, MS), unifying AI
in Chemistry, instrumental response and modern chemical data processing
methods.
2.   Computers as Scientists   9
Intelligent laboratory systems, chemometrics in spectroscopy, modern
machines learn chemical patterns, expert systems, dendritic algorithms.
3. Machine learning for drug development & vaccine delivery systems   10
Predictive modelling of molecular properties, QSAR relationships, Latent
Dirichlet allocation, fragmentation algorithms, Chemical 2D fingerprinting,
Interpretation of spectra of various drug molecules, molecular electronics.
4.   Automation in chemical synthesis   10
Nature of chemical data: Organic synthesis of selected drug molecules,
molecular descriptors, automated chemical lab bench.
SVKM’s NMIMS
Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering
Retrosynthesis: predicting reaction outcomes for organic reactions (aldol,
hydrolysis and substitution), optimizing reaction conditions using advanced
expert systems.
5.   AI, chemometrics and COVID - 19 outbreak   8
COVID-19 outbreak and cybersecurity concerns in drug discovery and
development, role of vaccines in human health, machine learning and deep
learning in contact tracing and vaccine development pipelines.
