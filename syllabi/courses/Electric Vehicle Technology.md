Course Objective
The course is designed to familiarize the students with the essential knowledge of modern transportation
issues and sustainable mobility. It highlights new technologies that can be adopted such as electric vehicles,
hydrogen fuel systems and smart mobility platforms and their impact to the environment, economy and
society. This is also to sensitize students about sustainability targets and to help them assess sustainable
mobility solutions to future transportation needs.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1.   Differentiate the various modes of transportation and their environmental impacts.
2. Explain the working principles and components of electric and alternative fuel-based mobility systems
3. Summarize the different sustainable mobility technologies and smart transportation solutions
4. Apply basic economic and environmental assessment tools to compare mobility options
5. Analyze the innovative, sustainable transportation solutions considering technical, societal, and policy
constraints
