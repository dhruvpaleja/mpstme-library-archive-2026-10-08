Outcomes:

After completion of this course, students would be able to:

Describe basic design philosophies of reinforced concrete structures

Design the beams of rectangular and flanged sections

Design simply supported one way, two way and cantilever slabs

Design columns and isolated column footings subjected to axial loads and
moments
