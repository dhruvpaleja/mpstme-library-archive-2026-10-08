Course Objective
The course aims to develop life and social skills to equip students to deal with life’s demands and
challenges effectively; it encourages them to establish life goals and develop robust social skills
and inter-personal relationships to ensure holistic development of self.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate self-awareness to deal with challenges, fears and insecurities effectively
2. Develop interpersonal and leadership skills for self-empowerment and empowerment of
others while working individually or in group.
3. Determine appropriate life goals with managing stress and time effectively.
