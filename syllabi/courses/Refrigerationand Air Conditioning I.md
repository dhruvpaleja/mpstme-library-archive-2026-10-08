Objectives:
To impart the knowledge of working of vapour compression and vapour
absorption refrigeration systems.
To provide the understanding of different processes in psychrometry and
air-conditioning, and introduce cooling Ioad calculations, duct design
methods, fans and blowers.
To introduce the applications of refrigeration and air conditioning.
Outcomes:
After successfulcompletion of the course, the students should be able to
Understand the working of and indicate vapour compression refrigeration
cycle on T-s, h-s and p-h diagrams and calculate its COP.
Understand the properties of binary mixtures and the working of amrnonia-
water and water-lithium bromide vapour absorption refrigeration systems
and evaluate their performance.
Represent different air-conditioning processes on psychrometric chart and
calculate loads.
Design ducts for air-conditioning.
Understand the refrigeration requirements for applications such as food
processing and preservation and air-conditioning requirements for industrial
and commercial applications.
