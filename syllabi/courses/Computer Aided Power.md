Objectives:
1, To impart knowledge in computer application for modeling of practical and large
interconnected power system networks power system operation and its control.
2. To study parameters for monitoring and control of power system in real time.
3. To be proficient with computer algorithms pertaining to power system to assess
the stability performance.

Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, the students will be able to
1. Assess the recent techniques and computer application for modeling of practical
and large interconnected power system networks using programming languages.
2. Compare recent methodologies for simulation and analysis of power system
networks like real and reactive power flows and optimal scheduling.
3, Understand effect of outage of any important component of power system on the
operation and reliability of power systems.
4, Build algorithm required to find out parameters for monitoring and control of
power system in real time from actual: measurement data.
5, Solve and build computer algorithms used to solve algebro-differential
pertaining to power system to assess the stability performance of power systems,
