Program: B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: IX
Course: Metal Forming Processes   Code:
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End

week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Manufacturing Processes – I & II , Materials Engineering
Course Objective
This course provides comprehensive understanding and analysis of conventional metal forming
processes such as forging, rolling, extrusion, sheet metal working and also includes recently developed
forming processes.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Connect the forming processes with their defects and remedies to avoid defective products
2. Analyse the different metal forming operations like forging, rolling, blanking, coining and
extrusion
3. Summarize the advanced metal forming processes used in the industries
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Fundamentals of Metal Forming   09
Introduction of forming processes. Concept of Formability, formability limits and
formability diagram. Wire and Tube Drawing- Introduction rod and wire drawing
machines - construction and working. Preparation of stock for wire drawing. Wire
drawing dies, material and design. Heat treatment, variables in wire drawing,
Maximum reduction in wire in one pass, forces required in drawing. Multiple
drawing, defects in drawing and remedies, work hardening, lubrication in wire
drawing. Tube drawing: Methods, force calculations. Lubrication in tube drawing.
2.   Forging   07
Introduction, classification of forging processes, forging equipment- Hammers,
presses, furnaces etc. construction working capacities and selection of equipment.
Basic forging operations such as drawing, fullering edging, blocking etc.
Forgeability tests, design of forging as a product, friction in forging. Forging defects
and the remedies. New technologies: liquid metal forging, isothermal forging, draft
forging, Rotary swaging, Roll forging, lubrication in forging.
3.   Rolling of Metals   08
Scope and importance of rolling. Effect of grain direction in rolling Types of Rolling
Mills- construction and working. Deformation in rolling and determination forces
required. Process variables, redundant deformation, Roll bite and friction. Roll
flattening, Roll camber - its effect on rolling process, mill spring. Defects in rolling.
Automatic gauge control- Roll pass classification & design. Lubrication in rolling.

4.   Sheet Metal Working   07
Sheet Metal properties, gauges and surface conditions. Various cutting and forming
operations, types of dies used, force requirement, theory of shear, methods of force
reduction, defects, lubricants used. Miscellaneous sheet metal working operations:
Metal spinning, fine blanking, coining, embossing, rubber forming, stretch forming.
Coining Embossing, Curling, Spinning and fine blanking.
5.   Extrusion   07
Extrusion types: Direct, reverse, impact, hydrostatic extrusion. Dies for extrusion,
stock penetration. Extrusion ratio of force equipment (with and without friction),
metal flow in extrusion, defects. Role of friction and lubricants. Manufacture of
seam-less tubes.
6.   Advanced Metal Forming Processes   07
Introduction to High velocity forming- principles, comparison of high velocity and
conventional forming processes. Explosive forming, Magnetic pulse forming,
electro hydraulic forming. Stretch forming, flow forming advantages, limitations
and application of the process.
Total   45
