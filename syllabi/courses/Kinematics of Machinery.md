Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics (BTIME03011)
Objectives:
 To understand different machines mechanisms and its motion analysis.
 To analyse the velocity, acceleration of links and joints of mechanisms.
 To analyse cam follower motion for different motion specifications.
 To understand the working of spur gears and to analyse gear trains
Outcomes :
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
 Understand classification of kinetics and kinematics based on relative motion
and inversion of chains.
 Analyze instantaneous centers and to calculate the velocity and acceleration
using graphical and analytical method.
 Identify various types of Cam profile and the standard motion of the follower.
 Outline the working of spur gears and to analyze gear trains effectively.
