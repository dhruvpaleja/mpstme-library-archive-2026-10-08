Pre-requisite: Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulics; Hydraulics and Fluid Machinery
Objectives:
1. To introduce various hydraulic problems related to open channel flow
2. To understand GVF,RVF and surges and relate the theory and practice of problems in
hydraulic engineering
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Apply their knowledge of fluid mechanics in addressing problems in open channels.
2. Possess the skills to solve problems in uniform, gradually and rapidly varied flows in steady
state conditions.
3. Possess the skills to solve problems in surges
Detailed Syllabus: (Per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Gradually Varied Flow: Dynamic Equation   of Gradually Varied   10
Flow, Classification of channel bottom slopes, Classification of surface
profile, characteristics of surface profile.
2. Water surface profile: Computation of water surface profile by graphical,   08
numerical and analytical approaches. Direct Step method, Graphical
Integration method and Direct integration method.
3.   Non-Uniform Flow : Specific energy, Specific energy curve, critical flow,   10
discharge curve Specific force Specific depth, and Critical depth.
Channel transitions. Measurement of Discharge and Velocity – Venturi
Flume, Standing Wave Flume, Parshall Flume, Broad Crested Weir.
Measurement of Velocity- Current meter, Floats, Hot-wire anemometer.
4.   Hydraulic Jump: Theory of Hydraulic jump, Elements of characteristics of   10
hydraulic jump in a rectangular channel, Length and height of jump, location
of jump, Types, applications and location of hydraulic jump. Energy
dissipation and other uses.
5.   Waves and Surges: Unsteady flow, Types of Surges, Positive surge, Negative   07
surge, Celerity
Total   45
