week)   week)
0   2   0   1   Marks Scaled to 50   -
Pre-requisite: None
Course Objectives: This course equips students with the essential skills of critical thinking,
problem solving, and design thinking to foster a mindset of innovation. Students will learn how
to analyze problems, generate creative solutions, and develop ideas into tangible innovations.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Apply critical thinking techniques to problem-solving and decision-making. (K3)
2. Recognize and analyze arguments, biases, and cognitive errors. (K4)
3. Use design-thinking principles to develop human-centered innovations. (K3)
4. Transform abstract ideas into prototypes and innovative solutions. (K3)
Detailed Syllabus:
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Foundation of Thinking and Innovation:   4
Introduction to Thinking: Critical, Analytical & Creative Thinking, Design
Thinking – Empathy, Defining, ideating, prototyping, Testing, Understanding
the Brain and Decision-Making Processes, Growth vs. Fixed Mindset,
Concept of Innovation and its Role in Engineering
2   Human-centered approach to problem definition:   4
Identifying and Defining Problems, The Importance of Empathy in
Innovation, Tools for Understanding and Ideation (Observation, Interviews,
Journey Mapping), Case Study: Infant Incubator (Understanding real-world
impact)
3   Decision-Making approach:   4
Logical Reasoning: Deductive vs. Inductive Thinking, Fallacies in Reasoning
and Cognitive Biases, The Role of Skepticism and Assumptions in Decision-
Making, fast vs. slow Thinking (Daniel Kahneman’s Model)
4   Leading Innovation by Concurrent Thinking:   6
Brainstorming, Brainwriting, Reframing Problems as ‘How Might We’
Questions, and Ideations, Case Study (Branch Specific)

6   Translating Ideas into Implementable Solutions:   6
Evaluating Feasibility: Technological, Business, and Social Viability, Case
