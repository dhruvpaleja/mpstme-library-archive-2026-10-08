Pre-requisite: Soil Mechanics, Foundation Engineering, Advanced Geotechnical Engineering
Objectives:
1. To introduce the concepts of planning of for geotechnical investigations
2. To identify the situation-based selection of sampling technique
3. To get acquainted with the different instrumentation techniques and equipment
4. To analyse the data obtained from geotechnical investigation for interpretations
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Apply the principles pf planning of geotechnical investigations
2. Evaluate the geotechnical situation for proper selection of sampling techniques and method
of field testing
3. Analyse the proper use of appropriate instrumentation for geotechnical investigations and
