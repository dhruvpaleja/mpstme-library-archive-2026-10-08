Objectives:

This course offers a brief introduction to the Vector calculus and Linear Algebra required to

build many common machine learning techniques.

e Learn a set of tools for making calculus and linear algebra easier and faster.

e Learn how to calculate vectors that point up hill on multidimensional surfaces and even
put this into action using an interactive game.

e Learn matrices and finding inverse of matrices.

e Know how to calculate determinant, eigenvalue and eigenvector and know their
application in Machine learning.

Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
e Understand vector space, projection of point, line and place to its corresponding line
or plane
e Differentiate and integrate a vector function.
e Perform operations on Matrices.

e Calculate determinant, eigenvalue and eigenvector and know their application in
Machine learning.
