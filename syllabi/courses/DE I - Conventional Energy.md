Course Objective
The Course focus on understanding the characteristics of conventional fuels, their economics, the methods and
equipments used for energy generation and their sustainability principles. The course also delves into the importance of
waste heat recovery, process integration and the process of achieving it.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1.   Identify and classify the combination of fuel and generator for sustainable energy generation
2. Apply the sustainable solutions into energy generation by convention source.
3.   Analyze the Chemical processes and be able to apply the integration principle to conserve energy.
