Pre-requisite: Soil Mechanics; Foundation Engineering

Objectives:
1. To estimate stresses in soil
2. To analyse stability of earth slopes
3. To analyse earth retaining structures
4. To estimate bearing capacity of shallow and group of pile foundation

Outcomes:
After completion of this course, students would be able to:
1. Estimate stresses in soil using appropriate theories
2. Distinguish between types of slope failures, and analyze the stability of earth slopes
3. Analyse earth retaining structures like gravity and cantilever retaining walls
4. Analyse and estimate bearing capacity and settlement of shallow and pile foundations

Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)

Unit Description Duration

1 Stress distribution in soils: 06
Introduction, Boussinesq’s theory, vertical stress distribution diagrams,
construction of isobars, Pressure bulbs, Influence coefficients, vertical stresses
beneath loaded areas - point load, line load, uniformly loaded rectangular and
circular areas, Newmark’s influence chart, Westergaard’s analysis

2 Consolidation of soils: 06
Introduction to consolidation, basic definitions, spring analogy, computation of
total settlement, Terzaghi’s one-dimensional theory of consolidation, secondary
consolidation, degree of consolidation, time rate of consolidation, computation
of rate of settlements, determination of cv from consolidation test results, square
root of time and logarithm of time methods

3 Stress and strain behaviour of soils: 04
Shear strength of sands and clays, triaxial test - drained and undrained
behaviour of sands and clays, Skempton’s pore pressure Strain softening and
strain hardening, Octahedral plane, Mohr - Coulomb’s criterion, ideal, plastic,
and real soil behaviour

4, Stability of slopes: 06
Introduction, types of slope failure, different factors of safety, analysis of
infinite slopes, Stability of finite slopes- plane failure, Swedish circle method,
friction circle stability numbers and charts

5. Design of shallow foundations: 08
Types of shear failure - general, local and punching; ultimate bearing capacity
in case of general and local shear failure, Vesic method, IS code method. Plate

of settlements, bearing capacity based on standard penetration tests

6. Analysis of pile foundations: 05
Pile load test as per IS: 2911 specifications, negative skin friction, capacity of
single piles, pile groups, ullimate capacity of groups, settlement of pile groups
in sand and in clays as per IS 2911 and critical depth method, Introduction to
piled raft foundation, NDT for piles

7. Earth pressure on retaining structures: 06
Analysis of gravity retaining wall
8. Braced cuts: 04

Lateral earth pressure distribution, apparent pressure diagrams, estimation of
strut forces and loads in deep cuts in stiff clay, sand, saturated clay. Bjerrum
and Eide method of analysis, use of touch piles, secant piles, anchors for
shoring system
