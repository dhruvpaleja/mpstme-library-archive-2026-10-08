Pre-requisite: Engineering Thermodynamics, Applied Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer
Course Objective
This course focuses in detail on industrial refrigeration and air-conditioning systems, which are
crucial to modern space conditioning. Additionally, it aims to impart knowledge regarding the
choice of HVAC plant equipment and control systems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Apply HVAC theory in the design and selection of HVACR plant equipment
2. Analyze the different air conditioning and industrial refrigeration systems
3. Explain different components and control systems in the HVAC and refrigeration industry
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   06
Introduction of HVACR, requirement of a space conditioning, Psychrometric
chart, psychrometric processes, Cooling/heating load, latent heat/ sensible
heat load, cooling load calculations. Energy-efficient HVAC design.
Classification of Air Conditioning Systems
Window air-conditioner, split air-conditioner, dessert Cooler, air-washer,
chillers, VRV system, cassette, roof tops, FCUs.
2.   Components of Refrigeration Plant   03
Types of compressors, types of evaporators, types of condensers, types of
throttling devices, ventilation and air filtration, cooling towers.
3.   Industrial Refrigeration System   03
Vapour absorption refrigeration system- Ammonia vapor absorption system,
Lithium bromide absorption system, capacity control.
4.   Controls in HVAC Systems   03
Operating controls, temperature controls, humidistats and air stats, primary
and secondary controllers pneumatic control, variable frequency drive, safety
controls, pressure switches, antifreeze thermostat.
Equipment Selection
Chiller selection, compressor selection, condenser selection, selection of
thermostatic expansion valve, pipe sizing.
Total   15
Text Books
1. Frank Kreith, Shan K. Wang, Paul Norton, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Engineering,
CRC Press, 2018.
2. P. N. Ananthanarayanan, Basic Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, McGraw-Hill Education,
