Course Objective
This course is aimed at giving practical knowledge of manufacturing processes, taking care of
safety and standard operating procedures to fabricate jobs. The course also provides theoretical
principles to understand the manufacturing requirements of the job.
Course Outcomes (COs):
After completing the course, students will be able to:
1. Describe standard operating procedures and interpret machining job requirements
2. Identify and select appropriate tools and parameters for various machining operations
3. Apply safety practices in machining and welding processes
4. Explain the working and significance of CNC machines in industrial automation
