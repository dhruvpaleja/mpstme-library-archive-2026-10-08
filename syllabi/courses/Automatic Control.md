Pre-requisite:
Objectives:
 To develop an understanding of timing belt driven and pneumatic positioning
systems
 To develop an understanding of modeling of position control systems
 To develop an understanding of control algorithms used in control theory
 To develop an understanding of application of control algorithms in mechatronic
systems
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
 Understand the essential components of commonly used mechatronic systems
 Demonstrate the ability of modeling and analysis of motion control devices
 Synthesize motion control systems using appropriate modeling and analysis
techniques
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration

pneumatic positioning system, components, sensors, actuators
2   Modeling of Mechatronic Systems: Modeling of essential   9
conveying/positioning systems used in FDM 3-D printer and
pneumatic positioning systems
3   Drives and Pneumatic Systems: Modeling of timing belt drives and   5
electric motors used in FDM 3-D printer, modeling of pneumatic
positioning system, model validation
4   Control Theory: Transfer functions, systems theory block diagrams,
feedback control, Routh Hurwitz criterion, Bode plot, Nyquist plot,
9
Root locus plot, Kharitonov’s theorem for uncertainty in transfer
function coefficients
6   Controllers: PID controller design, derivative state feedback
controller, time and Laplace domain controller implementations,   6
