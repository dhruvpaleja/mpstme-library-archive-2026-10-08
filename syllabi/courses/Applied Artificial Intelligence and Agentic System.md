Pre-requisite: Python Programming
Course Objective
The Applied Artificial Intelligence course aims to provide foundational knowledge of AI, covering
intelligent agents, search strategies, and knowledge representation. It explores AI-based optimization,
probabilistic reasoning, and machine learning techniques. Students will gain hands-on experience in
analyzing designing AI-driven systems using deep learning architectures.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Define and describe key AI concepts, including search algorithms, knowledge representation, and
intelligent agents(K1)
2. Explain the theoretical principles of AI techniques such as heuristic search, inference mechanisms,
and probabilistic reasoning(K2)
3. Utilize AI methods to solve problems involving uncertainty, classification and optimization (K3)
4. Compare and evaluate various AI learning models(K4)
5. Analyze AI-driven applications using neural network (K4)
