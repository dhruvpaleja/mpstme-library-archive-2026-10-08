Objectives:
To provide exposure to examples demonstrating the value of data-based marketing in
managerial context
To enable tactical marketing decisions with help of marketing models
1. To build numerical skills by putting together data, models and analyses.
2. Building analytical skills in optimised pricing decisions
3. Building analytical skills in optimised resource allocation decisions
4. Building analytical skills in advertising budget allocation
5. Building effective and efficient sales force allocation for effective sales force management
6. Building analytical skills in harnessing data to maximise customer life time value.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Enables its participant to put together data, models and analyses and translate concepts into
context specific operational decisions such as advertising and communication decisions,
sales force and channel decisions, pricing and sales promotion decisions
2. Enhances skills in viewing marketing processes and relationships systematically and
analytically
3. Skills in identification and deployment of decision making tools and techniques for
optimized retail sales.
