Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous Term End Examinations

week)   per week)   week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks - 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics
Course Objective
This course provides in-depth details about steam, hydro, nuclear, gas turbine power plants which
play an important role in power generation along with recent advances and plant economics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Discuss the working principle and basic components of the various types of power plants.
2. Analyze the working and layout of power plants and the different systems comprising the
plant and discuss about its economic and safety impacts.
3. Discuss and analyze the economic aspects of electricity generation.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1. Introduction   03
Energy resources and their availability in India, different types of power plants,
selection of the plants, review of basic thermodynamic cycles used in power plants.
2. Hydro Electric Power Plants   06
Hydrology-hydrographs, rainfall and run-off measurements and plotting of
various curves for estimating stream flow and size of reservoir, power plants
design, construction and operation of different components of hydro-electric
power plants, site selection, comparison with other types of power plants.
3. Steam Power Plants   10
Types of thermal power plants, thermal power plants based on fossil fuels, Flow
sheet and working of modern-thermal power plants, super critical pressure steam
stations, site selection, coal storage, preparation, coal handling systems, feeding
and burning of pulverized fuel, ash handling systems, dust collection-mechanical
dust collector and electrostatic precipitator
4.   Nuclear Power Plants   08
Principles of nuclear energy, basic nuclear reactions, nuclear reactors PWR, BWR,
CANDU, Sodium graphite, fast breeder, homogeneous; gas cooled, Advantages
and limitations, nuclear power station, waste disposal
5. Combined Cycles   08
Constant pressure gas turbine power plants, arrangements of combined plants
(steam & gas turbine power plants), re-powering systems with gas production
from coal, using PFBC systems, with organic fluids, parameters affecting
thermodynamic efficiency of combined cycles.

6.   Recent Advances in Power Plants   06
IGCC (Integrated gasification combined cycle plant), super critical power plants,
etc. Case studies, Introduction to solar power generation, Sterling engine,
Decentralized power technologies.
7.   Power Plant Economics   04
Load curve, cost of electrical energy, tariffs methods of electrical energy,
performance & operating characteristics of power plants- incremental rate theory,
input-output curves, efficiency, heat rate.
Total   45
Text Books
1. P. K. Nag, Power Plant Engineering, Tata McGraw-Hill, 4th Edition 2017.
2. D. Vairagkar, Power Plant Engineering, 1st Edition, Wiley, 2019.
Reference Books
