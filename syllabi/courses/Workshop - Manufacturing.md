Course Rationale:

Workshop Practice is a basic practical engineering course. Students are able to perform various
operations using hand tool equipment and machineries in various shops. Working in workshop develops
the attitude of group working and safety awareness. This course provides miniature industrial
environment in the educational institute

Course Objectives:
1. To impart hands on experience of different workshop practices on various trades.
2. To impart knowledge of basic tools used for different workshop jobs.

3. To introduce basic concepts of electrical and electronic instruments and its applications

Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:

1. Demonstrate understanding of safety and operation practices in fabrication processes like welding.
2. Apply various hand tool operations like fitting and carpentry for the manufacturing.
3. Inculcate the fundaments of assembling Electronics and Electrical components.

Lectures, Experiential learning activities, Work based learning
