Course Objectives
• Understand and compare industrial business types and B2B business models
• Analyze strategic value creation mechanisms in industrial markets
• Discuss customer-centric solutions for complex buying situations
• Understand innovative industrial market strategies, including:
a. Product-service systems
b. Data-driven and platform-based business models
c. Advanced service and solution concepts
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Analyze and distinguish core industrial marketing strategy concepts and their strategic
implications
2. Evaluate value propositions and value creation logics in B2B contexts
3. Identify new industrial market opportunities based on customer problems and ecosystem
dynamics
4. Design innovative B2B business models and solution concepts with a strong focus on
customer value creation
