Prerequisite: Nil
Course Objective
This course aims to introduce students to the fundamental concepts of data and signal analysis. It
provides a strong mathematical foundation for signal representation, processing, and transformation
techniques. The course will help students understand signal characterization, and frequency-domain
analysis using various transforms. Additionally, students will learn the importance of sampling and its
role in digital signal processing, along with Fourier and Laplace transformations for analysing signals in
different domains.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamental concepts of data communication and perform mathematical operations
on signals (K1)
2. Explain convolution techniques & Z-transforms and apply them in analysing discrete-time signals
(K2)
3. Apply the Nyquist Sampling Theorem to determine the optimal sampling rate (K3)
4. Analyse signals using series and transforms to interpret and solve signal processing problems
(K3)
