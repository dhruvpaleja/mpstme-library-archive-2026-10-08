Objectives:

Life skills are critical for the success in life. This course provides a practical introduction to life skills like
team building, stress management, creative thinking, negotiation, decision-making and communication.

Outcomes:

After completion of the course, students would be able to :
1. Improve communication, team building, decision-making and negotiation skills
2. Understand causes of stress and manage it
3. Understand various lateral thinking techniques
