Pre-requisite: Engineering Mathematics, statistics and probability
Course Objectives
This course aims at utilizing applied mathematics as a thought-process and a toolbox for
the study and modelling of real-world phenomena along with their interpretation and
comparison for further improvement if any.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. understand the concept of mathematical Modelling,
2. develop models using ODE & PDE,
3. apply optimization techniques to the designed models.
