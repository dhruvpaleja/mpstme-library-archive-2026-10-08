Objectives:
1. To understand various testing and commissioning procedures for electrical

electrical systems.
Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, the students will be able to
1. perform testing of various electrical equipment as per standard procedure
and analyze results.
2. understand the process of commissioning.
3. appreciate and evaluate various maintenance methods / techniques.
4. suggest the remedial action to improve life of electrical equipment.
