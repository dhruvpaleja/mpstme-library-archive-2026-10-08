Pre-requisite: -
Course Objective
This course develops students' skills in human-centered design, interdisciplinary teamwork, and
problem-solving through structured project work. Students will engage in stakeholder analysis,
prototyping, testing, and iterative refinement while integrating engineering principles, creativity, and
sustainability. Emphasizing collaboration and ethical responsibility, the course prepares students for
industry and community-driven challenges by enhancing their project management, communication,
and critical thinking abilities.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to --
1. Critically evaluate complex, real-world challenges using systematic problem-solving
approaches.
2. Lead and collaborate in interdisciplinary teams to develop scalable and impactful solutions.
3. Apply advanced design methodologies and emerging technologies to prototype and refine
innovative solutions in research.
4. Integrate sustainability, ethics, and feasibility analysis into the design and implementation
process.
5. Demonstrate professional communication through technical reports, presentations, and
stakeholder engagement.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
The course covers Problem Analysis, where students assess the problem
context, constraints, stakeholders, and solution stage while conducting
literature reviews and benchmarking existing solutions. In Design Thinking &
Concept Development, they apply the phases of Design Thinking—
Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test—to develop and refine
1.   innovative solutions. Prototyping & Testing involves transforming ideas into   60
practical prototypes, conducting experiments, usability testing, and iterative
refinements. Through Project Management & Documentation, students assign
roles, track progress, and maintain structured records. Communication &
Review focuses on preparing presentations, conducting design reviews, and
incorporating feedback. Finally, Reflection & Improvement allows students to
analyze challenges, enhance their skills, and document key learnings.
