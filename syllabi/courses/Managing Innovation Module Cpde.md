Prerequisite: Contemporary Practices in Management

Course Objectives .
1. To learn concepts & processes related to Innovation in organizations that help them maintain
- leadership in highly competitive Business environment
2. The course is essentially about building and sustaining innovation within organizations by
creating an innovative and learning organization. :
3. The course encompasses models, tools & frameworks of Innovation that are used by various
types of organizations as a part of their Innovation process

Course Outcomes

After completion of the course, students would be able to:

1. Describe the concepts of innovation and it is implication in organizations.

2. Understand and explain how organizations use Innovation as their business strategy.

3. Apply frameworks and process of innovation management in their organizations.

4. Develop relevant skills needed at both strategic and operational levels and appreciate culture for
an innovative organization.
