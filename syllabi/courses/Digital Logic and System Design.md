To provide basic knowledge of logic circuits theory, elementary analysis and its implementation in

and sequential logic circuits for solving digital logic design problems.

After successful completion of this course, students will be able to-
1. Explain different number systems and their conversions,
2. Use Boolean algebra and K-map to simplify logic functions,
3. Design and implement combinational logic circuits,
4. Design and implement sequential logic circuits.
