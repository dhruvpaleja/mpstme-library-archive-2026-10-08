Pre-requisite: Introductory course in Environmental Studies, physics, chemistry, geography, computer

1. Introduction of the concept, principles and applications of Remote Sensing and Geographic
Information Systems (GIS).

organized and analysed.

1. Explore mapped data and Analyse simple spatial data, using GIS data analysis tools
2. Create basic maps and images to communicate spatial data in a meaningful way to others
3. Interpret basic GIS documents for environmental management principles and policies
