1. This course is designed to cover Biometric Identification Science and Technology.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand the importance of biometric based system.
2. Apply Image Processing techniques in designing the Biometric based solution.
3. Design and develop various biometric recognition systems considering their ethical
responsibility.
4. Understand the multimodal Biometric based recognition system.
