Pre-requisite: Basic Probability Theory and abstract algebra
Course Objective
This course provides an introduction to the elliptic curve cryptography. The course covers the
required mathematical background to understand the ECC. The course will cover the theory of
elliptic curves and its applications.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Implement various cryptographic schemes using elliptic curves, including variants of the
Diffie-Hellman key exchange, the ElGamal public key cryptosystem, and digital signatures
2. Apply techniques of elliptic curves over finite fields to the discrete logarithm problem
3. Survey pairing based cryptosystems on elliptic curves
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to elliptic curves   06
Definition   of   elliptic   curves. Group   law. Endomorphisms and
isomorphisms. j-invariant, Weierstrass and Edwards curves.
2.   Elliptic curves over finite fields   06
Torsion points. Frobenius morphism. Group structure and group order.
Hasse's theorem. Supersingular curves. Discrete logarithm problem.
3.   Elliptic curve cryptosystems   08
Diffie-Hellman key exchange. ElGamal encryption. Schnorr identification.
ECDSA. Bit coin.
4.   Generating elliptic curves for cryptography   06
Schoof's algorithm. Complex multiplication. Pairing based cryptography,
Abstract properties of pairings. Pairing-friendly elliptic curves. Generating
pairing-friendly curves.
5.   Discrete logarithm attacks   04
Baby step-giant step. Pollard rho. Menezes-Okamoto-Vanstone and Frey-
Rück reductions. Anomalous curves.
Total   30
