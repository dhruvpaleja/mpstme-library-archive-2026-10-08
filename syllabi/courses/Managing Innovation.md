Prerequisite:

Objectives:
 With Innovation being an imperative for any organization to maintain its leadership in
the highly competitive environment, it is important for all Business owners and future
employees of the organizations to understand the all-important concepts & processes
related to Innovation.
 The course is essentially about building and sustaining innovation within organizations
by creating an innovative and learning organization. It aims to focus on innovation
within business entities, from the technology product or process innovation programs,
to broader perspective addressing innovation as a holistic strategic management
imperative not limited to research & development or New Product Development.
 The course takes you through Innovation related concepts, models, tools and
frameworks born out of Creative thinking, Various types of Innovations, Design
Thinking, Process of Innovation, Process of Innovation Management and Developing
Innovation Strategy.
 The course intends to build the competency among participants to be able to practice
Innovation at an individual level (development of new products & services) as well as
Managing Innovation at an organizational level to get a competitive advantage.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Participant will be able to use concepts, models and tools born out of Design thinking
to conceptualize new products & services
2. Further they will learn how to assess market opportunity of Innovations and develop
Business plans to take them to market
3. Participants will be able to understand how organizations use Innovation as their
business strategy
