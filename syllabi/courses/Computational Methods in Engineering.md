week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Physics, Mathematics, Mechanics of Materials
Course Objective
The course develops an understanding of a numerical method used in analysis and design of
continuum. The course emphasizes the computational framework for the students for effective
grasping. The students learn to analyze practical problems with software tools and modeling
methods.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the theoretical concepts of mass and stiffness matrix, conduction matrix for basic
mechanical engineering continuum problems
2. Analyze scripting for FEA on a computational platform
3. Develop an understanding of solving practical problems on a versatile commercially
available platform
4. Analyze the designs for practical insights and design considerations
5. Synthesize designs for optimal performance and functionality
