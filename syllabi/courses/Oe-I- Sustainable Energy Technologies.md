Objectives
This subject provides insight of prevailing energy management practices in
Industries and make students aware about the energy efficiency elements to be
followed to achieve increased energy efficiency and sustainable operations.
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand the energy scenario and need for energy management.
2. Understand the concept of energy management and audit, and learn its application
as per industry type and need.
3. Apply the knowledge of energy efficiency in designing an innovative approach for
future applications.
