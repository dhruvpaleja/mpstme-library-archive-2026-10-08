Pre-requisite: Marketing Management
Objectives:
•   This course covers brand management – from defining, aligning and redefining identity to
identity-driven communication and storytelling with a favorable reputation and clear position
as outcomes
• The course is designed to create realistic situations that call for class discussions to enhance
students' judgment and management skills.
• Understand what a brand is and how to describe the evolution of brands in a wide cross-
section of industries and contexts.
• Demonstrate insight into relevant research on how a brand is managed, considering its
reputation held by various stakeholders, both internal and external.
Understand the process involved in building a corporate brand and its importance to a cohesive
and sustainable (business) strategy.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Apply key frameworks to analyze and manage brands for continued growth and profitability
(willingness-to-support in non-profit contexts).
2. Demonstrate an ability to participate in case discussions and role-plays on brand
management and leadership.
3. Demonstrate an ability to work in teams and to find solutions to relevant business cases.
4. Identify and assess the essential opportunities and challenges facing an organization or
business in aligning corporate brand identity and set out a course of action to address these
issues.
5. Assess the scientific, social, cultural, and ethical aspects of corporate brand management and
reputation.
6. Combine different perspectives and theories on corporate brands and reputation into realistic
managerial action plans and solutions.
