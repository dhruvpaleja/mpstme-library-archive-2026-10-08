Pre-requisite: Knowledge on types of application architecture
Course Objective
This course equips students with the skills to architect and deploy AI applications using monolithic
and microservices architectures, Kubernetes, APIs, and MLOps principles. It emphasizes hands-on
experience with containerization, Docker, and orchestration tools for scalable AI deployment.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Compare and contrast monolithic and microservices architectures, evaluating their
advantages and disadvantages in the context of AI applications.
2. Demonstrate the use of Kubernetes for managing and deploying AI models in production,
explaining its key concepts and functionality.
3. Apply MLOps principles, including data preparation, model deployment, and monitoring,
to optimize the end-to-end machine learning lifecycle.
4. Evaluate and implement containerization techniques using Docker and Kubernetes,
ensuring efficient and scalable deployment of AI applications.
