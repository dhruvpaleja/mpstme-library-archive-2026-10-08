Pre-requisite: Statistical Structures in Data and Inference, Foundations of Machine Learning
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to emphasize on the importance of nonparametric methods and equip
students with the conceptual and technical skills necessary to select and apply the appropriate
procedures in the absence of the underlying distribution to make statistical inferences. This
course will also provide information on the basic theory and computing tools required to perform
nonparametric tests
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Apply a range of nonparametric regression methods to solve problems
2. Explain the asymptotic properties of nonparametric estimators
3. Recommend multiple nonparametric approaches for appropriate applications
4. Propose new appropriate solution for nonparametric hypothesis testing procedures

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the foundational concepts of non-parametric inference, such as empirical
quantile functions, order statistics, and their asymptotic distributions. (L2 – Understand)
2. Apply goodness-of-fit and independence tests, including Chi-squared and Fisher’s exact
tests, to categorical data. (L3 – Apply)
3. Analyze and interpret results using linear rank statistics for problems involving scale
differences. (L4 – Analyze)
4. Perform and interpret non-parametric hypothesis tests like the sign test, Wilcoxon tests,
Kruskal-Wallis, and runs test to compare populations. (L3 – Apply, L4 – Analyze)
5. Evaluate bivariate associations using Kendall’s Tau and Spearman’s Rank and analyze
count data using McNemar’s and Fisher’s tests. (L5 – Evaluate)
