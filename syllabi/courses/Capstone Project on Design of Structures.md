Pre-requisite: Design of Reinforced Concrete Structures, Workshop on RCC and Structural Steel
Detailing
Objectives:
1. Understand the principles of planning and prepare architectural drawings
2. Understand the design principles and prepare the structural model using available software
3. To get acquainted with the postprocessing of the model and prepare structural drawings
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Prepare architectural drawings using principles of planning
2. Prepare the structural model using available software
3. Prepare structural drawings
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1 A project on planning and structural design of G+7 RCC Residential building.   60
Following are the deliverables
1. Prepare an architectural working drawing including plans, elevations,
sections, drawings for utilities and services using CAD
2. Prepare general structural layout and design basis report as per the latest
codal provisions
3. Prepare structural model of the building using available software
4. Extraction of results from the software used and their interpretation
5. Prepare structural drawings using CAD
Total   60
Text Books:
1. Shah & Karve (2010), “Illustrated Design of G+3 Building”, Structure Publication, Pune
2. P.Dayaratnam (2008), “Limit State Design of Reinforced Concrete Structures” Oxford & IBH
