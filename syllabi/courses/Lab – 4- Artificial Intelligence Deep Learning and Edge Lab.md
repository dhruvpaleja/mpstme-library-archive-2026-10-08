Pre-requisite: Deep Learning Theory & Concept
Course Objective
This lab course aims to equip students with practical skills in deep learning and edge AI, focusing on
developing, deploying, and securing AI models in real-time and low-latency environments. Students
will gain hands-on experience with frameworks like TensorFlow and PyTorch, explore ethical and
security aspects in AI systems, and implement edge computing solutions using modern tools.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Develop and fine-tune deep learning models using frameworks like TensorFlow and PyTorch
2. Identify and mitigate ethical issues, biases, and security vulnerabilities in AI systems
3. Design and deploy AI models in real-time edge computing and MEC environments
4. Utilize essential tools and platforms for professional AI development and deployment
