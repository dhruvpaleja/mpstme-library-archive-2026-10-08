Objectives
This course teaches the students techniques in solving problems based on Laplace
transform and Partial Differential Equations and further directs them to think
mathematically and apply these techniques to solve engineering problems.
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. use effective mathematical methods to solve partial differential equations,
2. solve problems based on Laplace transform,
3. apply the techniques of partial differential equations and Laplace transforms to
solve real life problems.
