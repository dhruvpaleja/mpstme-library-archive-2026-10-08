Pre-requisite: Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics and Heat Transfer
Course Objective
The present course aims at developing a familiarity with various types of heat exchangers, their
construction and applications. Conventional methods of heat exchanger analysis; brief design
methodology of typical heat exchangers.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Analyze the fundamentals and design procedure of heat exchanger
2. Design of different types of heat exchangers like shell and tube type, plate heat exchanger,
compact heat exchanger
3. Articulate performance enhancement of heat exchanger
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Heat Exchangers   08
Background, Application, Classification, Common terminologies.
Basic design methods for recuperators and regenerators: F-LMTD and
effectiveness-NTU method. Forced convection correlations, pressure drop,
fouling in heat exchangers.
2.   Shell and Tube Heat Exchangers   09
Basic design procedure, Kern method, Bell-Delaware method, stream analysis
method. TEMA code.
3.   Heat Transfer   08
Enhancement of heat transfer, extended surface or Fin, fundamental of extended
surface heat transfer, Fin tube heat exchanger
4.   Plate Heat Exchangers   10
Types, construction, design, application.
Compact Heat Exchangers
Construction, design, application.
5.   Heat Pipes   10
Construction, working principle, application, analysis. Special heat pipes.
Thermal Design of Condensers and Evaporators, Micro/Nano Heat Transfer.
