Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objectives
• Understand the Importance of Data Management.
• Explain Data Governance and Data Quality
• Demonstrate Proficiency in Data Acquisition and Integration
• Utilize Data Analysis and Business Intelligence Techniques
• Foster Data-Driven Decision-Making Culture
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
• Recognize the significance of data management in modern organizations.
Comprehend how effective data management contributes to better decision making and
organizational success
• Define data governance and its role in managing organizational data. Identify strategies to
ensure data quality, consistency, and reliability. Explore Data Lifecycle and Management
Processes
• Acquire data from diverse sources and formats. Integrate and transform data to create a unified
and usable dataset. Apply Data Storage and Database Management Concepts
• Apply data analysis methods to uncover insights and patterns in data. Use business
intelligence tools to visualize and communicate data-driven insights effectively.
Promote a data-driven decision-making culture within the organization. Encourage data
literacy and data-driven thinking among employees. Embrace Ethical Data Management
Practices
