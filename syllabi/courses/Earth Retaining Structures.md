3   0   1   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Soil Mechanics, Mechanics of Materials, Geology
Objectives:
1. To understand the fundamentals of earth pressure theories
2. To understand the mechanism of earth retaining structures
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. solve problems related to earth pressure
2. calculate the external stability and internal stability of backfilled walls
3. Study the stability parameters related to soil reinforcement and anchoring
4. Design sheet pile walls
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Earth pressure:   08
Introduction, classification of retaining walls in terms of structural functionality,
Lateral Earth Pressure at Rest, Rankine’s and coulomb’s active earth state,
Rankine’s and coulomb’s passive earth state, Culmann’s graphical solution,
earthquake effect on active and passive states: Mononobe Okabe equations,
numerical problems.
2   Introduction to design and backfilled walls:   02
Limit States for earth retaining structures, factors to consider in choosing a retaining
structure, types of structures, selection and acquisition of soil parameter, types of
analysis.
3   Backfilled walls:   08
External Stability - preliminary estimates of backfill forces, earth pressure
calculations, overturning, sliding, bearing capacity, overall stability, settlement and
tilt, numerical problems.
Internal Stability - masonry, gravity block and gabion walls, crib walls, reinforced
concrete cantilever walls. Design Problems.
4   Soil reinforcement and anchoring:   12
Mechanics of Reinforced soil - enhancement of shear strength, available
reinforcement force, stress-strain behaviour.
Components of reinforced soil walls - reinforcement, facing, backfill.
Principles of Design - general, preliminary dimensions, factors of safety, provisions
of FHWA NHI-0043 (2001)
External Stability - outward sliding, overturning.
Internal Stability - tension failure, pull-out failure.
Pull-out capacity of reinforcement - strip reinforcement, bar reinforcement (soil
nails), sheet/fabric reinforcement, wire mesh reinforcement, polymer grid
reinforcement, bar anchor reinforcement, hybrid anchoring systems, numerical
problems.
Ground Anchors - types of ground anchor, design of ground anchor, durability.

5   Sheet pile walls:   15
Ground water conditions - sheet pile wall driven into impermeable soils, sheet pile
wall driven into permeable soils. Factor of safety
Cantilever sheet-pile walls - preliminary design, determination of depth of
