Pre-requisite: Basics of calculus, statistics and probability.
Course Objectives
The course aims at developing an understanding of linear, nonlinear, integer programming and
some modern methods of optimisation. It also aims at training the students with various
optimisation techniques necessary to formulate, solve and analyse decision making problems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. solve problems based on linear, nonlinear and integer programming,
2. explore various methods of solving linear and nonlinear programming using software
tools,
3. apply the techniques of linear, integer and non-linear programming in decision making.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
