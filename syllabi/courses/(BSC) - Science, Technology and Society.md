Course Objectives
The objective of the course is to highlight the role of society, media and politics in the field of
science & technology, especially in a post artificial intelligence era.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. understand the complex relationship between science, technology and society,
2. develop an interdisciplinary understanding of scientific knowledge and technological
production,
3. evaluate empirical evidences and theoretical claims in science, technology and society along
with policy matters,
4. analyze the policy matters and technical governance for artificial intelligence, vaccine
development, stem cells and nanotechnology.
