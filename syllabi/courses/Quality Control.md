 To explain the concepts and strategies for implementing and managing quality

 To facilitate an understanding of the fundamentals of TQM, SPC, and Six
Sigma methods
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
 Understand the philosophy and fundamentals of Quality Control.
 Understand the Statistical foundations of Quality control.
 Apply the statistical process control and acceptance sampling
 Explain various Quality Management Systems.
