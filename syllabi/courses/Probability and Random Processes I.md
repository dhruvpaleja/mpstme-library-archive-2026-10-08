Objectives:
1. To develop the concepts and techniques associated with the understanding
of probability and random processes
2. To be able to analyse the chances of occurrence of error in communication
field.
- Outcomes: -
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
1. Analyze the different probability density functions and their applications in
telecommunications.
2. Recognize the statistical theory used in application areas like communication,
signal processing and control.
3. Formulate the concept of random process and   its importance in
communication field.
