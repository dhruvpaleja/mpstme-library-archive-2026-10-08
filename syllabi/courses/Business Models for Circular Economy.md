Pre-requisite: General Management, Operations Management
Objectives:
• Describe the criticality of circular economy in a business organization.
• Explain various circular economy concepts related to sustainable business operations.
• Appraise the students with skills needed to analyze various circularity functions throughout
company’s operations for effective implementation.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Define circular economy and responsible practices related to sustainable business operations and their
applications and undertake a detailed study on the various concepts and their implementation.
2. Develop graduates who have the necessary theoretical and practical and research knowledge in
circularity through a detailed study and learning of responsible business systems and different
business models for circular economy and its operationalization.
3. Assess the significance of circularity concepts in an organization and to understand their applications
throughout its operations.
