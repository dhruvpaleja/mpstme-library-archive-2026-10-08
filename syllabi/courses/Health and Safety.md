Occupational health schemes, Benefits of occupational health services, Sickness
absence control, Sickness absence procedure, Sickness absence monitoring
5.   Selection of work equipment: Principles of machinery safety, Other hazards   08
associated with machinery operation, Machinery guards, Machinery safety
devices, Planned maintenance, Lifting operations and lifting equipment,
Machinery safety assessment etc., safety of scaffolding and working platforms.
