Course Objective
The objective of this course is to introduce students to the role of engineering materials in everyday
life, enabling them to understand material selection, performance, sustainability, and emerging
material technologies without requiring deep mechanical background.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Identify commonly used materials in daily life and their applications
2. Relate material properties with functional performance in products
3. Evaluate material choices based on cost, sustainability, and usability
4. Interpret failures of everyday products from a material perspective
5. Recognize modern and smart materials used in advanced applications
