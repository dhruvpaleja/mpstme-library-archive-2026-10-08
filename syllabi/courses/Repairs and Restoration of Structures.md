Pre-requisite: Theory of Structures
Objectives:
1. To introduce rehabilitation of structures
2. To study non-destructive and destructive testing methods
3. To evaluate structural damage
4. To study materials for repair:
5. To develop capability to investigate appropriate rehabilitation technique
6. To understand guidelines for repair and rehabilitation works
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Estimate causes for distress and deterioration of structures
2. Understand NDT techniques for condition assessment of structures for identifying damages in
structures.
3. Select repair material and retrofitting strategy suitable for distress
4. Formulate guidelines for repair management of deteriorated structures
