Course Objective
The course aims to equip students with hands-on experience in customizing Teamcenter, focusing
on both server-side and client-side modifications. Students will gain proficiency in Server
Customization using ITK and SOA, enabling them to tailor Teamcenter functionalities to specific
requirements. Additionally, they will develop expertise in Client Customization using RAC,
enhancing their ability to modify and extend the Teamcenter user interface. Furthermore, the
course will enable students to effectively utilize inbuilt and custom SOA at the client side, ensuring
seamless integration and communication within the system.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to –
1. Get good experience in Server Customization of Teamcenter using ITK, SOA
2. Gget good experience in Client Customization of Teamcenter using RAC
3. Use inbuilt/Custom SOA at Client Side
