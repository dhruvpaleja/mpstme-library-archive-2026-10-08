Pre-requisite - Nil
Course Objective
This course aims to equip students with practical skills in electronics prototyping and provides hands-on
experience with essential tools, measuring instruments, PCB design, and fabrication techniques.
Additionally, students will gain knowledge in PC hardware assembly, Arduino-based circuit
programming, and aerial robotics, including drone assembly and flight basics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Understand and demonstrate the use of essential hand tools, measuring instruments, and
prototyping equipment for circuit design and testing, (Understand - Level 2)
2. Design and fabricate single-sided PCBs by selecting appropriate circuit components, routing
layouts using open-source software, and assembling electronic circuits, (Create- Level 6)
3. Analyze and troubleshoot common electrical and electronic components used in industrial
applications, including wiring systems and protection devices, (Apply- Level 3)
4. Assemble, configure, and troubleshoot PC hardware by understanding the functionality of
components such as CPU, RAM, motherboard, power supply, storage devices, and input/output
peripherals, (Understand - Level 2)
5. Develop and implement Arduino-based projects by coding and interfacing various sensors for
real-world applications, (Create- Level 6)
6. Assemble and configure aerial robotic systems (drones/UAVs) by understanding flight
principles, selecting appropriate components, and performing test flights in a controlled
environment. (Understand - Level 2)
Detailed Syllabus

Course Objective
This course aims to equip students with practical skills in electronics prototyping and provides hands-on
experience with essential tools, measuring instruments, PCB design, and fabrication techniques.
Additionally, students will gain knowledge in PC hardware assembly, Arduino-based circuit
programming, and aerial robotics, including drone assembly and flight basics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Understand and demonstrate the use of essential hand tools, measuring instruments, and
prototyping equipment for circuit design and testing, (Understand - Level 2)
2. Design and fabricate single-sided PCBs by selecting appropriate circuit components, routing
layouts using open-source software, and assembling electronic circuits, (Create- Level 6)
3. Analyze and troubleshoot common electrical and electronic components used in industrial
applications, including wiring systems and protection devices, (Apply- Level 3)
4. Assemble, configure, and troubleshoot PC hardware by understanding the functionality of
components such as CPU, RAM, motherboard, power supply, storage devices, and input/output
peripherals, (Understand - Level 2)
5. Develop and implement Arduino-based projects by coding and interfacing various sensors for
real-world applications, (Create- Level 6)
6. Assemble and configure aerial robotic systems (drones/UAVs) by understanding flight
principles, selecting appropriate components, and performing test flights in a controlled
environment. (Understand - Level 2)
