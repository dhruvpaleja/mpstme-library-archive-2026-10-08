Pre-requisite: Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics
Course Objective
The course nurtures the practical product development aptitude in students by requiring
them to conceptualize and ideate a product or a method and then test its feasibility. It
requires students to gather extensive information on the current available designs and their
functionality. The final proposal details a product or a method distinguishing itself from the
current availability.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the fundamental concepts of DC circuits, including Kirchhoff’s laws and
network theorems
2. Interpret the principles of AC circuit analysis, including resonance and three-phase
systems
3. Illustrate the working principles of transformers and different types of electrical
machines
4. Explain the basic operation of analog electronic components, including rectifiers,
transistors and operational amplifiers
5. Make use of logic gates and Boolean expressions for digital circuits

Laboratory Work

concerning general aspects of mechatronic systems.
