Pre-requisite: Knowledge of Electromagnetic Field Theory
Objectives:

1. To study different wave propagation techniques.

2. To introduce students about basic radiating elements, various types of
antennas.

3. The designing of various antennas in order to propagate radio waves.

Outcomes:
After the sticcessful completion of this course, the student will be able to
1. Know different media of wave propagation.
2. Know the basic concepts and working of the Antenna.
3. Ulustrate hertzian dipole equation for E - H field.
4, Formulate radiation patterns of different antenna array.
5. Demonstrate the functioning and performance of different antenna.
