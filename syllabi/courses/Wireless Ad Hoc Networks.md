Program: B Tech / MBA Tech (EXTC)   Semester: VII/VIII
Course: Wireless Ad hoc Networks   Code: 702EX0E009
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours   (Hours   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
per week)   per week)   per week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks -100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Computer Networks, Wireless Communication Technology
Course Objective
This course imparts fundamental knowledge of Ad hoc Networks and design issues at different layers.
It also provides information about the WiFi, WiMAX standards and UWB air interface with IEEE
802.20 for IP & roaming in high-speed mobile environments.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the concepts, architectures and applications of Ad hoc Network
2. Analyse the design issues of protocols used in Ad hoc networks at MAC, network and
transport layers
3. Compare WiFi and WiMAX standards for wireless broadband access
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Ad hoc Network
Introduction to generations in wireless systems, Introduction to wireless/Mobile   04
Ad-Hoc Networks (MANETs), Classification of mobile data networks, MANET
issues, and Wireless channel related issues.
2. MAC layer issues in Ad hoc Networks
Introduction, Issues in designing a Media Access Control (MAC) Protocol,   04
Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA) with hidden and exposed terminal issues,
Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance (MACA) and Multiple Access with
Collision Avoidance for Wireless (MACAW) protocols.
3.   Network layer issues in Ad hoc Networks
Introduction to routing algorithm, Issues in designing a routing protocol for Ad   05
hoc Networks, Table-Driven and Hybrid routing protocols, Wireless Routing
Protocol – Cluster head Gateway Switch Routing Protocol (CGSR) protocols,
Location aided, Hybrid protocols, Multicast protocols.
4.   Transport Layer issues
Introduction, Issues in designing a transport layer protocol for Ad hoc   05
Networks, data flow control mechanisms, congestion control protocols, security
aspects.
5.   Multimodal Sub-GigaHertz Communication
Mutimodal communication for IoT - SigFox UL, SigFox DL, NB-IoT, IEEE   05
802.15.4g, IEEE 802.11.ah, DASH, Multimodel communication and
management.
1

6. WiMAX – IEEE 802.16 Standards
Various 802.16 (a to e) protocols, Worldwide Interoperability for Microwave   05
Access (WiMAX) air interface / Physical layer, WiMAX architecture, WiMAX
and WiFi Interworking, WiMAX modes - TDD and FDD, QoS in WiMAX.
7. UWB -
Ultra Wide Band (UWB) air Interface and IEEE 802.20 (standard for mobile   02
broadband wireless access) and beyond
Total   30
Text Books
1. Jonathan Loo, Jaime Lloret Mauri, Jesús Hamilton Ortiz, Mobile Ad Hoc Networks: Current Status
and Future Trends, 1st Edition, CRC press, 2016.
2. Siva Ram Murthy, C. and Manoj, B. S., Adhoc Wireless Networks Architectures and Protocols, 2nd
Edition, Pearson Education, 2009.
Reference Books
1. Carlos De Morais Cordeiro and Dharam P Agrawal, Adhoc and Sensor Networks- Theory &
Applications, 2nd Edition, World Scientific Publishing Company, 2011.
2. Toh, C. K., Adhoc Mobile Wireless Networks Protocols and Systems, 3rd Edition, Pearson Education,
2007.
3. Pahlavan, Kaveh., Krishnamoorthy, Prashant., Principles of Wireless Networks, - A unified
approach, 2nd Edition, Prentice Hall of India, 2008.
Laboratory Work
8 to 10 practical exercises (and a practicum) based on the syllabus.
