Pre-requisite: Theory of Structures

Objectives:
1. To introduce rehabilitation of structures
2. To study non-destructive and destructive testing methods
3. To evaluate structural damage
4. To study materials for repair:
5. To develop capability to investigate appropriate rehabilitation technique
6. To understand guidelines for repair and rehabilitation works
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Estimate causes for distress and deterioration of structures

2. Understand NDT techniques for condition assessment of structures for identifying damages in
structures,

3. Select repair material and retrofitting strategy suitable for distress

| 4. Formulate guidelines for repair management of deteriorated structures
Detailed Syllabus: (per scssion plan)

Unit | Description

Duration

1 Introduction to Rehabilitation: 06
An overview of present repair practices, distress identification and repair
management, Causes of distress in concrete structures-Holistic Models for
deterioration of concrete, Permeability of concrete, aggressive chemical agents,
durability aspects, Condition Survey- Definition, objectives, different stages-
Preliminary inspection, planning stage, visual inspection, field laboratory testing
stage, consideration for repair strategy.

2 Non-Destructive and Destructive Testing Methods: 08
Non-Destructive evaluation tests, Concrete strength assessment , Rebound hammer
test , Ultrasonic pulse velocity tests, penetration resistance, pull out tests, core
sampling and testing, Chemical tests - Carbonation tests and chloride content,
Corrosion potential assessment, cover meter survey, half-cell potentiometer test,
resistivity measurement, Discussion of case studies of RCC buildings subjected to
distress, Identification and estimation of damage.

3 Evaluation of Structural properties: 08

Fire damage assessment, structural integrity and soundness assessment,
interpretation and evaluation of results, Evaluation of reserve strength of existing
structures, analysis necessary to identify critical sections, active and passive repairs,
modelling of repaired composite structures.

4 Repair materials and case studies: 10
Selection of repair materials for concrete, Essential parameters for repair materials,
Strength and durability aspects, cost and suitability aspects, Materials for repair,
Premixed cement concrete and mortars, polymer modified mortars and concrete,
epoxy and epoxy systems including epoxy mortars and concrete, polyester resins,
coatings, Discussion of case studies-RCC buildings, water tanks, industrial
structures, Identifying a suitable repair option for certain damage in a structure.

5 Repair/ Rehabilitation methods and strategies: 10
Rehabilitation and retrofitting methods, repair options, performance requirements
of repair systems, important factors to be considered for selection of repair
methods, Repair stages, Guniting, shotcreting, polymer concrete system,
reinforcement replacement, strengthening concrete by surface impregnation,
polymer and epoxy overlays, Resin/polymer modified slurry injection, plate
bonding technique, ferrocement jacketing, RCC jacketing, propping and
supporting, fiber wrap technique, foundation rehabilitation methods, chemical and
electrochemical method of repair, Repair/Rehabilitation strategies, Stress reduction
technique, repair and strengthening of columns and beams, Compressive strength of
concrete, cracks/joints, masonry, foundation, base isolation.

6 Guidelines for repair and rehabilitation works: 03
Guidelines for framing terms and conditions for repair and rehabilitation works

contracts, engagement of consultants, contractors, execution of work, post repair
inspection
