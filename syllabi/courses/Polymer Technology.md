Course Objective
The course provides a broad and fundamental knowledge of the polymers, their structure,
behavior, and method of polymerizations. Further, it also focuses on environmental aspect and
methods of processing along with overview on their application to various field.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Understand and identify the polymer structure.
2. Demonstrate a broad understanding of the synthesis methods and structural properties of
polymers.
3. Explain the environmental effect and various processing methods
4. Identify the application of polymers in advanced field of engineering.
