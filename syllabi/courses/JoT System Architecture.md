Course Objective
To impart knowledge on IoT Architecture and various protocols, and study their
implementations.

Course Outcomes

After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the architectural overview of IoT.
2. Describe the loT reference architecture and real world design constraints.
3. Understand the various IoT protocols applicable to the layers.
