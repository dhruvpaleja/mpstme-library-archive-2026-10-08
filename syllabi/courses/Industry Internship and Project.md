Objectives:
1. The Objective of the Industry Internship and Project is to provide the students
with experience in analysing, designing, implementation and evaluating
information systems.
2. It is an attempt to bridge the gap between the academic institution and
corporate world.
3. The Industry Internship and Project, which would be a simulation of real work
environment, requires that the students undergo the rigor of professional
environment both in form and substance. In the process it provides an
opportunity for students, to satisfy their inquisitiveness to know more details,
expose them to technical skills, and helps them to acquire social skills by
drawing them into communication with outside professional for continuous
interaction.
Course Outcomes:
1. Explain basic functioning of organization and role of various departments.
2. Apply theory to practice in the assigned tasks.
3. Demonstrate the steps of SDLC in assigned project.
Guidelines:
1. Project work may be done individually or in groups in case of bigger projects.
However if project is done in groups, each student must be given a
responsibility for a distinct module and care should be taken to see the progress
of individual modules is independent of others.
2. Student should choose a project which has System development (coding)
involved.
3. For proper coordination and ensuring organized and smooth conduct, each
student would be under the guidance of an MPSTME faculty (referred as
Internal Mentor). A representative of the industry/organization (referred as
Company Mentor) also guides the student and assists the faculty in monitoring
the student’s progress.

8. The project report will be duly accessed by the internal guide of the subject .

which also specifies the contents and methods of presentation.
