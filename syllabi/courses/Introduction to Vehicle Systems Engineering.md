Pre-requisite: Calculus
Course Objective
This course introduces students to the systems-level engineering of modern vehicles, with emphasis on
Electric Vehicles (EVs). It covers vehicle architecture, powertrain systems, energy storage, dynamics,
control, safety, and emerging technologies. Students also gain basic knowledge of the integration of
individual subsystems to achieve an efficient, safe, and sustainable vehicle.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understand vehicle systems architecture and subsystem integration
2. Describe Electric vehicle architecture
3. Differentiate between types of motors and power electronic systems
4. Apply fundamentals of vehicle dynamics and control
5. Explain battery systems, charging, safety, and emerging technologies.
