Program: B Tech (Mechanical & Mechatronics Engineering)   Semester: V
Course: Additive Manufacturing   Code: 702ME0E045
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorials   Internal Continuous Term End Examinations
Hours per Hours per Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   (TEE)
week   week   week   (Marks-50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Scaled to 50 marks   Scaled to 50 marks
Pre-requisite: Manufacturing Processes I and II, Machine Drawing
Course Objective
The objective of the course is to impart introductory knowledge of Additive Manufacturing (AM)
and its capabilities in the modern digital manufacturing industries. The course is structured to
acquaint the students with the operating principles and applications of most prominent
technologies in the field of additive manufacturing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Compare the fundamental principles of various additive manufacturing technologies in terms
of material requirements, manufacturing technologies and applications
2. Identify critical areas where AM can be applied to create value for the product
3. Apply the necessary skills in selecting the right AM process for a given application
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Additive Manufacturing   06
Introduction to AM, history of AM, definition, distinction between AM, CNC
& other digital manufacturing techniques, reasons to use AM, classifications
of AM processes, design for additive manufacturing (DfAM), AM industry:
present and future, AM process chain, AM file formats (STL, 3MF, AMF, PLY,
VRML, and LEO), part orientation, support generation, model slicing,
contour generation, machine set-up, build file preparation.
2.   Classification of Additive Manufacturing Techniques   12
Material Extrusion:
Process mechanism, process parameters, extrusion of: ceramics, metals,
biomaterials, composites, contour crafting, concrete printing
Vat Polymerization:
Process mechanism, process parameters, advantages, disadvantages,
applications, Digital Light Processing (DLP), recent advances, Large-Scale
Rapid Liquid Printing.
Binder & material jetting processes:
Binder jetting process workflow, material requirements, binder-powder
interaction, binder properties.
Material Jetting process workflow, thermal and piezoelectric inkjet, material
jetting fluid mechanics, drop-on-demand and continuous methods of MJ.
Powder bed fusion AM process:

Selective laser sintering (SLS), process mechanism, process parameters,
selective laser melting (SLM), process workflow and material requirements,
powder melting mechanisms, electron beam melting (EBM) process.
Sheet Lamination AM Processes:
Process mechanism, process parameters, ultrasonic additive manufacturing
(UAM), ultrasonic welding fundamentals, UAM process parameters
Directed Energy Deposition (DED) Processes:
Process workflow, materials feeding for DED: powder (Laser Engineered Net
Shaping) and wire, process parameters, cold spray.
3.   Post- Processing   04
Product quality evaluation, need for post-processing, support material
removal, surface finish improvement, aesthetic improvement, property
enhancements using non-thermal and thermal techniques.
4.   Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM)   04
Design guidelines: part orientation, support, geometry considerations, layer
thickness, part size, material consideration, residual stress, topology
optimization, case studies on design for AM.
5.   Applications of AM & Industry 4.0   04
Aerospace, biomedical, defence, automobile, rapid tooling, reverse
engineering, economic consideration, Industry 4.0 for AM, case studies on
application of AM in various industries.
Total   30
Text Books

Digital Manufacturing, 2nd Edition, Springer, 2015.

applications, 3D Hubs, 2017.

e Acquire introductory knowledge of Additive Manufacturing and applicability

using file repair algorithms.

Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
e Understand the importance of Additive Manufacturing and identifying
various industrial sectors where it can be applied.
¢ Include AM as a critical tool in the product design cycle and to rapidly develop
prototypes, thereby reducing the lead time
e Understand and operate FDM 3D Printers.
¢ Identify enterprising opportunities in the field of Additive Manufacturing.
