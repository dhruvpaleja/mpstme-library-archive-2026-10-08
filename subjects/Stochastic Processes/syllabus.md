Pre-requisite: Probability and Statistics
Course Objective
This course introduces students to stochastic processes, spectral representation and estimation, mean
square estimation, Markov chains, and queueing theory.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. 1. Define the fundamental concepts of stochastic processes and classify different types of
stochastic processes. (L1- Remembering, L2-Understanding)
2. Explain stationarity, ergodicity, and correlation functions in random processes. (L2-
Understanding)
3. Compute the power spectral density and autocorrelation functions for given stochastic
processes. (L3- Applying)
4. Analyze the response of linear systems to random inputs using autocorrelation and spectral
properties. (L4- Analyzing)
5. Distinguish between discrete and continuous Markov chains and illustrate their transition
properties. (L2 -Understanding, L4- Analyzing)
6. Apply probability models to solve queueing theory problems such as M/G/1 and G/M/1
systems. (L3 -Applying)
