Pre-requisite: Fundamentals of website Designing
Course Objective: This course covers the theoretical foundations, implementation problems,
and research issues of the emerging area of electronic commerce. It discusses technological,
conceptual and methodological aspects of electronic commerce.
Course Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, student will be able to
1. Analyse the need of E-Commerce, Legal and Ethical issues in related technologies
2. Analyse the core concepts of Ecommerce and E-business and its strategies.
3. Understand internet payment system and advance software of E-commerce.
4. Design and implement E- Commerce/M-Commerce website.
Detailed Syllabus:
Unit Description   Duration
1.   Introduction: Electronic commerce and Physical Commerce, different   5
type of ecommerce, some e-commerce scenario, Advantages of e-
commerce
2.   Legal & Ethical Issues in E-Commerce: Legal Issues: Borders &   6
Jurisdiction, Contracting & Contract Enforcement, Protection of
Intellectual Property in Online Business; Ethical Issues: Online Crimes,
Terrorism & Warfare; Social Issues.
3.   Internet Payment System: Characteristics of payment system, SET   6
Protocol for credit card payment, E-cash, E-check, Micropayment
system
4.   E-commerce strategies: Strategies for marketing, Sales and Promotions,   6
Strategies for Purchasing and support activities, Strategies for Web
Auctions, Virtual Communities, and web portals
5.   M-Commerce: Growth; Overview; Benefits & Drivers; Applications;   6
Mobile Computing Infrastructure; Inhibitors and Barriers of M-
Commerce Services; Stakeholders; M-Commerce Value Chain;
Pervasive Computing; L-Commerce.
