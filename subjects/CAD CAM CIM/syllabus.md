Course: CAD/CAM/CIM   Code: 702ME0C021

3   2   0   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Graphics and Design, Manufacturing Processes-I, Manufacturing
Processes-II
Course Objective
The principal objective of this course is to introduce basics of backend of drafting packages and to
give hands on experience on the 3D modeling software. The course has been structured to impart
knowledge of NC/CNC machines and programming of CNC using machine codes along with
providing concepts of GT, CAPP, CAQC and CIM.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the application of computers in design process and product life cycle management
2. Demonstrate mathematical models to represent various curves
3. Apply the CAD modeling knowledge for modeling of mechanical parts and assemblies
4. Analyze various geometric transformation and manipulation techniques
5. Develop CNC part programs to manufacture industrial components
6. Explain concept of group technology, computer aided quality control and computer integrated
manufacturing
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction and Elements of Interactive Computer Graphics   08
The design process, computers for design process, product life cycle and
CAD/CAM, concurrent engineering in product design and development,
collaborative engineering, CAD system architecture - software and hardware,
the role of modeling and communication, modeling using CAD
2.   Techniques for Geometric Modeling   06
Graphic standards, the parametric representation of geometry - Bezier curves,
cubic spline curve, B-spline curve, parametric representation of line, circle,
ellipse and parabola, constructive solid geometry (CSG), boundary
representation (B-rep), wire frame modeling, solid modeling, surface
modeling, feature based modeling, assembly modeling - different approaches
of creating an assembly, parent child relationship
3.   Transformation, Manipulation and Data Storage   08
2D and 3D transformations (translation, rotation, scaling and magnification),
concatenations, matrix representation, problems on transformations. object
transformation, mirror transformation, bill of materials from attribute data,
engineering data management system, relational data base for design, object
oriental database, design information systems
4.   NC and CNC Technology   10
Introduction, basic components of NC system, NC procedure, NC coordinate
systems, NC motion control systems, various actuation systems and feedback
devices like encoder, tachogenerator etc., applications, advantages and
disadvantages of NC machines, constructional details of CNC machines, CNC
