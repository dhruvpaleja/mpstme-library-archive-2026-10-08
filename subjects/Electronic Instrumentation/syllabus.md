Pre-requisite: Basic Electrical Engineering, Basic Electronics.
Objectives:

1. To understand the operation of signal generating and measuring instruments.
2. To understand the operation and applications of Sensors and Transducers.
3. To learn measurements of various Electronic quantities and use of various

Measuring Instruments.

Outcomes:
After the successful completion of this course, the student will be able to

1. Describe the configuration of measurement system and its characteristics.
2. Identify and apply sensors and Transducers for different process parameters.
3. Measure and compare the performance of measuring instruments for various

electrical parameters.
4. Describe the working of different signal generating and analyzing instruments.
