Pre-requisite: Fundamentals of Mechanics-Statics
Course Objective
This course focusses on the conventional machines and kinematics involved in machines. It intends to help
the students understand the turning moment diagrams of IC engines and the gyroscopic couples of
different automobiles.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamentals of link, machines and mechanism
2. Illustrate turning moment diagram of engines attached with flywheel
3. Apply the principle of gyroscopic couple to stabilize an automobiles, airplanes and ships
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Simple Mechanisms   14
Introduction, Kinematic Link or Element, Types of Links, Structure, Difference
Between a Machine and a Structure, Kinematic Pair, Types of Constrained Motions,
Classification of Kinematic Pairs, Kinematic Chain, Types of Joints in a Chain,
Mechanism, Number of Degrees of Freedom for Plane Mechanisms.
Application of Kutzbach Criterion to Plane Mechanisms, Grubler’s Criterion for
Plane Mechanisms, Inversion of Mechanism, Types of Kinematic Chains, Four Bar
Chain or Quadric Cycle Chain, Inversions of Four Bar Chain.
2.   Mechanisms with Lower Pairs   07
Introduction, Pantograph, Straight Line Mechanism- Peaucellier mechanism,
Watts’s mechanism, Modified Scott-Russel mechanism, Grasshopper mechanism.
Straight Line Motions for Engine Indicators-Simplex indicator, Steering gear
mechanism, Universal or Hooke's Joint, Double Hooke's Joint.
3.   Velocity in Mechanisms   06
Relative Velocity of Two Bodies Moving in Straight Lines, Motion of a Link,
Velocity of a Point on a Link by Relative Velocity Method, Velocities in- four bar
pin jointed and four bar slider crank Mechanism (Graphical approach), Rubbing
Velocity at a Pin Joint.
4.   Turning Moment Diagram and Flywheels   10
Working principal of internal combustion engines, turning moment diagram for a
single cylinder double acting steam engine, turning moment diagram for a four-
stroke cycle internal combustion engine, turning moment diagram for a multi-
cylinder engine, fluctuation of energy, determination of maximum fluctuation of

Pre-requisite: Fundamentals of Mechanics-Statics
Course Objective
The course is aimed at imparting the understanding of various kinematics involved in machines. This
course focusses on the conventional machines and mechanisms and helps the students to understand the
turning moment diagrams of IC engines and the gyroscopic couples of different automobiles.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Describe mechanisms used for general machineries
2. Illustrate turning moment diagram of engines attached with flywheel
3. Apply the principle of gyroscopic couple to stabilize an automobiles, airplanes and ships
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Developments of Machines   06
History of Machines, history of kinematics, popular machines, development of
ingenious mechanisms, gears and screws, beginning of the wheel, water wheels,
wind mills.
2.   Machines and Mechanisms   10
Machines - Raising water, clocks and automatons, war machines, lifting machines,
hydraulic machines, machine tools, machines for traction and transport, domestic
apparatus, mills, machinery and precious metals, textile machinery, handcraft
manufacturing, hydraulic machines, steam engines, automatic astronomical
devices.
3.   Kinematics   10
Basics mechanism and machines, rigid and resistant body, link, classification of
kinematics pairs, lower pairs and higher pairs. degrees of freedom, mobility,
Grashof’s criterion, inversions of four bar chain mechanism and its applications.
Peaucellier’s and Hart’s exact straight line generating mechanisms, Watt’s
approximate straight-line mechanism, Grass-hopper mechanism, pantograph, hook
joint- single and double.
4.   Turning Moment Diagram and Flywheels   10
Working principal of internal combustion engines, turning moment diagram for a
single cylinder double acting steam engine, turning moment diagram for a four-
stroke cycle internal combustion engine, turning moment diagram for a multi-
cylinder engine, fluctuation of energy, determination of maximum fluctuation of
