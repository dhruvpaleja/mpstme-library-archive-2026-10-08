Course Objective:
1. To explain supply chain management and logistics management in detail in order to understand its
principles, design of networks, infrastructural requirements, and sourcing, inventory and sustainability
issues.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Describe the Scope and importance of SCM, role of logistics management in SC and its importance
in warehousing, Transportation, Material handling, Information and Packaging
2. Explain & Measure SC Performance, Performance drivers & Metrics and achieving strategic fit in SC
and design of SC Network.
3. Describe Infrastructural requirements, planning and managing inventories in SC, sourcing and
sustainability issues.
4. Apply SCM & LM principles in present scenario.
