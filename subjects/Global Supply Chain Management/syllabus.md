Pre-requisite:
Operations Management, Logistics and Supply Chain Management
Objectives:
 To impart a perspective on characteristics of globally dispersed multiple owner supply chain
networks
 To sensitise students about the best practices in global supply chain networks.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Define the network of nodes adding value to the products/services that flow through the logistics
pipelines connecting the nodes.
2. Assess the factors affecting global supply chain networks, its performance and improvement and
the tremendous need for its integration into the supply chain network management strategies.

Governance of networked organizations.

global inventory, transportation and material handling, Global packaging, global
distribution centers.
5   Global Operations strategy: Global market channels strategy, make or buy   4
