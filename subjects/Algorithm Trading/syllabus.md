The aim of the course is to provide Students with basic consortium related to market trading. It also

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamental concepts of algorithmic trading including market structure, order

2. Analyze different chart patterns and technical indicators (e.g., SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD,

backtesting methods while identifying potential pitfalls.

or real-time trading environment using technical and algorithmic tools.

Course Objective
The aim of the course is to provide Students with basic consortium related to market trading. It also
provides information on development of real-time strategies. It focuses on creating trading engines that
will be supported by advance data analytics
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamental concepts of algorithmic trading including market structure, order
types, brokers, dealers, and various market participants.
2. Analyze different chart patterns and technical indicators (e.g., SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD,
Bollinger Bands) to interpret market trends and price movements.
3. Apply suitable trading strategies such as scalping, arbitrage, mean-reverting, and sentiment-
based strategies in different trading environments (e.g., equities, derivatives, forex).
4. Evaluate the performance and effectiveness of various algorithmic trading strategies using
backtesting methods while identifying potential pitfalls.
5. Design a simple rule-based or indicator-based trading strategy and implement it in a simulated
or real-time trading environment using technical and algorithmic tools.
