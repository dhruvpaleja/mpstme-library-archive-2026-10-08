## B TECH INTEGRATED · Mechanical · batch 2023-2029 · Sem 4

- Course as printed: Production Technology (code 701ME0C017)
- Source: `04_Syllabi_Archive/B TECH INTEGRATED/Mechanical/Batch ( 2023 To 2029 )/Semester - 4.pdf`, page 1-3

````text
Program: B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: IV
Course: Production Technology   Code: 701ME0C017
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours   (Hours   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
per week) per week)   per week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Scaled to 50 marks   Scaled to 50 marks
Pre-requisite: Basic Manufacturing Engineering
Course Objective
This course introduces fundamentals of material removal processes including non-conventional
machining processes. A focus is placed on different machine tools including CNC machines to
produce components having different shapes and sizes.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Describe the working principles and constructional features of various machine tools
2. Choose appropriate machine tools and processes to manufacture a product
3. Analyze various process parameters that affect machining processes
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   05
Overview of manufacturing processes, introduction to machining, types of
chips, cutting tool geometry and tool signature, cutting materials and
cutting fluids, classification of machine tools.
2.   Special Purpose Lathes   08
Limitations of centre lathe, capstan and turret lathes, tool layouts,
automatics lathes, cutting off machines, swiss type automatics, automatic
screw machines, multiple-spindle automatics, CNC Machines-Introduction,
classification, working, constructional features, advantages and limitations.
3.   Machine Tools   08
Introduction, classification, working and auxiliary motions, Types,
specifications, construction of drilling, milling, planing, shaper machines.
4.   Grinding and Metal Finishing Operations   10
Grinding process, machine types and operations, grinding wheels-
composition, specification, balancing, truing, dressing and shaping.
Finishing processes: reaming, honing, lapping, burnishing, polishing,
buffing. Surface finishing operations-chemical and mechanical cleaning,
coating operations-plating, physical vapor deposition, chemical vapor
deposition.
5. 5Gear Manufacturing Processes   05
Methods of manufacture: Casting, Machining & Forming, Gear finishing
processes.
6. 6Unconventional Machining Processes   09
Introduction, need for unconventional process, comparison between
traditional and   non-traditional machining, general   classification,
classification based on nature of energy employed in machining, working
principle of electric-discharge machining, electrochemical machining,
ultrasonic machining, chemical machining, laser beam machining, abrasive
water-jet machining, electron beam machining.
Total   45
````
