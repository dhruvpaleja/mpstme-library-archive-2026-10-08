Q2. A boutique clothing brand notices that despite posting regularly on Facebook, its posts are
reaching fewer users. Engagement has dropped even though content quality is high. The team realizes
that posts are not appearing on users’ feeds consistently. They suspect algorithm-related issues and
lack of interaction history with followers. The brand is unsure how to improve visibility organically.
Explain how Edge Rank factors influence this situation and suggest improvements.
Affinity: Low interaction history → Facebook shows fewer posts
Weight: Text-heavy/promotional posts have lower weight than video/interactive content
Time Decay: Older posts lose visibility quickly
Recommendations:
Encourage engagement (polls, comments)
Use high-weight formats (videos, reels)
Post consistently and at optimal times
Q3. An OTT streaming platform launches an X campaign to promote its new crime thriller series. The
ads are shown to a broad audience, including users who primarily engage with comedy and lifestyle
content. Engagement rates remain low, and subscriptions do not increase as expected. The marketing
team realizes that the messaging is not reaching the right viewers. They want to refine their targeting
approach. Explain how Demographic, Interest, Follower, and Behaviour Targeting can improve
results.
1. Demographic Targeting
•   Target users based on age, income level, and location
• Focus on higher-income, urban consumers more likely to purchase premium fashion
2. Interest Targeting
•   Target users interested in luxury fashion, designer brands, and premium lifestyle
• Ensures ads reach users with relevant preferences
3. Follower Targeting
•   Target followers of luxury fashion brands and influencers
• Helps reach users already engaged with premium fashion content
********** 2 **********
--- page 3 ---
4. Behaviour Targeting
•   Target users based on online behaviour and purchase patterns
• Focus on users who have shown interest in premium or high-value products
Recommendation:
By combining all four targeting methods, the brand can shift from a broad audience to a highly relevant
niche audience, improving engagement and conversions.
Q4. An affiliate platform successfully drives first-time purchases through product reviews and ads.
However, customers rarely return to make additional purchases. The affiliate does not offer incentives
for repeat usage and has limited engagement after the first transaction. Please recommend and
explain the various options available for the affiliate to improve repeat purchases and lifetime value?
1. Content / Niche Website
• Continuously create relevant content (reviews, tips, guides)
• Keep users engaged and encourage them to return to the website
2. Email Lists
•   Build an opt-in email list
• Send regular newsletters with relevant offers
• Helps maintain continuous engagement after first purchase
3. Loyalty / Cashback Sites
•   Offer cashback or reward points on purchases
• Share a portion of affiliate commission with users
• Encourages repeat purchases and long-term usage
4. Coupon and Promotion Sites
• Provide discounts and special offers
•   Attract users to return for better deals and savings
5. Search Advertising
• Use paid search ads to re-target users
• Reach users again when they search for related products
6. Personal Website as Central Platform
********** 3 **********
--- page 4 ---
•   Integrate all methods (content, offers, links)
• Act as a hub for engagement and repeat interaction
Overall Recommendation:
The affiliate should move from a single transaction focus to a multi-channel engagement strategy, using
content, email, rewards, and promotions to drive repeat purchases and increase lifetime value.
Q5. A healthcare information portal recently redesigned its website with better visuals and added new
articles. However, its search rankings have not improved, and traffic remains stagnant. The team focused
mainly on publishing content but did not evaluate technical performance, keyword positioning, or backlinks.
They are unsure why the redesign has not translated into better SEO performance. Explain how the company
should apply the SEO phases to improve results.
1. Audit Phase
• Conduct a content audit of all existing content (website, articles, etc.)
• Assess how well the content matches strategic needs of the brand and audience
2. Content Phase
•   Create content aligned with customer needs and buying cycle
• Ensure content is relevant and valuable to attract and engage users
3. On-page Optimization
• Improve structure of content (how it is organised and found)
• Ensure users can easily navigate and find information
4. Off-page Optimization
• Focus on improving external signals (e.g., links, visibility) to strengthen presence
5. Maintenance Phase
• Continuously monitor and update content
• Ensure content remains aligned with strategy and audience needs
Overall Recommendation:
The company should not rely only on content creation but follow a structured SEO process (audit → content
→ optimization → maintenance) to improve rankings and traffic.
********** 4 **********
--- page 5 ---
Q6. A home décor marketplace has noticed a steady decline in online sales despite offering quality
products. A quick check reveals mixed reviews across platforms like Google and marketplace listings, with
several negative comments going unanswered. The company has not actively encouraged satisfied
customers to leave reviews and does not monitor ratings regularly. As a result, potential buyers perceive the
brand as unreliable. How can the company improve its performance using the four components of Reviews
and Ratings Solution in ORM?
Motivating Customers for Reviews
• Encourage customers to give reviews by:
o Offering incentives
o Keeping short surveys
o Building customer communities/panels
2. Identifying Real and Fake Reviews
• Monitor reviews to differentiate genuine and fake feedback
• Ensure authenticity of reviews to maintain trust and credibility
3. Handling Negative Reviews
• Respond to negative reviews and address customer concerns
• Use feedback to improve service and maintain positive brand perception
4. Managing Fake Negative Reviews
•   Identify and handle fake negative reviews
•   Prevent misleading information from affecting brand image
Overall Recommendation:
The company should actively monitor, encourage, and manage reviews, as reviews and ratings form a major
part of online content influencing customer decisions.
Q7. A company produces biodegradable packaging solutions for businesses. While it highlights
sustainability and compliance with environmental norms, most clients perceive it as a costlier alternative
********** 5 **********
--- page 6 ---
rather than a value-driven brand. The company struggles to communicate its larger purpose. Apply the
Brand Pyramid to reposition the brand.
• Features & Attributes: Biodegradable materials, eco-friendly production
• Functional Benefits: Reduced environmental impact, regulatory compliance
• Emotional Benefits: Pride in sustainability, responsible business image
• Brand Persona: Responsible, forward-thinking, ethical leader
• Brand Idea: “Packaging that protects the planet”
Recommendation: Shift focus from cost to purpose-driven branding

• CPM stands for cost per thousand impressions (M is the Roman numeral for a thousand).

•   loads on the publisher’s page.

• CPC stands for cost per click. This means that the advertiser pays only when their advert is
