Pre-requisite: Fluid Mechanics, Strength of Materials and Design of Machine Elements
Course Objective
The course deals with the design and analysis of pressure vessels. The course addresses different
types of materials handling equipment and the design of belt conveyors. This course also discusses
about the design of pipelines which are the preferred mode of freight transport in many situations.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Design of pressure vessels for industrial needs
2. Choose a belt conveyor and hoisting mechanisms for different applications
3. Examine the design of pipelines for various needs
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Pressure Vessels   05
Pressure vessel construction codes, design procedure, selection of materials,
layout, elastic stress analysis, cylindrical and spherical shells, vessel components,
considerations in design.
2.   Material Handling   05
Hand trucks, Power trucks, Forklift trucks, Tractors, Parts of hoisting equipment,
hoists, winches, elevators, cranes, derricks
3.   Belt Conveyors
General applications, conveyor arrangements, characteristics of bulk materials,   10
capacities, belt widths, speeds, belt conveyor idlers, belt tension, power, drive
engineering, selection of belt.
4.   Pipelines
Friction factor, Equivalent Pipe Lengths, Hydraulic Radius, Compressible Flow,   10
Pipe Sizing, Basic Code Equations, Pipe Turns, Miter Bends, External Pressure
Total   30

(Classic)
