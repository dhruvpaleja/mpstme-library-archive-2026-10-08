Course Objective
Students will be familiar with basic rules of probability and will be able to use them in modelling uncertainty
in obtaining and recording data. They will be able to utilize graphical and numerical summaries of data in
understanding data generating processes. To enable the students to analyze data more effectively using
statistical software.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Classify, organize, summarize and visually represent qualitative and quantitative data using
appropriate tabulation and descriptive statistical tools and graphical techniques.( L1 – Remembering
, L2 – Understanding and L3 – Applying)
2. Measure, interpret and analyze variability, skewness, kurtosis, covariance and correlations, and
appropriately visualize relationships in data. (L3 – Applying and L4 – Analyzing)
3. Apply and evaluate concepts of probability, conditional probability, independence, Bayes’ theorem,
and compute probabilities using common discrete and continuous distributions.( L3 – Applying, L4 –
Analyzing and L5 – Evaluating)
4. Apply the concepts of discrete and continuous random variables to model uncertainty by defining
probability distributions (PMF, PDF, CDF), computing expected value and variance, and analyzing
joint and marginal distributions of single and two-dimensional random variables. (L3 – Applying, L4
– Analyzing)
5. Formulate statistical inferences by designing hypothesis tests to validate assumptions and draw
conclusions from sample data.( L6 – Create)
