Definition of an Open System (1.5 Mark)
An open system, also known as a Control Volume (CV), is defined by its
boundaries (the control surface) through which both energy (heat and work) and
mass can cross. Most engineering devices that involve fluid flow (e.g., pumps,
CO-3;   turbines, nozzles) are modeled as open systems.
