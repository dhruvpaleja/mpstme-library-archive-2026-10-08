Program: B Tech (Mechatronics Engineering)   Semester: III
Course: Thermodynamics   Code: 702MH0C024
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   per week)   per week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   0   1   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Calculus, Physics
Course Objective
The course starts out with elaborate illustrations of the first and second laws of thermodynamics.
The course emphasizes the relationship between work and energy by examples. It teaches the
students to use thermodynamic properties of materials and the analytical tools necessary to solve
engineering problems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify various forms of energy
2. Describe laws of thermodynamic principles related to various thermal systems
3. Apply the laws of thermodynamics to estimate the performance parameters of thermal
systems
4. Apply the understanding of Entropy in analysis of its implications in various thermal systems
5. Apply the principles and concepts to the analysis of various thermodynamic cycles
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
Introduction
Fundamentals: System, control volume, property,   state, process, cycle,
1.   06
equilibrium, Zeroth law, Principles of Thermometry, forms of energy, heat, work,
various forms of work
First Law of Thermodynamics
First law Analysis of closed system for cyclic & non-cyclic process, internal
2.   06
energy, enthalpy, free expansion process, application of First law to flow
processes, limitations of first law
Second Law of Thermodynamics
Qualitative difference between the heat and work, heat engines, refrigerators,
heat pumps, Kelvin-Planck & Clausius statements, PMM-II, Carnot Cycle,
reversible & irreversible cycles, statement of third law of thermodynamics and its
3.   importance   08
Entropy
Clausius inequality, definition of entropy, demonstration that entropy is a
property, T-s diagram, isentropic efficiency, available and unavailable energy,
concept of irreversibility and lost work, T-ds equations
Pure Substances and Gases
Properties of pure substances, water and steam - constant temperature and
4.   06
constant pressure heating, use of steam tables: saturation tables, superheated
tables, Mollier chart, ideal gas and their mixtures, real gases
Thermodynamic Cycles and Steam Generators
5.   Analysis of air standard cycles and vapor power cycles, steams generators, fire   04
tube and water tube boilers, boiler mountings and accessories
Total   30
