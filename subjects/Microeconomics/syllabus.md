Pre-requisite: Basic knowledge of Economics concepts
Course Objectives:
• To provide a better understanding and knowledge in the field of microeconomics and its
relevance for economic decision-making.
• To develop the basic skills in students required to conduct and interpret research
related to microeconomics dealing with issues at various levels in an economy.
• To analyze the impact of changes in microeconomic parameters and policies on economic
relations as well as our economy.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Illustrate basic concepts of economics (demand, supply, elasticity, scarcity) and
explain behaviour on individual, households, and firms and handle economic data
and write economic reports,
2. Apply utility analysis and production theories to understand consumer decision-
making and cost structures in different market conditions,
3. Understand the impact of Economic Policies and their implication on the Economy,
4. Differentiate between market structures such as monopoly, oligopoly, perfect and
imperfect competition, and explain price and output determination.
