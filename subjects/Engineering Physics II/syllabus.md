Objectives:
This course is aimed to teach the drawbacks of classical physics in explaining several experimental
observations and old quantum theory; and to discuss the necessity of new mechanics, laws of
thermodynamics and acoustics for engineers.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. understand the physics of acoustics.
2. explain the basic laws related to quantum mechanics and application.
3. elucidate the concepts of statistical mechanics and solve problems using the same.
4. Describe the concepts of thermodynamics and its application.
Detailed Syllabus: ( per session plan )
Unit   Description   Duration
1. Introduction to Acoustic   6
Sound, Intensity of sound, threshold of hearing, loudness of sound, Reverberation
time, sound absorption, Sabines’s formula for reverberation time, determination of
absorption coefficient, Acoustic of building.
2. Basic postulates of quantum mechanics   10
Introduction wave particle duality, Concept of wave function, Superposition
principle of eigenstates. Concept of collapse of wave function. Time dependent and
time independent Schrodinger Equation, Concept of free particle, particle in an
infinite and finite potential well.
3. Concept of Quantum Tunnelling   8
Reflection and Transmission coefficients. Few realistic examples of tunnelling, e.g.,
alpha decay, Probe microscopes (Scanning Tunnelling microscope). Simple
Harmonic Oscillator, explanation in 1D (no detailed derivation). Hydrogen atom.
4. Introduction to Statistical Physics   8
Ensembles (Canonical, Micro canonical and Grand canonical) Classical (Maxwell-
Boltzmann) and Quantum statistics, [Bose Einstein (BE) and Fermi Dirac (FD)].
Introduction to classical statistics and BE and FD statistics.
5.   Applications of Statistical physics   5
Equipartition of energy, Planck’s distribution, Bose-Einstein Condensation
