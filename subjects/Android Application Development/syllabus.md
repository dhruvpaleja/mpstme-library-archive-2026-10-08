Objectives
1. To understand the Android components, tools, and technologies and implement features like
multi-screen Navigation, Android Studio, Intents, Fragments, Widgets, Layout to build
modern applications.
2. To design and use architectural techniques to build user-friendly and interactive applications.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to -
1. Apply knowledge of object oriented programming and extend the knowledge
for designing and creating mobile applications,
2. Design, implement, and evaluate a process, component, or program on the
Mobile Platform to meet desired needs
