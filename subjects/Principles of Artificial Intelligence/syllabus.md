````text
Program: B Tech and MBA Tech IT   Semester: IV
Course : Principles of Artificial Intelligence   Code: 702IT0C035
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial
Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours   (Hours per   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
per week)   week)   per week)   (Marks- 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Computer Programming and Data Structures.
Course Objective
To provide a strong foundation of fundamental concepts in Artificial Intelligence and to enable the
student to apply these techniques in applications, which involve perception, reasoning and
learning.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to –
1. Explain the history & basic principles of AI in solutions that require problem solving
2. Discuss knowledge representation and apply learning techniques like Neural networks,
Bayesian networks etc. in various AI problems
3. Analyze and design a real-world problem for implementation of NLP methods
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Introduction   3
Overview and historical perspective, Turing test, physical symbol systems and
the scope of symbolic AI, agents & its types
2   Problem Solving   8
Problem spaces (states, goals and operators), problem solving by search,
uninformed search (breadth-first, depth-first, depth-first with iterative
deepening), heuristics and informed search (hill-climbing, generic best first, A*,
AO*), minimax search, alpha-beta pruning, two-player games (introduction to
minimax search), constraint satisfaction (backtracking and local search methods).
3   Knowledge and Reasoning   6
Building a knowledge base, propositional logic, first order logic, resolution and
theorem proving.
4   Learning   7
Overview of different forms of learning, learning decision trees, probabilistic
learning Bayesian networks, neural networks and SVMs.
5   Introduction to Natural Language Processing   6
Language models, n-grams, vector space models, bag of words, text
classification, information retrieval, PageRank, information extraction, question
answering.
Total   30
Text Books
1. Dan W. Patterson, Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and Expert Systems, 1st Edition, Pearson
Education, 2015.

SVKM’s Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies
Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering
2. Stuart Russel and Peter Norvig, Artificial Intelligence A Modern Approach, 4th Edition, Pearson
Education, 2010.
Reference Books
1. Elaine Rich, Kevin Knigh and Shivashankar B Nair , Artificial Intelligence, 3rd Edition, Tata
McGraw Hill, 2017.
Laboratory Work
8 to 10 Programming exercises (and a practicum) based on the syllabus.
