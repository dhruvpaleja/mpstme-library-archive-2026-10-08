Pre-requisite: Knowledge of Engineering Mathematics, Circuits and Network Technology, Signals
and Systems.
Course Objectives
This course aims to acquaint students with the fundamentals of the Basics theory of process and
control systems and System stability. It also helps to analyse and design the system for fulfilling the
performance and stability criterion.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the modelling of linear-time-invariant systems using transfer function and state space
representations
2. Describe the concept of stability and its assessment for linear-time invariant systems
3. Design simple feedback controllers
4. Analyse the state space variable and models
