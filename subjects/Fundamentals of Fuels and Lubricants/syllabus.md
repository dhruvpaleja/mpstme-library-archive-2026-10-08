Detailed Syllabus

Unit | Description Duration

1. Introduction & Theory of Fuels 9
Definition, Classification, Characteristics, Calorific value & numerical based on
calorific value, Different Types of fuels, Structure of petroleum, refining
process, fuels, thermal cracking, catalytic cracking, polymerization, alkylation,
isomerisation, blending, products of refining process.

2. Properties & Testing of Fuel 9
Properties and testing of fuels, relative density, distillation, vapour pressure,
flash point, spontaneous ignition temperature, viscosity, pour point,
flammability, ignitability, diesel index, API gravity, aniline point etc.

3. Combustion & Fuel Rating 9
Combustion of fuels & numerical based on combustion, Mechanism of
combustion, normal combustion, knocking, octane rating, fuel requirements.
CI Engine, mechanism of combustion, diesel knock, cetane rating, fuel
requirements.

4. Theory of Lubrication 9
Introduction, Classification, Properties Mechanism of Lubrication,
hydrodynamic lubrication, elasto hydrodynamic lubrication, boundary
lubrication, bearing lubrication, functions of the lubrication system,
introduction to design of a lubricating system. Current trends in tribology

5. Lubricants 9
Specific requirements for automotive lubricants, oxidation deterioration and
degradation of lubricants, additives and additive mechanism, synthetic
lubricants, classification of lubricating oils, properties of lubricating oils, tests
on lubricants. Grease, classification, properties, test used in grease,
