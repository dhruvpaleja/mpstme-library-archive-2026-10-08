Prerequisite: Basic Knowledge of Computer
Objectives:
This course is an introduction to the field of Cybersecurity. This course introduces various
aspects of cybersecurity to the students. Students will learn about various security threats
and countermeasures.
Outcomes:
After completion of this course, students will be able to
1. Understand various aspects of cybersecurity
2. Understand various cybersecurity vulnerabilities, threats and countermeasures
3. Understand various security technologies and mechanisms
