This course focusses on the basics of structural dynamics. It also focusses on the evaluation of

After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Determine the natural properties of SDOF and MDOF Systems
2. Evaluate the response of SDOF and MDOF systems
3. Analyze the structure using IS code method and response spectrum
