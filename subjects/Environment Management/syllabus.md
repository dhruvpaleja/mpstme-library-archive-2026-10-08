Pre-requisite: Nil
Objectives
To make aware the environmental and social impact due to various types of pollutants and to
asses environmental and social impact due to new projects. It will also include the concept of
Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) for evaluation of the environmental footprint of various systems
and technologies.
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Assess the environmental footprint of projects, products and systems.
2. Understand LCA and EIA process for new projects, products and systems
3. Apply the knowledge of LCA and EIA
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Introduction to EIA   10
Classification of Pollution and Pollutants, – Evolution of EIA (Global and
Indian Scenario)- Elements of EIA –– Screening – Scoping - Public Consultation -
Environmental Clearance process in India - Key Elements in EIA(Govt. of India )
latest Notification
2   Social Impact Assessment (SIA)   10
Causes and impacts of harm by socio-economic status and locations. SIA
identifies and involves all potentially affected groups; analyses impact equity;
focuses the assessment on significant impacts and establishes monitoring and
mitigation programs. Scoping, social mapping, rapid participatory appraisal,
needs assessment and other SIA tools.
3   Standards for Water, Air and Noise Quality   5
Environmental Management Plan- EIA- Case studies of EIA, Concept of Carbon
footprint and environment foot print

4   Life Cycle Assessment   10
Introduction to life cycle assessment, concepts on "cradle-to-grave" approach for
assessing products, processes, industrial system. Overview of LCA Methodology
- Goal Definition, Life Cycle Inventory, Life Cycle Impact Assessment, Life
Cycle Interpretation
5   Case Studies   10
Focused in-class exploration of issues in a specific place/context. Students
should prepare outside of class by completing readings and performing
web searches to provide general environmental, social, and economic
context and issues for the case being studied that can be shared with the
class.
