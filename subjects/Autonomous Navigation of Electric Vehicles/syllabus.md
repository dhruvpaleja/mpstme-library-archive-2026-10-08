Pre-requisite: Electric Hybrid Automotive Technology
Course Objective
This course is designed to encourage the students to provide a distinctive skill set which is useful in
securing employment globally in the automotive or intelligent mobility sectors with the growing
demand for highly skilled professionals within automotive manufacturers.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate the key principles of navigation and decision making algorithms
2. Analyze the components of an automotive control systems and its implementation
3. Evaluate models of vehicle interactions and the movement of groups of vehicles
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Design thinking for EV   07
Autonomous vehicle sensors and imaging. Sensor integration architectures and
multiple sensor fusion. Indoor autonomous surveillance, AI algorithms for
sensing and imaging: supervised learning―neural networks.
2.   Traffic Engineering and intelligent transportation   10
Traffic stream components, intelligent transportation system in India. Principles
of path planning, navigation and decision making for autonomous vehicles
approaches. Satellite navigation system, simultaneous localisation and mapping
(SLAM). Application examples of path planning, navigation and decision
making algorithms.
3.   Introduction to transport systems and optimization theory   07
Advanced optimization techniques, traffic modelling & simulation, electrified
transport systems. Optimal energy management system based on driving
pattern recognition.
4.   Modern automotive architectures   07
A review of modern automotive control hardware requirements and
architectures. The evaluation of current and future vehicle networking
technologies.
5.   Autonomous navigation of EV   10
An introduction to navigation systems, history, system architecture, application;
modes of navigation – land.
