Pre-requisite: Elements of Mechanical Engineering
Course Objective
This course is aimed at providing students with a comprehensive understanding of modern
automotive technologies, focusing on advancements in vehicle design, propulsion systems, safety
features, connectivity, and sustainability. The students will gain insights into the latest innovations
that are shaping the automotive industry.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamental principles of modern automotive technologies and compare
internal combustion engines, hybrid systems, and electric powertrains
2. Demonstrate advanced vehicle dynamics and control systems and evaluate safety
technologies and autonomous driving features
3.   Illustrate the role of connectivity and data-driven technologies in automotive design and
discuss the impact of automotive technologies on environmental sustainability
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Automotive Technology   04
Historical overview of automotive technology evolution, Current trends and
challenges in the automotive industry, Introduction to vehicle architecture and
systems.
2.   Powertrain Technologies   06
Internal combustion engines: Principles and advancements, hybrid powertrains:
series, parallel, and series-parallel configurations, Electric vehicles: Battery types,
charging infrastructure, and electric drivetrains.
3.   Vehicle Dynamics and Control Systems   06
Suspension systems and their impact on ride and handling, Steering systems and
electronic steering assistance, Braking systems: ABS, Electronic Stability Control,
and regenerative braking.
4.   Safety and Autonomous Driving   08
Introduction to Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), Sensor
technologies: LiDAR, radar, cameras, Levels of autonomy in vehicles.
5.   Connectivity and Human-Machine Interface   08
Vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication,
Infotainment systems and user interfaces, Cybersecurity considerations in
connected vehicles.
