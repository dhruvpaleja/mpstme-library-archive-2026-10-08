Pre-requisite: Nil

Course Objective

Graphology is one of the branches of diverse group of sciences of character reading. With the help
of graphology, one focuses on interpreting an individual’s character and personality traits by
analyzing the handwriting. Students can use graphology to understand the personality and traits of
any person. Students will develop the ability to lead and manage people.

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to-
1. Identify different personality traits by Handwriting Analysis (Graphology) and signature
Analysis
2. Apply principles of Graphology for personal development
3. Design Graphology based applications for organizational development
