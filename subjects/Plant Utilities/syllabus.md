Pre-requisite: Heat Transfer Operation, Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics.
Objectives
This course will help students to develop the understanding of different types
of utilities available in a chemical plant. It will develop the skills to design the
heating and cooling systems based on the requirement and impart the basic
knowledge of flaring, venting and vacuum system
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the application and importance of utilities in chemical plant.
2. Calculate the specification of utility system based on the heating-cooling
load and plant air requirement in the plant.
3. Analyse and design the process requirements for producing treated water
and flaring and venting system.
