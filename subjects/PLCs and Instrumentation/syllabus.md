Pre-requisite: Electronic systems, Introduction to Digital systems, Microprocessors and
Microcontroller
Course Objective
The course is designed to introduce the students in depth with various sensors and transducers
used in industrial situations. It also emphasizes the hardware and programming aspects of PLCs
for industrial applications.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Select and apply sensors and transducers for industrial applications
2. Analyze and compare the working principles, specifications, and performance of proximity
sensors
3. Describe the architecture, components, and addressing modes of PLCs
4. Create PLC ladder logic programs for sequential control mechanisms
5. Design and implement PLC-based automation solutions
