Course Objective
The main objective of this course is to equip the students with the ability to solve, assemble and
test simple AC and DC electrical circuits. Further, the course also enables the student to obtain
a basic understanding of the working principle and applications of electronics
devices.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the fundamental concepts of DC circuits, including Kirchhoff’s laws and network
theorems, (Understand- Level 2)
2. Interpret the principles of AC circuit analysis, including resonance and three-phase systems,
(Understand- Level 2)
3. Illustrate the working principles of transformers and different types of electrical machines,
(Understand- Level 2)
4. Explain the basic operation of analog electronic components, including rectifiers, transistors
and operational amplifiers. (Understand- Level 2)
5. Make use of logic gates and Boolean expressions for digital circuits.
