Course Objective
The main goal of the course is for the students to understand basic elements of mechatronic systems.
The students learn to analyze motion control and design aspects of mechatronic systems before
taking the next step to synthesize system functionality using analysis tools.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the design process and describe key elements of mechatronic systems
2. Develop and simulate block diagram models of electrical, mechanical, and fluid systems
3. Describe the architecture, functioning and working principles of smart sensors
4. Analyze and compare various types of actuators used in mechatronic systems
5. Design and tune controllers used for mechatronic applications
