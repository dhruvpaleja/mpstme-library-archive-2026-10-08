CO:01;   Explain the architecture and training process of a Variational Autoencoder (VAE) in detail   10
SO:02;   with neat diagram   M
BL:02
A VAE encodes input x to a probabilistic latent distribution q(z|x) (usually parameterized
by mean and variance) and decodes by sampling z ~ q(z|x) into p(x|z). The loss is ELBO:
reconstruction loss + KL divergence between q(z|x) and the prior p(z) (commonly N(0,I)).
Training uses reparameterization trick z = μ + σ * ε to allow backpropagation. VAEs learn
smooth latent spaces enabling sampling and interpolation.

respects privacy.
CO:01; b Compare discriminative AI and generative AI   5
SO:01;   M
BL:02   Discriminative models learn p(y|x) for prediction tasks (e.g., classifiers). Generative
models learn p(x) or p(x,y) to produce data. Discriminative is typically better for
classification performance; generative offers data synthesis and richer modeling.
