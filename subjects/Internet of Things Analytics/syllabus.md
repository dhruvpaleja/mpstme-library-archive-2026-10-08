Prerequisite: Programming for problem solving
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to educate students about the fundamentals of Internet of Things (IoT) and its
building blocks along with their characteristics. It will provide students with understanding of protocols
and standards designed for IoT and the current research on it. It will help students understand the recent
application domains of IoT in everyday life. It will provide information on associated technologies like
cloud computing in the domain of IoT
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamentals of IoT architectures, including M2M technologies, SMAC stack, and
communication protocols essential for IoT environments. L2 – Understand (Linked to Units 1 & 2)
2. Demonstrate the appropriate use of IoT reference models, networking architectures, and protocols
such as MQTT, CoAP, and 6LoWPAN in IoT systems. L3 – Apply (Linked to Units 2 & 3)
3. Connect IoT devices to cloud platforms using APIs, SDKs, and messaging protocols to enable
scalable and data-driven IoT applications. L3 – Apply (Linked to Unit 4)
4. Design and develop IoT-based applications using Raspberry Pi and GPIO programming for real-
time monitoring and control. L4 – Analyze (Linked to Unit 5)
5. Analyze real-world IoT use cases and propose innovative ideas through case studies, research
literature, and patent reviews for digital transformation. L4 – Analyze (Linked to Unit 6)
