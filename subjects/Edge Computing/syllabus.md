Pre-requisite: Basic understanding of cloud computing.
Course Objective
The aim of this course is to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of edge computing – its
purpose, benefits and real world applications. Students will learn to design Edge-AI systems by selecting
appropriate devices and tools as well as analyze the integration of Multi-Access Edge Computing (MEC)
within mobile network environments.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Recall the purpose and advantages of edge computing
2. Apply knowledge to design an EdgeAI system by identifying and selecting suitable devices
3. Analyze the role of Multi-Access Edge computing(MEC) within mobile network environments
4. Evaluate real-world use cases to gain insights into the practical applications of edge computing
