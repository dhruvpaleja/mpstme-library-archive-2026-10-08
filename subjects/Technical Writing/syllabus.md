Course Objective

This course aims to help the students develop skills that will enable them to produce clear and
effective scientific and technical documents. The focus is on basic principles of good writing, and on
types of documents common in scientific and technical fields and organizations

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate awareness of the principles of language and structured written communication required
to write clearly, correctly and effectively
2. Analyze the communication requirements and utilize appropriate technical formats available for
designing written document as a response
3. Evaluate the validity and authenticity of information and evidence available and utilize the same to
formulate and offer persuasive details and arguments while drafting technical documents

4. Create formal and evidence based technical documents after due analyses of the communicative

context
