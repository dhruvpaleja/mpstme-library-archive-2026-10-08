Pre-requisite: Intelligent Systems I, Probability and Random Variables
Course Objective
In this course, students will apply the concepts of uncertainty to represent the knowledge in uncertain
domains and draw inference. The students will also be introduced to concepts of fuzzy logic,
evolutionary algorithms, and reinforcement learning.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Quantify uncertainty using probability-based models
2. Analyze the applications of fuzzy logic to real world problems
3. Apply evolutionary intelligence algorithms to solve real world problems
4. Comprehend fundamentals of reinforcement learning approach
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Uncertain Knowledge and Reasoning
Quantifying Uncertainty: Quantifying uncertainty, Inference using full joint
distribution, Bayes rule, Naïve Bayes model
1.   07
Probabilistic Reasoning: Semantics of Bayesian networks, inference in
Bayesian networks, Graphical Models: Mixture of Models, Gaussian mixture
models (GMM), Expectation Maximization algorithm, Clustering using GMM.
Introduction to Fuzzy Inference System
Introduction to Fuzzy Logic, Fuzzy Rules, Fuzzy Properties - Operations,
Membership Functions, Fuzzification - Membership Value Assignments using
2.   Intuition Method, Defuzzification Methods - Mean of Maxima and Centroid   09
(Centre of Area) Methods, Fuzzy Inference System with reference to Mamdani
Model, Applications of Fuzzy Logic.
Introduction to Evolutionary Intelligence
Introduction, biological background, genetic algorithm(GA) and search space,
basic terminologies in GA- individuals, gene, fitness, population, simple and
3.   general GA, operators in GA-encoding, selection, crossover, mutation,   08
stopping condition for GA flow, constraints in GA, GA approach to solve
traveling salesman problem, ant colony optimization algorithm, swarm
optimization algorithm.
Introduction to Reinforcement Learning
Introduction, Markov processes, Markov Reward Process (MRP), Markov
4.   06
Decision Process (MDP), policies, value functions and Bellman equations,
applications of reinforcement learning.
