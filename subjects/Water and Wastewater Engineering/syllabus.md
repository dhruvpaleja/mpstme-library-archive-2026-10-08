Pre-requisite: -
Course Objective
This course focusses on the basics of Water supply, Conventional Water treatment, Water
Distribution patterns, Sewage generation, Sewage collection and conveyance and Conventional
Waste Water Treatment.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Have knowledge of characteristics of water, drinking water standards and necessity of
treatment
2. Calculate the capacity of water supply scheme
3. Demonstrate and Design Waste Water Management Schemes Units
4. Perform different tests to ascertain physical, chemical and biological characteristic of
given water sample
5. Understand the importance of levels of BOD & COD in a waste water treatment and
know various methods to determine the same
6. Demonstrate and Design Water Supply Schemes Units
