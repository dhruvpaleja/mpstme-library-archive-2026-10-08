Course Objective
To understand what blockchain is and why it is used, to be able to explain the different
components involved with blockchain.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1.   Explain the overview and characteristics of blockchain technology and requisite
crypto foundation
2.   Describe various blockchain data structures including consensus mechanisms
3.   Elaborate the Smart Contract and Ethereum
4.   Explore different applications of Blockchain

Unit   Description   Duration
1   Overview of Blockchain Technology   06
Defining Blockchain and Distributed Ledger, Blockchain Properties
Decentralized, Transparent, Immutable and secure. Blockchain
Applications. Types of Blockchain: Public, private, and consortium
based blockchain, When to use, and when not to use Blockchain,
History of Blockchain.
2   Introduction to computing models and P2P networking   06
Centralized, Decentralized and Distributed Systems, Decentralization
vs distributed, P2P systems, properties of P2P systems, P2P
communication architecture. P2P network applications: File sharing,
P2P network for blockchain.
3   Blockchain Data Structure   09
Cryptographic Hash Functions, Digital Signatures, Public Keys as
Identities, Hash Pointers and Hash chain and Merkel tree, Consensus
mechanisms: Proof of Work (PoW), Practical Byzantine Fault
Tolerance (PBFT), Proof of Stake (PoS).
