Course Objective
The course is aimed at developing a thorough understanding of physical and mathematical
principles used in mechanics. This course will help the students to get acquainted with the
various systems of forces, effects of friction on equilibrium, the importance of centre of gravity
and simple lifting machines.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Apply the concept of the resolution and composition of different force systems
2. Analyze the structure for system of forces in equilibrium and stability through centre of
gravity
3. Evaluate the performance of lifting machines
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   04
Introduction to Mechanics and Idealization of Bodies, Laws and
fundamental principles including Law of transmissibility of a force and
Newton’s laws of motion; Force system and its classification.
2.   Resolution and composition of Forces   08
Resolution of a force into components, Parallelogram Law, Polygon Law,
resultant of coplanar force system, resultant of a parallel forces acting in
same direction and in opposite direction, couple. Resolution of a force into a
force and a couple. Moment of a force and theorem of Varignon.
3.   Equilibrium   08
Equations of equilibrium, types of supports and support reactions, Free
body diagram with different types of loads, equilibrium of a body subjected
to two forces and three forces, application of Lami’s Theorem, numericals on
different types of beams.
4.   Friction   08
Introduction to friction; Dry friction - Laws of friction, angle of friction, cone
of friction, Equilibrium of bodies on rough horizontal and inclined plane;
simple problems on friction involving blocks.
5.   Centroid and moment of inertia   09
Concept of centroid, Centroid of two dimensional body (square, rectangle,
triangle, circle, semi-circle, quarter circle), Centroid of composite plane
figures (composed of not more than three geometrical figures), moment of
inertia of an area of plane figure, polar moment of inertia, and parallel axis
theorem.
6.   Simple Lifting Machines   08
Introduction, Simple machines and definitions, ideal machines and
Efficiency of a machine, Condition of irreversibility of a machine, Law of a
machine, Friction of a machine, Important Lifting Machines- Simple wheel
and axle, Differential wheel and axle, Worm and worm wheel.
Total   45

The course is aimed at developing thorough understanding of physical and mathematical
principles used in mechanics - statics. This course will help students get acquainted with the
various systems of forces, effects of friction on equilibrium, analysis of forces in trusses and the
importance of center of gravity. It is designed for the students to master the concept of static
equilibrium and its applications. The topics covered are tailored to suit the requirements of an
intermediate undergraduate level in engineering.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to-
1. Determine centroid of irregular shape areas,
2. Determine the resultant / equilibrant of various coplanar force systems
3. Analyse the system of forces in equilibrium with and without friction
4. Conduct experiments for better understanding of various principles
