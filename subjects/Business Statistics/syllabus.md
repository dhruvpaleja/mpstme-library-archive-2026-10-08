Objectives:
1. Understand fundamental statistical concepts, including descriptive and inferential

Outcomes:

1. Examine business data using descriptive statistics, measures of central tendency, and
variability.

business decision-making.
3. Apply statistical techniques to identify trends and relationships in business data.
4. Assess the accuracy and reliability of statistical findings and use them for strategic
decision-making.
Develop data-driven business strategies using statistical models and software tools.
