Course Objective
This course aims to emphasize the significance of various optimization techniques and apply those
methods to solve business as well as engineering problems.

After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Define suitable optimization technique for given problems
2. Demonstrate use of decision making and game theory
3. Solve assignment and transportation problems
4. Apply concepts of linear programming and integer linear programming to solve real life problems
