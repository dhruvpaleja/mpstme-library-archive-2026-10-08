Prerequisite: Student should have 10+2 level knowledge in chemistry, biology and physics.
Course Objective
The course introduces students to forensic investigation. It will enable students to understand and
appreciate various analytical techniques such as fingerprint analysis, digital forensic techniques,
chemical and instrumental methodologies. This will help students understand the procedures of
crime scene investigation. Overall, the course will allow students to appreciate above techniques
which are reliable and sensitive tools for detailed investigation. It will also help students in
developing strong analytical skill development, keen observation skills and patience.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to
1. Recognise the fundamentals of forensic science and its importance to human civilization
2. Develop technical skills that can be used for proper analysis of the evidence found in the
crime scene to give a lead to the investigators
3. Connect the importance of forensic science for perseverance of the society
