Pre-requisite: Knowledge of Communication Systems, Electromagnetic Field Theory
Objectives:
1. To introduce the students to the theory of smart antennas and different beam
forming techniques.
Outcomes:
After the successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
1. Understand various types of antenna systems with their usefulness to various
need.
2. Understanding the antenna systems and their issues to be rectified by smart
antennas.
3. Analyze various indoor and outdoor channel characteristics and their analysis is
done to enhance the smart antenna design
Detailed Syllabus:
Unit Description   Duration

Antenna Arrays, Antenna Classification: (Isotropic Radiators,   05

Introduction, Need for Smart Antennas, Overview: (Smart

Architecture of a Smart Antenna System: Receiver, Transmitter,
Benefits and Drawbacks, Basic Principles, Mutual Coupling Effects.

Array Response Vector, Received Signal Model, Conventional   10
DOA Estimation Method, Subspace Approach to DOA estimation,
