Program: B Tech (Civil Engineering)   Semester: V to VI
Course: Building Utilities and Services   Code: 702CI0E019
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End Examinations
(Hours   (Hours   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   (TEE)
per week) per week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Construction Technology, Building Design and Drawing
Course Objective
This course focuses on various services used in buildings like electrical systems, lifts and escalators,
HVAC systems, plumbing systems and fire-fighting systems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify different building services & utilities required for smooth functioning of the building
2. Illustrate various technical processes involved for installation of building services
3. Discuss the rules and regulations of building services
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Fire Safety installation
Causes of fire in buildings, safety regulations, NBC- planning considerations in
buildings like non-combustible materials, construction, staircases and lift lobbies,
1   10
fire escapes and A.C. systems. Heat and smoke detectors, Fire alarm system,
snorkel ladder, Fire-fighting pump and water storage, Dry and wet risers,
Automatic sprinklers, fire chutes
Electrical Systems in Buildings
Protective devices in electrical installations, earthing for safety, Types of earthing,
2   Types of wires, wiring systems and their choice, Planning electrical wiring for   05
building, Main and distribution boards, Transformers and switch gears, Layout of
substations
Plumbing Systems in Building
Plumbing services: Water distribution system, Storage tanks, types of pipes based
on materials and their suitability, testing of pipes – hydraulic, odour, smoke tests,
Service connection - size of service pipe, Water meter – size and installation, Valves
3   – types and suitability, miscellaneous plumbing fixtures, pressure valves and   12
pressure tanks in high rise buildings.
Drainage system: different types of pipes and traps with their suitability, System of
plumbing, Septic tank, Soak pit.
Rainwater harvesting system, STP operations and maintenance.
Lifts and Escalators
Lift Act, Construction and Essential features of lifts, passenger traffic requirements,
4   05
incoming, two way and outgoing traffic. Essential features and components of
escalators. Difference between lifts and escalators
