Course Objective
The aim of the course is to provide accessible coverage of the key areas of AI including game playing, expert
systems, natural language understanding, vison, robotics, agents and modelling cognition. It will provide
information on various AI based algorithms and methodologies used to solve problems with an AI based
approach.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the theory behind graph search algorithms, classification, optimization, reinforcement
learning, and other topics in artificial intelligence and machine learning
2. Solve the uncertainty & optimization problems using the AI methods
3. Design intelligent systems of their own

After completion of the course, students will be able to -

frameworks. (L4- Analyzing)
4. Analyze the performance and trade-offs of AI algorithms in solving real-world problems. (L2-
Understanding, L3-Applying)
5. Evaluate and compare different learning algorithms to select the appropriate method for a given
task. (K5- Evaluating)
6. Design intelligent systems of their own (K6 – Creating)
