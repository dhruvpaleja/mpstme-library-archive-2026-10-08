Pre-requisite: Information Security
Objectives:
The primary objective of this course to provide an overview of risk management process.
Students will understand various risk assessment techniques and risk management
frameworks.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Identify and evaluate various risk and suggest mitigation strategies appropriate to
them.
2. Develop risk management plan, business continuity plan and disaster recovery plan
3. Understand various risk management frameworks
Detailed Syllabus: (Per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1. Risk Management Fundamentals: Introduction, threats, vulnerabilities,   3
and impact, risk management lifecycle, Risk identification techniques,
Risk management techniques
2. Risk Assessment: Introduction, critical components of risk assessments,   4
types of risk assessments (qualitative and quantitative), identification and
evaluation of threats (including IPR infringement), vulnerabilities and
countermeasures
3. Risk Management Planning: Objectives and scope of risk management   6
plan, assigning responsibilities, reporting requirements, plan of action
and milestones, understanding and assessing the impact of legal and
compliance issues, prioritizing risk elements, performing cost benefit
analysis
4. Business impact analysis: Introduction, Objectives and scope of BIA,   4
steps of BIA process
5. Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster recovery plan (DRP):   4
Need for BCP and DRP, Elements of BCP, elements of DRP, Best practices
for DCP and DRP
6. Risk Management frameworks: NIST Risk Management Framework,   9
CERT OCTAVE, ISO 27005, FAIR approach
Total   30
