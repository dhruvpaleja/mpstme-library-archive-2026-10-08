## B TECH · Mechanical · AY 2025-26 · Sem 6

- Course as printed: Heat Transfer (code 702ME0C018)
- Source: `04_Syllabi_Archive/B TECH/3RD YEAR/MECHANICAL/SEM VI/B Tech, Mechanical, 3rd Year, Sem VI, Year 2025-2026.pdf`, page 1-3

````text
Program: B Tech /B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: VI / X
Course: Heat Transfer   Code: 702ME0C018
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   2   0   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Differential and Integral Calculus
Course Objective
This course is designed to provide knowledge of different modes of heat transfer with different laws
of conduction, convection and radiation. It also helps to provide understanding of different
applications of conduction, convection and radiation.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify and explain different modes of heat transfer
2. Applying heat transfer principles to calculate heat transfer rates in steady and transient state
conditions in different geometries and fins
3. Analysing forced and natural convection heat transfer, including the use of empirical
correlations to determine convection heat transfer
4. Apply radiation heat transfer principles to calculate heat exchange between surfaces with
different emissivity and geometries
5. Apply basic principles of heat transfer to design heat transfer devices
6. Analyze real-world engineering problems using heat transfer principles
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Basic Concepts   15
Modes of Heat Transfer, its mechanism and mathematical models.
Conduction Heat Transfer
Fourier’s general conduction equation in Cartesian, Cylindrical and Spherical co-
ordinates (for cylindrical and spherical walls derivation of Fourier’s three-
dimensional equation is not included), thermal resistance, solution of Fourier’s
equation for one dimensional steady state heat conduction through various
configurations such as plane, cylindrical, spherical wall, composite medium.
Critical thickness of insulation and its importance.
Heat Transfer from Extended Surfaces
Types of fins, Governing equation, fin effectiveness, efficiency of fins, concept of
corrected fin length, application of fins.
Unsteady State Heat Conduction
Lumped System Analysis
2.   Convection Heat Transfer   15
Forced and natural convection, hydrodynamic and thermal boundary layer,
Convection heat transfer coefficient, Momentum and energy equation for laminar
boundary layer, Laminar and Turbulent flow, principles of dimensional analysis,
physical significance of dimensionless numbers, Nusselt number, Prandtl
number, Reynolds number, Grashof number.
B Tech/Mechanical/Sem-VI/2025-26/Page 1
SVKM’s NMIMS Deemed-to-be University
Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering
Empirical relations and their use for forced internal and external convection for
circular and non-circular ducts.
Empirical relations and their use for free convection on vertical, horizontal flat
plate and cylinders.
Boiling and Condensation
Physical phenomenon and Pool boiling curve.
3.   Thermal Radiation   07
Introduction to physical mechanism, concept of black body, grey body and
radiation properties. Microwave Heating.
Basic laws of radiation; Planck’s law, Kirchoff’s law, Stefan-Boltzman law, Wein’s-
displacement law and Lambert’s Cosine law, Intensity of radiation, radiosity,
shape factor for simple geometries, heat exchange between non-black
bodies, Radiation Shield.
4.   Heat Exchangers   08
Classification, temperature distribution in parallel and counter flow arrangement,
overall heat transfer coefficient, fouling factor, log mean temperature difference
method, Effectiveness-NTU method.
Total   45
Text Books
1. Theodore Bergman & Adrienne Lavine, Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer, 8th Edition, Wiley
Publication 2017.
2. C.P. Kothandaraman & S. Subramanyan, Heat and Mass Transfer Data Book, 9th Edition, New Age
International 2018.
Reference Books

2. Frank P Incropera and D.P. Dewitt, Introduction to Heat Transfer, John Wiley & Sons, 2011.
````
