and working of transformer, measuring devices and sensors. Along with this, the course
is designed such that it will help students become familiar with methods of wiring and
safety systems such as earthing.

1. explain DC network theorems and apply them to solve DC circuits,
2. understand AC fundamentals and apply them to solve AC circuits,
3. understand the basic concepts of electrostatics, electromagnetics and transformer,
4. describe various types of measuring devices, sensors, wiring and electrical safety
systems.
