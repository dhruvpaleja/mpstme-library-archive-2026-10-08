Pre-requisite: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
Course Objective
The aim of this course is to foster a holistic understanding of the composition and working of the
Earth and be able to differentiate between minerals, rock types and landscape types with reference to
their genesis. Also, the course focuses on the application of plate tectonic theory to differentiate
structural features in rocks, decipher seismicity probabilities and predict geological hazards,
fundamentals of Geo-Economics and use geological principles in Civil Engineering.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Differentiate between Earth materials, Earth processes and landscapes
2. Explain structural geology and tectonics
3. Discuss natural disasters based on geological parameters
4. Test and interpret Earth Materials
5. Test and interpret Geological Maps
