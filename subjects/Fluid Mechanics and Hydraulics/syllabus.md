Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics
Course Objective
This course focusses on the properties of fluid, fluid statics and kinematics, buoyancy and its
applications in engineering problems. It also gives understanding of flow through pipes.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe fluid properties and basic fluid behavior
2. Apply fluid statics principles to compute pressure and forces
3. Analyze buoyancy and stability of floating bodies
4. Apply Bernoulli’s and Euler’s equations to solve flow problems
5. Evaluate flow using Venturimeters, Orifices, Notches, and Weirs
6. Analyze pipe flow for head losses and power transmission

This course focusses on the properties of fluid, fluid statics and kinematics, buoyancy and its
applications in engineering problems. It also gives understanding of flow through pipes.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Assess types of fluids based on their properties
2. Calculate fluid flow using various flow measuring devices
3. Determine losses in pipe flow
