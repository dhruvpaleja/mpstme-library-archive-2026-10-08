Course: Foundation Engineering   Code: 702CI0C041
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme

week)   per week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   1   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Soil Mechanics
Course Objective
This course focusses on the theories for lateral earth pressure, concepts of bearing capacity for
shallow and pile foundation. It also aims to provide an overview of ground improvement
techniques.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Classify active and passive conditions of lateral earth pressure
2. Apply knowledge of Rankine’s and Coulomb’s theories for cohesive and noncohesive soils
3. Analyse the effect of different shape of footing on Bearing capacity
4. Classify different ground improvement techniques for cohesive and non-cohesive soils
5. Design Shallow Foundations based on geotechnical parameters
6. Design a single pile under axial load
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Lateral Earth Pressure Theories
Introduction, applications of earth pressure theories, different types of earth
pressures - at rest, active and passive pressures, Rankine’s earth pressure theory,
1   active earth pressure and passive earth pressure for horizontal and inclined   10
backfills, earth pressure for cohesionless and cohesive soils, Coulomb’s wedge
theory, Coulomb’s active and passive earth pressures in cohesionless soils,
Introduction to shore piles.
Bearing Capacity
Definitions of ultimate bearing capacity, gross, net and safe pressures, allowable
bearing pressure, types of shallow foundations, modes of failures, bearing
2   capacity theories by Terzaghi and IS code, concept behind derivation of   10
Terzaghi’s general bearing capacity equation, bearing capacity equations for
rectangular, square and circular footings, factors influencing bearing capacity,
water table effect, performance of footings in different soils.
Axially Loaded Pile Foundations
Introduction to pile foundations, necessity of pile foundation, classification of
3   piles, installation techniques of concrete bored piles, driven cast in-situ piles, pile   07
capacity based on static analysis piles in sand, piles in clay, dynamic methods,
and their limitations.
