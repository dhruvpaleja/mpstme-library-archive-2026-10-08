Program: B Tech (Mechatronics Engineering)   Semester: VI
Course: Biomedical Instrumentation   Code: 702MH0E010
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   per week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Electronic systems, Signals and
Systems
Course Objective
This course helps the students to understand the basic principles and working of various
biomedical monitoring devices. It is aimed at developing the core competency and skill to design
and develop new health care systems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Summarize the working of various biomedical devices
2. Analyze the workings of life saving and life support systems used in the real world
3. Grasp the basic principles and working of the health monitoring equipment
4. Analyze the subsystem architectures and interfacing in medical devices
5. Synthesize conceptually modern devices for relevant medical applications
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Basic principle, working and technical specifications of
ECG, EMG and EEG machines, LEAD configurations, 10-20 electrode system
1.   measuring techniques for EOG, ERG and phonocardiography,   06
Patient Safety: Electric shock hazards, leakage currents, safety codes for
electromedical equipment.
Cardiac Pacemakers
Need for a pacemaker, modes of operation, classification codes for pacemaker,
2.   external and implantable pacemaker, programmable pacemaker, Power   06
sources for pacemakers, leads and electrodes, recent developments of
Implantable Pacemakers.
Patient Monitoring Systems and Telemetry
Measurement of heart rate, pulse rate, blood pressure, temperature and
respiration rate, apnea detector. Heart rate variability measurement, Basics of
3.   08
Telemetry, Multi-channel Telemetry
Point of care devices and their design considerations for homecare devices:
glucometer, pulse oximeter
Basic principle and working of Audiometers and hearing aid
4.   Basic audiometer, pure tone and speech audiometer, evoked response   05
audiometry, introduction to hearing aids and cochlear implants.
Basic principle and working of Fetal and Neonatal Monitoring System
5.   Cardiotocograph, methods of monitoring of fetal heart rate, monitoring of   05
labour activity, incubator and infant warmer
Total   30
