4.   Case Study:   6
An Indian sportswear brand, Puma India, had partnered with cricketer Virat Kohli as its brand
ambassador to strengthen its market position among young consumers. Kohli’s popularity and
strong personal brand image aligned with Puma’s positioning of energy, style, and
performance. His endorsement not only created trust but also gave the brand a competitive
edge in India’s growing sportswear market. In addition to celebrity endorsement, Puma India
actively uses social media platforms like Instagram, YouTube, and Twitter to connect with its
target audience. Campaigns such as #DoYou and #LetThereBeSport are shared widely online,
encouraging participation and influencer tie-ups. By doing this, Puma can directly interact with
customers and measure how well the campaign is performing. This combination of brand
ambassadors and digital engagement reflects how Indian brands today leverage both traditional
trust-building methods and modern, interactive media to reach wider audiences.

4.   Case Study:   6
A new health drink brand, entered the Indian market promising to energize busy young
professionals. Before launch, the team conducted research to understand why people usually
picked up energy drinks. They found that most buyers looked for quick refreshment and an
image of vitality. Based on this insight, the company developed a strong communication
message that connected with the lifestyle of working youth. The central theme revolved around
the idea of “staying ahead in the race.” The visuals showed a young professional confidently
balancing long hours at work and sports with friends after office. The brand’s message was
showcased across television, social media reels, and outdoor billboards. The storytelling relied
on an engaging mix of humour and aspiration, capturing attention while also making the brand
memorable. The campaign was launched simultaneously in metro cities with a consistent tone
and design across all media. Before rolling out, the creative agency prepared a structured note
aligning the product promise, target audience, and brand personality. The marketing team
evaluated every element carefully to ensure the communication stood apart from competitors.
