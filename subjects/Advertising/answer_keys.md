1. Answer: (Introduction to ASCI: 2 Marks:) The Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI),   8
established in 1985, is committed to the cause of self-regulation in advertising, ensuring the
protection of the interests of consumers. ASCI seeks to ensure that advertisements conform to
its Code for Self-Regulation, which requires advertisements to be legal, decent, honest and
truthful, and not hazardous or harmful, while observing fairness in competition
(Critical Analysis of ASCI Guidelines: Any relevant points: 6 Marks)
I. Truthful & Honest Representation
Advertisements must be truthful Where advertising claims are expressly stated to be based
on, or supported by independent research or assessment, its source and date should be
indicated in the advertisement.
Advertisements shall neither distort facts nor mislead the consumer by means of
implications or omissions
Advertisements shall not be so framed as to abuse the trust of consumers, or exploit their
lack of experience or knowledge.
II. Non-Offensive to public
To ensure that advertisements are not offensive to generally accepted standards of public
decency.
Advertisements should contain nothing indecent, vulgar, especially in the depiction of
women, or nothing repulsive which is likely, in the light of generally prevailing standards of
decency and propriety, to cause grave and widespread offence.
III. Against Harmful Product/Service/Situations:
Advertisements addressed to children shall not contain anything, whether in illustration or
otherwise, which might result in their physical, mental or moral harm, or which exploits
their vulnerability Advertisements shall not, without justifiable reason, show or refer to
dangerous practices, or manifest a disregard for safety, or encourage negligence.
Page 6 of 9
--- page 7 ---
Advertisements should contain nothing which is in breach of the law, nor omit anything
which the law requires.
IV. Fair in Competition
It is clear what aspects of the advertiser’s product are being compared with what aspects of
the competitor’s product.
The subject matter of comparison is not chosen in such a way as to confer an artificial
advantage upon the advertiser or so as to suggest that a better bargain is offered than is truly
the case.
The comparisons are factual, accurate and capable of substantiation.
OR
Introduction
ASCI is a self-regulatory voluntary organization of the advertising industry in India.
Established in 1985 to ensure advertising is truthful, decent, and not misleading.
Works on the principle of protecting consumer interests while promoting responsible
advertising.
Consumer Complaints Council (CCC)
Independent body under ASCI that reviews complaints.
Composed of representatives from civil society, medical experts, educationists, consumer
activists, and industry members.
Evaluates if an advertisement violates ASCI’s Code for Self-Regulation in Advertising.
Provides recommendations for corrective action, modification, or withdrawal of ads.
Filing Complaints
Any consumer or organization can file a complaint against an advertisement.
Complaints can be submitted through ASCI’s website, email, WhatsApp, or mobile app.
Complaints should include details of the ad, the medium (TV, print, digital, etc.), and the
reason it is objectionable.
ASCI acknowledges the complaint, reviews it via CCC, and informs the complainant about
the action taken.

ASCI Guidelines :
Truthful & Honest – Ads must not mislead consumers with false claims.
Decent & Non-Offensive – Should not contain obscene, vulgar, or derogatory content.
Safe & Not Harmful – Must not encourage unsafe practices or harm to
people/animals/environment.
Fair in Competition – Should not unfairly discredit or attack competitors’ products.
