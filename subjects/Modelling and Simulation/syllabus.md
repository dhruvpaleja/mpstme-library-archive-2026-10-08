Course Objective
1. Students will develop an understanding of applied mathematics as a thought-process and a
toolbox for the study and modelling of real-world phenomena.
2. Students will study how to compare the modelling results to observations and how models can be
improved.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to-
1. Understand the concept of mathematical Modelling.
2. Develop models using ODE & PDE.
3. Apply optimization techniques to the designed models.
