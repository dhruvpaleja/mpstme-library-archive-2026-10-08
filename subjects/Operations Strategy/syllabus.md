Pre-requisite: Operations Management
Course Objectives
 To understand the scope and importance of operations strategy and its relationships
with other strategies/functions in the organization.
 To analyse issues involved in preparing operations strategy keeping business
objectives, core strengths
 and market requirements of the organisation.
 To understand different decision areas and generic performance objectives, while
formulating operations strategy matrix.
 To understand implementation of operations strategy in the organization.
Course Outcome
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand operations strategy, its different perspectives, decision areas, formulation
of operations strategy matrix, five performance objectives. and relative importance of
each objective and their tradeoffs.
2. Understand capacity strategy, selection of location, influence of market requirements on
capacity strategy. the supply networking strategy, global sourcing & outsourcing options.
3. Apply scale/scalability, Automation/ Analytic content and coupling/connectivity to
process technology and its benefits.
4. Evaluation of process technology and its implications on product and service development
