- Course as printed: Composite Materials (code 702ME0E043)

````text
Program: B Tech (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: V
Course: Composite Materials   Code: 702ME0E043
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   per week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Materials Engineering
Course Objective
The main objective of this course is to provide basic understanding of composite materials used in
various engineering applications. The course also explains mechanical properties, micromechanics
and manufacturing methods of composite materials.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Relate processing, characterization and applications of various composite materials
2. Apply failure criteria and evaluate the results of composite materials
3. Evaluate the relative merits of using alternatives (corresponding to various simple and composite
materials) for important engineering and other applications
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   03
Definition, history, characteristics, concept of composite materials, comparison
with metals, composite materials in engineering, industrial scenario, advantages
and limitations of composites
2.   Types of Matrix Materials and Reinforcements   06
Functions of a matrix, desired properties of a matrix, polymer matrix
(thermosets and thermoplastics), metal matrix, ceramic matrix, carbon matrix,
glass matrix etc., role and selection of reinforcement materials, types of fibers -
glass fibers, boron fibers, carbon fibers, organic fibers - aramid fibers, ceramic
fibers - oxide fibers and nonoxide fibers, alumina fibers, silicon carbide fibers,
tungsten carbide fibers, multiphase fibers, whiskers, flakes etc.
3.   Types of Composites   14
Based on matrix materials, polymer matrix composites (PMC), metal matrix
composites (MMC), ceramic matrix composites (CMC), carbon fiber composites;
based on reinforcements, fiber reinforced composites, fiber reinforced polymer
(FRP) composites, laminated composites, particulate composites, polymer
matrix composites (PMC) – processing, structure and properties, applications,
metal matrix composites (MMC) – types, processing, properties, applications,
ceramic matrix composites (CMC) - processing, properties, applications, carbon
fiber composites - processing, properties, applications.
4.   Micromechanics and Macromechanics of Composites   06
Density, mechanical properties - prediction of elastic constants, elastic constants
of an isotropic material, elastic constants of a lamina.
5.   Monotonic Strength and Fracture   08
Tensile strength of unidirectional fiber composites, compressive strength of
unidirectional fiber composites, fracture modes in composites - single and
multiple fractures, deboning and delamination fracture.
B Tech/Mechanical/Sem-V/2026-27/Page 21
SVKM’s NMIMS Deemed-to-be University
Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering
6.   Advanced Composites   08
Nanocomposites, hybrid composites, self-healing composites, self-reinforced
composites, in-situ composites, smart composites, industrial applications of
composite materials - aerospace industries, automobile and other surface
transport industries, packaging industries, house hold and sports components
etc., recycling of composites
Total   45
Text Books
1. Krishan K. Chawla, Composite Materials: Science and Engineering, 4th edition, Springer, 2019.
2. M. Balasubramanian, Composite Materials and Processing, 1st edition (Reprint), CRC press, 2017.
Reference Books

CRC press, 2002.
````
