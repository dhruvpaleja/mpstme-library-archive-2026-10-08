Pre-requisite: Knowledge of Basic Electrical Engineering
Course Objective
This subject is designed to make students competent in AC and DC circuit analysis techniques and
several methods of simplifying networks. Steady state and transient analysis will make them
understand deeper aspects of circuit behaviour. They would also be acquainted with the
fundamental principles of circuit theory and network synthesis.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify the methods of mesh and node analysis for circuits with independent and
dependent AC and DC sources. (L1- Remembering)
2. Explain the use of network theorems, including Thevenin’s, Norton’s, and superposition
theorems, in analyzing electrical circuits. (L2- Understanding)
3. Apply graph theory principles, including trees, cut sets, and tie sets, to model and analyze
electrical networks. (L3- Applying)
4. Solve first- and second-order differential equations to evaluate transient and steady-state
responses in electrical circuits. (L3-Applying)
5. Determine the parameters and functions of two-port networks, including Z, Y, ABCD, and
hybrid parameters, for analyzing circuit behavior. (L5- Evaluating)
