Objectives:
 Formulate scientific questions and solve problems with the aid of abstraction and
modeling.
 Understand and implement the game playing strategies & develop basic AI
systems.
 Impart knowledge about industrial robots for their control and design.
Outcomes:
After completion of this course, students would be able to
1. Build intelligent agents for search and games
2. Solve AI problems through programming with Python
3. Perform kinematic and dynamic analyses with simulation.
4. Design control laws for a robot & Integrate mechanical and electrical hardware for
a real prototype of robotic device
