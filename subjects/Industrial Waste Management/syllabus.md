Pre-requisite: Environmental Engineering
Course Objective
This course covers identification, characterization, quantification, treatment and management of
wastewater, solid waste, emission and noise from the industry.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Discuss industrial waste treatment strategy based on waste type
2. Evaluate type and quantity of industrial waste
3. Design of various units of Effluent Treatment Plant
