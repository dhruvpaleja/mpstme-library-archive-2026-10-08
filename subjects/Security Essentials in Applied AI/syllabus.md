Pre-requisite: NA
Course Objective
This course introduces students to the principles of building secure AI applications, emphasizing
responsible AI practices, data privacy, and cybersecurity compliance. Students will explore techniques
to secure AI application deployment and usage, understand regulatory requirements like GDPR, and
apply privacy-preserving methods. By course end, they will be equipped to design, secure, and ethically
manage AI systems in diverse domains.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Apply Responsible AI principles to design and implement AI applications effectively.
2. Analyze and implement security measures to safeguard AI application deployments.
3. Evaluate and apply techniques for ensuring secure usage and operation of AI applications.
4. Design a compliance audit framework tailored for an AI system, ensuring it addresses key
regulatory requirements and mitigates identified risks.
