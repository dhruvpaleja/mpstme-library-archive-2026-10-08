Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to provides comprehensive instruction using spreadsheet software as a
productive decision making tool. Further, it will enable students to apply spreadsheet capability, analyze
various models influencing variables from period to period and evaluate data patterns to solve possible
outcomes of an uncertain event.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamental capabilities, notations, and functions of spreadsheets for creating data
models and solving mathematical expressions.
2. Apply probability distributions, decision trees, and regression techniques in spreadsheets to
handle uncertainty and randomness in business decision-making.
3. Develop spreadsheet-based decision models including cash-flow, what-if, and sensitivity
analyses for evaluating business scenarios and performance.
4. Solve optimization problems such as linear programming, transportation, assignment, and
multi-objective problems using Excel Solver and other spreadsheet tools.
5. Construct simulation and forecasting models using Monte Carlo simulation, inventory and
queuing simulations, and time series analysis (e.g., exponential smoothing, seasonality, trend).
