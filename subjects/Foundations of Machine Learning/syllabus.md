Pre-requisite: Statistical Structures in Data and Inference
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to focus on the basic principles of machine learning and use the concepts for
developing models. The course will emphasize on topics from matrix algebra, calculus, optimization, and
probability theory those are having strong linkage with machine learning
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Recall and define key concepts of machine learning, including types of learning, model evaluation
metrics, and essential terminology. (L1- Remembering)
2. Explain the fundamental differences between supervised vs. unsupervised learning, classification
vs. regression, and concepts like bias-variance. (L2-Understanding)
3. Apply foundational machine learning techniques to both supervised and unsupervised learning
problems, including regression, classification, and dimensionality reduction. (L3- Applying)
4. Assess and interpret model performance using key evaluation metrics, understand the bias-variance
trade-off, and distinguish quality of fit in various settings. (L5 – Evaluating)
5. Understand and apply fundamental concepts of optimization, including gradients, Jacobians,
Hessians, gradient descent, and constrained optimization, with applications to training neural
networks and building feedforward architectures. (L2-Understanding and L3- Applying)
6. Perform and interpret dimensionality reduction techniques, such as Principal Component Analysis
(PCA) and Fisher Discriminant Analysis, and apply support vector machines for classification with
kernel methods and geometric interpretations. ( L4-Analyzing)
