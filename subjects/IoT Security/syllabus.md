Course Objective
This course introduces the concepts of security and privacy used in IoT. It also helps to analyse the
security used to protect cloud-based infrastructure, services, applications and data.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the security and ethical issues of the Internet of
Things
2. Interpret the cryptographic primitives and their role in IoT
3. Identify vulnerabilities, including recent attacks, involving the Internet of Things
4. Summarize the use of various clouds and their security
