Course Objective
The aim of this course is to make students acquainted with fundamentals of wireless
communication systems, and understand the diversity & spatial multiplexing phenomenon in
MIMO system. This course also helps to comprehend the application of multiuser access in a
cellular communication scenario and the propagation mechanisms in an urban mobile
communication environment for different networks.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand fundamental wireless network concepts, ITU-T & TRAI regulations,
cellular system principles, and multiple access technologies. (L2 - Understanding)
2. Analyze large-scale and small-scale radio propagation effects, including path loss
models and multipath fading. (L4 - Analyzing)
3. Evaluate different diversity techniques and MIMO configurations for wireless
communication performance enhancement. (L5 - Evaluating)
4. Compare LTE advancements such as Carrier Aggregation, enhanced MIMO, CoMP,
and heterogeneous networks. (L4 - Analyzing)
5. Assess 5G and 6G network architectures, radio access technologies, and propagation
models for next-generation communication. (L5 - Evaluating)
