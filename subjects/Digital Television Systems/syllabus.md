1. To provide knowledge and principle of Colour TV and Advanced TV

4. To give an insight of the concepts of digital signal transmission and principle
of Digital TV, HDTV, IPTV and 3D TV.

Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
1. Analyse and comprehend the working of various colour TV systems.
2. Recognise the principle of various advanced TV technologies.
3. Compare various display technologies.
4, Analyse the fundamentals of digital signal transmission.
