Types of DSS

1. Data-Driven DSS: Focuses on accessing and manipulating large datasets.
Example: Business Intelligence tools like Tableau or Power BI.

2. Model-Driven DSS: Emphasizes analytical models to simulate scenarios and
optimize decisions. Example: Supply chain optimization using linear
programming.

3. Knowledge-Driven DSS: Provides recommendations based on expert
systems or knowledge bases. Example: Diagnostic systems in healthcare.

4, Communication-Driven DSS: Facilitates collaboration and group
decision-making. Example: Group decision systems like Microsoft Teams for
brainstorming sessions.

5. Document-Driven DSS: Manages and retrieves documents relevant to
decision-making. Example: Legal case analysis systems.

Types of E-Commerce

1. B2C (Business-to-Consumer): Businesses sell directly to consumers (e.g.,
Amazon, Flipkart).

eBay).

businesses (e.g., freelancers on platforms like Upwork).

5. B2G (Business-to-Government): Businesses provide goods and services to
government entities.

Antimalware Software:

Antimalware software (often referred to as antivirus software) is designed to
detect, prevent, and remove malicious software (malware) from computers
and networks. Malware can include viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware,
spyware, and more. Here are the key aspects of antimalware software:
Key Functions

1. Detection:

© Signature-Based Detection: Scans files and programs for known
malware signatures. This is effective against known threats but

may not catch new or modified malware.

© Heuristic Analysis: Analyzes the behavior of programs to identify
suspicious activities. This method can detect previously

unknown malware based on its actions.

2. Prevention:

© Real-Time Protection: Monitors files and applications in real

time, blocking malware before it can execute.

2 Scheduled Scans: Regularly scans the system for malware,

allowing for early detection of threats.

3. Removal:

© Once malware is detected, antimalware software can quarantine

or remove the infected files and restore the system to a clean

state.

A. Updates:

© Regular updates are crucial to ensure the software can detect the

latest threats. This includes downloading new virus definitions

and program updates.

5. System Optimization:

© Some antimalware solutions include tools for optimizing system
performance by removing unnecessary files or managing startup
programs.
