Program: B. Tech CSE (Cybersecurity)   Semester : VII
Course : Human and Legal Aspects of Cybersecurity   Code: 702IT0C027
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   per week)   week)   (Marks- 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Fundamental Knowledge of Cybersecurity
Course Objective
Security posture of an organization is affected by end user’s action. Human users are considered
as the weakest link in the cybersecurity. Students should also understand legal aspects of
cybersecurity as it guides the developments of security programs and solutions. This course
provides an understanding of the human and legal aspect of cybersecurity. Students will be
exposed to various national and international laws relevant to cybersecurity from a layman’s
perspective.
Course Outcomes
After successful completion of the course, student would be able to -
1. Design a SETA programs for improving behaviour of end users
2. Explain ITA 2000 and ITAA 2008
3. Explain various international laws related to cybersecurity and privacy
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   6
Role of human in securing organizational assets, security and usability
triangle, types of cybersecurity behaviours, Role of Security Education,
Training, and Awareness (SETA) program, decision making in cybersecurity
(System 1 and 2 thinking), security fatigue, heuristics and biases,
cybersecurity program design using ADDIE model.
2.   Cybersecurity Culture (CSC)   8
Need for cybersecurity culture, Developing and implementing a successful
CSC program, Organisational requirements for a successful CSC, Elements
and resources for successful CSC programs, measuring CSC program.
3.   Cyber Laws   4
Introduction to Indian judicial system, types of laws, need for cyber laws,
cybercrimes and cyber criminals, classification of cybercrimes, reporting
cyber incidents.
4.   Indian IT Act 2000 and ITAA 2008   4
ITA 2000 introduction, features and scope of ITA 2000, important sections of
ITA 2000, offenses, penalties and appeal under ITA 2000, need for ITAA 2008,
amendments, cyber appellate tribunal, relevant cases.
5.   Global Cyber and Privacy Regulations   8
Introduction to SOX, GLBA, HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR and Indian Privacy bill.
Total   30
