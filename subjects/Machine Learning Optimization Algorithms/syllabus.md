Pre-requisite: Linear Algebra, Calculus.
Course Objective
This course makes students familiar with different optimization algorithms. It enables students
to apply optimization approaches in machine learning to extract essential knowledge from
huge volume of data.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, student will be able to:
1. Explain different optimization algorithms and their role in machine learning.
2. Apply optimization algorithms for feature extraction and model building.
3. Analyze and compare optimization algorithms.
