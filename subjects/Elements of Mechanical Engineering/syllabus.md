Course Objective
This course aims to introduce students to the fundamental principles of mechanical engineering,
focusing on core concepts such as mechanics, materials, energy systems and thermal dynamics.
It seeks to develop problem-solving skills, enabling students to analyze and address basic
engineering challenges systematically. Additionally, it promotes interdisciplinary awareness by
emphasizing the role of mechanical engineering in multidisciplinary projects and its integration
with other fields.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to -
1. Understand fundamental mechanical engineering concepts, including force, work, energy,
and material properties.
2.   Illustrate the principles of mechanics, thermodynamics, and fluid mechanics to analyze
material behavior, energy systems, and fluid flow.
3. Identify the real-world mechanical engineering applications by integrating knowledge of
materials, thermodynamics, and fluid mechanics.
4. Identify the applications of advanced manufacturing processes used in mechanical
engineering.
5. Understand the role of emerging technologies like IoT, AI, and Robotics in the context of
Industry 4.0.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1.   Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering   6
Introduction to Mechanical Engineering: Scope and importance; Basic
mechanical concepts: Force, Work, Energy, and Power; Properties and
applications of engineering materials; Measurement systems, SI units, and
dimensional analysis.
2.   Mechanics of Materials   8
Stress and Strain: Definitions and types; Hooke’s Law and modulus of
elasticity with their applications; Mechanical properties: Tensile strength,
ductility, and hardness; Factor of Safety and its applications; Thermal stresses
and their impact on materials and its applications.

3.   Thermodynamics and Energy Systems   9
Laws of Thermodynamics: Zeroth, First, and Second Laws with practical
applications; Heat and work interactions in thermodynamic processes; Heat
engines: Principles, efficiency, and components; Internal Combustion
Engines: SI and CI engines; Basics of renewable energy systems with its
applications: Solar, wind, and geothermal energy
4.   Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulic Machines   7
Properties of fluids: Density, viscosity, and surface tension; Continuity
equation and Bernoulli’s theorem; Types of fluid flow: Laminar and turbulent
flows; Applications in pumps, turbines, and hydraulic systems; Basics of lift
and drag in fluid mechanics and its applications.
5.   Manufacturing Processes   9
Overview of manufacturing processes: Casting, forming, and machining;
Additive manufacturing: Introduction to 3D printing technologies;
Machining processes: Turning, drilling, and milling; Welding processes: Arc
welding, TIG, and MIG; Introduction to CNC machining and automation,
Introduction to assembly line production system.
6.   Advanced Topics and Industry Trends   6
Emerging technologies: IoT, AI, and robotics in mechanical systems; Smart
manufacturing and Industry 4.0; Case studies in renewable energy systems
and electric vehicles; Sustainability in mechanical engineering: Lifecycle
analysis and green manufacturing.
