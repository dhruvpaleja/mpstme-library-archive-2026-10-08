Program: B. Tech CSE (Cybersecurity)   Semester : VII
Course : Human Centered Cybersecurity   Code: 702IT0E034
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per (Hours per (Hours per Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks- 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Fundamental Knowledge of Cybersecurity
Course Objective
Technical challenges of the cybersecurity are better understood as compared to human challenges.
Cybersecurity is fundamentally a problem of human interaction with technology. This course
provides an overview of human interactions with cybersecurity technology, from users to system
designers. This course looks at application of theories from other domains like economics and
behavioural science to the cybersecurity context.
Course Outcomes
After successful completion of the course, student would be able to -
1. Apply various theories from other domains to cybersecurity decision making
2. Apply game theory to cybersecurity context
3. Design cybersecurity awareness campaign using marketing and behavioural theories
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Economics   6
Classical and Behavioural economics, asymmetric information, auction theory,
the economics of security and dependability, application of Kahneman-Tversky
approach to cybersecurity, Heuristics and biases in cybersecurity decision
making.
2. Game Theory and Cybersecurity   8
Introduction, payoff, matrix games, mixed strategy, Saddle point, dominated
strategies, applications of game theory to cybersecurity.
3.   Personality Theory and Cybersecurity   5
Personality traits and cybersecurity, Profiling in the cybersecurity context,
profiling matrices, ABCD Analysis
4.   Marketing and Communications for Cybersecurity Awareness Campaign   6
The communication conundrum,   similarity between marketing and
cybersecurity awareness campaigns, tracking   results and measuring
effectiveness.
5.   Behaviour Management for Security Awareness Leaders   5
System 1 and System 2 thinking, working with human nature, Fogg Behaviour
Model, Nudge and frames, designing and debugging behaviour, tracking
results and measuring effectiveness.
Total   30
Text Books

engineering methods.
2. Examine the emerging technologies impact on usability of devices and its applications.
3. Evaluate the consequences of cybersecurity tools and policies based on predictions
about human behaviour
