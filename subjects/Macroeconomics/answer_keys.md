Q2. CO2 SO2   Elaborate subject matters of Macroeconomics,   10
BL-Applying   also explain its main features and why it is
important for understanding how a country’s
economy works?
Ans: Macroeconomics studies the behavior of
the economy as a whole rather than individual
markets. It examines broad indicators such as
national income, employment, investment,
inflation, and economic growth.
Main Features:
Aggregate Study: Focuses on total output,
total demand, and total supply.
Interdependence: Analyses how different
sectors—household, business, government, and
foreign trade—interact.
Policy Orientation: Helps frame fiscal and
monetary policies to maintain stability.
Dynamic Nature: Studies short-run
fluctuations (business cycles) and long-run
growth.
Importance:
Macroeconomics helps understand overall
economic performance, identify causes of
inflation or unemployment, and guide policy
decisions. It is essential for economic planning,
improving living standards, and ensuring
sustainable growth.

BL-   policy and its impact on economy with suitable
Evaluating   example.
Ans: A liquidity trap occurs when nominal
interest rates are very low and savings rates
remain high, making monetary policy ineffective
in boosting economic activity. In this situation,
households and businesses prefer holding cash
instead of investing, regardless of increases in
the money supply. The classic example is Japan
in the 1990s, where persistent deflation and zero
interest rates failed to revive consumption and
investment. As a result, the economy faces
stagnation, high unemployment, and subdued
growth. The government may need to rely on
expansionary fiscal policy, like increased public
spending or tax cuts, to stimulate demand when
monetary interventions are not sufficient to
break the trap.
