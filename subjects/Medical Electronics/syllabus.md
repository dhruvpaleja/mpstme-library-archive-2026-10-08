Pre-requisite: Electronic Instrumentation

Objectives:

1. Studying the subject, students should be in position to get acquented with the
applications of electronics, various medical instruments and their use.

2, They can choose project work in biomedical areas such as B.P. Measurement, nervous
system measurement, Cardiovascular system measurement etc.

3. Medical science uses various imaging techniques to get additional information about
internal organs which is helpful in diagnosis.

4. Students get acquented with equipments such as B.P. Measurement, pulse rate
measurement, ECG measurement, EEG measurement etc.

Outcomes:
After successfully completion of this course, students should be able to
1. Electronics students, get aware of the applications of various electro-medical gadgets
use in medical diagnosis.
2. Students would have advantage of selecting Projects in medical application areas.
3. Students would be aware of medical science in somewhat more details to take care of

themselves
