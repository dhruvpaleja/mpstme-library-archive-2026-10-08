Program: B Tech /B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: VII/XI
Course: Computational Fluid Dynamics   Code: 702ME0E025
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   per week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Differential and Integral Calculus, Fluid Mechanics, Heat Transfer
Course Objective
The principal objective of this course is to provide brief introduction of CFD along with analysis of
heat transfer and fluid flow problems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the need for modelling and simulation as well as the working of CFD software
2. Apply standard direct and iterative techniques to solve a system of linear algebraic equation
3. Analyze the heat transfer and fluid flow problems using techniques of CFD
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Modeling and Simulation   06
Experimental and analytical approach, Modeling in engineering, scope and
application of CFD, solution methodology of CFD software - preprocessing,
solver, post processing.
Mathematical Modeling
Concept of mathematical modeling, basic conservation equation (continuity
equation, momentum equation, energy Equation). Initial and boundary
conditions
2.   Discretization Methods
Introduction to Finite Difference method, Finite Element method and Finite
Volume method. Central, Forward and Backward difference. Von Neumann   06
stability analysis, grid generation, uniform and non-uniform grids, structured
and unstructured grids, Explicit and Implicit schemes of Finite Difference
Method.
3.   Solution Methods of Linear Algebraic Equations   03
Direct methods: Gauss elimination, Tri-diagonal Matrix Algorithm (TDMA)
etc. Iterative methods: Gauss Seidel method
4.   Application of CFD to Conductive and Convective Heat Flow
Steady one- and two-dimensional conduction, unsteady one and two
dimensional conduction. Steady one-dimensional and two-dimensional   10
convection-diffusion, unsteady one and two-dimensional convection-
diffusion.
5.   Application of CFD to Incompressible Fluid Flow   05
Stream function-vorticity method, determination of pressure for viscous flow.
The SIMPLE, SIMPLER Algorithm, basic theories of turbulence, Introduction
to turbulence models.
