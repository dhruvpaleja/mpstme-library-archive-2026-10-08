Pre-requisite: Physics, Calculus
Course Objective
This course describes the fundamentals of Thermodynamics including thermodynamic systems and
properties, work and heat interactions, relationships among the thermo-physical properties, the laws of
thermodynamics and applications of these basic laws in thermodynamic systems such as various
thermodynamic cycles. This course will provide the essential tools required to study thermodynamic
systems in Applied Thermodynamics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Explain fundamental thermodynamic concepts and principles, including energy, equilibrium,
and laws of thermodynamics
2. Analyse the properties of pure substances using phase diagrams, Mollier charts, and steam
tables
3. Evaluate the first and second laws of thermodynamics for steady and unsteady flow processes
4. Compare the performance and efficiency of vapor and gas power cycles, including Rankine,
Otto, Diesel, and Brayton cycles
5. Apply thermodynamic principles to real-world applications, including refrigeration, heat
pumps, and energy systems
