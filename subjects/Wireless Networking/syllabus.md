Pre-requisite: Principles of Communication Engineering (BTIT04007), Computer

Objectives: This is an introductory course in wireless networking providing a
comprehensive treatment of wireless data and telecommunication networks. This
course addresses the fundamentals of wireless networking, including architectures,
protocols, and standards. It describes concepts, technology and applications of
wireless networking as used in current and next-generation wireless networks. It
explains the engineering aspects of network functions and designs.

Outcomes:

After successfully completion of this course, students should be able to

e Explain the architectures and elements of a wireless network,

¢ Classify and compare the signaling schemes and MAC layer protocols used in
wireless networks,

e Evaluate the operation and performance of wireless protocols in network and
transport layers.

e Compare the performance of ad hoc network protocols,

¢ Identify the various security issues related to wireless networks.
