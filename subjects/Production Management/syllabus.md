## B TECH · Mechanical · AY 2026-27 · Sem 5

- Course as printed: Production Management (code 702ME0I011)
- Source: `04_Syllabi_Archive/B TECH/3RD YEAR/MECHANICAL/SEM V/B Tech, Mechanical, 3rd Year, Sem-V, Year, 2026-27.pdf`, page 43-45

````text
Program: B Tech programs except B Tech CSBS and B Tech CSE (DS) Semester: V/VI
Course: Production Management   Code: 702ME0I011
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours   (Hours   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
per week) per week)   per week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: - NA
Objective
The objective of this course is to help the students understand the principles and applications
relevant to the planning, design, and operations of manufacturing or service firms. This course
also develops an understanding about quality management practices in organizations that facilitate
organizational effectiveness.
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Develop an understanding of manufacturing and service operations management as a
subject and outline the strategic concerns involved in operations decisions
2. Apply the principles, philosophy underpinning inventory management techniques, just-
in-time and MRP systems along with their limitations
3. Analyze the economic aspects related to production management
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Concept of Production Management   07
Meaning of production, production process system, scope of production
management, objectives of production management, functions and scopes
of production management, scope of production management, brief history
of production management, responsibilities of production manager.
2.   Productivity   04
Introduction, meaning of productivity, factors affecting on productivity,
productivity improvement, measurement of productivity.
3.   Economic Analysis   06
Factors to be considered in product design. production cost concepts;
fixed cost, variable cost, break even analysis,
P-V chart, and make-buy decision.
4.   Plant Location and Plant Layout   06
Meaning of plant layout, objective of a good plant layout, factors to be
considered in plant location, types of plant layout, process layout.
techniques of plant layout, concept of group technology, group layout.
5.   Production Planning and Control (PPC)   08
Objective of PPC, functions of PPC, capacity planning, manufacturing
resource planning and scheduling, JIT system, Kanban pull system of
scheduling, line balancing.
B Tech/Mechanical/Sem-V/2026-27/Page 43
SVKM’s NMIMS Deemed-to-be University
Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering
6.   Materials Management   07
Bill of materials, quantity planning, inventory control, economic order
quantity (EOQ), lead time, consumption rate, different ordering systems, Q
system, P system, the store’s function and ABC analysis.
7.   Quality   07
Introduction to quality control, quality management, function of quality,
quality control stages, function of inspection, statistical quality control,
statistical quality control, control charts, acceptance sampling.
Total   45
````

````text
Objective

The objective of this course is to help the students understand the principles and applications
relevant to the planning, design, and operations of manufacturing or service firms. This course
also develops an understanding about quality management practices in organizations that facilitate
organizational effectiveness.

Outcomes
````
````text
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Develop an understanding of manufacturing and service operations management as a
subject and outline the strategic concerns involved in operations decisions
2. Apply the principles, philosophy underpinning inventory management techniques, just-
in-time and MRP systems along with their limitations
````
````text
3. __ Analyze the economic aspects related to production management

Detailed Syllabus

Unit | Description Duration

1. Concept of Production Management 07
Meaning of production, production process system, scope of production
management, objectives of production management, functions and scopes
of production management, scope of production management, brief history
of production management, responsibilities of production manager.

2. Productivity 04
Introduction, meaning of productivity, factors affecting on productivity,
productivity improvement, measurement of productivity.

3. Economic Analysis 06
Factors to be considered in product design. production cost concepts;
fixed cost, variable cost, break even analysis,
P-V chart, and make-buy decision.

4. Plant Location and Plant Layout 06
Meaning of plant layout, objective of a good plant layout, factors to be
considered in plant location, types of plant layout, process layout.
techniques of plant layout, concept of group technology, group layout.

5. Production Planning and Control (PPC) 08
Objective of PPC, functions of PPC, capacity planning, manufacturing
resource planning and scheduling, JIT system, Kanban pull system of
scheduling, line balancing.
````
