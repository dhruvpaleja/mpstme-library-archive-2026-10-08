Course Objective
The course focuses on implementation of the control and automation strategy in chemical engineering plant
and focuses on the response of different unit operation subjected to various disturbances. It will also
introduce the latest control schemes practiced in the chemical Industry.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Understand the different aspect of control strategies in the chemical plant.
2. Evaluate the response of system in open and closed loop system.
3. Design the process & instrumentation diagram based on controllers and instruments.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   03
Need for control and automation, control logic, control structures (feedback vs. feed-
forward), servo and regulatory control, block diagram, process and instrumentation
diagrams.
2.   Application of Laplace transform   04
Process dynamics in time and Laplace domain, solution of ODEs using Laplace
transform.
3.   Response of First and Second Order system   09
Models of First system, response of first order systems for: step, impulse and sinusoidal
forcing function. Problems based on response of first order system, First order system in
series. Second order systems. Higher order systems, transportation lag and dead time.
4.   Closed loop and Open loop system   09
Linear closed loop and open loop systems, development of block diagrams, classical
feedback controllers. Problems based on controllers. Final Control element (control
valve) process and instrumentation diagrams. Block diagram reduction techniques.
Transfer function of servo and regulatory problems. Transient behaviour of closed-loop
system.
5.   Basic and Advanced Instruments   12
sensors, transmitters, transducers, control valves, and converters. Measurement devices
for process variables: temperature, pressure, level, flow, pH, humidity, density, and
viscosity. Control valves, actuators, positioners
6.   Advance Control Strategies   08
Introduction to advance control strategies, Cascade control, Feed-forward and ratio
control. Smith predictor, computer-based control systems: PLC, DCS, SCADA.
