2   0   1   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Thermodynamics, Applied Thermodynamics
Course Objective
This course covers alternative fuels and the analysis of their properties. The course also provides a
comprehensive overview of the recent advances in the production and utilization aspects of different
types of liquid and gaseous alternative fuels.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the need for alternative fuels, their availability, and standard properties
2. Compare and analyze the properties, combustion characteristics, and emissions of liquid and
gaseous alternative fuels
3. Evaluate the performance and emission characteristics of alternative fuels
4. Conduct an experimental analysis of alternative fuels
5. Assess fuel quality and properties
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   04
Estimation of petroleum reserve, need for alternate fuels, availability and
properties of alternate fuels, American Society for Testing of Materials (ASTM)
standards.
2.   Liquid Fuels   08
Petroleum based liquid fuels and refining.
Liquid Alternative Fuels
Alcohols, general use of alcohols, properties as engine fuel, gasoline and alcohol
blends, performance in spark ignition engine, methanol and gasoline blend,
combustion characteristics in engine, emission characteristics. algae as a source
for fuels.
Vegetable Oils
Soybean oil, jatropha, pongamia, rice bran, mahua etc. as alternate fuel and their
properties, esterification of oils, biodiesel and emulsified fuels, lubricating oils
composition and effect of alternate fuels.
3.   Gaseous Alternative Fuels   07
Hydrogen production, hydrogen as an alternative fuel, fuel cell, availability of
compressed natural gas, properties and modification required to use in engines,
performance and emission characteristics of compressed natural and liquefied
natural gas in spark ignition and compression ignition engines, dimethyl ether,
and hythane as an alternative fuel.
B Tech/Mechanical/Sem-V/2026-27/Page 11
SVKM’s NMIMS Deemed-to-be University
Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering
4.   Analysis of Gaseous Bio-fuel   06
Determination of methane, carbon dioxide, carbon monoxide, hydrogen sulphide,
hydrogen in biogas, volatile fatty acid (VFA) and its constituents, alkalinity (total
and partial), biological oxygen demand (BOD), Chemical oxygen demand (COD)
and conversion efficiency.
5.   Analysis of Fuel Qualities/Properties   05
Bomb calorimeter, Junker’s calorimeter, flash point, pour point, viscosity,
kinematic viscosity, acid value, saponification, fatty acid composition, iodine
value, cetane index, octane value, oxidative stability and shelf life determination.
Total   30
Text Books
1.   Sergio Capareda, Introduction to Biomass Energy Conversions, CRC Press, 2013.
2.   Colin R. Ferguson, Allan T. Kirkpatrick, Internal Combustion Engines: Applied Thermosciences,
Wiley, 2015.)
Reference Books
1. Sunggyu Lee, James G. Speight, Sudarshan K. Loyalka, Handbook of Alternative Fuel
Technologies, CRC Press, 2018.
2. Akhilendra Pratap Singh, Avinash Kumar Agarwal, Nirendra N. Mustafi, Yogesh C. Sharma,
Alternative Fuels and Their Utilization Strategies in Internal Combustion Engines, Springer
Singapore, 2019.
