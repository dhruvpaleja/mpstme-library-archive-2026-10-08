against VFA buildup.
CO5 BL1   e   (2 Marks) Acid Value: Measures the amount of free fatty acids (FFA); high
AV indicates poor quality/degradation and leads to soap formation/corrosion
in the engine (limit for biodiesel).

(2 Marks) Saponification Number: Measures the average molecular

Q2 CO2 BL3   a   (4 Marks) Properties: Contrast Octane (Alcohol high) vs. Cetane (Oil high), Latent 10
Heat (Alcohol high), Viscosity (Oil high), and Oxygen Content (Both high).
(3 Marks) Performance: Discuss CR potential (Alcohol higher), starting issues
(Oil needs pre-heating), and power output.
(3 Marks) Emissions: Compare CO/HC (Alcohol generally lower but higher
aldehyde), NOx (Alcohol lower peak T), and PM (Oil/Biodiesel low PM).
CO3 BL2   b   (3 Marks) Production: Explain Steam Methane Reforming (SMR) and 10
Electrolysis, noting energy requirements.
(3 Marks) Storage: Discuss challenges and methods (compressed gas at high
pressure, cryogenic liquid, metal hydrides).
(4 Marks) Engine Modifications: Address backfire prevention (port vs. direct
injection), need for lower compression ratio or NOx reduction, and special
materials for H2 compatibility.
Q3 CO3 BL3   a   (5 Marks) Dimethyl Ether (DME): Properties (high Cetane ≈55, high O2), Use 10
(excellent for CI engines, mono-fuel possible), Performance/Emissions (ultra-low
PM, requires fuel system pressurization).
(5 Marks) Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG): Properties (Methane ≈90%, cryogenic
T), Use (SI engines, dual-fuel CI engines), Performance/Emissions (CO2 lower,
high NOx risk, Methane Slip challenge, good knocking resistance).
CO4 BL4   b   (5 Marks) Composition Analysis: CH4 (50-75%) is the fuel; CO2 (25-45%) is the 10
diluent. Methods of quantification (Gas Chromatography, NDIR sensors).
(5 Marks) Effect on Efficiency: CH4 content determines heating value/power
output (higher CH4 is better). CO2 is inert, reducing flame temperature and energy
density, which lowers combustion efficiency and engine power (requires
enrichment/upgrading).
Q4 CO5 BL4   a   (3 Marks) Flash Point: Must be high enough for safe handling/storage 10
(especially for diesel substitutes); low FP increases fire risk.
(4 Marks) Kinematic Viscosity: Must be within limits for proper fuel
atomization, spray penetration, and pump/injector lubrication; too high/low
leads to poor combustion/wear.
(3 Marks) Oxidative Stability: Fuel's resistance to reacting with O2 (formation
of gums/sediments over time); critical for long-term storage and preventing
filter/injector clogging.
CO5 BL3   b   (5 Marks) Procedure: Detailed description of the apparatus (sealed O2 chamber, 10
inner bucket with water, outer jacket, fuse wire, thermometer/sensor) and steps
(weighing sample, ignition, measuring ΔT of water, applying corrections).

(5 Marks) Interpretation: Calculation of Gross Calorific Value (GCV) by equating
heat released to heat absorbed by water and calorimeter parts. Mentioning need
for correcting heat of fusion for H2O to get NCV.
Q5 CO2 BL3   a   (5 Marks) Biodiesel: Mechanism (contains oxygen, zero aromatic/sulfur content). 10
Emissions: Significant reduction in PM and HC, but NOx may slightly increase
(due to higher bulk modulus and shorter ignition delay).
(5 Marks) Emulsified Fuels (Water-in-Fuel): Mechanism (water micro-explosion
leading to secondary atomization). Emissions: Significant reduction in NOx
(water evaporates, lowering peak combustion temperature) and PM (improved
mixing/combustion). Discuss stability challenges.
CO3 BL4   b   (4 Marks) Hydrogen (H2): Performance (highest thermal efficiency due to fast 10
flame/lean burn, high ON), Safety (wide flammability limits, low ignition energy,
requires careful handling/sensor).
(3 Marks) LNG (Methane): Performance (high ON, excellent knock resistance,
good efficiency), Safety (cryogenic storage risk, lighter than air (disperses)).
(3 Marks) DME (Dimethyl Ether): Performance (low ON, difficult to use in SI,
needs high compression or additives), Safety (liquefied gas, similar to LPG
handling).
Q6 CO4 BL3   a   (5 Marks) Determination: VFA are intermediate products of anaerobic digestion. 10
Determined via titration (pH method) or Gas Chromatography.
(5 Marks) Impact on Bio-fuel Quality: High VFA concentration indicates digester
instability (overloading or imbalance in microbial populations). High VFA lowers
the pH, inhibiting methanogens, leading to lower methane content (poor fuel
quality) and potential digester failure.
CO5 BL4   b   (5 Marks) Iodine Value (IV): Measure of unsaturation (double bonds) in the fatty 10
acid chains. Impact: High IV indicates high reactivity/poor oxidative stability
(gum/sediment formation) and, critically, correlates with poor cold flow properties
(higher IV usually means lower cloud/pour points but higher storage instability).
(5 Marks) Cetane Index (CI): Calculated value (not measured) that estimates the
CN based on density and distillation data. Impact: High CI leads to shorter ignition
delay and smooth combustion (good for CI efficiency). Low CI causes engine
knock and incomplete combustion, reducing efficiency.
Q7 CO2 BL3   a   (4 Marks) Production: Explain cultivation (open ponds/photobioreactors), 10
harvesting, lipid extraction, and conversion (e.g., Transesterification for biodiesel
or HTL for biocrude). High yield per area and CO2 sequestration are key benefits.
(6 Marks) Engine Effect: Algae-based biodiesel is chemically similar to
vegetable/conventional biodiesel. Performance: Good CN, lower energy density.
Emissions: Significantly lower PM, SOx, and CO; NOx may be a concern. Need
to meet ASTM standards (7b) for viscosity/acid value to prevent wear/corrosion.
********** 3 **********
--- page 4 ---
CO1 BL2   b   (5 Marks) ASTM Standards: Discuss the role of ASTM (e.g., ASTM D6751 for 10
biodiesel, ASTM D4806 for ethanol, relevant standards for LNG/CNG) in
establishing technical specifications, test methods, and minimum quality
requirements.
(5 Marks) Significance: Ensures Fuel Quality Consistency (preventing engine
damage), promotes Interchangeability between suppliers, enables Global Trade,
and ensures Safety and environmental compliance.

CO2 BL1   b   (1 Mark) High Octane Number: Better knock resistance.
(1 Mark) Lower Energy Density: Requires higher fuel consumption.
(1 Mark) Higher Latent Heat of Vaporization: Aids charge cooling, increasing
volumetric efficiency.
(1 Mark) Corrosiveness: Requires special handling/materials in the fuel
system.
CO3 BL2   c   (1 Mark) High Energy Content per Mass: Highest among all fuels.
(1 Mark) Clean Combustion: Produces only water vapor (H2O) and trace NOx
(zero carbon emissions).
(1 Mark) Storage Challenge: Requires high pressure or cryogenic
temperatures.
(1 Mark) Safety Challenge: Wide flammability limits and low ignition energy.
CO4 BL1   d   1 Mark per component (These are intermediate products in anaerobic
digestion): Acetic Acid (most dominant), Propionic Acid, Butyric Acid, and
Valeric Acid.
CO5 BL1   e   (2 Marks) Bomb Calorimeter: To accurately determine the Higher Heating
Value (HHV) or Gross Calorific Value of a fuel.

value, and cetane/octane numbers for both.
(3 Marks) Combustion: Compare volatility, latent heat, need for modification

(3 Marks) Emissions: Compare CO, HC, PM, and NOx emissions (e.g., alcohol

(5 Marks) Engine Modifications: Discuss port vs. direct injection (to prevent

Q3 CO3 BL3   a   (4 Marks) Dual-Fuel Mode: Necessity of a pilot fuel (diesel) for ignition due to 10
high auto-ignition temperature of methane.
(4 Marks) Performance: Higher thermal efficiency (lean burn), lower CO2/PM
emissions, but potential for Methane Slip (unburned CH4).
(2 Marks) Challenges: Low density, cryogenic storage requirements, and high
NOx at high loads.
CO4 BL4   b   (5 Marks) Biological Parameters: Explain the role of Anaerobic Digestion phases 10

stability.

Q6 CO4 BL3   a   (5 Marks) Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD): Definition (amount of dissolved 10

Procedure: Incubation for 5 days at 20∘C, measuring DO before and after.
Relevance: Indicates the level of biodegradable pollution.
(5 Marks) Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD): Definition (amount of oxygen
required to oxidize all organic compounds chemically), Procedure: Digestion with
strong oxidizing agents (e.g., potassium dichromate). Relevance: Measures total
organic content (biodegradable and non-biodegradable) and effluent quality.
CO5 BL4   b   (5 Marks) Octane Number (SI Engines): Measures resistance to knocking/auto- 10

reducing efficiency and damaging the engine.

complete combustion, leading to higher efficiency and lower noise. Low CN
causes rough running and incomplete combustion.
