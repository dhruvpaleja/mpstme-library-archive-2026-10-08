Prerequisite: Basic knowledge of mathematical concepts
Objectives
The objective of this course is to familiarize students with the principles of basic mathematics
as well as to develop an analytical and systematic approach towards solving problems and
learn to apply the concepts in related technology problems.
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to
1. Identify terms in the binomial expansion,
2. Demonstrate the fundamental concepts of complex numbers,
3. Apply the basic concepts of matrices, probability and statistics to solve real life
problems,
4. Solve a two – dimensional linear programming problem graphically.
