Course Objective
The course is aimed at emphasizing the importance of manufacturing science in the day-to-day
life, and to understand the basic manufacturing processes such as turning, casting, welding,
forging, rolling, extrusion, and drawing. The course is delineated particularly to understand
industrial applications of various processes and equipment used in manufacturing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, student will be able to -
1. Understand fundamental operations of lathe and drilling machines.
2. Develop a comprehensive understanding of sand-casting processes.
3. Explore forming techniques such as forging, rolling, and extrusion.
4. Demonstrate metal joining processes such as gas welding, arc welding, TIG, MIG, Soldering
and brazing.
5. Familiarize with innovative advancements in manufacturing technologies.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Manufacturing Processes   12
Introduction, definition, classification of manufacturing processes.
Fundamentals of Lathe and Drilling Machines
Basics of Machining: Single point cutting Tool and its nomenclature, Types
of Chips; Lathe machine: Classification, Basic parts and accessories like
chucks (three jaw, four jaw, and magnetic chuck), mandrels, rests,
faceplate, centres and angle plate of centre lathe and their functions; Lathe
operations: facing, plain turning, taper turning, thread cutting,
chamfering, grooving, knurling and cutting parameters like speed, feed,
depth of cut and machining time; Drill machine: Classification, Basic parts
of drilling machine and their function ; Drilling machine operations:
Cutting parameters- speed, feed, depth of cut and machining time.

2.   Sand Casting   10
Pattern making: Introduction, use of patterns, pattern materials, types of
patterns - single pattern, split pattern, loose-piece pattern, sweep pattern.
concept of cope, drag, runner, riser; Cores - core sands, core prints;
Casting: Melting of cast iron, charging a furnace, melting and pouring both
ferrous and non-ferrous metals, degasification, casting defects and
inspection; Introduction to ProCAST: Casting Simulation Software.
3.   Moulding Processes   06
Types and properties of moulding sands, moulding sand composition, different
moulding processes in plastics such as injection molding, blow molding,
compression molding, extrusion molding.
4.   Forming Processes   08
Drop forging: Introduction to forging, upset forging, press forging, open
die and closed die forging operations; Rolling: Principle of rolling, hot and
cold rolling and applications, rolling mill; Wire Drawing: Principle,
Classification, Advantages, Limitations and applications; Press tool:
Various operations performed on press, simple, compound and
progressive dies and applications.
5.   Metal Joining Processes   09
Welding Processes: welding and weldability, types and classification of
welding processes; Gas welding: gas welding equipment, oxy-acetylene
welding; Arc welding: arc welding equipment equipments, flux shielded
metal arc welding, TIG and MIG welding; Soldering and brazing process,
Comparison, merits, demerits and applications; Defects in welding joints:
causes and remedies; Introduction to COLDFORM software
Total   45
