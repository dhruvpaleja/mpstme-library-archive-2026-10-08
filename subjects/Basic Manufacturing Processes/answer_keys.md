A.   Define Single piece pattern and loose piece pattern.
Correct Answer – 4 Marks
Single Piece Pattern
A single piece pattern is a pattern made in one solid piece without any
joints or separation. It is used for simple castings where the shape can be
easily removed from the mould without damage.
Key points:
• Simple and easy to manufacture
• Used for small and uncomplicated shapes
• No parting line complications
• Low cost
Loose Piece Pattern
A loose piece pattern is a pattern that includes detachable parts (loose
pieces) to facilitate the removal of the pattern from the mould when the
shape is complex.
Key points:
• Used for intricate shapes with projections or undercuts
• Loose parts are removed separately after the main pattern
• Prevents damage to mould cavity
•   Slightly complex and time-consuming

Classification of Manufacturing Processes:
1. Casting Process – Producing parts by pouring molten metal into
moulds.
2. Forming Process – Changing shape of material without adding or
removing material (e.g., forging, rolling).
3. Machining Process – Removing unwanted material to get desired
shape (e.g., turning, milling).
4. Joining Process – Combining two or more parts (e.g., welding,
brazing, riveting).
5. Surface Finishing Process – Improving surface appearance or
properties (e.g., polishing, plating).
6. Additive Manufacturing – Building components layer by layer
(e.g., 3D printing).
