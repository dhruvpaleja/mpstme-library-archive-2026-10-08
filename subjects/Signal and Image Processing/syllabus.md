Course Objective
This course aims at introducing the concept of discrete time signals and systems. Taking image as a
discrete time signal, some methodologies for digital image processing are explored. This course would
enable the students to develop a foundation for research in the fields like computer vision, machine
learning, pattern recognition, etc.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to
1. Classify discrete time signals and systems
2. Implement Discrete Fourier Transform on discrete time signals
3. Apply spatial domain and frequency domain techniques for image enhancement
4. Demonstrate the basic morphological operation such as dilation, erosion, opening and closing
on the given image
5. Analyze images processed by various segmentation techniques
6. Explain the motion estimation and compensation techniques in video processing
