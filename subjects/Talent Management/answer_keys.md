Concept: The talent management lifecycle is a continuous process of attracting, developing,
engaging, retaining, and re-engaging employees to achieve organisational goals and ensure
long-term sustainability.
Lifecycle
1.   Recruitment: Recruitment involves identifying manpower needs and attracting suitable
candidates through effective sourcing and selection, ensuring the availability of skilled and
capable employees.
2.   Orientation / Onboarding: Onboarding introduces new employees to organisational
culture, policies, and job roles, helping them settle quickly and improving early productivity.
3.   Employee Engagement: Employee engagement focuses on motivating and involving
employees through recognition, communication, and participation, leading to higher
commitment and lower attrition.
4.   Learning and Development: Learning and Development enhances employee skills
through training and career development, helping close skill gaps and preparing employees
for future responsibilities.
5.   Offboarding: Offboarding manages employee exits through formal procedures and
knowledge transfer, protecting organisational interests and improving HR practices.

Driving Competitive Advantage through Non-Traditional Approaches to Engagement
Surveys (3 marks)
Traditional annual engagement surveys often fail to capture real-time employee sentiment in
today’s fast-changing work environment. Non-traditional approaches to engagement surveys
focus on continuous listening, qualitative insights, and data-driven decision-making. These
methods provide timely, actionable feedback and help organizations respond quickly to
employee needs. By adopting innovative engagement measurement techniques, organizations
can improve agility, retain talent, and enhance performance. Such approaches transform
engagement surveys from diagnostic tools into strategic assets. This enables organizations to
gain a sustainable competitive advantage.

Social Recruiting: It refers to the use of social media platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram,
Facebook, and professional online communities to attract, engage, and hire talent. It goes
beyond posting job vacancies and focuses on building relationships with potential candidates
through employer branding, content sharing, and direct engagement. Organisations use social
recruiting to reach both active and passive candidates who may not be searching on traditional
job portals. This approach allows recruiters to showcase company culture, values, and
employee experiences, making the organisation more attractive to job seekers. Social
recruiting also enables targeted hiring through data analytics and social ads. Overall, it is a
cost effective, faster, and more interactive recruitment method in today’s digital hiring
environment
