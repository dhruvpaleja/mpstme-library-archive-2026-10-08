The objective of the course is to introduce the basic principles of chemistry such as periodicity of elements, mole
concept, and basic organic chemistry & to familiarize with the concepts and applications of solution
stoichiometry, chemical bonding & organic reactions.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Define the periodicity of elements.
2. Apply principles of mole concepts in various real-time problems.
3. Develop an interest in the fundamentals of chemical bonding, Basic Organic chemistry & Organic
reactions.
4. Discuss the different effects of dilutions in aqueous solutions and solve numerical based on
stoichiometry.
