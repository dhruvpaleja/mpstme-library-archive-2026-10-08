Pre-requisite: Soil Mechanics; Foundation Engineering
Course Objective
This course focusses on advance geotechnical analysis including evaluation of stability of slopes,
analyses of earth retaining structures and estimation of stresses in soil. The course also focuses on
estimation of field bearing capacity of footing and design of group piles based on Indian
Standards.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Estimate stresses in soil using appropriate theories
2. Distinguish between types of slope failures, and analyze the stability of earth slopes
3. Analyse of earth retaining structures like gravity retaining walls
4. Estimation of field bearing capacity and settlement of shallow and pile foundations
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
