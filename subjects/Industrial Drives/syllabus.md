The primary objective of this course is to familiarize students with the operation of power

2. Demonstrate dc converter drives for industrial applications
3. Design chopper fed dc drives for various real time applications
4. Design Induction Motor Drives for industrial applications
5. Analyze the performance of Synchronous motor drives
