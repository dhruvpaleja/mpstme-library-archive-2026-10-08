= Distance * 2 Ways * No of Trips * No of Days
= 40 km*?2 ways*2 trips*(4weeks*Sdays a week)
= 3,200 Kms (2 Marks)

b) Effective Km-Tonnes = Run * Load

= Distance * No of Ways Loaded * No of Trips * No of Days* Maximum Capacity* Used
Capacity

= 40 kms*1 way fully loaded*2 trips*(4weeks*Sdays a week) *20 tonne* 100%
= 1,600Kms*10 Tonnes
= 16,000 km-tonnes (2 Marks)

Notes: Here on return journey, lorry returns empty (without any load), hence while calculating
Kilometre-tonnes only 1-way journey (80 kms*1 ways fully loaded) is considered.

= 32,800/16,000
