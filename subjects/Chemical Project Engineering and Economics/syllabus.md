Course Objective
The course provides basic knowledge of project engineering and stages involved, with in-depth
understanding of selection criteria. The course also deals with economic analysis of projects with the cost
estimation, profitability evaluation, and financing of projects.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Classify and analyse the chemical projects
2. Apply the optimum design of the plant and its layout
3. Estimate the capital investment, operating cost and project profitability including product pricing
