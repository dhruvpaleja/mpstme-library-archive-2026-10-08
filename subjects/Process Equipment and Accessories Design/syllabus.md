Objectives
The principal objective of this course is to understand and apply various design
fundamentals for the basic design of heat transfer, mass transfer equipments and
pressure vessel from available process design. Students will also be able to learn
and interpret the detailed drawing of the above equipments
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Design Reaction vessels.
2. Design Heat Exchangers.
3. Design Distillation Columns.
4. Design Pressure Vessels.
