c. 1. Elasticity   [6]
Elasticity is the property of a material by which it regains its original shape and size after removal
of the applied load, provided the deformation is within limits.
2. Elastic Limit
Elastic limit   is the maximum   stress up to which a material behaves   elastically.
Beyond this point, the material will not return completely to its original shape and permanent
deformation begins.
3. Young’s Modulus (E)
Young’s modulus is the ratio of normal (tensile or compressive) stress to the corresponding
longitudinal strain within the elastic limit.
4. Modulus of Rigidity (Shear Modulus, G)
Modulus of rigidity is the ratio of shear stress to shear strain within the elastic limit.

c. 1. Elasticity   [6]
Elasticity is the property of a material by which it regains its original shape and size after removal
of the applied load, provided the deformation is within limits.
2. Elastic Limit
Elastic limit   is the maximum   stress up to which a material behaves   elastically.
Beyond this point, the material will not return completely to its original shape and permanent
deformation begins.
3. Young’s Modulus (E)
Young’s modulus is the ratio of normal (tensile or compressive) stress to the corresponding
longitudinal strain within the elastic limit.
4. Modulus of Rigidity (Shear Modulus, G)
Modulus of rigidity is the ratio of shear stress to shear strain within the elastic limit.
