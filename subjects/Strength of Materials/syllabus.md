Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics
Course Objective
The course is aimed at providing adequate understanding of the fundamentals of stresses and strains
in deformable bodies subjected to various types of static loads. It is designed to impart the knowledge
of basic concepts of deformation & deflection, identifying the relevant tests to be performed on beams
and power transmitting shafts. It develops the ability to assess the strength of engineering materials and
subsequently examine the failure criteria of structural members using relevant analysis.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain fundamental concepts of stress, strain and material properties in structural analysis
2. Construct shear force and bending moment diagrams for statically determinate beams under
various loading conditions
3. Analyze beam bending stresses, shear stresses, and deflections using beam theory
4. Analyze torsional stresses in solid and hollow circular shafts used in power transmission
5. Determine principal stresses, maximum shear stress, and stress transformation using Mohr’s circle
6. Determine mechanical properties and access failure characteristics by material testing using UTM
or similar equipment.
