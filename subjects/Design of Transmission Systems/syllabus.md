Program: B Tech /B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)Semester: VII/XI
Course: Design of Transmission Systems   Code: 702ME0E017
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous Term End Examinations
(Hours per   (Hours per (Hours per Credit   Assessment (ICA)   (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics, Materials Engineering, Strength of Materials, Design of
Machine Elements
Course Objective
This course is designed to impart knowledge of the principles and procedures for the design of
mechanical power transmission components. It also helps to understand the standard procedure
available for design of transmission of mechanical elements.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Articulate the design of belt drives and selection of belt, rope and chain drives
2. Analyze forces to design spur gears and helical gears
3. Design bevel and worm gears
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1.   Design of Flexible Elements   10
Design of Flat belts and pulleys - Selection of V belts and pulleys – Selection of
hoisting wire ropes and pulleys
2.   Spur Gears and Parallel Axis Helical Gears   10
Speed ratios and number of teeth-Force analysis, Tooth stresses, Dynamic effects,
Fatigue strength, Factor of safety, Gear materials, Design of straight tooth spur &
helical gears based on strength and wear considerations, Pressure angle in the
normal and transverse plane, Equivalent number of teeth-forces for helical gears.
3.   Bevel, Worm and Cross Helical Gears   10
Straight bevel gear: Tooth terminology, tooth forces and stresses, equivalent number
of teeth. Estimating the dimensions of pair of straight bevel gears. Worm Gear:
Merits and demerits terminology. Thermal capacity, materials-forces and stresses,
efficiency, estimating the size of the worm gear pair. Cross helical: Terminology-
helix angles-Estimating the size of the pair of cross helical gears.
Total   30
Text Books
1. J. F. Shigley, Mechanical Engineering Design, 13th Edition, Tata McGraw Hill, 2014.
2. V. B. Bhandari, Design of Machine Elements, 5th Edition, Tata McGraw Hill Publication, 2020.
