Q5   20
Professional networking plays an important role in career development
as it helps individuals build meaningful connections, discover new
opportunities, and gain valuable insights into industry trends. In
professional settings, it is also essential to understand the difference
between formal and informal meetings. Formal meetings are usually
structured, follow a set agenda, and require a professional tone,
whereas informal meetings are more flexible, conversational, and
relaxed. During interviews, questions like “Tell me about yourself”
should be answered with clarity and confidence, focusing on one’s
strengths, communication skills, and career goals in a concise manner.
At the same time, candidates must be mindful of their behaviour, as
actions such as interrupting the interviewer or checking their phone can
appear disrespectful and unprofessional, ultimately harming their
credibility and chances of selection. To maintain a positive
professional image, it is important to follow ethical guidelines such as
being honest, respecting others’ time, maintaining professionalism in
communication, and following up politely after interactions. Together,
these practices create a strong and lasting impression in both
networking situations and interviews.
