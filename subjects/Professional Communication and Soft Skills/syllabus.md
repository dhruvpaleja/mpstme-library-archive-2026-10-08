Pre-requisite
Completion of Business communication and Value Science-I, Business Communication And
Value Science-II & Technical Communication and Leadership Skills.
Course Objective
This course aims at creating in the students an ability to manage critical and stressful
situations through applying effective and proper communication principles and tools. Also
this course aims to impart business correspondence skills and business etiquette awareness
towards enabling the stakeholders for impactful Professional Communication.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Effectively gauge the context and the environment of a particular situation to develop,
articulate and disseminate appropriate oral and written scripts to suit the situation.
2. Utilize appropriate soft skills to create work life balance
3. Apply emotional Intelligence at workplace to successfully manage their professional and
personal relationships as well as to positively communicate during feedback sharing,
personal interviews etc.
4. Utilize suitable communication tools to resolve conflicts and mediate and negotiate to
create win-win solutions
