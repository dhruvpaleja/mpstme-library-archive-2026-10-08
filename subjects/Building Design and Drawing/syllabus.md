Course: Building Design and Drawing   Code: 702CI0C029
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours   (Hours   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
per week) per week)   per week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
1   2   0   2   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Graphics and Design
Course Objective
This course aims to acquire knowledge about the principles of planning of residential buildings as
well as public buildings. It also aims to preparation of different drawings for residential/public
buildings as per building bye laws for planning of residential/public buildings.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the principles of planning, architectural planning and building byelaws while
designing and preparing building drawings
2. Design buildings including its various components for the given requirements of the building
as per Development Control laws of local governing bodies
3. Plan and develop drawings for residential buildings
4. Draw various buildings components
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Drawing Basics   02
Introduction to concept of drawings, optimal layout of drawings and Scales,
1   commonly used symbols, conventions and abbreviations used in architectural
drawings, Planning drawings   to show information   concisely and
comprehensively and Interpretation of typical drawings.
Building bye-laws   04
Study of building Bye-laws and documents / permissions required from
commencement to completion of the building according to National Building
2   Code (N.B.C.) of India and local Development Control (D.C.) rules, Following
important bye-laws: Plot area & Built-up area, Size of rooms, Margins,
Heights, Passages, Ventilation, Circulation, Open space, setback distances,
carpet area and floor space index (FSI).
Principles of planning   03
For residential/public buildings: Principles of planning such as room
3
dimension, area, heights, privacy, ventilation, access, circulation, economy,
drainage, aspect, prospect, orientation, grouping, mass composition etc.
Planning residential buildings   03
Given situations & plot area; prepare detailed drawing of a single storied
4   residential building (Load Bearing & RCC) i.e. line plan, detailed plan,
elevation and section of the building, Site plan, location plan, Other plans and
tables required.
