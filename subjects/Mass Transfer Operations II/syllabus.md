Objectives
The principal objective of this course is to understand the design method of
distillation column. The subject also delves into understanding the basic
concept of extraction, leaching, adsorption, crystallization and the relevant
equipment design. The students will also introduce the concepts of membrane
separation and ion exchange process
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand equilibrium in all separation processes.
2. Understand mass transfer equipments.
3. Design distillation column.
4. Selection of the separation operations which will be economical for the process.
