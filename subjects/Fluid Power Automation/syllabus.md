Pre-requisite: Physics and Mathematics
Course Objective
The course develops an understanding of basic principles of hydraulic and pneumatic
equipment and circuits used in industrial automation. It elaborates the electronic hardware
and software aspects of the setups so that the students learn to analyze practical problems with
schematics as well as the actual hardware.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the functionality of industrial hydraulic and pneumatic systems
2. Select the correct tools for solving problems in practice
3. Develop the basic framework of the setup to aid the design process
4. Analyze the overall system performance using key impact parameters and
requirements
5. Synthesize industrial systems for basic automated operation
