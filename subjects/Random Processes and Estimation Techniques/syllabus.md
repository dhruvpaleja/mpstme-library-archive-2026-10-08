Course Objective
The primary objectives of the outlined points are to cultivate a comprehensive understanding and
proficiency in statistical theory, computation, and practical application. This includes a focus on
theoretical foundations as well as computational techniques within the realm of statistics. Additionally,
the aim is to foster competence in grasping the intricacies of random processes and various estimation
methods, emphasizing a well-rounded comprehension of statistical concepts and techniques for
effective analysis and decision-making.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Know the basics of random processes
2. Know the spectral representation of random signals and calculate power spectral density, cross-
spectral density
3. Recognize the applications of different types of random processes
4. Demonstrate the ability to understand the concepts of estimation, parametric and non-
parametric inference
5. Apply knowledge of parametric and non-parametric techniques in estimation and statistical
inference
