Program: B Tech/B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: VI / X
Course: Hydraulic and Pneumatic Systems   Code: 702ME0E010
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objective
This course is designed to encourage the students to understand the role of fluid power in automation.
It helps them to identify the various components of an automation system and design circuits to
control the physical output work.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Relate hydraulic power, hydraulic actuators and motors
2. Analyze the different components of hydraulic systems
3.   Identify different pneumatic controls, actuators and control valves
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Hydraulic Power   08
Role of drive-in automation system, comparison of drives. Definition of hydraulic
system, advantages, limitations, applications, Pascal's law, structure of hydraulic
control system, problems on Pascal's law. The source of Hydraulic Power: Pumps
Classification of pumps, pumping theory of positive displacement pumps,
construction and working of Gear pumps and Vane pumps.
2. Hydraulic Systems   07
Hydraulic Power Pack, Hydraulic Accumulator, Symbolic representation and
working of Control Valves – Direction Control Valves, Flow Control Valves,
Check valves, shuttle valves, pressure valves. Use of valves for speed and pressure
control. Hydraulic circuits in power tools and other applications.
3. Pneumatic Systems   07
Definition of pneumatic system, advantages, limitations, applications, Choice of
working medium. Characteristics of compressed air. Structure of Pneumatic
control System, Direction control valves, single and double acting linear actuator;
direct and indirect control of DCV, Speed control circuits.
4.   Fluid power circuits with electrical control   08
Ladder Logic Diagram, Electro-hydraulic/pneumatic control, Use of relays and
limit switches, Sequencing Circuits with and without electrical control, multi-
cylinder circuit applications.
Total   30
