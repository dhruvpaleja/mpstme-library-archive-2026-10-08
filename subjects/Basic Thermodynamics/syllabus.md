Prerequisite: Mathematics I & II
Course Objective
This course describes the fundamentals of thermodynamics including thermodynamic systems
and properties, work and heat interactions, relationships among the thermo-physical properties,
the laws of thermodynamics and applications of these basic laws in thermodynamic systems such
as various thermodynamic cycles. This course will provide a strong foundation required to study
applications of thermodynamics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to -
1.   Understand the fundamentals of thermodynamic systems, equilibrium, and system
properties.
2.   Illustrate heat transfer processes in thermodynamic systems
3.   Apply the first law of thermodynamics to analyze cyclic and non-cyclic processes and
energy storage systems
4.   Analyze thermodynamic systems using the second law of thermodynamics, including heat
engines, pumps, and Carnot cycle
5.   Interpret the properties of pure substances and use diagrams, steam tables, and Mollier
charts for calculations
6.   Analyze real-world thermodynamic applications, such as engines, pumps, and
refrigeration systems, using basic principles
