Course Objective

The course introduces students to the fundamental concepts and techniques of optimization,
emphasizing their significance in decision-making and data science. Students will gain the
ability to model real-world scenarios mathematically, understand various optimization
methods, and apply them effectively in practical and engineering contexts.

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Use a suitable optimization method for a given problem.
2. Demonstrate use of decision making and game theory principles.
3. Solve assignment and transportation problems.
4. Apply optimization methods to solve real-life problems.
5. Solve optimization problems using computational tools.
