As the miniaturization of electronic devices enables the arrival of new gadgets, it is important
to note that the devices that we still use were invented decades ago. Over the period these

student to understand the construction, working principle, application of basic electronic
devices, operational amplifiers, and its application.

After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain construction and characteristics of various types of diodes and illustrate simple
circuits with diodes
2. Understand BJT, FET, and MOSFETS, their modes of operation and their applications

4. Compare different oscillator circuits
