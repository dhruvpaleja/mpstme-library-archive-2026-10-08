Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics
Course Objective
The course is aimed at developing the detailed understanding of various mechanism used in devices or
machines. This course focuses on analysis of the motion of mechanisms and to design mechanisms that
yield the desired motion. The course is designed for the students to understand and master the concepts
of displacement, velocity and acceleration of simple mechanisms and machine components. These
include cam profile and its analysis, gear kinematics with gear train calculations, belt drives and chain
drives, and friction clutches and brakes.
Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamentals of links, mechanisms, machines, brakes and clutches
2. Analyse planar mechanism for displacement, velocity and acceleration graphically and analytically
3. Construct various type of Cam profiles and analyze the standard motion of the follower
4. Illustrate the machine components like belt, ropes and chains for required mechanisms
5. Estimate the performance and efficiency of brakes and clutches, considering frictional forces and
their impact on machine operation
6. Evaluate simple, compound, reverted, and epicyclic gear trains to determine velocity ratios
Detailed Syllabus
