Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme

week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   2   0   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Physics
Course Objective
The objective of this course is to develop awareness and basic skills necessary for the reliable
measurements and measuring instruments and impart knowledge on methods of measurement of
different engineering parameters such as pressure, temperature, displacement, etc. and different
metrological instruments for linear, angular, taper and surface finish measurements.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Describe various terminologies associated with measurement and metrology
2. Understand the objectives of metrology, methods of measurement, standards of measurement
& various measurement parameters
3. Choose sensors, devices and methods for measurement of specific parameters with required
accuracy
4. Demonstrate the ability to apply precision measurement techniques to ensure accuracy and
quality control in mechanical systems
5. Measure and derive important dimensions of thread forms and gears
6. Analyze the impact of various factors on the measurement process
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Measurement   04
Need of measurement, Study of instruments, measurement methods, generalized
measurement system & its functional elements, instrument characteristics - static
& dynamic characteristics, calibration.

Thermometry

measurement, resistance thermometry, pyrometer.

Bourdon tubes, dead weight pressure gauge tester, diaphragm gauge, LVDT,
Piezo-electric pressure gauge, Low vacuum gauges - McLeod gauge, thermal

pressure measuring instruments.
3.   Strain Gauges   04
Classification, electrical strain gauge: working principle and types, gauge factor
(analytical treatment), analysis of Wheatstone’s network using strain gauges,
mounting of strain gauges.
