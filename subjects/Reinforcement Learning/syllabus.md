The aim of the course is to give an introduction to the basic aspects of reinforcement learning and its

1. Explain RL concepts, terminology, elements, and real-world applications.

3. Explain Markov Processes, MDPs, and the role of Bellman equations in optimal decision-making.
4. Analyze function approximation techniques and evaluate the performance of RL algorithms.
5. Design RL models using policy gradient and Actor-Critic methods for real-world applications.
