A shunt capacitor filter is the simplest filter used after a rectifier to reduce ripple.
• A capacitor (C) is connected in parallel (shunt) with the load resistor
RLR_LRL
•   It is placed at the output of the rectifier (half-wave or full-wave)
Working   03
The operation depends on the charging and discharging of the capacitor.
During Positive Half Cycle (Charging)
• The rectifier conducts → output voltage rises
• Capacitor charges rapidly to the peak value (Vm) of the rectified waveform
• Charging is fast because:
o Internal resistance is small
o Diode is forward-biased

Working Principle – 3M
A universal motor operates on the principle of electromagnetic induction. It works
similarly to a DC series motor, where both the field winding and the armature
winding are connected in series. When current flows through these windings, it
generates a magnetic field, which interacts with the armature's magnetic field to
produce torque and cause rotation.
The direction of the current in both the armature and the field windings reverses
simultaneously with the supply polarity. This ensures that the torque direction
remains the same, whether the motor is powered by AC (alternating current) or
DC (direct current).

Working Principle – 3M
********** 7 **********
--- page 8 ---
A universal motor operates on the principle of electromagnetic induction. It works
similarly to a DC series motor, where both the field winding and the armature
winding are connected in series. When current flows through these windings, it
generates a magnetic field, which interacts with the armature's magnetic field to
produce torque and cause rotation.
The direction of the current in both the armature and the field windings reverses
simultaneously with the supply polarity. This ensures that the torque direction
remains the same, whether the motor is powered by AC (alternating current) or
DC (direct current).
The universal motor can work on both AC and DC due to these reasons: 3M
Series Winding Configuration:
The armature and field windings are connected in series. When the supply current
reverses (in AC), both windings reverse together, keeping the magnetic field and
torque direction consistent.
Magnetic Field Synchronization:
Whether AC or DC is supplied, the magnetic field generated in both windings
remains in the same phase, ensuring smooth motor operation.
Commutator Action:
The commutator reverses the current direction in the armature, maintaining
continuous rotation regardless of the input power type.
Advantages (any two) – 2M
High Starting Torque:
It delivers very high starting torque, making it ideal for heavy-load applications
like power tools and appliances.
Compact and Lightweight Design:
Universal motors have a small size relative to their power output, making them
suitable for portable devices.
High-Speed Operation:
Capable of operating at very high speeds (up to 20,000 RPM or more), making
them ideal for drills, mixers, and vacuum cleaners.
Simple and Cost-Effective Construction:
The design is simple, using a series-wound configuration, leading to low
manufacturing costs.
Applications – 2M
Household Appliances:
Used in mixers, blenders, vacuum cleaners, and sewing machines due to their
ability to deliver high-speed and compact size.
Power Tools:
Found in drills, grinders, and saws because of their high starting torque and
ability to operate on standard AC supply.
