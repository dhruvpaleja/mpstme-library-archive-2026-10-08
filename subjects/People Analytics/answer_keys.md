Q.1(d)
Q-Explain why “correlation does not imply causation” is a major limitation in People Analytics. Illustrate
with an example. Write the implications and solutions. What would you conclude?
Synoptic Answer
✓ Correlation shows association between variables, not cause–effect
✓ High correlation may be due to hidden variables (confounders)
✓ Leads to wrong managerial decisions
✓ Example:-
Data shows: Training ↑ → Performance ↑ (r = 0.80)
But actual cause may be: High performers receive more training and Motivation drives both
✓ Thus, training may not be the real cause
✓ Implication - Misinterpretation leads to poor HR interventions
✓ Solution - Use regression with controls / experiments / SEM
✓ Conclusion - Analytics must distinguish association from causality

with an example. Write the implications and solutions. What would you conclude?
Synoptic Answer
✓ Correlation shows association between variables, not cause–effect
✓ High correlation may be due to hidden variables (confounders)
✓ Leads to wrong managerial decisions
✓ Example:-
Data shows: Training ↑ → Performance ↑ (r = 0.80)
But actual cause may be: High performers receive more training and Motivation drives both
✓ Thus, training may not be the real cause
✓ Implication - Misinterpretation leads to poor HR interventions
✓ Solution - Use regression with controls / experiments / SEM
✓ Conclusion - Analytics must distinguish association from causality
