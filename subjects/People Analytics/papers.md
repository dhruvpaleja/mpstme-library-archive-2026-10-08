Selecting the right HR metric is critical because wrong metrics lead to wrong decisions. HR primarily
aims to ensure adequate hiring (vacancies filled) and ensure retention (low turnover). Thus, commonly
used metrics include the job vacancy rate and turnover rate. However, the same metric can mislead if not
defined properly. The problem with poor metric design (traditional vacancy formula below) creates
confusion because the 'denominator changes dynamically’ and is 'not stable for comparison' –
Open Positions
Vacancy Rate =   × 100
Filled Positions
For example,
Given the dataset below: -
Scenario   Budgeted Positions   Filled Positions   Open Positions
1   1000   900   100
2   1000   500   500
3   1000   250   750
Using the wrong formula above, three scenarios emerge for vacancy rates, which is a problem because as
the denominator keeps changing, that leads to misleading insights:-
Scenario   Vacancy Rate
1   11%
2   100%
3   ??? (confusing)
A company has 1000 budgeted positions. Currently: 700 are filled, and 300 are open.
1.   What correct metric (formula) would you recommend?
2.   Calculate the vacancy rate using: a) Open / Filled; b) Open / Budgeted
3.   Identify which metric is more appropriate.
4.   Interpret the result.
