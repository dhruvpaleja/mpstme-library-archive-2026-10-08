Pre-requisite:
Knowledge of fundamental concepts in Algebra, Differential and Integral Calculus.
Course Objectives:
This course aims to instil in prospective engineers’ knowledge of concepts and techniques in
Linear Algebra and Differential Equations. It also prepares the students to deal with advanced
level of Mathematics and applications that would be essential for their disciplines.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to-
1. Solve systems of linear equations using the matrix method and understand linear algebra
concepts, including vector spaces, subspaces, basis, and dimension (K3).
2. Analyze linear transformations by understanding their matrices, kernels, ranges and
compute Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors of matrices and apply them to solve problems
related to diagonalization (K3).
3. Solve first-order ordinary differential equations using standard techniques for exact and
linear equations (K3).
4. Utilize the operator method and the method variation of parameters to find solutions of
higher-order linear differential equations and the Euler-Cauchy equation (K3).
5. Form partial differential equations and employ direct integration and separation of
variables techniques to find solutions (K3).
6. Apply concepts from linear algebra and differential equations to model and address real-
world problems in engineering, physics, and applied sciences (K3).

Pre-requisite: Knowledge of fundamental concepts in Algebra, Differential
and Integral Calculus.
Course Objective
This course aims to instil in prospective engineer’s knowledge of concepts and techniques
in Linear Algebra and Differential Equations. It also prepares the students to deal with
advanced level of Mathematics and applications that would be essential for their disciplines.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to-
1. Understand the fundamental concepts of linear algebra, including vector spaces, subspaces,
basis, and dimension, and apply them to solve problems related to linear independence and
dependence.
2. Analyze and apply linear transformations, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and diagonalization
techniques, including the Rank-Nullity Theorem and the Cayley-Hamilton Theorem, to
solve problems involving matrices.
3. Solve first-order ordinary differential equations using exact equations, integrating factors,
and special methods such as Bernoulli’s equation and orthogonal trajectories.
4. Formulate and solve higher-order linear differential equations using methods such as the
operator method, undetermined coefficients, variation of parameters, and power series
solutions, including Legendre and Bessel equations.
5. Understand the formation and classification of partial differential equations and apply direct
integration and separation of variables techniques to solve them in Cartesian coordinates.
6. Apply concepts from linear algebra and differential equations to model and solve real-world
problems in engineering, physics, and applied sciences.
