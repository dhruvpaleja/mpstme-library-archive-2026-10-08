Course Objective
This course focuses on the multidisciplinary scientific bases for climate change, paleo-climatic change
and impact on communities. It also focuses on the legal and financial aspects of climate change,
adaptation and mitigation.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the climate system and global circulation
2. Investigate climate projections from the past
3. Discuss global climate change policy and its implementation, mitigation & adaptations

Pre-requisite: Introductory course in Environmental Studies, physics, chemistry, geography
Objectives:
1. Interpret multidisciplinary scientific bases of climatic change: basic mechanisms that govern the
climate system and its responses to drivers of change
2. Analyse paleo-climatic change and assess recent historical variability to project future climate
projections
3. Investigate the impact of climate change on communities of human population and global
economy
4. Gain insight into legal and Financial aspects of Climate change
5. Investigate fundamentals of the fields of Geo-engineering
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Explain how the climate system works and understand the physical basis of climate change
2. Investigate climate projections from the past and predict possible future climate change on Earth
3. Analyse how human activities are influencing Climate Change and Global Warming
4. Evaluate global climate change policy and its implementation, options for mitigation and
adaptation
5. Evaluate aspects of Climate change finance
