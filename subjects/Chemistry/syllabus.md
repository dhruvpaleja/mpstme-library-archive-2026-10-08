Introduction, types of corrosion, chemical and electrochemical theories of

liquids), factors affecting corrosion, preventive measures for corrosion-   5
Cathodic and anodic protection methods, use of protective coatings

paints and varnishes).

Course Rationale:
This course is intended to provide essential concepts in Chemistry. The contents have covered fundamentals of
chemistry required for students to apply the subject knowledge in engineering and technological applications.

Course Objectives:

1. To familiarize with atomic, molecular structure and chemical bonding of elements

2. To introduce electromagnetic spectrum ranges that are applied for various applications.

3. To acquaint with periodic properties of elements, thermodynamic parameters, cell emf and Nernst
equation.

4. To familiarize with stereochemistry and various organic reactions.

Course Outcomes:

After completion of the course, students would be able to :

1. understand basic concepts of atomic, molecular structure and chemical bonding of elements.

2, interpret various spectroscopic techniques like, electronic, vibrational-rotational and magnetic resonance.
3. rationalise periodic properties of the elements with electrochemistry.

4. solve numerical problems based on free energy & Nernst equation, EDTA as chelating agent.

5. recognize synthesis of organic molecules with respect to their structure and reactivity.

Pedagogy:

Experimental learning activities, presentations and lecture method.
