Course: Finite Element Analysis   Code: 702ME0C022
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme

3   2   0   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Mathematics I, Mathematics II, Engineering Mechanics, Strength of Materials, Heat
Transfer
Course Objective
This course presents an introduction to the finite element methods. The guidelines for selecting
displacement functions and the concepts that should be considered when modeling are discussed as
the course progresses. This course describes how to treat different types of forces for two-dimensional
elements and to solve one-dimensional heat transfer problems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understanding the basic principles of FEA, including discretization, element types and shape
functions
2. Derive the stiffness matrix for the beam and Constant-Strain Triangular element
3. Illustrate FEA characteristic equations to analyze plain stress, plain strain and derive element
matrices for CST elements
4. Develop FEA Characteristic equations for Isoparamteric problems and heat transfer problem
5. Analyze solution accuracy by performing error estimation, mesh refinement studies, and
convergence analysis to improve simulation reliability
6. Use ANSYS in real-world mechanical, thermal, and structural engineering problems to analyze
their behavior
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   07
Brief history, matrix notation, general steps, applications and advantages of the
FEM, computer programs for the FEM, stiffness matrix for a spring element,
spring assemblage, direct stiffness method, boundary conditions.
2.   Truss   08
Stiffness matrix for a bar element, displacement function, transformation of
vectors in two dimensions, global stiffness matrix for bar arbitrarily oriented in
the plane, computation of stress for a bar in the x – y plane, solution of a plane
truss, use of symmetry in structures, inclined supports.
3.   Beam and Frame   10
Beam stiffness, assemblage of beam stiffness matrices, direct stiffness method,
distributed loading, beam element with nodal hinge, two-dimensional arbitrarily
oriented beam element, rigid plane frame, skewed supports, beam element

Objectives:
To introduce conceptsand applicationsof FEM in Engineering problems
To impart knowledge of one dimensional problems by FEM
To get acquainted with the analysis of beams, frame and truss problems by
FEM
To acquire the knowledge of two dimensional problems by FEM
Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
Describe the basic concept and applications of FEM in Engineering problems.
Solve one dimensional problems by FEM.
Analyze the beam, frame and truss elementsby FEM.
Solve two dimensional equations by FEM
