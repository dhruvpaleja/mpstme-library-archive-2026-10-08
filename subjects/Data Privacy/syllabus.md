Pre-requisite: Basic knowledge of technology
Course Objective
We live in a technology enabled world where data is collected and process as we interact with any
technology product or service. This data is used by the companies to predict our tastes, our habits,
or future possible health problems. This use of data has created a big privacy threat. Various
governments have developed stringent privacy laws like GDPR to protect the privacy of their
citizens. Effective information privacy is very difficult. Globally organizations are approaching
privacy problem based on two concepts: Privacy by design and privacy engineering. In this course
students will be exposed to both the concepts.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify data privacy threats and suggest their countermeasures
2. Implement various privacy models
3. Design privacy program for an organization
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   06
Data privacy need and concepts, privacy by design principles, information
privacy requirements and guidelines, information privacy threats and
vulnerabilities, privacy risk assessment, privacy and security control
selection, privacy integration, testing and evaluation, trade-offs between
privacy and security, usable privacy.
2.   Data Privacy Attacks and Models   07
The evolving threat environment, privacy threat taxonomy, NIST threat
model, privacy attacks (Linkage Attack, re-identification Attack, Statistical
Attack), De-identification of direct identifiers,
Basic Privacy Models K- Anonymity, L-Diversity, t-closeness, Differential
Privacy, Generalisation, Suppression, Perturbation, Randomization.
3.   Online Privacy   07
The online eco-system for personal data, online privacy threats, online
privacy requirements, privacy notices and tracking, Privacy Preserving Data
Sharing and Publications, Privacy Preserving Access Control Models.

Information privacy governance, privacy roles, privacy program plan, key
areas of privacy management, privacy policy, NIST privacy framework,
