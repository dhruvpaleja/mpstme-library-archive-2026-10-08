Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics (MBAB01004 and MBAB02005), Materials
Engineering (MBME03004) and Basics of Design Engineering (MBME05003 and
MBME(06005).

Objective:

e To introduce the fundamental physical principles of the classical theory of
friction, wear and lubrication.

e To demonstrate basic understanding of friction, lubrication and wear
processes

e To understand the fundamental principles of high contact stresses (Hertz
stresses), fatigue-failure, and Elasto-hydrodynamic (EHD) lubrication in
rolling bearings and gears.

Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
e Understand the principles for selecting compatible materials for minimizing
friction and wear in machinery.
e Understand the principles of bearing selection and bearing arrangement in
machines.
¢ Computations requirements for selecting bearings in machines.
