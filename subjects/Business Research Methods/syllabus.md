Objectives:

To familiarize students on the following:
e Provide research techniques and tools required to conduct business research projects.

e Highlight significance of research frameworks and literature review.

Outcomes:
After completion of this course, students should be able to;

1. Ability to work on independent research projects, acquire the skill to write business
research reports.

2. Understand basic research process, its requirements and objectives, formulate research
problem, types of variables and its use in research. carry out literature survey and
literature review. develop theoretical framework in terms of variables. learn how to
develop hypotheses.

3. Understand Research Design and its types. sampling design and its importance in data
collection and different data collection methods.

4, Learn about data pre-processing, reliability and validity testing of Data. (both
quantitative and qualitative.).
