Tata Projects is an engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC)
company delivering large-scale industrial and infrastructure projects.
Further, they also supply industrial equipment to infrastructure and
construction companies. For a new project and new set of customers, the
firm is considering different pricing approaches. Analyze the different
pricing strategies that the company can use.
Synoptic Answer
There are four methods:
•   Cost-based pricing
• Value - based pricing
•   Competition-based pricing
•   Transfer pricing
1. Cost-Based Pricing
This approach is internally focused, ensuring the firm covers all
expenditures and achieves a specific profit margin.
Q7   •   Application: G&T would first estimate the variable costs per unit
CO-2;   (materials and direct labor for the equipment) and then allocate a
BL-   portion of fixed costs (overheads like factory rent and   [10]
Analyze   administration).
•   Final Price: The company then adds a markup or target profit to
this total cost base.
•   Suitability: This is effective for ensuring baseline profitability but
may fail to account for how much value the construction firm
actually places on the equipment.
2. Value-Based Pricing
This method sets the price based on the customer’s perception of value
rather than the seller’s cost.
•   Application: Instead of looking at production costs, G&T would
quantify the economic benefits the equipment provides, such as
"project efficiency" (faster completion) and "long-term savings"
(lower energy or maintenance costs).
•   Total Cost Perspective: G&T would position their product as
having a lower total cost of ownership. Even if the purchase price is
higher, the long-term savings make it the more economical choice
for the buyer.
• Augmentation: The firm might include "augmented properties" like
superior technical support or extended warranties to further justify
a premium price based on value.
3. Competition-Based Pricing

secure the contract.
o Negotiated Pricing: In commercial deals, they might match
