Course: Basics of Machine Drawing   Code: 701ME0C008
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme

(Hours   (Hours   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
per week)   per week)   per week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks -100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Graphics and Design
Course Objective
This course is aimed to impart knowledge of machine drawing as a standard language of
communication among engineers and its role in the industry. This course will help students to
understand and draw the fundamentals of intersection of solids, auxiliary views, sectional views and
the various types of technical drawings on any CAD platform.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate the basic conventions of machine drawing, pipe layout and GD & T sheet layout
for mechanical drawings
2. Illustrate the importance of lines/curves of intersection and draw the same when two solids
are interpenetrating
3. Create auxiliary views, sectional views and various types of drawings over a CAD platform
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Conventional symbols of Machine parts   05
Materials – metals, glass, concrete, insulating, Fasteners- forms of threads,
schematic representation of threaded parts, bolts, washers, Riveted joints- Butt
joint, lap joint, single riveted- single strap, double riveted – single strap, double
riveted zig-zag butt joint
2.   Projection and Intersection of Solids   10
Types of Solids: Prism, Cylinder and Cone, Significance of Line/Curve of
Intersection.
Combinations: Two Prisms, Two Cylinders.
Conditions: When Axes are Orthogonal, Intersecting and Bisecting.
3.   Pipe Layout   05
Introduction, Pipe fitting- GI Pipe fittings, CI pipe fitting
4.   Auxiliary & Sectional Views   06
Introduction to Auxiliary planes.(Basic Object auxiliary projection)
Conventions and Rules for Drawing Sectional Views, Types of Sections,
Conversion of Pictorial View into Sectional Orthographic Views (Simple
Machine Parts).
5.   Introduction to Types of Technical Drawings using CAD   04
Orthographic Drawing, Isometric Drawing, Details Drawing, Assembly
Drawing and Production Drawing.
Sheet Layout and GD&T in CAD.
Total   30
