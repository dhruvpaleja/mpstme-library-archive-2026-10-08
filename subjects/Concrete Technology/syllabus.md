This course aims to acquire the knowledge of properties of fresh, hardened and special types of
concrete. It also aims to formulate the mix design of concrete.

After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain properties of concrete
2. Design concrete mix of different grade

4. Identify the requirements of RMC and components of RMC plant
5. Test different properties of fresh and hardened concrete
