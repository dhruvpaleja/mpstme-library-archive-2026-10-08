- Course as printed: Generative AI (code 702AI0C030)

Course: Generative AI   Code: 702AI0C030
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End

per week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Prerequisite: Deep Learning, Advanced Techniques in Machine Learning
Course Objective
This course aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of fundamental concepts, advanced
architectures, and practical applications of Generative Artificial Intelligence. The course emphasizes
both theoretical foundations and hands-on learning, equipping students with skills to build,
evaluate, and deploy generative AI models responsibly.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the foundations of Generative AI including its applications
2. Analyze various Large Language Model (LLM) architectures
3. Apply retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT)
techniques to optimize AI model performance.
4. Develop Generative AI applications with Diffusion and Large Vision Models
5. Evaluate Generative AI models using quantitative metrics.
6. Recognize the practical considerations of Agentic AI

3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: NA
Course Objective.
The aim of the course is to provide students with a thorough understanding of the development and
advancements in generative AI technologies. Students will learn to distinguish between generative and
discriminative AI methods, explore their applications across diverse industries, and critically assess the
potential and challenges associated with these technologies.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Describe the evolution of generative AI
2. Differentiate between generative and discriminative AI techniques
3. Evaluate the applications of generative AI across various fields
4. Analyze the need for responsible AI practices in generative AI
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Introduction to Generative AI
Overview of Generative AI: Key concepts, probability and statistics basics,
generative modeling techniques, and data handling, Ethical Considerations in
1.   Generative AI, Historical Evolution, Mathematical Foundations: Probability   08
theory, linear algebra, optimization, information theory, calculus, Tools and
Software: TensorFlow, Keras, PyTorch, JAX, cloud services, GPU, open-source
libraries.
Deep Dive into Generative Models
Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs): Fundamentals, advanced architecture,
training and stabilization techniques, Variational Autoencoders (VAEs): VAE
2.   basics, implementation, advanced techniques, optimization, and applications,   10
Advanced Generative Models: Diffusion models, interactive and conditional
models, Optimization: Architectural improvements, advanced training, loss
function optimization, parameter tuning, scalability, and efficiency.
Advanced Topics and Techniques
Advanced GAN Techniques: Progressive GANs, Conditional GANs, StyleGAN,
video generation, adversarial defense, Transformer Neural Networks: Transformer
architecture, attention mechanisms, advanced models (BERT, GPT), multihead and
3.   12
self-attention, Large Language Models (LLMs): LLM architecture, training,
applications, challenges, Generative AI Hallucinations: Understanding, detecting,
and mitigating hallucinations, Ethics of Generative AI: Bias, fairness, privacy, and
ethical concerns.
