Pre-requisite: Applications of Machine Learning, Computer Vision and Applications
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to make the students understand the use of Neutral networks in deep learning.
It will provide an understanding of applying CNNs, RNNs, LSTM and other fundamental concepts to
analyze and understand patterns and trends within the data
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Explain the major trends driving the rise of deep learning, and understand where and how it is
applied today. (L2 – Understanding)
2. Analyze the key computations underlying deep learning, and apply them to synthesize and train
deep neural networks. (L4 – Analyzing)
3. Improve deep neural networks using techniques such as hyperparameter tuning, regularization,
and optimization. (L5 – Evaluating)
4. Build successful machine learning projects and apply end-to-end learning, transfer learning, and
multi-task learning. (L5 – Evaluating)
5. Create projects using convolutional neural networks for image data and recurrent neural
networks for natural language, audio, and other sequence data. (L6 – Creating)
