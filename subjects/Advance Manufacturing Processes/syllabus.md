Pre-requisite: Manufacturing Processes-I and II
Course Objective
This course introduces fundamentals of different manufacturing processes like advanced casting,
welding and forming. The enhancement of surface properties of products, application of modern
joining processes and advanced machining processes are elucidated in this course.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify the requirement of appropriate advanced manufacturing processes.
2. Classify and explain the various advanced manufacturing processes.
3. Select appropriate advanced material process based on design of component.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Surface Performance Enhancement   06
Scope, cleaners, Methods of cleaning, Surface coating types, electro
forming, physical vapor deposition, chemical vapor deposition, thermal
spraying, ion implantation, diffusion coating, diamond coating and
cladding.
2.   Advanced Casting and Forming Processes   10
Need and classification of advanced casting process, metal mould casting;
stir casting, squeeze casting, vacuum mould casting, evaporative pattern
casting, ceramic shell casting.
Classification of various advanced forming processes, high energy rate
forming process, electro-magnetic forming (EMF), explosive forming,
electro-hydraulic forming (EHF), stretch forming, contour roll forming.
3.   Development in Joining Processes   06
Electron beam welding (EBW), laser beam welding (LBW), ultrasonic
welding (USW), friction Stir Welding (FSW), under water friction stir
welding (UWFSW), joining of dis-similar metals, adhesive bonding and its
applications
