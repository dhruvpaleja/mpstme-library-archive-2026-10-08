Mother Tongue-Based Education under NEP 2020
The National Education Policy 2020 introduced a major reform by recommending the mother
tongue or local language as the medium of instruction in early education, especially up to
Grade 5. This policy aims to make education more holistic, inclusive, and effective in India’s
multilingual society.
In many Indian schools, especially in rural and tribal areas, children were taught in English or
Hindi, which were not their first languages. This created learning difficulties, low confidence,
poor academic performance, and higher dropout rates. Students often felt disconnected from
their culture and learning environment.
NEP 2020 promoted mother tongue-based education, along with the three-language formula
(mother tongue, Hindi, and English). Teachers were trained to teach in local languages, and
efforts were made to develop textbooks and digital learning materials in regional languages.
The goal was to improve comprehension, preserve cultural identity, and ensure inclusive
education. Mother tongue education helped students understand concepts more easily,
improved confidence, and enhanced creativity and critical thinking. It also strengthened
cultural identity, emotional stability, and social responsibility. This approach reduced
educational disparities, especially among rural, tribal, and marginalized students, and promoted
equitable access to education.

--- page 3 ---
The policy faced challenges such as shortage of trained teachers, lack of teaching materials in
local languages, and parental preference for English-medium education. Developing resources
for many languages was also complex and costly. Mother tongue-based education under NEP
2020 has the potential to improve learning outcomes, preserve linguistic diversity, and promote
inclusive and holistic development. With proper implementation, teacher training, and
awareness, it can transform India’s education system and create confident, culturally rooted,
and globally competent learners

reflects Mahatma Gandhi’s philosophy of Basic Education.
