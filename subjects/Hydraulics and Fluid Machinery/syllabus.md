Pre-requisite: Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulics
Course Objective
This course focuses on the analysis of water distribution system and prismatic channels. This course
includes the dimensional analysis and model analysis. It also aims at imparting the knowledge of
impact of jet on plates and working principles of pumps and turbines.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Analyze the flow network and three reservoir problems
2. Use dimensional analysis to derive relationships between physical quantities, and identify
dimensionless quantities
3. Compute the flow through open channels
4. Experiment with the impact of jet on stationary and moving plates
5. Demonstrate the working of hydraulic machines like pumps and turbines
