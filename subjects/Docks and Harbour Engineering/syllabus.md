Pre-requisite: Soil Mechanics, Transportation Engineering
Course Objective
The objective of this course is to illustrate the various types of harbors, their components and the impact
of tides, and waves on harbor design. It also covers the importance of soil survey procedure involving
various dredging equipment, marine structures, and marine surveys.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Illustrate the different types of harbors and their components
2. Explain the impact of tides and waves on harbor design
3. Distinguish between the different types of breakwaters, docks, and berthing structures
4. Describe the different dredging equipment, marine structures, and marine surveys

Course Objective
The objective of this course is to illustrate the various types of harbors, their components and the impact
of tides, and waves on harbor design. It also covers the importance of soil survey procedure involving
various dredging equipment, marine structures, and marine surveys.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Illustrate the different types of harbors and their components
2. Explain the impact of tides and waves on harbor design
3. Distinguish between the different types of breakwaters, docks, and berthing structures
4. Describe the different dredging equipment, marine structures, and marine surveys
