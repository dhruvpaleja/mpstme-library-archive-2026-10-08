Pre-requisite: Basic Engineering Mathematics, Control System Engineering.
Course Objective
The primary purpose of this course is to impart knowledge about industrial robots for their control and
design. It also helps to develop an automated system using sensors, hydraulics, pneumatics and
programmable logic controllers.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Classify various robots using direct and inverse kinematic analysis
2. Make use of image processing algorithms for representation of robotic tool movement
3. Explain various sensors, actuators, and hydraulic, pneumatic and PLC components used in
automation
4. Design automated systems for simple tasks

Pre-requisite: Basic Engineering Mathematics, Automation and Control.
Objectives:
1. To impart knowledge about industrial robots for their control and design.
2. To understand and analyse the various applications of robots.
3. To provide knowledge to learn essential concepts behind control system
elements and operations.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Perform kinematic and dynamic analyses with simulation.
2. Understand image representation for robotic movement.
3. Understand usage of various controllers, sensors and actuators in the system.
4. Learn and apply essential concepts behind control system elements and
operations in hydraulics automation.
