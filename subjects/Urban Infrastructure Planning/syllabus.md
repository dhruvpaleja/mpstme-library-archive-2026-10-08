3   0   1   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: NIL
Objectives:
1. To introduce concepts related Urban Infrastructure Planning & Management.
2. To create the student’s ability to articulate between urban development and infrastructure
provision.
3. To give an experience in the implementation of engineering concepts in field of Infrastructure
Engineering.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Address urban infrastructure challenges.
2. Do short and long range planning alternative infrastructure systems while designing for present
and future cities and regions.
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Planning for Urban Infrastructure:   03
Urban Infrastructure , Role of Planner in provision of urban networks and services,
feasibility studies for infrastructure projects, Planning for major infrastructure
projects, Various Infrastructure Programs and policies, PPP in infrastructure
projects
2   Water Supply:   08
quality and quantity requirements; sources; collection and conveyance of water;
treatment methods; treatment plant location; planning distribution systems and
their zoning with respect to urban structure.
3   Sewage &Sanitation:   08
Collection, transportation and treatment of sewage, Different methods of sewage
treatments.
Biological/ Environmental/ Cultural concepts in environmental sanitation, low cost
sanitation technologies and concepts as related to Indian context.
4   1. Electricity & Fire services:   08
Planning for fire protection services and space standards, Planning for
electrification, general scenario, services and space standards of Transformers
space standards for electricity networks, space standards for burial around
cemetery etc.
2. Urban Energy systems:
Energy Management, energy requirement, non conventional energy systems,
management of solar energy, wind energy, tidal energy, biomass energy, energy
from waste.
5   Solid waste management:   08
Planning for solid waste; Types of solid waste-organic & inorganic; Solid waste

6   Urban Social infrastructure:   03
qualitative and quantitative techniques of assessing requirements, planning

7   Urban Transportation:   07
Introduction: Scope of urban transport planning, interdependency of land use and
traffic system, traffic system approach to urban transportation.
Stages in Urban Transportation: Trip generation, trip production, trip distribution,
modal split and trip assignment.
Urban Transportation surveys: Definition of study area, zoning, types of surveys,
Forecasting traffic in relation planned land use. Principles of planning and design
of road network and engineering classification of roads, intersections and
elevated roads.
Land Use Transport models: Lowry model, Garin-Lowry Model, applications in
India.
Public Transportation modes: Systems in India, problems and prospects, present
practices in urban transportation. Metro, mono, and high capacity buses. System
selection.
Intersection Design: Elements of intersection maneuver area, types of
intersections, flyover, grade separators, subways, underpass, and suitability of
