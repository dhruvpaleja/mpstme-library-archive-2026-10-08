Prerequisite: Engineering Mechanics
Course Objective
The course is aimed at imparting knowledge of the deformable bodies subjected to different types
of loads. It is designed to lay the foundation for the detailed understanding of structural behavior
under loading. This course will help students to determine the failure criteria of a body subjected
to various stresses. Students will be able to identify the different tests to be performed on the
materials.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to -
1. Understand the concepts of Stress, Strain and interpret the stress strain curve
2. Construct Shear force and bending moment diagrams for statically determinate beams
with different loading conditions.
3. Analyse effects of different cross-sectional shapes by plotting bending stress distribution
4. Apply the transverse shear stress concept to analyze its distribution across cross section of
the beam
5. Determine slope and deflection in beams.
6. Apply concepts of factors of safety, elastic constants, and their interrelations to solve
engineering problems.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1.   Stress and Strain: Definitions and problems based on the concepts of –   11
Stress – Strain Curves, modulus of elasticity, modulus of rigidity, bulk
modulus, yield stress, ultimate stress, factor of safety, shear stress, Poisson’s
ratio, Elongation under self-weight, elastic constants, and their relationships
2.   Shear Force and Bending Moment   11
Types of beams, supports and loadings. Concepts of bending moment and
shear force and their Sign conventions, Axial force, Shear force and bending
moment diagrams for statically determinate beams subjected to point loads,
uniformly distributed loads, couple and their combinations, significance of
point of zero shear. Problems based on Simply Supported Beam only.

4.   Shear Stress in Beams   07
