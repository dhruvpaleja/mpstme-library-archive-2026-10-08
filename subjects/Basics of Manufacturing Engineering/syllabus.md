Pre-requisite: Mechanical Workshop
Course Objective
The course is aimed at emphasizing the importance of manufacturing science in the day-to-day life,
and to understand the basic manufacturing processes such as casting, welding, forging, rolling,
extrusion, and drawing. The course is delineated particularly to understand industrial applications
of various processes and equipment used in manufacturing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, student will be able to -
1. Describe different metal casting processes and forming processes along with defects associated
with them
2. Recognize various joining processes
3. Identify proper manufacturing processes meeting the requirements to the solution of
manufacturing problems
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Manufacturing Processes   02
Introduction, definition, classification of manufacturing processes.
2.   Sand Casting   09
Introduction, use of patterns, pattern materials, single pattern, split pattern,
loose-piece pattern, sweep pattern, moulding sand composition, concept of
cope, drag, runner, riser; Cores - core sands, core prints; Melting of cast
iron, charging a furnace, melting and pouring both ferrous and non-ferrous
metals, degasification, casting defects and inspection.
3.   Rolling Processes   04
Introduction to Hot working and cold working of metals.
Rolling
Elementary theory of rolling, principle, classification, Advantages,
Limitations and applications.
4.   Wire Drawing and Extrusion Processes   05
Wire Drawing

Extrusion
Principle, Classification, Smith forging operations, Advantages, Limitations
and applications.
5.   Welding Processes   10
