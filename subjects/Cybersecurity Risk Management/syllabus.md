Pre-requisite: Fundamental Knowledge of Cybersecurity
Course Objective
Cybersecurity risks have critical impacts on overall business. The primary objective of this course
to provide an overview of risk management process to the students. Students will understand both
qualitative and quantitative risk assessment techniques and various risk management frameworks.
Understanding these frameworks will help students articulate cybersecurity risk as the business
consequences.
Course Outcomes
After successful completion of the course, student would be able to -
1. Explain various cybersecurity risk management framework and methodologies
2. Identify and model cybersecurity risks using various qualitative and quantitative methods
3. Develop risk management plan, business continuity plan and disaster recovery plan
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Risk Management Fundamentals   3
Introduction, threats, vulnerabilities, and impact, risk management lifecycle,
Risk identification techniques, Risk management techniques
2.   Risk Assessment   4
Introduction, critical components of risk assessments, types of risk
assessments (qualitative and quantitative), identification and evaluation of
threats, vulnerabilities and countermeasures
3.   Risk Management Planning   6
Objectives and scope of risk management plan, assigning responsibilities,
reporting requirements, plan of action and milestones, understanding and
assessing the impact of legal and compliance issues, prioritizing risk elements,
performing cost benefit analysis
4.   Business impact analysis   4
Introduction, Objectives and scope of BIA, steps of BIA process
5.   Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster recovery plan(DRP)   4
Need for BCP and DRP, Elements of BCP, elements of DRP, Best practices for
DCP and DRP
6.   Risk Management frameworks   6
NIST Risk Management Framework, CERT OCTAVE, ISO 27000 series, FAIR
approach
7.   Risk, Resilience and Decision Making   3
