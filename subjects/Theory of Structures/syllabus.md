Pre-requisite: Strength of Materials; Engineering Mechanics
Course Objective
This course explains the concept of influence lines and plastic analysis. It also aims to acquire
knowledge of analysis for determinate and indeterminate structures and deflections of structures.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Apply knowledge of general theorems related to elastic structures
2. Examine the deflections of statically determinate structures
3. Construct influence lines diagrams for determinate beams
4. Analyze indeterminate structures by flexibility and stiffness method
5. Analyze column for various end conditions
6. Analyze beams using plastic theory
