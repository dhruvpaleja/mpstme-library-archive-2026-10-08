b. Types of Launching Techniques
1. Incremental Launching Method
Description
In this method, the bridge deck is constructed in segments behind one abutment
and pushed forward gradually into its final position.
Procedure
1. Concrete or steel deck segments are constructed at one end of the bridge.
2. Hydraulic jacks push the completed segments forward.
3. Additional segments are added and pushed until the deck reaches the
opposite side.
Advantages
• Requires minimal scaffolding
•   Suitable for long bridges
• Safe and efficient method
Applications
• Highway bridges
• Railway bridges
2. Cantilever Launching Method
Description
In this method, the deck is constructed outward from the bridge pier in balanced
sections.
CO-1   Procedure
1. Deck segments are attached to the pier.   [10]
2. Segments are added on both sides of the pier to maintain balance.
3. The process continues until spans meet.
Advantages
•   Suitable for deep valleys and rivers
• No need for ground support
Applications
• Long span bridges
• Mountain bridges
3. Crane Lifting Method
Description
Heavy deck segments are lifted and placed into position using large cranes.
Procedure
1. Deck segments are transported to the site.
2. Cranes lift the segments to the required height.
3. Segments are placed on bridge piers and connected.
Advantages
• Quick installation
•   Suitable for medium span bridges
Disadvantages
• Requires large cranes
• Limited lifting capacity
4. Launching Girder Method

Description
A launching girder is a special steel structure used to lift and place bridge
segments between piers.
Procedure
1. Launching girder is installed on bridge piers.
2. Precast segments are lifted and placed between piers.
3. Girder moves forward to the next span.
