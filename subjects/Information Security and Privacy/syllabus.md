Course Objective
The course aims to provide students with a fundamental understanding of information security principles,
cryptographic techniques, firewall and data privacy. Students will learn to identify security attacks, apply

PCI DSS compliance. The course will equip students with essential knowledge and skills for securing data

1. Explain the fundamental principles of information security(K2)

data(K3)

Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objective
To give an introduction in the field of information and computer network security. Understanding the
working of Encryption standards and learn basic key exchange algorithm. Describe the need and
importance of data privacy for organizations.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the fundamental principles of security including confidentiality, authentication,
integrity, and non-repudiation, and describe basic network security models. (L2 –
Understand)
2. Apply symmetric and asymmetric cipher techniques such as DES, AES, Diffie-Hellman,
and RSA to ensure secure communication. (L3 – Apply)
3. Analyze the functions of OSI model layers, intrusion detection systems (IDS), firewall
configurations, cookies management, and digital certificate verification. (L4 – Analyze)
4. Evaluate the importance of data privacy and security compliance, including good
documentation practices and the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI
DSS). (L5 – Evaluate)
