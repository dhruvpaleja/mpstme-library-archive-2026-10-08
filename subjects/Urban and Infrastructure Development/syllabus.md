3   0   0   3   50   50
Objectives:
 To introduce economic reforms and infrastructure development
 To get acquainted with the formulation and approval of various
infrastructure projects and financing issues in infrastructure development
 To explain the urban and rural development and town planning
 To introduce the urbanization of cities, suburbs, region and Hinterland
Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, students should be able to
 Describe Economic Reforms and Infrastructure Development
   Illustrate the Formulation and Approval of Various Infrastructure Projects
and Financing Issues in Infrastructure Development
 Differentiate between Urban and Rural Development and Town Planning
 Discuss urbanization of cities, suburbs, region and Hinterland

Unit   Description   Duration

Development:
Performance of the States: a review, growth performance,

level, plan expenditure, human resource and quality of
infrastructure.
Strategy for slow growing states: Development of economic and
social infrastructure, the problem of state finances, the policy
environment and governance, the   role of the Central
Government,
2.   Sectoral Infrastructure Development:   03
Overview, liberalization of FDI regulations, Introduction of
Public Private Partnerships (PPP), Sector Specific Opportunities

3.   Formulation and Approval of Various Infrastructure Projects   05
based on project cost:
Institutional Structure, Applicability, Project Identification, Inter-
ministerial Consultations, ‘In Principle’ Approval of PPPAC,
Expression of Interest, Formulation of Project Documents,
Appraisal/Approval of PPPAC, Invitation of Bids, Time Frame,
Exemption from the above Procedure
4.   Financing Issues in Infrastructure, Projects Management and   05
Development:
The IIPDF and its role, Background of the IIPDF, The purpose of
the IIPDF, IIPDF funding sources, The IIPDF’s organisational
structure,   Disbursements   by   the   IIPDF,   Operational
management, Funding from IIPDF, Evaluation Procedures and
Timeframe, Monitoring, Recovery of Project Development
Funding with Returns, Risk management
5.   Introduction to Urban Development:   05
Urbanization in India with reference to growth of cities, pattern,
growth its trends, causes and prospects of urbanization, cost of
urbanization, growth of urban population, resources of urban
development, spatial pattern of urbanization, problems of
metropolitan cities in India policy issues and their need, trends
and projections for urbanization.
6.   Integrated Rural and Urban Development:   03
Policy background, Principal rural urban socio-economic
interactions, factors contributing to rural-urban interaction,
effects of economic liberalization policies, recommendations.
7.   Urbanization of Cities, Suburbs, Region and Hinterland:   03
Plans for cities and suburban areas, metropolitan regions, role of
peripheral region in urban development, concept of hinterland,
small towns, tributaries/twin cities of metropolitan cities.
8.   Town Planning:   06
Review of planning legislation and acts relating to urban and
regional planning. Building bye laws planning agencies and their
functions, D.C. rules or city. Concept of FSI, fungible FSI and
TDR. FSI in few important cities across Indian region and around
the World. Norms for FSI utilization for different kind of
buildings like residential, commercial, education and hospitals.
Redevelopment of building in cities. Reforms in D.C rules by U.D
department of state government and municipal authorities.
9.   Notifications :   03
such as CRZ coastal and marine areas, Coastal management
