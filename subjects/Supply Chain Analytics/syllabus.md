Program: B Tech, MBA Tech & B Tech Integrated Data Science   Semester: VI/ X
Course: Supply chain and Analytics   Code: 702DB0E012
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Python for Data Analytics, Optimisation Methods, Foundations of Machine Learning
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to familiarize students with current supply chain management trends and
apply them in practice using latest analytical tools and demonstrate the problem solving ability to
provide solutions. It will also educate students on improving operational efficiency and effectiveness
by enabling data-driven decisions at strategic, operational and tactical levels
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the fundamental concepts of supply chain management including forecasting,
network design, inventory types, and supply chain integration.
2. Apply prescriptive analytics techniques such as optimization models, facility location, and
scheduling to improve supply chain decision-making.
3. Analyze the use of supervised machine learning techniques such as regression, decision trees,
random forests, and neural networks in solving supply chain problems.
4. Evaluate unsupervised learning algorithms such as clustering and association rule mining for

Course Objective
This course provides the understanding of key drivers of supply chain performance and their inter-
relationship with strategy. This course also introduces design issues and helps students understand
how information technology is used in supply chain optimization.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Describe the functions of supply chain network and its process
2. Evaluate the various model of supply chain management
3. Illustrate complexity involved in supply chain analytics
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Strategic Framework, Supply Chain Network and Ware house management   10
Introduction of supply chain analytics, supply chain stages, types of supply chain-
horizontal and vertical, supply chain flows, process view of supply chain - cycle
view and push/pull view. Role of distribution in supply chain network, factors
involved in designing the distribution network, design options for a distribution
network, factors influencing network design decisions. Concept of strategic
storage, types of warehouses, warehouse functionality, warehouse operating
principles, material handling and packaging in warehouse.
2.   Modelling in Supply Chain Management   06
Introduction, analytics framework based on SCOR model, supplier selection
analytics, analytical hierarchy processing method, technique of order preference
by similarity to ideal solution method (TOPSIS), VIKOR method.
3.   Supply chain Analytics   08
Demand forecasting in supply chain, Bullwhip effect and time series analysis,
exponential smoothening method of forecasting, measures of forecasting errors,
inventory management, safety stock and reorder level, Economic Order Quality
(EOQ), multi echelon inventory management, optimum level of product
availability in supply chain, predictive modeling in forecasting in supply chain.
4.   IT in Supply Chain   06
IT framework, Customer Relationship Management (CRM), Internal supply chain
management, Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) and transaction
management, RFID systems, Lean supply chain, Reverse logistics, Green supply
chain.
