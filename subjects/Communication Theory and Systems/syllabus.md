Pre-requisite: Signals and Systems, Probability and Stochastic Processes
Course Objectives
The aim of this course is to provide knowledge of the basic elements of various types of
Analog & digital modulation and demodulation techniques. This course also makes student
acquainted with different types of coding, decoding and spread spectrum techniques.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the principles and concepts of different analog & digital modulation techniques.
2. Implement different base band shaping techniques for data transmission and detection.
3. Analyze different algorithms for source and error control coding.
4. Describe the concepts and applications of spread spectrum modulation.
