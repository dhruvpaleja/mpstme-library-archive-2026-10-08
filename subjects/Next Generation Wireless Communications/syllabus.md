Program: B Tech / MBA Tech (EXTC)   Semester: VII/VIII
Course: Next Generation Wireless Communications   Code: 702EX0E011
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours   (Hours   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
per week)   per week)   per week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks -100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Wireless Communication Technology
Course Objective
This course aims to introduce the basic concepts of Next Generation Wireless Systems such as 6G and
beyond, massive MIMO techniques and their limitations. It also helps to provide knowledge about
Terahertz Communications and its antenna design to predict performance.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the evolution of next generation wireless systems
2. Describe massive MIMO techniques and its multi antenna systems
3. Elaborate on Terahertz Transceiver devices and Channel Modelling
4. Design and simulate Terahertz Antennas
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Technology evolution of wireless communications
Introduction, Historical Background and Evolution of Wireless Systems,
Application Scenarios of Next Generation Wireless Systems, Need for 6G and   05
Beyond, Enabling Technologies of Next Generation Wireless Systems.
2.   Massive Multiple Input Multiple Outputs (MIMO) Techniques Introduction,
MIMO techniques, Multi-User MIMO Maturing, Massive MIMO, Limiting
Factors of Massive MIMO, MIMO implementations, Radio Propagation and   05
Orthogonality of Channel Responses.
3.   Multi antenna Systems - Large-Scale MIMO and Massive MIMO
Introduction, Massive MIMO Uplink and Downlink, Spectral Efficiency (SE),
Area Throughput, Precoding, Single-Cell (SC) Precoding Techniques, Precoding   08
Techniques for the Downlink of Multicell Scenario, Hybrid Precoding, Massive
MIMO with Linear Precoding and Detection, Energy Efficiency (EE).
4.   Terahertz Transceiver and Devices
Terahertz communications with resonant tunneling diodes: Status and
Perspectives Characterization of Emitters and Detectors.   06
Terahertz Channel Characteristics and Modelling
Fundamentals of interference modelling by stochastic geometry in THz
networks, Terahertz communication channel characteristics and measurements,
An overview of the Terahertz communication networks and Line-of-Sight (LOS)
and Non-Line-of-Sight (NLOS) Propagation Techniques.
5.   Terahertz Antenna Design
Advancements in Terahertz Antenna Design and Their Performance, Antenna
Misalignment and Blockage in THz Communications, Hybrid Beamforming in   06
