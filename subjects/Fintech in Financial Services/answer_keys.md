disrupt conventional financial services provided by banks, insurance companies, and other
financial institutions. Instead of relying solely on physical branches and manual processes,

solutions.

Suppose an investor downloads a robo-advisory app such as Groww or INDmoney.
Step-by-Step Process
1. Download and Register
The user downloads the app and signs up using a mobile number or email ID.
Complete KYC verification (PAN, Aadhaar, bank details).

The app asks questions related to:
§ Income level
§ Investment goals
§ Risk tolerance (low, moderate, high)
§ Investment time horizon
3.   Goal Selection
The user selects financial goals such as:
§ Retirement planning
§ Child education
§ Wealth creation
§ Buying a house

Based on the inputs, the robo-advisor suggests a diversified portfolio, for example:
§ 60% Equity Mutual Funds
§ 30% Debt Funds
§ 10% Gold ETFs
5. Review and Confirmation
The investor reviews expected returns, risk level, and allocation details before confirming.
6.   Investment Execution
The app automatically invests the funds in recommended schemes.
7.   Monitoring and Rebalancing
The robo-advisor tracks performance and periodically rebalances the portfolio to maintain the
target asset allocation.
