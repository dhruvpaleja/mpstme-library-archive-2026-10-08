Afterpay – Popularizing Buy Now, Pay Later
Background
Founded in 2014, Afterpay has become a leading figure in the “Buy Now, Pay Later” (BNPL)
payment industry. This model allows consumers to purchase goods immediately and pay them
in instalments without the initial financial burden.
Key Challenges
1. Consumer Credit Behaviour: Changing traditional credit and buying behaviours, where
immediate payment or standard credit lines were the norm.
2. Retail Integration: Convincing retailers to adopt a BNPL payment option, which may
involve financial and operational adjustments.
3. Risk Management: Managing the credit risk of allowing consumers to delay payments.
Strategies Implemented
1. Seamless Integration: Ensures easy integration of its services with online retailers,
facilitating a smooth checkout process.
2. Transparent Terms: Provides clear, straightforward terms with no hidden fees, appealing to a
consumer base wary of traditional credit.
3. Market Education: Educates the market on the benefits and responsibilities of BNPL to
foster responsible use.
Results Achieved
1. Retail Adoption: Partnered with thousands of retailers worldwide, significantly increasing its
market presence.
2. Consumer Acceptance: Gained a substantial consumer base who appreciate the flexibility
and simplicity of the service.
3. Market Influence: Influenced the global retail and financial sectors, with many competitors
and traditional financial institutions introducing similar offerings.
Answer the following: -
1. Analyse how Afterpay’s Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) model changed consumer purchasing
behaviour and influenced the retail industry. (3 marks)

2. Evaluate the risk management and transparency strategies adopted by Afterpay to ensure
sustainable growth. (3 marks)
