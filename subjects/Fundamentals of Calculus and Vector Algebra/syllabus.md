Prerequisite: Differentiation, Integration, Scalar and Vector product
Objectives:
The objective of this course is to familiarize students with the principles of basic mathematics as
well as to develop an analytical and systematic approach towards solving problems and learn to
apply the concepts in related technology problems.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students will be able to
1. Recognize series to obtain solutions for finite and infinite series expansion,
2. Apply vectors to solve related application problems,
3. Find area and volume by using integration techniques,
4. Use derivatives to solve problems and identify ways to express partial derivatives.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Series
Arithmetic Progression: Definition, nth term, arithmetic mean.
Geometric progression: Definition, nth term, sum of first terms, sum to   8
infinity of a G.P, geometric mean.
Special series: Introduction, exponential series, logarithmic series.
2.   Vector Algebra
Scalar product of two vectors: Geometrical meaning, work done as a scalar
product.
Vector product of two vectors: Geometrical meaning, moment of a force.   10
Product of three and four vectors: Geometrical interpretation, scalar product of
three vectors, vector triple product, scalar and vector product of four vectors,
coplanarity of three vectors, volume of parallelopiped and volume of
tetrahedron.
3.   Integral Calculus
Integration: Method of substitution, integration by partial fractions, special
integrals.   10
Definite integrals: Evaluation of definite integral, theorems on definite integrals,
reduction formulae for sin n x and cos n x (only results).

Applications of integration: Area between two curves, volume of solid of
revolution.
4.   Differential Calculus
Differentiation: Differentiation of composite functions, implicit functions,
inverse trigonometric functions, successive differentiation up to second order.
Homogeneous differential equations: First order first degree ordinary   10
differential equations reducible to homogeneous form.
Applications: Rate of change of quantities, tangents and normal, applications of
first order first degree differential equations – population growth, radioactive
decay, LCR circuit, Newton’s law of cooling.
5.   Partial Differentiation
Partial Differentiation: Introduction, partial derivatives, successive partial   7
derivatives, Euler's theorem on homogeneous functions, partial differentiation
of implicit functions, total differentiation.
Total   45
