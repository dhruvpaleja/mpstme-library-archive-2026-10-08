Pre-requisite: Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulics
Course Objective
This course covers groundwater hydrology and well hydraulics for understanding the
groundwater processes. It also focuses on groundwater modelling for flow and contaminant
transport.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate the occurrence, distribution and characteristics of sub-surface water
2. Analyse groundwater flow-transport phenomenon for groundwater modelling
3. Appraise remedial measures for groundwater contamination

Pre-requisite: Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulics
Course Objective
This course covers groundwater hydrology and well hydraulics for understanding the groundwater
processes. It also focuses on groundwater modelling for flow and contaminant transport.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate the occurrence, distribution and characteristics of sub-surface water
2. Analyse groundwater flow-transport phenomenon for groundwater modelling
3. Appraise remedial measures for groundwater contamination
