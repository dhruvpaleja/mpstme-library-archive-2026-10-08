Course Objective

4. To apply cryptographic algorithms to generate digital signature

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Apply classical encryption techniques to protect data.
2. Use different standards of private key encryption.
3. Apply the knowledge of number theory for public key encryption.
4. Use techniques to achieve message authentication.
