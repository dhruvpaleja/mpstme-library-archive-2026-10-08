Course Objective
The objective of this course is to provide a comprehensive understanding of microprocessors and
microcontrollers, covering their evolution, architecture, and applications in embedded systems.
Students will analyze various instruction sets, memory architectures, and data handling techniques
while developing proficiency in programming microcontrollers using assembly and embedded C.
The course explores peripheral interfacing, real-time operating systems, security mechanisms, and
power optimization techniques. Additionally, students will evaluate emerging trends such as AI-
driven embedded systems, RISC-V open-source processors, and IoT applications, equipping them
with the knowledge required for modern embedded system design and development.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the students will be able to -
1. Explain the architecture, working principles, and classification of microprocessors and
microcontrollers.
2. Analyze instruction sets, addressing modes, and memory architectures of 8086, ARM Cortex-
M, and RISC-V processors.
3. Develop assembly and embedded C programs for microcontrollers in real-time applications.
4. Design and implement interfacing solutions for peripheral devices and communication
protocols.
5. Evaluate embedded system security, low-power designs, and real-time operating systems
(RTOS).
6. Investigate emerging technologies in AI-driven embedded systems, IoT, and open-source
processors (RISC-V).
