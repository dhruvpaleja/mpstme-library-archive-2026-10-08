Pre-requisite: Basics of Excel
Course Objective
Learn foundations of analytics and how it is becoming a competitive strategy for many
organizations. Understand different types of analytical models such as descriptive analytics,
predictive analytics, and prescriptive analytics. Understand the basic concepts in descriptive
analytics and how it is used in data-driven decision making. Learn data visualization and various
types of visual charts. Learn fundamentals of regression, clustering and its importance in predictive
analytics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify data required to solve business problem
2. Apply appropriate model for business problem
3. Interpret results obtained from built model
4. Provide visualization of results
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Introduction to Business Analytics   04
Why Analytics, Business Analytics: The Science of Data-Driven Decision-
Making Business Context, Technology & Data Science, Categories and
characteristics of management science modeling techniques: Descriptive,
Predictive Analytics, Prescriptive Analytics, Framework for Data-Driven
Decision Making, Challenges in Data-Driven Decision Making and Future.
2   Descriptive Analytics   04
Introduction to Descriptive Analytics, Data Types and Scales, Types of Data
Measurement Scales, Population and Sample, Measures of Central Tendency,
Percentile, Decile, and Quartile, Measures of Variation, Measures of Shape, Data
Visualization.
3   Introduction to Probability   04
Probability Theory – Terminology Fundamental Concepts in Probability –
Axioms of Probability, Application of Simple Probability Rules – Association
Rule Learning, Bayes’ Theorem.
4   Sampling and Estimation   04
Population Parameters and Sample Statistic, Sampling, Probabilistic Sampling,
Sampling Distribution, Central Limit Theorem, Sample Size Estimation for
Mean of the Population, Estimation of Population Parameters, Introduction to
Confidence interval.

Galton’s Regression Model, Simple Linear Regression Model Building,
Estimation of Parameters Using Ordinary Least Squares, Interpretation of
Simple Linear Regression Coefficients, Multiple Linear Regression, Logistic

Distance and Dissimilarity Measures used in Clustering, Partitioning based
