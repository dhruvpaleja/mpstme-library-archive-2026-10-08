Pre-requisite: Basic Electrical Engineering, Engineering Mechanics and Strength of Materials
Course Objective
This course presents an introduction to wind energy and wind turbines. It introduces the current
challenges in modern wind turbine analysis, design, and development and provides a comprehensive
examination of state-of-the-art technologies.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Categorize various types of wind turbines and its principal components
2. Determine the mechanical power, types of stresses and the control parameters
3. Analyze the economics, effects on the environment, safety in operation and the potential hazards
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   10
Importance of renewable, technical issues, wind farm development, wind turbine
siting, new and future technologies, energy in a moving object and moving fluid,
power in the wind, power absorption by a turbine, wind speed variation, force
from wind, aerodynamic force, lift and drag coefficients, airfoils.
2.   Types and Components of Wind Turbine   10
Turbine classification, propeller wind turbine, The H-rotor, Darrieus turbine,
Savonius rotor, mechanical components - tower, foundation, rotor, nacelle,
gearbox, turbine brake, electrical components - generators, transformers and
anemometer.
3.   Propeller   05
Mechanical power, diagrams, mechanical power in a turbine, turbine power
variation with angular speed, daily energy production by a turbine, annual energy
production.
4.   Wind Turbine Design   10
Power accord with the wind speed, wind speed measurement, power calculations,
output power quality and requirement, blade pitch control, turbine operating
power curve, yaw control, parking brake, fault control, various operational states
of a turbine, loads on the rotor, aerodynamic loads on the blade, other blade loads,
vibrations, blade fatigue, blade construction.
