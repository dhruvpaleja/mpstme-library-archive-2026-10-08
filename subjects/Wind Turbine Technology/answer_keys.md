Q1   Answer Briefly:   [4*5
=20]
CO1   BL-1   a   (2 Marks) Definition: Conversion of wind's kinetic energy into useful
mechanical or electrical energy.
(2 Marks) Importance: Renewable, clean, reduces dependence on fossil fuels,
contributes to energy security, and reduces CO2 emissions.
CO2   BL-1   b   1 Mark per component pair (Component + Function)
(e.g., Rotor/Blades: Capture wind energy; Nacelle: Houses drivetrain/controls;
Generator: Converts mechanical to electrical energy; Tower: Supports the
structure at height).
CO3   BL-2   c   (2 Marks) Explanation: System to adjust the angle of the blades relative to the
wind.
(2 Marks) Purpose: Regulate rotor speed and power output, especially in high
winds, and to feather the blades for shutdown/braking.
CO5   BL-2   d   1 Mark per hazard
(e.g., Working at height (falls); Blade/Component failure (falling debris);
Electrical hazards (high voltage in nacelle); Fire (due to electrical faults or oil
leaks); High noise levels).
CO4   BL-2   e   (2 Marks) Lift: The primary force (perpendicular to relative wind) that drives
the rotor and generates power.
(2 Marks) Drag: The resistive force (parallel to relative wind) that hinders
rotation; minimized for efficiency but used in stall control.

Q2   CO1   BL-2   a   (5 Marks) Site Factors: Wind resource assessment (speed, turbulence), proximity   10
to grid, topography/soil conditions, environmental constraints (noise, wildlife),
accessibility, and land use/cost.
(5 Marks) Future Technologies: Floating offshore wind, larger turbine size (higher
capacity), advanced materials (lighter/stronger blades), smart controls, integration
with energy storage.
CO2   BL-2   b   (2 Marks) Neat sketches showing Horizontal Axis Wind Turbine (HAWT) and   10
Vertical Axis Wind Turbine (VAWT).
(4 Marks) HAWT: Description (axis parallel to wind, high efficiency, common
design), main features.
(4 Marks) VAWT: Description (axis perpendicular to wind, omni-directional,
low starting torque), main types (e.g., Darrieus, Savonius).
Q3   CO2   BL-3   a   (3 Marks) Darrieus: Working principle (aerodynamic lift-driven, high speed, need   10
for external start).
(3 Marks) Savonius: Working principle (aerodynamic drag-driven, self-starting,
low speed/torque).
(4 Marks) Comparison: Advantages (e.g., Darrieus: higher Cp, simple blade shape;
Savonius: excellent self-starting, simple construction, omni-directional).
CO3   BL-3   b   (3 Marks) Process: Kinetic energy → Mechanical energy (rotor rotation) →   10
Electrical energy (generator).
(2 Marks) Kinetic Power: .
(3 Marks) Mechanical Power (Extracted): (mentioning Cp and its limit ≈0.593).
(2 Marks) Explanation: Relate power to key variables (ρ, A, V) and efficiency (Cp
).
Q4   CO4   BL-3   a   (4 Marks) Draw Curve: Labeling axes (Wind speed vs. Power output) and clearly   10
marking Cut-in speed, Rated speed, and Cut-out speed.
(6 Marks) Explanation: Power is zero below Vcut-in, increases cubically
(P∝V3) between Vcut-in and Vrated, is constant at Prated between Vrated and
Vcut-out, and drops to zero above Vcut-out.
CO4   BL-4   b   (5 Marks) Design Considerations: Aerodynamics (airfoil selection, twist, taper),   10
Structural strength (withstanding extreme loads), Material selection (low density,
high stiffness/strength), Manufacturing cost/ease.
(5 Marks) Blade Fatigue: Explanation of fatigue (failure due to repeated cyclic
loading—aerodynamic, gravity, operational). Mitigation: Use of composite
materials, strict manufacturing control, design for finite life/load cases, regular
inspection.
Q5   CO2   BL-2   a   (4 Marks) Generator: Function (convert mechanical to electrical), types   10
(Induction/Synchronous), and variable vs. fixed speed operation.
(3 Marks) Transformer: Function (step-up voltage) for efficient transmission to the
grid, location (nacelle or base).
********** 2 **********
--- page 3 ---
(3 Marks) Anemometer: Function (measure wind speed), use in control system for
power regulation and protection.
CO3   BL-3   b   (4 Marks) Main Design Parameters: Rotor diameter (D), cut-in/rated/cut-out   10
speeds, generator type/size, tower height, nominal wind speed Vrated.
(6 Marks) Assumptions and Calculations: Assumption of Vrated (e.g., 10 m/s), air
density ρ, and power coefficient Cp (e.g., 0.4). Calculation of required D (e.g.,
D≈5.6 m assuming ρ=1.225,Cp=0.4).
Q6   CO5   BL-4   a   (3 Marks) Birds: Mortality from collision, migration path effects. Mitigation:   10
Siting, painting blades, radar shutdown.
(3 Marks) Marine Life (Offshore): Noise/vibration during construction (piling),
habitat disruption, reef effects of foundations.
(4 Marks) Environmental Sustainability: Reduction in fossil fuel use, land-use vs.
energy output, resource usage in manufacturing (LCA).
CO5   BL-3   b   (3 Marks) Capital Expenditure (CAPEX): Turbine cost, foundation/tower,   10
electrical infrastructure (substation, grid connection), land acquisition/lease.
(3 Marks) Operating Expenditure (OPEX): Maintenance and   repairs
(scheduled/unscheduled), insurance, land lease payments, labor.
(4 Marks) Other Costs: Financing/Interest, Project development (feasibility study,
permitting, wind resource assessment), Decommissioning.
Q7   CO4   BL-4   a   (5 Marks) Fault Conditions: Overspeed (high wind), Grid loss/voltage dip, Gearbox   10
failure, Blade imbalance, Yaw misalignment, High vibration.
(5 Marks) Control Mechanisms: Pitch Control (limit power/speed), Yaw Control
(correct alignment), Parking Brake (emergency stop), Fault Detection Sensors
(vibration, temperature), Cut-out System (shut down above Vcut-out).
CO4   BL-3   b   (5 Marks) (a) Parking Brake System: Function (holds rotor stationary during   10
maintenance or fault), types (e.g., hydraulic, caliper disc), need for reliable
operation separate from aerodynamic braking.
(5 Marks) (b) Power Curve Analysis: Definition (plot of electrical power vs. wind
speed), importance (performance evaluation, annual energy yield calculation), role
in turbine commissioning/monitoring.
