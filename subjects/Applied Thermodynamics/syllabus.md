- Course as printed: Applied Thermodynamics (code 702ME0C033)

````text
Program: B Tech (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: IV
Course : Applied Thermodynamics   Code: 702ME0C033
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours   (Hours per   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
per week)   week)   per week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks -100)
3   2   0   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Thermodynamics
Course Objective
This course describes the application of Engineering Thermodynamics to model the energy
conversion phenomenon. With this course one can impart knowledge of air compressors and
components of steam power system. This course provides basics of I.C engines, refrigeration
systems and air conditioning systems and also build a bridge for these courses.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Analyse the working principles and performance parameters of reciprocating and rotary air
compressors
2. Evaluate the design, efficiency, and critical pressure conditions in nozzles and diffusers
3. Apply thermodynamic principles to assess the performance of steam generators and turbines
4. Examine the performance and working conditions of condensers and cooling towers
5. Assess different refrigeration and air-conditioning systems, including refrigerants, cycles,
and working principles
6. Evaluate the performance of internal combustion (IC) engines, including heat balance
calculations and valve/port timing diagrams
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Reciprocating Air Compressors   09
Working principle, work done, Effect of clearance volume, Single and
multistage compressors, Volumetric efficiency, Intercooling in multistage
compressors, calculation of power requirement, Rotary compressors.
Review of Thermodynamics, Vapour Power cycles, Binary Vapour Cycle,
Combined Cycle and Co-generation.
2.   Steam Generators   08
Fire Tube Boilers, Water Tube Boilers, Boiler Mountings and Accessories,
High Pressure Boilers. Draught, Performance of Boilers, Combustion of Fuel.
3.   Nozzles and Diffusers   09
Theory and Design, Efficiency and Critical Pressure, General Relationship
and supersaturated Flow.
Steam Turbines
Impulse and Reaction turbine Principles - Compounding – Types - Velocity
diagrams for simple and multistage turbines - Speed regulations –
Governors.
