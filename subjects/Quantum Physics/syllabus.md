Course Objectives:
The objective of this course is to teach the limitations of classical physics in explaining several
experimental facts leading to the development of quantum physics. Also, this course will
expose the students to different applications of quantum mechanics, such as quantum
tunneling and quantum computation.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand the need for Quantum mechanics (K2)
2. Explain the basic laws related to quantum mechanics and apply them to solve
related problems. (K3)
3. Apply the concept of quantum mechanics to real-life instrumentation and applications.
(K3)
4. Understand the requirement of quantum computation and its various aspects. (K2)
5. Gauge the theoretical principles through laboratory experimentation, enhancing problem-
solving abilities and technical proficiency in modern physics applications. (K3)
