Pre-requisite: Linear and differential equation.

Objectives:
1. To develop the understanding of units, dimensions and processes and

process variables.

2. Motivate students to solve the problem of flow-sheeting by Degree of
freedom analysis and solving mass balance with and without chemical
reaction for acyclic and cyclic processes.

3. Motivate students to solve the problem of flow-sheeting by solving energy

balance problems.
4. Imparting the basic knowledge of combustion of fuel and use of

psychometric chart.

Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Analyse the fundamental of units, dimension and important process

variables.

2. Calculate mass and energy flow rates into and out of various process
equipments.

3. Calculate conversion, selectivity for various reactions with and without
recycle.

4, Understand the problems based calorific values of fuels and use of
psychometric chart.
