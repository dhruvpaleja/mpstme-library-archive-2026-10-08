Pre-requisite: Physics and Mathematics
Course Objective
The course elaborates the basic elements of robot motion with its mathematical modelling. The
students learn to analyze motion control aspects of robotics before advancing to the task of
synthesizing robot motion using mathematical and software tools.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Define the fundamental concepts of robotics
2. Explain the working principles of PID controllers and velocity profiles used in robotic
motion control
3. Apply forward and inverse kinematics to quantify the motion of robotic systems
4. Analyze Denavit-Hartenberg parameters and HCTM for standard robotic arms
5. Evaluate robotic simulation results using the tool configuration vector and then the
equation of motion
