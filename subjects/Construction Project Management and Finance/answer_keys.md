Q5a   • S-Curve: Graph showing cumulative progress (cost/time) vs. schedule; typically S-
shaped due to slow start, rapid progress, and leveling off near completion.
•   Uses: Tracks planned vs. actual performance; visual tool for forecasting delays or
cost overruns.
• Earned Value Method (EVM): Quantitative tool integrating scope, schedule, and
cost.
• Key Parameters:
o PV (Planned Value) – Budgeted cost of scheduled work.
o EV (Earned Value) – Budgeted cost of work performed.
o AC (Actual Cost) – Actual cost incurred.
o SV (Schedule Variance) = EV – PV.
o CV (Cost Variance) = EV – AC.
o SPI (Schedule Performance Index) = EV / PV.
o CPI (Cost Performance Index) = EV / AC.
•   Interpretation: SPI/CPI < 1 → behind schedule/over budget; > 1 → ahead of
schedule/under budget.
