Pre-requisite: -
Course Objective
This course is intended to help the students understand various aspects of construction project
management including various scheduling and project control techniques. It also aims to make the
students aware of the financial aspects associated with construction projects, sources of funds and their
disbursement for the project.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the fundamental construction project concepts, including project
stakeholders and their roles, as well as the project life cycle
2. Understand Industrial and Labor Act
3. Apply knowledge of basic concepts of construction project such as risk management
process, risk identification, etc
4. Classify sources of funds and their disbursement required for the project
5. Plan and prepare the schedule for construction project activities using tools such as Bar
Chart, CPM, PERT
