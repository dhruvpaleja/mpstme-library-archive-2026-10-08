Course Objective
This course will help students learn to evaluate and apply design principles and human
factors to apparel, housing and residential technologies and other consumer products.
Exploring the use and design of products through history, in different cultures, and by
diverse users adds to the knowledge base for all consumers.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify and discuss design principles and elements and their application to
consumer products for diverse users, such as apparel, housing and residential
technologies
2. Explain the historical and cultural context of design and consumer products through
pre-industrial and industrial periods and modern era.
3. Apply interpretive strategies or methodologies in design.
4. Discuss the design process and the importance of diverse user needs in the creation
of consumer products.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Design Process   02
Introduction, procedures, thinking, looking, doing, critique
2   Unity   02
Introduction, gestalt, ways to achieve unity, unity with variety, unity at
work.
3   Emphasis and focal points   04
Introduction, ways to achieve emphasis, degree of emphasis, absence of
focal points.
4   Scale and proportion   04
Introduction, scale of art, scale within art, manipulating scale, Proportion.
5   Balance   04
Imbalance, symmetrical balance, asymmetrical balance, Radial balance,
Crystallographic balance.
6 Rhythm   04
Introduction, rhythm and motion, alternating rhythm, progressive
rhythm, polyrhythmic structures.
7   Design Elements   10
Line, Shape, Pattern and Texture, illusion of space, illusion of motion,
Value, Color
