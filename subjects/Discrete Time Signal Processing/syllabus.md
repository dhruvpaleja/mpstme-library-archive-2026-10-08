Course Objective
This course introduces concepts and techniques to design discrete time systems for processing of discrete
time signals. It will help to develop applications based on image, speech and biomedical signals.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Analyze the frequency response, pole-zero plots, and phase distortion of LTI systems to
determine system characteristics. (L4 - Analyze)
2. Apply Discrete Fourier and Fast Fourier Transform their properties for system analysis
applications. (L3 - Applying)
3. Design FIR and IIR filters using appropriate techniques such as windowing, bilinear
transformation, and frequency transformation for various applications. (L6 - Creating)
4. Evaluate the computational complexity of DFT and FFT algorithms, comparing their efficiency
in signal processing applications. (L5 - Evaluating)
5. Implement FIR and IIR digital filters using different structures (Direct Form, Cascade, and
Parallel) to achieve desired frequency response. (L3 - Applying)
6. Examine the impact of finite word-length effects such as quantization, truncation, and round-
off noise on digital filter performance. (L4 - Analyzing)
