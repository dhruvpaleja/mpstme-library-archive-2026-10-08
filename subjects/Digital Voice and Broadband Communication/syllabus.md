Pre-requisite: Computer Network
Course Objective
The objective of this course is to introduce digital switching and signaling techniques. It provides
knowledge about the transition process from traditional to broadband communication networks and
explores Voice over IP.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Outline the basic concepts of telephone traffic theory
2. Classify network synchronization and signalling techniques
3. Describe the voice coding techniques and different protocols used for VoIP
4. Compare broadband access technologies
Detailed Syllabus

Course Objective
The principal objective of this course is to provide the knowledge of basic Telephony and to study
various types of digital switching and signaling techniques to understand the need and process of
transition from traditional communication networks to broadband communication networks and to
explore Voice over IP and the functionality of different building blocks of broadband technology.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Explain the basic concepts of telephony.
2. Analyse and characterize the traffic in telephone network and Broadband Access Technologies.
3. Compare the different broadband network access techniques of cable modem service, optical
fiber based access, and broadband wireless access techniques of Wi-Fi and Wi-MAX networks.
4. Describes the voice coding techniques and different protocols used for VoIP.
