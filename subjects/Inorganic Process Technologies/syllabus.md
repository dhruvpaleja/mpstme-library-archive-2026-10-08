Pre-requisite:
Chemistry, Advanced Chemistry, Heat Transfer Operations.
Objectives
This subject provides insight into some of the common inorganic chemical
processes, their chemistry and technology used for manufacturing these
chemicals and to develop the industrial process including trouble shooting.
The subject also helps students understand the integration and optimization
that can be applied in designing of Chemical Plants.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1.Understand and reproduce the fundamentals and processes used in chemical
industries.
2. Discuss the formation of process flow diagrams and draw it.
3.Optimize the process parameters
4.Understand the economics of inorganic chemical projects
