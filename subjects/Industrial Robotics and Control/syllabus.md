Objectives:
1. To provide knowledge to students with the concepts and techniques in robot manipulator
control.
2. To expose students to evaluate, choose and incorporate robots in engineering systems and
programming of robots.
3. To understand and analyze the various applications of robots
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Apply the knowledge of vectorial mathematics and geometry for kinematics (Direct and
Inverse) motion.
2. Perform trajectory planning and work space analysis for robots.
3. Use image representation for robotic movement.
4. Perform six-axis Industrial Robotic programming.
