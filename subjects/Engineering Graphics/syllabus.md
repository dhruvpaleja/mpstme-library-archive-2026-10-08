Pre-requisite: -
Course Objectives
This course is aimed at providing basic understanding of the fundamentals of Engineering
Graphics; mainly visualization, graphics theory, standards & conventions of drawing, the tools
of drawing and the use of drawings in engineering applications. The course has been structured
to include sufficient simulations which would aid the student in visualization of three-
dimensional objects and developing the drawing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Apply basic concepts of geometrical constructions to create engineering curves and
projection of lines using drawing instruments
2. Apply the basic principles of planes and solids to draw front view and top view using
AutoCAD software
3. Apply the basic principles of sectional views to generate development of lateral surfaces
of different solids
4. Apply the basic principles of projections in converting pictorial views into orthographic
views with the help of AutoCAD software
5. Apply the basic principles of projections in converting orthographic views into isometric
drawing using AutoCAD software
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Engineering Drawing   04
Principles of engineering graphics and their significance, usage of drawing
instruments, lettering, numbering;
Conic sections (ellipse, parabola, hyperbola - general method only)
including the rectangular hyperbola; cycloid, epi-cycloid, hypo-cycloid
and involutes.
2.   Projections of Lines and Planes   05
Introduction to projections of points, conventions; points locating in all
quadrants.
Projections of Lines
Introduction, lines inclined to one plane and parallel to other plane, lines
inclined to both planes.
Projections of Planes
Introduction, types of planes, plane surface inclined to both reference
planes, projection of auxiliary planes
3.   Projections of Regular Solids   05
Introduction to projection of regular solids, types of solids; Projections of
regular solids (prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones) covering those inclined
to both the reference planes
4.   Section and Development of Regular Solids   04

Introduction to section and development of regular solids;
Section of regular prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones;
Development of surfaces of right regular solids namely prisms, pyramids,
cylinders and cones.
5.   Orthographic Projections   04
Principles of orthographic projections, conventions used in quadrant
formation, conversion of isometric models to orthographic views and vice-
versa, orthographic views of geometrical solids and objects from industry.
6.   Sectional Orthographic Projections   04
Principles of sectional orthographic projection, need of sectional views,
types of sections, hatching of sectioned part and principles, sectional
orthographic views of geometrical solids and objects from industry.
7.   Isometric Projections   04
Principles of isometric projection-isometric scale, isometric views,
conventions; isometric views of lines, planes, simple and compound
solids; conversion of orthographic views to isometric models to and vice-
versa; isometrics projections of given views.
Total   30

Program: B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: III
Course: Engineering Graphics   Code: 701ME0C015
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 50)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Knowledge of basic geometrical shape.
Course Objective
This course provides the basic understanding of the fundamentals of engineering graphics and the
use of drawings in engineering applications. The course is aimed at applying computer-aided
geometric design concepts and creation of working drawings.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Apply the techniques, skills and modern tools to create projections of machine components.
2. Interpret and communicate drawings effectively using different types of curves, lines, planes
and solids
3. Sketch the section of right regular solids with their development of lateral surfaces.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1. Curves used in Engineering Practice   07
Conic sections - Ellipse (General method, Concentric circle method, Oblong
method), Hyperbola and Parabola (General method, Rectangle method); Involute
of Circle with different string length; Cycloidal Curves - Cycloid, Epicycloid &
Hypocycloid.
2. Projections of Lines and Planes   05
Projections of Lines: Introduction, lines inclined to both planes. (First quadrant
only); Projections of Planes: Introduction, plane surface inclined to both reference
planes. (First quadrant only)
3. Projections of Solids   08
Projection of solids like Prism, Pyramid, Cylinder and Cone inclined to the one
reference plane; Development of Surfaces: Development of Lateral surfaces of
solids cut by inclined plane (Prism, pyramid, cone and cylinder) with sectional
view.
4. Orthographic Projections   06
Projections of various objects having flat surfaces, slots, ribs and cylindrical
surfaces. (Use First Angle Projection Method only)
Sectional views of Orthographic Projections: Conversion of pictorial view with
sectional orthographic projection (Use First Angle Projection Method only)
5. Isometric Projections   04
Isometric view and projection of linear and curvilinear features. Problems related
to objects having flat surfaces, slots, ribs and cylindrical surfaces.
Total   30
