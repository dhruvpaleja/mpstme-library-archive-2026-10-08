Pre-requisite: Chemistry, Mathematics.
Objectives:
1. To develop the understanding basic molecular properties of engineering
materials.
2. Imparting the basic knowledge of structure of different materials and
processing.

3. To provide the skills for surface treatment and corrosion prevention.
4. Imparting the basic knowledge of experimental technique for
characterization.

Outcomes:

After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Analyze the properties of different materials.
2. Develop the relation between structure of materials and its properties.
3. Select the methods for surface treatment and corrosion prevention.
