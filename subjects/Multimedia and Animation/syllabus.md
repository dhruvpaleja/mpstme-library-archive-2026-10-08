Pre-requisite: -
Course Objective
The objective of this course is to provide concept about an application, which uses a collection of
multiple media sources e.g. text, graphics, images, audio, animation and video. The course focusses
on computer-controlled integration of text, graphics, drawings, still and moving images (Video),
Animation, audio, and any other media where every type of information can be represented,
stored, transmitted and processed digitally.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1.   Describe basics of Multimedia technology
2. Apply various tools for Image and Audio/ Video editing
3.   Develop Animation with action script
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   04
What Is Multimedia? Multimedia in various context like business, schools,
Educations, Entertainment, Advertising etc., AR/VR, Delivering Multimedia,
CD-ROM, DVD, Flash Drives
The Internet, Components of a Multimedia System, Desirable Features for a
Multimedia System, requirements of Multimedia Communication.
2.   Representation of Multimedia Objects   04
Representation of Analog Signals, A/D: Sampling and quantization process,
Text: Font and their representation (bitmap, true type)
Graphics: Raster & Vector representation, aliasing problems, Introduction to
BMP, GIF, TIFF, PNG and JPEG formats.
3.   Audio and video   04
The Power of Sound, Digital Audio, Making Digital Audio Files, MIDI
Audio, MIDI vs. Digital Audio, Multimedia System Sounds, Audio File
Formats, Vaughan’s Law of Multimedia Minimums, speech and wideband
audio, sampling rate and aliasing, MP3, WMA, WAV, MIDI etc.)
Video (frame rate and resolution, interlaced and non-interlaced video, color
planes (YCBCR, YUV), Video broadcast standards (PAL, NTSC, and
SECAM), HD Video, 3D TV, Video representation: AVI, MPEG).
4.   Introduction to Color   04
Color modes- RGB, CMYK, grayscale, LAB, bitmap, Hue, saturation, and
brightness, Browser safe colors , Shadows, highlights and midtones of an
image Photoshop Interface, Tools and Options.
