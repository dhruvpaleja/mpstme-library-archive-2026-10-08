Pre-requisite: Communication theory and systems, Electromagnetic wave theory.
Course Objective
The aim of this course is to provide knowledge of the basic elements of optical fiber
transmission and structure & characteristics of optical sources and detectors. This course also
makes student acquainted with different types of losses, signal degradation, optical
budgeting, and the concept of optical networks.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1.   Illustrate the basic concepts of optical fiber communication system
2. Compare various sources, detectors and communication links used in optical fiber
communication
3. Evaluate the performance of optical fiber system with the help of various
measurement parameters
4. Differentiate between various types of optical network architectures.
