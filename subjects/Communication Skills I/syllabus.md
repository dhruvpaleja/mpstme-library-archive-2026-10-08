To equip students with critical thinking abilities through diverse reading exercises. To enhance

speaking skills. To support students’ language skills requirements such as vocabulary and
grammar. To build students’ confidence in participating in formal communication such as

conversations. To help students in understanding interpersonal and group dynamics and to
inculcate the principles of team work.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Draft formal and academic documents as per the standard principles of written communication
2. Engage in effective oral communication in both formal and informal communication context
3. Create formal and persuasive presentation
4. Comprehend oral and written texts critically
5. Work effectively in groups with sound understanding of groups dynamics
