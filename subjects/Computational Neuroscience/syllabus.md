Course Objective
The aim of this course is to investigate brain function using mathematical tools and theories. It
includes methods of neuron encoding and decoding and concept of synaptic plasticity. It also helps
to comprehend the functioning of brain by creating different computational models.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Explain the concept of structural and functional neuroscience
2. Examine the various modalities of brain research
3. Create descriptive, Mechanistic and Interpretative models of brain
4. Interpret computational models using biophysics of neurons

Pre-requisite: Probability and random processes ,Statistics , Machine learning and Signals and
system
Objectives:
1. This course provides an introduction to basic computational methods for understanding
what nervous systems do and for determining how they function.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand Neurobiology
2. Learn the various modality of brain research
3. Understand biophysics of neurons
4. Create descriptive and mechanistic model
