Pre-requisite: Engineering mathematics
Objectives
This course will help students to understand the particles and their
characteristics with respect to different solid – fluid operations such as size
reduction, enlargement, separations, mixing, and conveying. It will impart the
knowledge of working, construction and diagrams of the equipments used for
solid – fluid operations and skills to choose the equipment for desired solid – fluid
operation of chemical industries
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1 Understand and reproduce the fundamentals of particles size measurements
and its characteristics
2   Identify and select appropriate heat exchange equipment for various
applications
3   Understand, and reproduce the concepts of various solid – fluid operations
like size reduction, enlargement, separations, mixing and conveying
operations
