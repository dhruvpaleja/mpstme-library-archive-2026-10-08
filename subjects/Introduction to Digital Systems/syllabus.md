Course Objective
The basic objective is to provide fundamental concepts of digital logic families & digital systems as
well as their applications. The course helps the students to design and analyse the combinational and
sequential logic circuits.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Choose simplification techniques for optimized implementation of Boolean expressions using
logic gates
2. Build combinational logic circuits
3. Construct sequential logic circuits which can be applied for real time applications of complex
digital circuits and systems
