Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objective
This course will help students learn to evaluate and apply design principles and human
factors to apparel, housing and residential technologies and other consumer products.
Exploring the use and design of products through history, in different cultures, and by
diverse users adds to the knowledge base for all consumers. This course is to sensitize the
students about the Design domain and help them collaborate with designers in their professional
practice.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify and discuss design principles and elements and their application to consumer
products for diverse users, such as apparel, housing and residential technologies
2. Explain the historical and cultural context of design and consumer products through pre-
industrial and industrial periods and modern era
3. Apply interpretive strategies or methodologies in design
4. Discuss the design process and the importance of diverse user needs in the creation of
consumer products
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Introduction to Design   04
Idea of Design, Understanding its landscape, Design VS Art , Design VS
Engineering, Definitions, Design and its relevance to the Data Sciences, Brief
History of Design, Cultural Contexts of Design, Users & User Centric
Design, Position of Design across Knowledge Domains
2   Design Process   04
Introduction, iterative nature of Design, Design Methodology, Emergence of
Design Thinking, Design as a Creative Process, Prototyping as a testing and
validation process.
3   Elements of Design   04
Line, Shape, Pattern and Texture, illusion of space, illusion of motion, Value,
Colour Consumer Products
4   Principles of Design   04
Emphasis, Balance, Contrast, Repetition, Proportion, Movement, White
space, Unity
5   Ergonomics & Human Factors   04
Introduction, Idea of Fit, Anthropometry, Affordances, Gestalt, usability,
Introduction to Visual Semiotics.
