Pre-requisite: HSC level Chemistry
Course Objective
To familiarize with atomic structure, periodicity of elements in periodic table and chemical
bonding of elements .To introduce electromagnetic spectrum ranges converted to different
technologies used for various applications. To acquaint with concepts of electrochemistry and basic
organic reactions. To study important engineering materials and management of e-waste.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Explain the foundational knowledge of the subject using chemistry principles
2. Identify newer chemical materials for better product designing
3. Analyze the impact of science and its progress on society and provide solution for cleaner
environment
4. Evaluate the various aspects of chemistry to convert into useful applications.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Atomic structure and Periodic properties of elements   8
Study of atom structure with reference to Bohr’s model, wave particle
duality, Schrodinger wave equation. Concept of valency and electronic
configuration. Explanation of Aufbau principle, Hund’s rule and Pauli’s
Exclusion Principle that helps in writing electronic configuration.
Introduction to Modern periodic table, Nomenclature of s, p, d, f group
elements. Trends in physical properties: effective nuclear charge, atomic
radii, ionization energy, electron affinity, electronegativity. Concept of
semiconductors and metalloids. Concept of acids and bases.
2   Chemical bonding   6
Intermolecular forces: Ionic, covalent, co-ordinate bonds and types of
intramolecular interactions (with one example for each type). Introduction to
co-ordination chemistry and Co-ordination number, magnetism of the co-
ordinate complexes.
3   Electrochemistry   6
Concept of electrochemical cell & electrolytic cell. Cell potential, EMF of Cell,
Relation of free energy with EMF of cell, Faraday’s law, Derivation of Nernst
equation, Applicability of Nernst equation
4   Important Engineering Materials   6
Nanomaterials: Structure, properties, applications of CNTs, Fullerenes,
Graphite.
Liquid Crystals: Definition, classification, properties with applications.

E-waste management: Definition, classification and management of e-waste

photochemical reactions and redox reactions.
