Pre-requisite: Mathematics III

Course Objective

The students will be able to use computational techniques and algebraic skills essential for the study of matrices,
three-dimensional geometry, probability, statistics and numerical methods. It also aims to develop an
understanding of differential equations and partial derivatives.

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to-
1. Recognize the formation of Differential equations, Partial derivatives and apply its knowledge to different
engineering problems
2. Evaluate matrices and understand the relation between line and plane
3. Apply measures of dispersion and basic concepts of probability
4. Solve a linear system of equations using an appropriate numerical method

Detailed Syllabus

Unit | Description Duration

1. Matrices
Definition, Algebra of matrices, types of matrices, adjoint and inverse of a square matrix,
concept of elementary row and column operations, Inverse by row operations, solving
system of equations using Cramer’s rule, matrix inversion method.

10

2. First order and first degree differential equations
Concept of differential equations, order, degree and formation of differential equation,
solution of differential equation - variable separable, homogeneous differential equation, 08
linear differential equation, and applications of differential equations related to real life
problems.

3. Three Dimensional Geometry - Lines and Planes
Relation between direction cosines of a line, equations of lines - passing through a given
point and parallel to the given vector, passing through two given points, equations of
plane in normal form, equation of a plane passing through a given point and
perpendicular to a given vector, distance of a point from a plane and line in space, angle
between the planes.

10

4. Probability & Statistics
Statistics: Mean, Variance, standard deviation of grouped/ungrouped data. 08
Probability: Random experiment, Events and probability of events, Addition theorem,
