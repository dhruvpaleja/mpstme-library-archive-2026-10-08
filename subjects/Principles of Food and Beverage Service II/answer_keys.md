Q1. Define Menu and Factors Affecting Menu Planning (10 Marks)
Definition of Menu
A menu is a list of food and beverage items offered to guests along with their prices, serving
as a communication tool between the kitchen and the customer.
Factors Affecting Menu Planning
1. Type of Establishment
•   Fine dining → elaborate menu
•   Fast food → limited menu
Impact: Ensures operational suitability
2. Target Customers
• Age, income, nationality, preferences
Impact: Increases customer satisfaction
3. Availability of Raw Materials
•   Seasonal and local ingredients
Impact: Reduces cost and ensures freshness
4. Cost & Pricing
• Food cost vs selling price
Impact: Maintains profitability
