Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objective
It aim of the course is to educate Students for data preparation, building predictive models, assessing and
implementing models for decision making
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understand and classify the structure of programming environments, including libraries,
diagrams, and data sources within SAS Enterprise Miner. (L2 – Understand)
2. Apply decision trees and regression models to perform predictive analysis and optimize model
complexity using SAS tools. (L3 – Apply)
3. Evaluate statistical measures and model fit statistics to assess and design supervised and
unsupervised models effectively. (L5 – Evaluate)
4. Analyze and implement advanced predictive models using neural networks and additional
modeling tools to solve real-world data problems. (L4 – Analyze)
5. Apply unsupervised learning techniques such as clustering and market basket analysis for pattern
discovery in large datasets. (L4 – Analyze)
