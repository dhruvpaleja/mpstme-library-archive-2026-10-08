Pre-requisite: Introduction to Business Statistics, Analytics and Modeling-I
Course Objective
To study quantitative methods used in managerial decision-making. The course emphasizes model
formulation and model building as well as the interpretation of the software output.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Formulate mathematical models for a variety of business applications and solve them using
linear programming and optimization techniques.
2. Formulate and solve variety of network optimization problems.
3. Apply knowledge of decision making to select the best alternatives.
4. Use forecasting techniques to predict future behaviour based on past experiences.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Linear Programing   07
Introduction to Linear programming, General Characteristics. Feasible
solution and optimal solution.
Graphical Solution of LPP with two variables. Illustrations of special cases
such as unique solution, multiple solutions, infeasible solutions, unbounded
solutions.
Formulating special types of LPP such as Diet problem, work-scheduling
problem, Capital budgeting problem, Blending problems etc.
Standard LPP, Simplex method of solving LPP- Main Steps-Illustrations.
Sensitivity Analysis Graphical Approach.

Transportation problem- Formulation, Method of finding Basic Feasible

Assignment Problem- Formulation and examples.
Solution of Assignment Problem- Hungarian method.
3   Network Models   07
Network Models- Basic Definitions.
Shortest-Path Problems-Dijkstra’s Algorithm.
Maximum-flow Problems-The Ford–Fulkerson Method.
CPM and Pert- Basic concepts- Finding Critical Path.
