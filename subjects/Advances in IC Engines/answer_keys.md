Q.1   Answer briefly:
a.   • Compression ratio significance: Ratio of cylinder volume at BDC to clearance volume at [4]
TDC.
•   Higher ratio → better thermal efficiency, but increases knocking tendency.
b. Battery vs electronic ignition:   [4]
•   Battery ignition uses mechanical contact breaker;
•   electronic ignition uses solid-state devices,
• more reliable, precise timing, less maintenance.
c. Scavenging:   [4]
• Process of removing exhaust gases from cylinder and replacing with fresh air/fuel
mixture in CI engines.
•   Essential for efficiency and preventing dilution.
d. Gasoline direct injection:   [4]
•   Fuel injected directly into combustion chamber,
• improves fuel economy,
• reduces emissions,
•   allows stratified charge operation.
e. Major contributors to emissions:   [4]
• CO, HC, NOx,
•   particulate matter from incomplete combustion,
•   fuel evaporation,
•   crankcase blow-by.

Q.2   a. Air vs liquid cooling:   [10]
•   Air cooling → simpler, lighter, used in motorcycles; less effective in high-power engines.
•   Liquid cooling → efficient, uniform cooling, used in cars/trucks, but heavier and
complex.
b. Fixed jet carburetor:   [10]
•   Maintains constant jet size,
•   fuel metered by venturi effect,
•   simple design,
• but less adaptable to varying loads.
Q.3   a. Diesel combustion systems:   [10]
•   Direct injection (DI),
•   indirect injection (IDI),
•   divided chamber.
•   DI → efficient, high power;
•   IDI → smoother combustion, but lower efficiency.
b. Optical engines:   [10]
•   Transparent cylinder heads/windows allow visualization of spray, flame, turbulence.
• Used with laser diagnostics (PIV, interferometry) to study combustion.
Q.4   a. Torque characteristics:   [10]
•   Single-cylinder → uneven torque, high vibration.
•   Multi-cylinder → smoother torque, better balance, higher power output.
b. SCR system:   [10]
•   Injects urea/DEF into exhaust,
•   converts NOx into N₂ and H₂O over catalyst.
•   Effective for heavy-duty diesel emission control.
Q.5   a. Gasoline injection systems:   [10]
•   Port fuel injection (PFI), direct injection (GDI).
•   PFI → fuel injected into intake port, simpler.
• GDI → higher efficiency, lower emissions.
b. Diesel particulate filter:   [10]
•   Traps soot particles, periodically regenerates by burning off deposits.
• Reduces PM emissions significantly.

Q.6   a.   [10]
b Improved scavenging:   [10]
•   Cross-flow, loop, and uniflow scavenging.
• Modified designs improve fresh charge replacement,
• reduce short-circuiting.
Q.7   a. Magneto ignition:   [10]
•   Self-contained ignition using rotating magnet,
• produces current without battery.
•   Reliable for small engines.
b. European emission standards:   [10]
• Euro I–VI progressively stricter limits on CO, HC, NOx, PM.
• Aim to reduce vehicular pollution.
