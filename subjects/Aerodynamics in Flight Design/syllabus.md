- Course as printed: Aerodynamics in Flight Design (code 702ME0E014)

````text
Program: B Tech / B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: V/ X
Course: Aerodynamics in Flight Design   Code: 702ME0E014
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics and Strength of Materials
Course Objective
To provide an understanding of the basics of aerodynamics in aircraft. To impart knowledge of
boundary layer theory. To introduce the aerospace vehicle and space vehicle design. To introduce wind
tunnel testing
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Analyse the basic flight mathematical aspects and potential flows
2. Discuss structural design of aircraft and space vehicles
3. Classify types of wind tunnel and describe wind tunnel testing technique
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Aerodynamics   05
Importance of Aerodynamic, four principles of aerodynamics, three laws of
aerodynamics, six fundamentals of flight.
2.   Applied Aerodynamics   10
Panel methods, pressure distribution, Kutta conditions, flow around airfoil
(unsteady potential flow, compressible flow, axisymmetric flow) and slender
body theories. Flight vehicle aerodynamics, rotor aerodynamics – low Reynolds
number aerodynamics
3.   Aircraft Vehicle Design   10
Design considerations for civilian and military aircraft, weight estimation, airfoil
and geometry selection, thrust to weight ratio and wing loading, initial sizing,
propulsion, landing gear and subsystems, aerodynamics, stability, control, and
handling qualities, flight mechanics and performance issues, aircraft layout and
configuration, structural aspects and constraint analysis.
4.   Space Vehicle Design   10
History of rocketry & launch vehicles, Current & future launch vehicles.
Orbit/trajectory requirements and missions. Launch vehicle sizing, launch into an
orbit, rocket propulsion (configuration and structural design), design for re-entry
5.   Wind Tunnel Techniques   10
Introduction to wind tunnel, Classification of wind tunnels, Elements of wind
tunnel, Conventional measurement techniques, wind tunnel testing technique.
Total   45
