Course Objectives
 To enhance one’s ability to be fully self aware by helping oneself to overcome all fears
and insecurities and to grow fully from inside out and outside in
 To increase one’s knowledge and awareness of emotional competency and emotional
intelligence at place of study/work.
 To develop interpersonal skills and adopt good leadership behaviour for
empowerment of self and others.
 To set appropriate goals, manage stress and time effectively.
 To achieve excellence with ethics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate a set of practical skills such as persuasion, time management, self-
management, servant leadership, etc.
2. Become conscious practitioners of righteousness.
3. Realize their potential as human beings and conduct themselves properly in the ways
of the world.
