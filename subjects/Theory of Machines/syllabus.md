Prerequisite: Engineering Mechanics
Course Objective
This course focuses on the conventional machines and kinematics involved in machines.   It
intends to help the students understand the turning moment diagrams of IC engines and the
gyroscopic couples of different automobiles.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Describe the fundamentals of link, machines, and mechanism.
2. Analyze the degree of freedom in mechanisms and its implications
3. Create an application based simple four bar mechanism using CAD modelling
4. Determine velocity in a four-bar linkage mechanism
5. Illustrate and interpret turning moment diagrams for engines equipped with flywheels
6. Apply the principle of gyroscopic couples to ensure the stability of airplanes and ships
