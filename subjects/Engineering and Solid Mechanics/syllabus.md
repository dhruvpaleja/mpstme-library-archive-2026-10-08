Pre-requisite: Physics and Mathematics

Objectives:

1. To familiarize the students with an introductory treatment of Engineering
Mechanics for taking up advanced courses in the area in the subsequent
semester's

2. To provide students with a working knowledge of statics with emphasis on
force equilibrium and free body diagrams, understanding of formation and
determination of stress and deformations in wide range of applications.

3. To provide understanding of various design aspects of chemical equipments

Outcomes: .
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understanding of concept of forces and equilibrium, centre of gravity and
moment of inertia
2. To calculate the stress and strain in materials undergoing different loading
and conditions.
3._ To apply the concepts in chemical equipment design like thin shell. .
