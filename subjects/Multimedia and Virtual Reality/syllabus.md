Objectives
understand and use various tools, techniques and technologies involved in processing,

understand how multimedia can be used in various application areas.
Course Outcomes: After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Explain the basic concepts of multimedia systems.
2. Understand multimedia I/O technologies and digital imaging.
3. Explore the concepts of animation and Virtual Reality.
