Objectives:
1. To introduce various industrial engineering tools for enhancing productivity.

After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Apply work study techniques for improving productivity.
2. Create an effective work place layout using industrial engineering tools.
3. Understand the fundamentals of value engineering, ergonomics and quality management.
4. Apply statistical quality control techniques for quality improvement.
