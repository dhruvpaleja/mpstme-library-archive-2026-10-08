Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics
Course Objective
This course aims to explain the index properties, classification soil and it’s engineering behavior. It
also aims to impart knowledge of the effect of compaction, consolidation and shear characteristics of
soil.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Illustrate the basic terminologies and soil properties and interrelations between the properties
2. Classify soils and evaluate their suitability for geotechnical applications
3. Analyze the soil – water characteristics and assess their effect on effect on soil behavior
4. Explain compaction, consolidation, and shear strength properties of soil
5. Test the index properties of soils and interpret their engineering behavior
6. Test and interpret the compaction properties of soils
