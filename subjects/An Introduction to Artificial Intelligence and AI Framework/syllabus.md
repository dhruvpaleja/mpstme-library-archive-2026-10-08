Course Objective
The aim of the course is to provide broad-stroke coverage of classic topics of Artificial
Intelligence, particularly for students from diverse academic backgrounds. Classic AI topics to
be covered include intelligent agents, search techniques, knowledge representation and
reasoning with uncertainty. Further, an overview of AI applications in Machine Learning,
Natural Language Processing and Image Recognition shall be given, with a briefing on some
recent trending topics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to –
1. Apply classic AI techniques to create intelligent agents
2. Leverage methods of dealing with uncertainty
3. Get an overview of various technology enablers for solving AI problems including
various Machine Learning techniques, Deep Learning and some of its applications to
Natural Language Processing and Image Recognition
