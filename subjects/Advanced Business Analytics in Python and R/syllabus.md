Pre-requisite: Introduction to Business Statistics, Analytics & Modelling-I, Introduction to
Business Statistics, Analytics & Modelling-II, Introduction to Programming in Python
Course Objective
To strengthen the data analytics skills using Python and R programming.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Employ simple and multiple linear regression, statistical experimentations, exploratory   data
analysis in Python and R to inform business decisions.
2. Explain concepts such as confidence intervals, dummy variables, correlation, multicollinearity,
confounding variables, interactions and main effects and outliers.
3. Apply classification methods such as Naïve Bayes, discriminant analysis, logistic regression to
inform business problems.
4. Apply Model evaluation techniques such as ROC curves, AUC and lift.
5. Apply machine learning techniques such as k-nearest neighbours, tree models, principal
component analysis and hierarchical clustering.
