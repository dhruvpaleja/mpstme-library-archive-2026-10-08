Pre-requisite: Knowledge of 12th grade level Physics
Course Objectives:
The knowledge of Physics relevant to engineering is critical for converting ideas into technology.
An understanding of Physics also helps engineers understand the working and the limitations of
existing devices and techniques, eventually leading to innovations and improvements. This
course aims to help students understand the basic concepts of physics thoroughly and lay the
foundations for the various engineering courses.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Describe the fundamental properties of semiconductors and elucidate the operational
principles of semiconductor devices. (K2)
2. Apply interference and diffraction principles to analyze optical phenomena and diffraction
grating applications. (K3)
3. Explain the working principles of LASER technology and fiber optics, and apply them to
real-world communication and engineering problems. (K3)
4. Interpret the concepts of gradient, divergence, and curl in scalar and vector fields, and
recognize their applications in basic engineering problems related to electromagnetism. (K2)
5. Explore the properties and applications of modern engineering materials like nanomaterials,
superconductors, and dielectrics. (K3)
6. Gauge theoretical principles through laboratory experimentation, enhancing problem-
solving abilities and technical proficiency in modern physics applications. (K3)
Detailed Syllabus:

and their applications.
