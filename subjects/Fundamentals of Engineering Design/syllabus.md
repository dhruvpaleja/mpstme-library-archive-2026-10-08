Pre-requisite: Fundamental of Mechanics-Statics and Solid Mechanics
Course Objective
This course is designed to encourage the students to apply scientific information and
imagination for the development of new improved machines or mechanical systems.  It also
helps to design the machine components using concepts of stress analysis, theories of failure
and materials science.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Articulate the design procedure and selection of material for a specific application
2. Explain the behaviour of material and its usefulness for engineering applications
3. Analyze the different types of loads and stresses acting on components
Detailed Syllabus
Unit  Description    Duration
1.   Introduction   07
Definition, classification of machine design, basic requirement of
machine elements, general consideration of machine design,
traditional design methods, general procedure of machine design,
engineering materials, material properties, materials selection.
2.   Fundamentals of Design   08
Sources of design data, use of standards, synthesis and creativity,
simple stresses in machine parts, torsional and bending stresses in
machine parts, factor of safety, service factor, maximum normal stress
theory, maximum shear stress, maximum distortion energy theory.
3.   Manufacturing Considerations in Design   10
Selection of manufacturing methods,  design considerations of casting,
design consideration of forging, design considerations of forging,
tolerances, types of fits, BIS system of fits and tolerances, selections of
fits, tolerances and manufacturing methods, surface roughness
4.   Design Against Static Load   10
Forms Modes of failure, stress strain relationship, stresses due to
bending moment, stresses due to torsional moment, eccentric axial
loading, stresses in composite bars, stresses due to changes in
temperature-thermal stresses, impact stresses, resilience
5.   Design of Fasteners    10
Bolted joints, riveted joints, and welded joints. Design for transverse
and eccentric loading.
Total    45
