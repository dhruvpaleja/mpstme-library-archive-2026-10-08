Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme

week)   week)   week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Fluid Mechanics
Course Objective
The course is aimed at imparting knowledge of the role of the sensors in a manufacturing system and
their working principles. It will also guide students to select the suitable type of transducer for a
desired application based on their performance characteristics.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify appropriate sensors for a given application
2. Analyze the sensor data and interpret appropriately
3. Develop applications based on the knowledge of sensors
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   04
Sensors and Transducers, Definition, application and differentiation, roles of
sensors in automotive systems and subsystems.; basic electromechanical,
electromagnetic and electrohydraulic actuator mechanisms,
2.   Thermal measurement   06
Working principle and application of thermocouple, Resistance type sensors;
liquid expansion and pressure sensors, bimetallic type, glass thermometer.
3.   Pressure and flow measurement   07
Bourdon type, piston type, capsules and bellows, diaphragms. strain gauge
type, piezoelectric type and other electric pressure sensors. differential pressure
flow meters- orifice meter, venturi meter and nozzle meter
4.   Force and Position Sensors   06
Mechanical, Hydraulic and Electrical strain gauge type. Optical, linear, rotary,
Inductive, ultrasonic and pressure based depth gauge.
5.   Sensors application
Processing data from sensors, interfacing smart sensors with microcontroller
07
and simple programs for basic operations, sensor selection and justification for
industrial applications.
Total   30
