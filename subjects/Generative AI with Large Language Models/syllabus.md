Pre-requisite: Basic understanding of GAN. Introductory knowledge of natural language processing
(NLP) techniques.
Course Objective
The course aims to equip students with the skills to adapt pre-trained large language models (LLMs)
for specialized tasks using fine-tuning and reinforcement learning strategies. Students will gain
expertise in transformer architectures, generative AI workflows, and model optimization, enabling
them to design, deploy, and enhance LLM-powered applications for diverse real-world challenges.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understand core concepts of generative AI, transformer architecture, and the principles of pre-
training in LLM-based text generation.
2. Apply instruction and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) methods, including LoRA and
soft prompts, to enhance LLM performance and scalability.
3. Utilize reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) to align LLMs with human
values for improved real-world application.
4. Evaluate and implement LLM applications that leverage reasoning techniques, retrieval-
augmented generation (RAG), and external application interactions, optimizing for task-
specific goals.
