Objectives:

1.To understand the construction and operating characteristics of AC motors.

Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to
1. Understand construction and operating principle of induction motor and synchronous
machine.
2. Acquire knowledge on characteristics of induction motor and synchronous machine for
different operating conditions.
3. Test and calculate performance parameters of induction motor and synchronous machine.
4. Analyse and select machine for specific application.
