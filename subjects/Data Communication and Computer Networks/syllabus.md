Pre-requisite: Basic Understanding of Computer Systems, Fundamentals of Programming
Course Objective
To introduce fundamental concepts of data communication and networking. To provide an
understanding of network architectures, models, and protocols. To develop practical knowledge
of basic network devices and transmission media. To familiarize students with the functionalities
of network layers and emerging technologies.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Demonstrate the concepts of data communication, including transmission modes, network
topologies, and practical applications.
2. Compare OSI and TCP/IP networking models, explaining their layer-wise functionalities
and data transmission processes.
3. Explain data link layer protocols and transmission media, focusing on framing techniques,
access control mechanisms, and multiplexing methods.
4. Apply networking principles by performing IPv4 addressing, subnetting, and configuring
network protocols such as ARP, RARP, TCP, and UDP using simulation tools for real-world
problem-solving.
5. Analyze advanced networking technologies, including wireless communication, network
security, IoT, SDN, and cloud-based networking.
