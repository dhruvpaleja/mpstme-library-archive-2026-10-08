Course Objective

Actuarial science uses mathematics and statistics to assess financial risks and value at risk of financial
and insurance industry products. It uses certain tools to predict financial implications of a future
uncertain event. The course introduces the students to this extremely useful life skill paradigm that
they can utilize in their professional and entrepreneurial endeavors. The course also equips students
with mathematical and statistical techniques that are of particular relevance to actuarial work. It
instils knowledge of Insurance Models, pricing and risk.

Course Outcomes

After the successful completion of this course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify the mathematical and statistical techniques used in actuarial science
2. Apply mathematical techniques used to model and value cash flows that are dependent on
mortality and morbidity risks
3. Interpret the results of the linear regression model, generalised linear model and theories on
interest rates

4. Analyse data using appropriate statistical analysis and modelling techniques
