Course Objective
The purpose of studying this course is to imbibe the knowledge of fluid properties, behaviour
and effects of fluid in static, dynamic conditions with perspective of applications in pressure
measurement, flow rate measurement, flow through pipes and to give information on
fundamental aspects of various hydraulic machines like turbines, pumps in comprehensive
manner
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to -
1. Understand the fundamental properties of fluids and their significance.
2. Describe fluid pressure concepts and demonstrate the working of devices like manometers
and Bourdon gauges for pressure measurement.
3. Analyze hydrostatic forces on immersed surfaces and discuss the equilibrium of floating
bodies.
4. Apply continuity and Bernoulli’s principles to fluid flow and interpret the working of flow
measurement.
5. Articulate the working principles of hydraulic turbines and evaluate their performance.
6. Illustrate the working principles of centrifugal and reciprocating pumps and discuss their
applications in various systems.
