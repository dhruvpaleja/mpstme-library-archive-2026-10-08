Pre-requisite: Engineering mathematics

Objectives:

1. To familiarize with basics of chemical engineering thermodynamics.

2. To define various thermodynamic functions and estimate from measurable
parameters.

3. To use thermodynamics rules to find the phase equilibrium and apply same
to various systems.

4, To be able to calculate and trace the equilibrium concentration and
conversions of a reversible reaction.

Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand the basic concepts and laws of thermodynamics.
2. Calculate thermodynamics parameters.
3. Apply concepts of phase equilibria, chemical reaction equilibrium for
different systems.
