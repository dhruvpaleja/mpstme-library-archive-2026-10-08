According to the fundamental postulate of statistical mechanics, all the possible
microstates conforming to the desired macroscopic condition are equally
probable. In other words, the probability of finding the phase point in one region
is identical with that in any other region subject to equal correspondence of the
regions with the given condition. Therefore, it is called the postulate of equal a
priori probability.
