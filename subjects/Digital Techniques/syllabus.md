Pre-requisite: Basic Understanding of semiconductor theory and Basic Electronics.
Course Objective
To introduce the fundamental concepts of and techniques underlying the construction of
digital systems. To provide an understanding of basic building blocks of digital logic. To
develop practical knowledge of designing digital logic circuits using basic building blocks
and necessary techniques required in computer hardware design. To familiarize students
with the application of digital system and emerging technologies.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, student will be able to-
1. Understand number Systems and codes
2. Explain the concept of digital system and logic simplification
3. Develop combinational digital circuits
4. Design sequential circuits for various applications
5. Implement Basic Digital Circuits using VHDL
6. Explore Logic Families and Programmable Devices
