Course Objective
This course provides basic orientation towards macroeconomic principles and help them
understand the functions of economy at Macro level. To combine elements of basic variables of
macroeconomics. To familiarize with importance of macroeconomic approach in managing
economy. To understand the application of macroeconomic theories at aggregate level. To
emphasize the role of macroeconomic policies and analyse their implication for economy along
with international linkages.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Learn various macroeconomic variables, circular flow of income and their linkages to macro
economy
2. Analyze aggregates of macroeconomics and understand goods market and money market,
Indian Financial System, Business fluctuations and Stabilization Policies
3. Orient oneself towards higher levels of learning related to economic planning, international
trade, foreign exchange market and international organizations at macro level
