Pre-requisite: Chemistry
Objectives:
1. To familiarize reactive intermediates and their role in chemical reactions.
2. To introduce theories of catalysts in chemical transformations.
3. To acquaint with mechanism of chemical reactions applicable to industrial
processes.
4, To familiarize chromatographic and spectroscopic techniques for
identification of compounds,
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand role of reactive intermediates/ species in chemical reactions.
2, Recognize concept of catalysis applied in chemical processes.
3. Relate use of kinetic & thermodynamic concepts to elucidate mechanisms of
organic reactions in green industrial processes.
4. Apply the separation & spectroscopic techniques to interpret structures of
chemical compounds.
