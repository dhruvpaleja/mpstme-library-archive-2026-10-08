Course Objective
To impart basic knowledge of science of materials and physical metallurgy amongst students
and develop an understanding of their applications in industry.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to -
1. Understand the basics of engineering materials
2. Demonstrate the mechanical behaviour of metals using destructive and non-destructive
testing
3. Interpret phase transformations in Iron-Carbon Alloys
4. Recommend heat treatment processes with the help of phase diagrams
5. Select appropriate ferrous materials for industrial applications
6. Select smart and nano materials for diverse engineering applications
