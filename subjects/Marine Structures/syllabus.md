## B TECH · Mechanical · AY 2026-27 · Sem 5

- Course as printed: Marine Structures (code 702ME0E042)
- Source: `04_Syllabi_Archive/B TECH/3RD YEAR/MECHANICAL/SEM V/B Tech, Mechanical, 3rd Year, Sem-V, Year, 2026-27.pdf`, page 19-21

````text
Program: B Tech (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: V
Course: Marine Structures   Code: 702ME0E042
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Strength of Materials
Course Objective
The course deals with the design of marine structures subjected to different types of environmental
loads and also discusses the detailed design of major and sub components of offshore structures.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify the function of marine and multipurpose offshore structures and systems
2. Relate the physical problem of the fluid–structure interaction in marine structures
3. Design different structural components in offshore applications
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Marine Structures   12
History of offshore structures, overview of field development, types of offshore
platforms, types of ships, design codes, offshore wind turbines, wave energy
converters, tidal energy converters, combined offshore energy systems,
multipurpose offshore structures and systems, submerged floating tunnels,
floating bridges, aquaculture and fish Farms.
2.   Offshore Environmental Conditions   10
Gravity load, impact load, wind load, wave load, current load, earthquake load,
ice loads, marine growth, scour.
3.   Offshore Structures Design   10
Guide for preliminary design, structure analysis, cylinder member strength,
tubular joint design, fatigue analysis, topside design.
4.   Helidecks and Boat Landing Design   08
Helideck design, design load conditions, boat landing design, riser guard design.
5.   Construction and Installation   05
Engineering the execution, construction process, installation process, lifting
analysis.
Total   45
Text Books
1. Madjid Karimirad, Constantine Michailides and Ali Nematbakhsh, Offshore mechanics: structural
and fluid dynamics for recent applications, 1st Edition, John Wiley & Sons, 2018.
2. Yong Bai and Wei-Liang Jin, Marine Structural Design, 2nd Edition, Tata McGraw Hill, 2016.
````

````text
Course Objective
The course deals with the design of marine structures subjected to different types of environmental
loads and also discusses the detailed design of major and sub components of offshore structures.
Course Outcomes
````
````text
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify the function of marine and multipurpose offshore structures and systems
2. Relate the physical problem of the fluid–structure interaction in marine structures
3. Design different structural components in offshore applications
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Marine Structures   12
History of offshore structures, overview of field development, types of offshore
platforms, types of ships, design codes, offshore wind turbines, wave energy
converters, tidal energy converters, combined offshore energy systems,
multipurpose offshore structures and systems, submerged floating tunnels,
floating bridges, aquaculture and fish Farms.
2.   Offshore Environmental Conditions   10
Gravity load, impact load, wind load, wave load, current load, earthquake load,
ice loads, marine growth, scour.
3.   Offshore Structures Design   10
Guide for preliminary design, structure analysis, cylinder member strength,
tubular joint design, fatigue analysis, topside design.
4.   Helidecks and Boat Landing Design   08
Helideck design, design load conditions, boat landing design, riser guard design.
5.   Construction and Installation   05
Engineering the execution, construction process, installation process, lifting
analysis.
````
