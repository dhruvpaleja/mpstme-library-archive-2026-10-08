Pre-requisite: Basic knowledge of Wireless Communication, and Introduction to

Course Objective
1. To understand network security and security protocols.
2. To study current security standards in wireless and mobile systems.
3. To learn threats and attacks and their solutions.

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Outline various approaches and mechanisms related to security.
2. Explain different network security protocols.
3. Understand concepts of cryptography.
4. Understand the various building blocks used in wireless network security.
