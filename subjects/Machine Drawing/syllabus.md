Program: B Tech (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: III
Course: Machine Drawing   Code: 702ME0C028
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks -100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Graphics and Design
Course Objective
The course is aimed at exposing the students to the concepts of symbolic representations, limits-fits-
tolerances and assembly-part detail drawings for machine components. This course is designed for
students to master the assembly and part detail drawings of machine components using symbols and
tolerances. The course uses a CAD software for rendering the assembly part details. The students are
also exposed to the concepts of auxiliary views and interpenetration of solids.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the conventions and prepare drawings of auxiliary views and intersection of solids
2. Apply the tolerances, limits, fits and surface finish on the existing and new drawings, as per
standards
3. Analyze the field of application of given machine and the functional requirement of individual
parts
4. Create 2D assembly drawings using CAD software packages
5. Create 2D part detail drawings using CAD software packages
