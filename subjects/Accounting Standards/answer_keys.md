As per AS 4, ‘Contingencies and Events Occurring after the Balance Sheet Date’,
adjustments to assets and liabilities are required for events occurring after the balance
sheet date if such event provides/relates to additional information to the conditions
existing at the balance sheet date and is also materially affecting the valuation of assets
and liabilities on the balance sheet date.

Since the lawsuit was pending before 31st March 2024 and the loss was confirmed later,
it is an adjusting event because it provides additional evidence of a liability that existed
on the balance sheet date. The company should Recognize the liability ofRs50 lakh in
