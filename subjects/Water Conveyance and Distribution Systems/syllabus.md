Pre-requisite: Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulics; Hydraulics and Fluid Machinery
Course Objective
This course focuses on various analyse & design procedures and optimization techniques for Water
distribution systems (WDS). It also focuses on pumps, valves in WDS.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Analyse and design WDS with different methods
2. Demonstrate pump, reservoir & valve behavior and rising main design in WDS
3. Optimize WDS with various methods
