Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Discern statements, premises, conclusions, assumptions, fallacies in any discourse and
thereby think clearly for themselves
2. Apply critical thinking to the teaching-learning experience self reflectively
3. Apply the principles of critical thinking to understand contemporary society through the

love for humanity and participatory action
