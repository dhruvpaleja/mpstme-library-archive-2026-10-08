Pre-requisite: Physics and Mathematics
Course Objective
The course emphasizes the understanding of the basic elements of servomotors with mathematical
modelling. The students learn to analyze motion control aspects of robotic drives before going on to
the advanced training of synthesis of robot motion planning using software tools.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Model the voltage pulses used for motion in dc/ac servomotors and stepper motors
2. Analyze the programming for motion planning in robotics
3. Develop an understanding of the software aspects in contemporary robotics
4. Analyze the motion planning aspects of industrial robotics
5. Synthesize robot configurations and motion for industrial problems
