Course Objective
This course intends to build a deep understanding of the ‘Smart City’ concept and the technology that

sustainable smart city.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Interpret the ‘Smart City’ concept & different models of Smart cities in the world
2. Identify and measure requirements of a smart city.
3. Propose innovative solutions for smart cities
