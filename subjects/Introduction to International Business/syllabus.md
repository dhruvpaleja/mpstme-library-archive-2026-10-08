Pre-requisite: Principles of Economics –I, II
Course Objective
This course seeks to equip students from all backgrounds with a basic understanding of the
international business environment and the fundamentals of international business strategy. In
addition, this course aims to develop the following skills in students
•   Critical thinking
•   Collaboration and teamwork
• Research and writing skills
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Explain how differences between countries create additional challenges for firms doing
business internationally
2. Explain how firms can create value by doing business internationally
3. Identify key decisions related to foreign market selection, market entry modes, and
international strategy that firms need to make when going international
4. Apply theoretical frameworks to make informed recommendations for such firms
5. Describe how foreign exchange markets work; derive business/economic implications
6. Evaluate arguments for and against government restrictions on trade and investment;
derive business/economic implications
7. Identify ethical challenges related to international business
8. Explain long-run trends and development in the international business environment;
derive business/economic implications.
