Pre-requisite: Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Network Security, Unix Programming
Course Objective
This course will provide a baseline of knowledge necessary for understanding and
performing digital forensic process.
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, student will be able to:
1. Understand the process and methodology of Incident Response Process.
2. Articulate and differentiate between the data collection of Windows and Unix System.
3. Describe the forensic duplication process and forensic reporting.
4. Implement the concepts of Network Forensics.
