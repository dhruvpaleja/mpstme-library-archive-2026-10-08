Program: B. Tech CSE (Cybersecurity)   Semester : VII
Course : Digital Image and Video Forensics   Code: 702IT0E032
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   per week)   week)   (Marks- 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Digital Forensics Fundamentals
Course Objective
Image and video evidence is a growing concern during an investigation as more and more of our
lives are captured in some form. From cell phones to CCTV security cameras in public places to
cell phone cameras, our everyday activities are constantly being recorded. This evidence, when
properly collected and analysed, can be crucial in solving crimes. This course will introduce
students to the concepts of video and image forensics. The course will cover how to properly
identify, collect, and handle digital video and image evidence in a way that maintains evidence
integrity and is consistent with best practices in the field.
Course Outcomes
After successful completion of the course, student would be able to -
1. Forensically extract image and video evidence from given media
2. Perform image and video authentication and detect tampering
3. Create and detect deepfake
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   4
Image and Video as legal evidence and related issues, principles of forensic
analysis of digital images and video, protocols and guidelines related to
collection, recovery, enhancement, analysis, and interpretation of image and
video evidence.
2.   Fundamentals of image and video processing and analytics   8
Basic concepts, image and video formats and standards, Overview of technical
issues related to video recordings (Data losses and impairments, visual
distortions and artefacts, video quality evaluation metrics) Various categories
of digital videos, based on the kind of recording device, placement of the
camera, mode of capture, and environmental conditions at the time of
recording, video analytics algorithms.
3.   Image and Video Authentication and Tamper Detection   12
Image and Video Source and Content Authentication, basic Tamper Detection
and their limitations (Hash value analysis, Hex editor analysis, Metadata
analysis, and Error Level Analysis (ELA)), Study of various methods of digital
image and video manipulation and their detection techniques (i.e., various
kinds of forgeries such as copy paste aka splicing, object removal forgeries,
upscale-crop, and resampling, frame-based tampering, Re-compression and
transcoding detection, etc.)
4.   Deepfake Detection   6
