Course Objective
This course will enable the students to appreciate the essential complementarity between 'VALUES' and
'SKILLS’ to ensure sustained happiness and prosperity, which are the core aspirations of all human beings. It
will facilitate the development of a Holistic perspective among students towards life and profession as well
as towards happiness and prosperity based on a correct understanding of the Human reality and the rest of
existence. Such a holistic perspective forms the basis of Universal Human Values and its movement towards
value-based living in a natural way. Moreover, it will highlight plausible implications of such a Holistic
understanding in terms of ethical human conduct, trustful and mutually fulfilling human behavior and
mutually enriching interaction with Nature. Finally, this course is intended to provide a much-needed
orientation in value education to the young minds.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Appraise that technical education without study of human values can generate more problems than
solutions.
2. Cultivate that natural acceptance (intention) and ‘respect’ is right evaluation, and only right
evaluation leads to fulfilment in relationship and living in harmony.
3. Design sustainable solutions to the problems in society and nature.
4. Integrate utilization of knowledge in streams of Technology/Engineering/Management/any other
area of study to ensure mutual fulfilment e.g. mutually enriching production system with rest of
nature, which needs to be nurtured, protected utilized in the right manner.
