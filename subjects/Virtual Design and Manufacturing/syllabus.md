Pre-requisite: Engineering Graphics & Design, Manufacturing Processes
Course Objective
The course emphasizes the use of software platforms used in the real world for computer aided
design, analysis and manufacturing, and computer aided manufacturing. The process of
culmination of computer aided engineering drawing into Computer Numerically Controlled
machine programming is elaborated in the course.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Create accurate and precise geometry of complex engineering systems and use the geometric
models in various engineering applications
2. Apply the concepts of machining for the purpose of selection of machining parameters and
cutting tools for CNC milling and turning
3. Program the four-axis CNC simulator and the actual CNC machine for creating a finished
specimen with a given geometry
4. Analyze the performance for specific machining sequence
5. Synthesize optimal machining designs for desired performance
