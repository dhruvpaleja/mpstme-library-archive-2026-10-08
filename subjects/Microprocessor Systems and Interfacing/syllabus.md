Pre-requisite: -
Course Objective
The course introduces microprocessor architecture and enables students to learn programming and
interfacing of microprocessor. This course will also help the students to understand the concepts of
advanced processors, programming and interfacing of microcontrollers.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Define the fundamental concepts of computer organization
2. Identify various types of microprocessors and microcontrollers based on chip
number and internal architecture
3. Explain the instruction   set, addressing modes and assembler directives used in
microprocessor and microcontroller programming
4. Describe digital interfacing concepts and their applications
5. Apply microprocessor and microcontroller programming concepts to develop solutions for
embedded control applications
