Pre-requisite: Basics of calculus, statistics and probability.
Course Objectives
The course aims at developing an understanding of linear, nonlinear, integer
programming and some modern methods of optimisation. It also aims at training the
students with various optimisation techniques necessary to formulate, solve and analyse
decision making problems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to -
1. solve problems based on linear, nonlinear and integer programming,
2. explore various methods of solving linear and nonlinear programming using
software tools,
3. apply the techniques of linear, integer and non-linear programming in decision
making.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1. Introduction to Optimization   5
Statement of an optimization problem, classification of optimization

sufficient conditions for constrained optimization problems (KKT

2. Linear Programming   11
Applications of linear programming in decision making, standard
form and geometry of LPP, Simplex algorithm, Two phases of the
simplex method, Duality in simplex method, sensitivity analysis,
Demonstration of problem-solving using software.
