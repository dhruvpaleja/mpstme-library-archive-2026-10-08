b. (8 marks) A structured model linking creativity, idea generation methods, creative problem
solving, and innovation can be explained as a sequential and cyclical process that transforms
original thinking into marketable outcomes.
Integrated Creativity–Innovation Model
1. Creativity (Foundation Stage)
Creativity is the starting point. It involves generating original thoughts, insights, and
perspectives.
It is influenced by curiosity, knowledge, imagination, and openness to new experiences.
2. Idea Generation Methods (Idea Development Stage)
Creative thinking is channelled into structured methods that systematically produce workable
ideas.
Methods: Brainstorming, Mind mapping, Design thinking, SCAMPER technique, Market
observation
These methods help convert abstract creativity into multiple potential solutions or
opportunities.
3. Creative Problem Solving (Refinement Stage)
At this stage, generated ideas are evaluated, refined, and improved to solve specific problems.
Key activities: Identifying constraints, Evaluating alternatives, Selecting the best solutions,
Testing feasibility
Creative problem solving ensures ideas are practical, relevant, and implementable.
4. Innovation (Implementation Stage)
Innovation occurs when refined ideas are successfully implemented into products, services, or
processes that create value.
Forms of innovation: Product innovation, Process innovation, Business model innovation.
Innovation converts ideas into real-world applications and market value.
Flow of the Model
Creativity → Idea Generation Methods → Creative Problem Solving → Innovation
Feedback Loop (Continuous Improvement): Innovation generates new experiences,
knowledge, and market feedback. This feedback stimulates fresh creativity, making the
process continuous and dynamic.
