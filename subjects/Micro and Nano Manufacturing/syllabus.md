- Source: `00_Original_Downloads/SYLLABUS/B TECH/12169__4TH YEAR.zip :: MECHANICAL/SEMESTER VII/B_Tech__Mechanical__4th_Year__Sem_VII__Year-2026-27_JzDgGsihdb.pdf`, page 25-27

````text
Program: B Tech /B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: VII/XI
Course: Micro and Nano Manufacturing   Code: 702ME0E024
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: -
Course Objective
Nanotechnology has found many applications in mechanical engineering and is projected to be of
greater relevance to mechanical systems in future. The main objective of the course is to expose students
with emerging manufacturing techniques for producing micro and nano level products. The course is
aimed at giving in-depth idea of the different techniques used in micro and nano
machining/manufacturing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Relate micro and nano mechanical systems and their applications in mechanical engineering.
2. Demonstrate machinability studies using traditional and advanced micromachining, joining,
and deposition processes.
3. Recognize the need and identify suitable applications of nano and micromachining in industry.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Micro and Nano Mechanical Systems   06
An overview of micro and nano mechanical systems and their applications in
Mechanical Engineering, Nano-machines, Nano-mechanics, Nano-metrology,
Nano-tribology, Nano-fluidics, Nano-manufacturing
2.   Conventional Processes: Micro-turning, Micro-drilling and Micro-milling   08
Introduction, micro-turning, micro-drilling, micro-milling, product quality in
micromachining
Micro-grinding and Ultra‐Precision Processes
Introduction, micro and nano-grinding, nano-grinding tools
3.   Non‐Conventional Processes: Laser Micromachining   09
Fundamentals of lasers, laser microfabrication, laser nanofabrication, micro-EDM,
micro-ECM.
Micro Forming Techniques
Laser micro-bending, micro-deep drawing, and micro-extrusion
4.   Micro and Nano Finishing Processes   09
Need for nano finishing, magnetic abrasive finishing, magnetorheological
finishing, elastic emission finishing, magnetic float polishing, Ion beam finishing.
5.   Micro Joining   06
Introduction to micro joining, challenges, micro resistance welding, ultrasonic
welding, micro TIG welding.
````
