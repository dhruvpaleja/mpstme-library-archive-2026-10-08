Pre-requisite: Basic understanding of neural networks and their architecture. Familiarity with
fundamental machine learning concepts. Proficiency in Programming, particularly in Python.
Course Objective
This course provides an in-depth understanding of deep learning principles, architectures, and
applications, focusing on key models like CNNs, RNNs, and GANs. It emphasizes practical skills in
building, optimizing, and deploying AI systems using frameworks like TensorFlow and PyTorch for
real-world domains such as NLP, computer vision, and healthcare.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understand the foundational principles of neural networks, including key architectures, training
algorithms, and optimization techniques in deep learning.
2. Analyze advanced neural network models such as CNNs, RNNs, and GANs, and their
applications in solving real-world problems like computer vision, NLP, and audio processing.
3. Implement practical deep learning strategies, including regularization, hyperparameter tuning,
and transfer learning, using frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Keras.
4. Evaluate and enhance deep learning models using techniques such as data augmentation, batch
normalization, and optimization algorithms to address challenges like overfitting and bias-
variance trade-offs.
