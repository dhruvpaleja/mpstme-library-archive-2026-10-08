Pre-requisite: Knowledge on natural language processing (NLP) techniques and applications.
Course Objective
The Large Language Models course is designed to provide students with a deep understanding of
transformer architecture and its core components, including self-attention and multi-head attention
mechanisms. Students will gain hands-on experience in implementing transformer-based models
using popular frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Hugging Face.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Identify the key components of transformer architecture, such as self-attention and multi-head
attention.
2. Implement transformer models using frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Huggingface
3. Evaluate various use cases of Large Language Models (LLMs)
4. Apply effective prompt engineering techniques to optimize results
