Most widely used process for manufacture of Benzaldehyde is by vapor phase oxidation of
Toluene. The catalyst used is*a mixture of Uranium oxide and Molybdenum oxide
impregnated on asbestos carrier. Additional small amount of Copper oxide is added to
catalyst mixture to reduce by product maleic anhydride formation. A mixture of toluene
vapor and air is blown in the reactor provided with catalyst bed maintained at 500°C. The
reaction gases are cooled in a condenser to around 120°C. The non - condensable gases are
removed and the condensable products, which include Benzoic acid, Anthraquinone and
Maleic Anhydride are taken to distillation column. Here, Toluene comes out as distillate,
which is recycled and crude benzaldehyde along with heavy ends forms the bottom product.
The bottoms are further taken to 2" distillation column where the crude benzaldehyde is
separated from the heavy ends. This crude benzaldehyde is taken to activated Carbon
absorber where impurities are removed and a pure benzaldehyde is obtained. Temp:500° C,

Compressed propylene is mixed with tripropyl aluminimu (TPA)
catalyst and carrier solvent (saturated naptha) at 200 atm pressure in a
stirred tank reactor fitted with heat transfer surface to remove
exothermic heat of reaction. A conversion of about 60% is achieved at
the temperature range of 150 — 300 C.

The reactor effluent passes to a flash drum where the carrier solvent
and catalyst is separated from products. The top products from flash
drum, enters distillation tray where light hydrocarbons, unreacted
propylene and 2-methyl-1-pentene are separated in the given order.
Isomerization occurs in a packed bed reactor. At the process
conditions of 150 — 300 C. The product 2-methyl-2pentene is
separated from 2-methyl-l-pentene, as distillate in the distillation
column.

The final step involves pyrolysis of 2-methyl-2-pentene to isoprene, in
a tubular cracking furnace with HBr as catalyst and steam as diluent.
Reaction occurs at the temperature range of 650 — 800 C and residence
time of 0.3 sec. The hot vapors from furnace are quenched with a
solvent to recover HBr and recycle it. The hydrocarbons are fractioned
to separate methane and lighter hydrocarbons in 1* distillation column
and to recover product as distillate in 2"! column.
