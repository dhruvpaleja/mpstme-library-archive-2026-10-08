Course Objective
The objective of this course is to introduce students to the concepts, principles, and practical
applications of virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) technologies. Emphasizing the
design and development of interactive VR and AR experiences, this course covers the historical
context, fundamental theories, and various interaction techniques in these fields. Students will gain
hands-on experience in developing applications for modern VR and AR systems.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify the differences in AR/VR concepts and technologies
2. Apply the acquired knowledge to develop functional AR and VR applications
3. Design and deploy basic AR/VR applications
4. Acquire essential scripting skills in C# to develop interactive AR and VR experiences.

This course is designed to give numerous perspectives on Virtual reality and Augmented
Reality based on fundamentals of sensation, perception, engineering aspects next generation
technologies and gaming
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the working of Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) systems
2. Illustrate the use of hardware that enables design and implementation of VR & AR
systems
3. Apply the concepts of the visual perceptions in VR-AR technologies
4. Utilize the concepts of motion, sensor and audio in VR & AR systems
