Pre-requisite: Physics, Chemistry
Course Objective
The course is aimed at imparting basic knowledge of science of materials and physical metallurgy. This
course introduces and cements the concept of structure property correlations. It is designed to lay the
foundation for the detailed understanding of mechanical behavior of materials, phase diagrams, heat
treatment and failure of materials. The students are also introduced to certain applications of ferrous,
non-ferrous and new age materials.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Relate mechanical properties with the mechanical processing of materials
2. Demonstrate the mechanical behaviour of metals using destructive and non-destructive testing
3. Analyze the root cause of failure in different types of materials
4. Interpret Phase Transformations in Iron-Iron Carbon Alloys
5. Recommend suitable heat treatment processes with the help of phase diagrams.
