Pre-requisite: Mathematics-I, Mathematics-II
Objectives
The course aims to acquaint students with Vector Algebra, Integration and its applications,
Differential Equations, Complex Numbers and Probability all of which are fundamental in
science, engineering and technology. Additionally, the course fosters the development of critical
thinking, enhances problem-solving skills, and prepares students for advanced studies.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to
1. Execute operations on vectors and complex numbers
2. Determine the equations of lines and planes in three dimension and compute distances and
angles
3. Find indefinite and definite integrals of functions using appropriate methods and apply
them to find areas under curves
4. Solve first order and first degree variable separable and homogeneous differential
equations
5. Understand the fundamentals of probability and find probabilities of events
6. Apply the concepts of vector algebra, integration, differential equations, complex numbers
and probability to solve real life problems
