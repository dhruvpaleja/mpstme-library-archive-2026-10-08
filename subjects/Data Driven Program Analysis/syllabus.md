2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Programming for problem solving
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to provide an introduction to Data mining and concepts of Business Intelligence. It
also outlines data mining as a step in KDD and further provides information on classification methods and
frequent pattern mining
Course Objectives
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Describe the key concepts, processes, and advantages of data mining and its applications in real-
world business scenarios.
2. Explain the architecture, components, and development phases of Business Intelligence systems and
Decision Support Systems.
3. Illustrate the Knowledge Discovery in Database (KDD) process and differentiate between data matrix
and dissimilarity matrix with respect to data mining functionalities.
4. Construct and apply basic classification models such as ID3 and Naïve Bayes to solve simple
prediction and categorization problems.
5. Analyze association rules using support, confidence, and frequent itemset mining techniques (e.g.,
Apriori algorithm) for market basket analysis and recommendation systems.
