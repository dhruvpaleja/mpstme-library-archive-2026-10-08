Pre-requisite: Engineering Mechanics, Materials Engineering, and Strength of Materials
Course Objective
This course is designed to encourage the students to apply scientific information and imagination for
the development of new improved machines or mechanical systems. It also helps to design the
machine components using concepts of stress analysis, theories of failure and materials science.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1.   Articulate the design procedure and selection of material for a specific application
2. Design machine elements such as cotter joints, knuckle joints, and levers subjected to axial
loads
3. Apply design methodologies to develop shafts, keys, and couplings for industrial applications
4. Analyse different types of power screws based on their load-carrying capacity, efficiency, and
application suitability
5. Design various springs considering strength, stiffness
6. Evaluate the performance of fasteners and welded joints under various loading conditions
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Fundamentals of Design   05
Basic procedure, sources of design data, use of standards, synthesis and creativity,
material selection, factor of safety, service factor, stress concentration, theories of
failure, maximum normal stress theory, maximum shear stress, maximum
distortion energy theory.
2.   Design of Simple Machine Parts   06
Cotter joint, knuckle joint, levers, and turn buckle.
3.   Design of Shafts   10
ASME code, design of shaft on strength and torsional rigidity basis, design of
transmission shaft.
Design of Keys
Saddle, sunk, feather, and woodruff.
Design of Coupling
Muff, flange, flexible and bush pin coupling.
4.   Power Screws   08
Forms of thread, multiple threads, torque analysis with square and trapezoidal
threads, self-locking, collar friction torque, stresses in power screw, screw jack.

6.   Design of Fasteners   07

(BTMA04013)
Objectives:
1. To understand the impact of different types of stresses on components
2. To introduce the fundamental concepts of design of simple machine parts.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand the importance of materials in design and select appropriate materials for
safe design.
2. Apply the principle and basic procedure of machine components design.
3. Analyze the different types of loads and stresses acting on components.
4. Design of various machine components like shafts, couplings, springs, joints, gears and
belts.
Detailed Syllabus: (per session plan)
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Stress Analysis:   09
Design process-Selection of materials-preferred numbers, fits and
tolerances, direct, bending and shear stress-combined stresses-eccentric
loading on machine members-stress concentration and notch
sensitivity.
2.   Design of Shafts, Coupling and Springs:   12
Design of solid shafts based on strength, rigidity. Design of muff
coupling and rigid flange coupling, Design of coil springs.
3.   Design of Joints:   10
Design of bolted joints including axial and tensile loading, Knuckle
joints, Cotter joints, Bolt of uniform strength, Thread locking devices,
Design of riveted joints for structures.
4.   Design of Gears:   08
Design of spur gear based on contact stress and beam strength and
based on Lewis and Buckingham equations.
5.   Design of Flexible Drives: Design of flat belt and V-belt drives.   06
