Program: B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: V
Course: Introduction to 3D Printing Technology   Code: 701ME0C018
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous Term End Examinations
(Hours per   (Hours   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   (TEE)
week)   per week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Scaled to 50 marks   Scaled to 50 marks
Pre-requisite: Basic Manufacturing Processes, Basic Machine Drawing
Course Objective
The objective of the course is to impart introductory knowledge of 3D printing and its capabilities in
the modern digital manufacturing industry. The course has been structured to acquaint students with
the operating principles and applications of most prominent 3D printing technologies.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students would be able to -
1. Summarize the fundamental principles of various 3D printing technologies
2. Use the right materials for respective 3D printing technologies
3. Identify critical areas where AM can be applied to create value for a product
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1. Introduction to 3D Printing   07
The world of 3D printing, growth of rapid prototyping and 3DP systems, reverse
engineering, traditional prototyping Vs. rapid prototyping, rapid tooling, rapid
manufacturing, classification of rapid manufacturing processes: additive,
subtractive, formative, generic RP process. CAD model preparation, data
interfacing, part orientation and support generation, slicing.
2. Materials for 3D Printing   03
Use of multiple materials in 3D printing, multifunctional and graded materials in
3D printing, polymers, common materials used – ABS, PLA, PC
3. Rapid prototyping systems   10
Material extrusion process, vat polymerization, Laminated Object Manufacturing.
4. Post-Processing in 3D Printing   05
Support material removal, improvement of surface texture, accuracy improvement,
and aesthetic improvement.
5. Applications of 3D Printing Technologies   05
Applications of 3D printing in various industries - aerospace, automotive,
biomedical and other industries.
Total   30
Text Books
1. C.P. Paul, Jinoop, “Additive Manufacturing”, McGraw Hill,1st edition, 2021
2. Gibson I, Rosen D W, Stucker B, “Additive Manufacturing Technologies: Rapid Prototyping to Direct
Digital Manufacturing”, Springer, 2nd edition, 2015(Classic Book)
