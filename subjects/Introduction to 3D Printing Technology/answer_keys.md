Prepare the 3D printer by loading the filament or resin, calibrating the bed, and setti
ng parameters such as layer height, print speed, and temperature.
4. Printing Process
The printer deposits material layer by layer according to the sliced file, gradually b
uilding the 3D object from bottom to top. The process can take minutes to several ho
urs depending on size and complexity.
5. Post-Processing
Remove the printed object and clean it from supports or excess material. Additional
steps may include sanding, painting, curing (for resin prints), or assembly if multiple
parts were printed separately.
These steps provide a clear, generalized workflow for 3D printing applicable across
most technologies such as FDM, SLA, or SLS.
