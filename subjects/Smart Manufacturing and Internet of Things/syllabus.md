````text
Program: B Tech (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: V
Course: Smart Manufacturing and Internet of Things   Code: 702ME0E038
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Engineering Mathematics I, II, III
Course Objective
The advent of modern technology and fourth Industrial revolution, particularly the industrial Internet
of things, has brought enormous changes to the manufacturing industry. This course offers a
simplistic but comprehensive introduction of the facets of primarily the industrial IoT, and growth of
smart factories.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Describe the components and control concepts of Internet of Things
2. Relate sensing principles and Wireless Sensor Network
3. Create IoT applications for smart machines
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Things and Internet   03
IoT ecosystem, applications, challenges and development approach.
2.   Network of ‘Things’   03
The primitives, elements, additional considerations.
3.   IoT Building Blocks – Hardware and Software   10
IoT building blocks, the smart things, smart thing sensor, smart thing
communicator,   smart thing actuator,   smart thing controller,
microcontroller (MCU), development boards, packet tracer and MCUs,
smart thing capabilities, the IoT gateway, network infrastructure, IoT
cloud, virtual resource pool, application server, database servers, load-
balancing servers, IoT analytics, IoT analytics – tools and techniques, IoT
analytics   life cycle, IoT applications.
4.   Smart Manufacturing   10
Manufacturing concerns, industry 4.0 and related models, smart manufacturing,
framework   for green manufacturing, collaborative IoT   for advanced
manufacturing, IoT implementation factors, three dimensions of smart
manufacturing processes.
B Tech/Mechanical/Sem-V/2026-27/Page 27
SVKM’s NMIMS Deemed-to-be University
Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering
5.   Smart Applications   04
Online   predictive modeling, monitoring and   intelligent   control   of
machining/manufacturing and logistics/supply chain processes; smart energy
management of manufacturing processes and facilities.
Total   30
Text Books
1. Ravi Ramakrishnan, “Internet of Things: approach and Applicability in Manufacturing”, 1st Edition,
CRC Press, Taylor and Francis Group, 2019.
````

````text
Pre-requisite: Engineering Mathematics I, II, III
Course Objective
The advent of modern technology and fourth Industrial revolution, particularly the industrial Internet
of things, has brought enormous changes to the manufacturing industry. This course offers a
simplistic but comprehensive introduction of the facets of primarily the industrial IoT, and growth of
smart factories.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Describe the components and control concepts of Internet of Things
2. Relate sensing principles and Wireless Sensor Network
3. Create IoT applications for smart machines
````
