Pre-requisite: Kinematics of Machinery (BTIME05008)
Objectives:
 To introduce estimation of inertia forces in reciprocating parts of the engines.
 To impart knowledge of theory and working of flywheel, brakes and clutches.
 To provide the understanding about working of belt and rope.
 To learn the types of governors and gyroscopic effects on different vehicles.
Outcomes :
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
 Analyze the static and dynamic forces of flywheel for reciprocating prime movers
and machines.
 Explain turning moment diagram for engines, working of flywheel, clutches,
brakes and governors.
 Select the machine components like belt, ropes and chains for required
mechanisms.
 Apply gyroscopic couple on stability of an automobile, airplane and ship.
