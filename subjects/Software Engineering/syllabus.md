Course Objective
The objective of the course is to familiarize the students with Software engineering principles,
practices and standards required to develop quality software. The course also intends to develop
the ability and skills for requirement analysis, design and modelling.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the students will be able to -
1.Explain the characteristics of various process models used in the development of a software
project (K1)
2.Demonstrate various Analysis and Design models that provide a basis for the software
development (K2)
3.Apply UML concepts for modeling software functionality for a given scenario(K3)
4.Compare architectural design for real world problems (K4)
5.Evaluate different project estimation techniques such as Lines of Code (LOC), Function Point
(FP), Use Case-based Estimation (K5)
6.Develop test strategies for conventional and object-oriented software, including validation and
system testing(K3)
