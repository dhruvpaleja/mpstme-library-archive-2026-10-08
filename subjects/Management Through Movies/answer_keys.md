Q1 (a) Differentiate between Classical and Neo-classical approach. [5]
Ans: The Classical approach focuses on efficiency, hierarchy, and structure (Taylor,
Fayol, Weber), viewing workers as tools for productivity. The Neo-classical approach
emphasizes human relations, motivation, and communication, recognizing employees as
social and emotional beings (Elton Mayo).
Q1 (b) Explain Push and Pull factors for women entrepreneurs. [5]
Ans: Push factors include necessity-based reasons such as unemployment, financial
pressure, or job dissatisfaction. Pull factors arise from opportunity—like independence,
innovation, and self-achievement—motivating women to pursue entrepreneurship.
Q1 (c) Explain Holistic Marketing. [5]
Ans: Holistic marketing integrates all marketing activities—internal, integrated,
relationship, and societal marketing—to create unified value for customers and
stakeholders, ensuring consistent brand and organizational synergy.
Q1 (d) Describe any two strategies that organizations can use to ensure ethical business
practices (Ref: Enron). [5]
Ans: (1) Strengthen corporate governance and transparency in reporting. (2) Implement
ethics training and whistleblower policies. Enron’s collapse shows the danger of
neglecting accountability and ethical oversight.
Q2 A consumer electronics company is expanding into rural markets. As a management
consultant, suggest how POCCC principles can be applied to ensure smooth operations
and resource optimization. [20]
Ans: By applying Planning (set clear goals), Organizing (assign roles and resources),
Commanding (clear leadership), Coordinating (integrate marketing and logistics), and
Controlling (monitor results), the company can streamline rural operations efficiently.
Q3 (a) How can STP strategies help position a newly launched Echo Bot effectively? [10]
Ans: Through Segmentation, identify market groups (tech-savvy users, households);

Targeting, choose the most profitable group; and Positioning, create a unique brand
image based on innovation and convenience to gain a competitive edge.
Q3 (b) Compare and contrast the roles of an Entrepreneur and an Intrapreneur. [10]
Ans: An Entrepreneur creates and manages a new business with personal risk (e.g., Elon
Musk), while an Intrapreneur innovates within an existing firm (e.g., Google’s Gmail
project). Both drive innovation but differ in ownership and risk.
Q4 (a) Examine how Environmental CSR can enhance sustainability and performance.
[10]
Ans: Strategic CSR initiatives like waste recycling, energy efficiency, and sustainable
sourcing reduce environmental impact and enhance corporate reputation, leading to long-
term profitability and stakeholder trust.
Q4 (b) How can a farmer use AI tools to improve crop health and deal with challenges
like pests or weather? [10]
Ans: AI tools such as predictive analytics, drones, and soil sensors detect pest infestations,
predict rainfall, and guide fertilizer use—improving yield and reducing input costs in real-
time applications.
Q5 (a) Critically evaluate intrinsic and extrinsic motivation strategies in improving
employee performance. [10]
Ans: Intrinsic motivation (achievement, purpose) sustains long-term engagement, while
extrinsic motivation (pay, rewards) drives short-term performance. A balanced mix,
supported by Herzberg’s theory, enhances both morale and productivity.
Q5 (b) Analyze InnovateX’s culture using strong vs. weak culture and suggest
interventions. [10]
Ans: A weak culture shows inconsistent values, low morale, and poor communication.
Strengthening culture needs (1) leadership reinforcement of core values, (2) internal
communication channels, and (3) recognition for aligned behaviors.
Q6 (a) Examine how differing leadership approaches affect performance at ABC
Electronics. [10]
Ans: Autocratic leaders create control but reduce morale; democratic leaders foster
engagement but may slow decisions; laissez-faire leaders encourage innovation but risk
ambiguity. Variation causes uneven performance across teams.
Q6 (b) Assess what leadership balance can address morale, innovation, and coordination.
[10]
Ans: A situational leadership blend—directive when clarity is needed, participative for
innovation, and supportive for morale—can harmonize performance and motivation across
departments.
Q7 Analyze factors influencing success or failure of change at Alpha Textiles and suggest
strategies. [20]
Ans: Success depends on employee readiness, communication, leadership support, and
trust. To mitigate failure, management should ensure transparent communication,
employee involvement, skill training, and phased implementation.

Ans: Empowering women drives economic growth (job creation), social progress (education,
equality), and innovation (new market ideas). Encouraging female entrepreneurship fosters inclusive

Priya’s transformational approach inspires innovation but lacks consistency. Balanced leadership is
essential for sustained success.
Q6 (b) Assess long-term implications of transformational vs. transactional leadership.
Ans: Overreliance on transactional leadership risks burnout and rigidity, while excessive
transformational focus may cause goal drift. Integrating both fosters innovation with accountability
in dynamic tech environments.
Q7 Analyze factors driving change at ABC Manufacturing Ltd.
Ans: Internal factors include outdated technology, process inefficiency, and employee skill gaps;
external factors involve market competition, regulation, and customer demand. Addressing both
ensures readiness and smooth transition during change.
