Pre-requisite: Computer Programming-I
Course Objective
The objective of this course is to familiarize the learner with the principles of Artificial Intelligence
and their applications in the domain of Mechanical Engineering.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Formulate a system based approach towards problem solving
2. Explain prolog, knowledge representation, predicate logic and heuristic search
3. Apply Artificial Intelligence for solving problems in Mechanical Engineering
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration

systems
Prolog   05

Prepositional Logic
Syntax, semantics knowledge base and inference
Predicate Logic
Facts, representation of fact, mapping between facts and representation,
3.   properties and issues of knowledge representation system, instance relationship,
LSA, GT and LT predicates, unification algorithm, resolution in predicate and
prepositional logic
Rules
Procedural versus declarative knowledge, forward versus backward reasoning,
forward & backward chaining rule system
Problems, Problem Spaces and Search   02
Need of a system to solve a problem, problem as a state space search, requirement
4.
of a formal description of a problem, production system, control strategy & its
requirement, breadth first search and depth first search
