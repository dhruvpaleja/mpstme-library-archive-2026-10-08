Program: B Tech (Mechatronics Engineering and Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: VI/V
Course: Electric and Hybrid Automotive Technology   Code: 702MH0E026
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours per   Credits   Assessment (ICA)   Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Calculus, Engineering Mechanics,
Classical Physics
Course Objective
The objective of this course is to familiarize the students with the electric and hybrid vehicle
architecture, technologies and fundamentals. The use of tools to design and simulate electric and
hybrid vehicles is also covered.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Explain electric and hybrid vehicle technology
2. Compare motors, batteries and controllers used in electric and hybrid vehicles
3. Analyze the overall system performance using key metrics
4. Simulate electric hybrid vehicle systems
5. Synthesize modern EV technology with specific requirement
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Electric and Hybrid Vehicles Introduction
1.   EVs and hybrids, correlation of cost and emissions, some myth busting facts, EV   04
layouts and architecture, hybrid vehicle layouts
Vehicle Dynamics
2.   Introduction, Forces acting on a Rolling vehicle, Power required for rolling   06
motion, Modeling Real-World conditions.
Motors
Introduction, the Lorentz Force law, Coulomb’s, Biot-Savarts law, brush and
3.   05
brushless motors, reluctance Motors, Faraday’s law, AC induction motors, motor
efficiency, maximum acceleration.
Batteries
4.   Introduction, battery fundamentals, lithium-ion batteries, battery characteristics,   02
electric vehicle charging.
Controllers
5.   Introduction, circuit elements, step-down DC controllers, step-up/down DC   03
controllers, AC controllers.
Incentives and Barriers
6.   Introduction, national incentives, state incentives, FAME-I, FAME-II, e-AMRIT.   04
Barriers to development of EV infrastructure in India.
Modeling and Simulation of Electric and Hybrid Vehicles
7.   Fundamentals of vehicle system modeling, HEV Modeling using simulation   06
software.
