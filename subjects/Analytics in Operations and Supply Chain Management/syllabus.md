Prerequisite
• Business Statistics

Course Objectives
• Understand key analytics concepts in Operations and Supply Chain Management
• Apply statistical and optimization techniques to operational problems
• Develop data-driven solutions for supply chain decision-making
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand and apply key concepts of Operations and Supply Chain Analytics and apply
descriptive analytics tools to summarize and interpret operational data.
2. Apply predictive analytics and optimization models to analyze and solve structured
problems in Operations and Supply Chain.
3. Design and develop data-driven analytical models and solutions for real-world Operations
and Supply Chain problems, integrating appropriate analytical techniques and contemporary
tools.
