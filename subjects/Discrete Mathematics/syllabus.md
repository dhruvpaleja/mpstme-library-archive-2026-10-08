Course Objective
The principal objective of the course is to develop students' logical reasoning and problem-solving
skills by introducing fundamental concepts of discrete mathematics essential for computer science
and engineering applications. It provides a strong foundation in set theory, logic, combinatorics,
graph theory, and abstract algebra, enabling students to analyze and construct mathematical
arguments. Through the study of proof techniques, recurrence relations, and discrete structures,
students will gain the ability to model computational problems and prove the correctness of
algorithms. The course also highlights the practical applications of discrete mathematics in real-
world contexts, such as data structures, cryptography, network security, and algorithm design,
equipping students for advanced studies and careers in computing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Apply fundamental concepts of set theory, relations, and functions to solve mathematical
and computational problems.
2. Construct valid mathematical arguments using propositional and predicate logic, proof
techniques, and induction methods.
3. Analyze combinatorial structures and recurrence relations to model and solve discrete
problems in engineering applications.
4. Implement graph and tree algorithms to solve optimization and network-related problems.
5. Demonstrate an understanding of abstract algebraic structures and their applications in
coding theory.
