# Principles and Practices of Management

**22** unique papers (76 copies across programmes) · 2006-07 to 2014-15 · 7 programmes · Sem 4, Trim 4, Trim 5, Trim 6

Download only this subject, sorted by academic year:

```powershell
python organize_by_subject.py restore --destination D:/Study --subject "Principles and Practices of Management"
```

Files land in `D:/Study/PYQs_By_Subject/Principles and Practices of Management/<academic year>/`. Identical copies of one paper (the portal often files the same PDF under several programmes) are placed once.

Text of the papers, ready for reading or AI analysis: [`papers.md`](papers.md) (every paper in full, newest first), [`questions.jsonl`](questions.jsonl) (one record per question with marks, year, exam, semester and department) and [`papers.jsonl`](papers.jsonl) (one record per paper). Answer keys, where the portal published them, are in `answer_keys.md`. See [`question_bank/README.md`](../../question_bank/README.md) for the fields.
