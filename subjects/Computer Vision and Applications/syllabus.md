Pre-requisite: Python for Data Analytics, Introduction to Data, Signal and Image analysis,

Course Objective
This aim of the course is to familiarize the students with the fundamental concepts of Computer
Vision. It will provide information on the techniques and algorithms that are currently used in
real-world computer vision systems and their applications. It will encourage students to
develop new systems based on integrating machine vision and deep learning.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the theoretical features and apply the practical aspects of computing with
images
2. Determine the foundation of image formation and image analysis. Explain the basics of
2D and 3D Computer Vision
3. Estimate fundamental perceptions tasks such as image segmentation, object tracking, and
object recognition
4. Summarize the key application areas of computer vision
