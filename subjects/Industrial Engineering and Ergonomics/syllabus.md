Program: B Tech Integrated (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: VI
Course: Industrial Engineering and Ergonomics   Code: 701ME0C011
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours per Credit Assessment (ICA) Examinations (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   2   0   4   Marks Scaled to 50 Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Basics of Manufacturing Engineering, Production Technology
Course Objectives
This course provides an understanding of various approaches to productivity and imparts
the knowledge of work-study techniques for examination of human work. This course clears
the concept of ergonomic principles to enable working effectively with minimization of
occupational stresses and its applications.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify the methods to measure parameters such as time and magnitude of activities
performed
2. Illustrate the productivity of resources for improvements
3. Describe the man and machine system, environmental factors, anthropometric data in the
ergonomics
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1.   Productivity   08
Industrial Engineering-Meaning, Definition, Objective, Need, Scope and
Evolution.
Definition of productivity, Individual enterprises, Productivity of
materials, land, building, machine and power. Measurement of
productivity,   factors   affecting   the   productivity,   productivity
improvement programs, wages and incentives.
2.   Method Study   08
Basic procedure of method study. Factors in selecting a job to a studied.
Recording the facts with charts and diagrams. Questioning technique for
development of new method. Role of Management and workers in
implementation.
3.   Facility & Workplace Design   05
Aligning the physical facilities- plant layout, equipment and work flow
as per the production processes. Principles of Motion Economy.
Standardisation of the process.
4.   Work Measurement   10
Significance   of   work   measurement.   Techniques   of   work
measurement. Need of work sampling technique. Conducting work
sampling study. Analytical estimation of work. Concept of rating factor.
Consideration of various allowances. Standard time for work.
Predetermined time standards. Use standard data. MOST for work
measurement. Use of time standards.

SVKM’s NMIMS Deemed-to-be University

5.   Ergonomics   07
Introduction, areas of study under ergonomics, system approach to
ergonomics model, man-machine system. Components of man-machine
system and their functions – work capabilities of industrial worker, study
of development of stress in human body and their consequences.
Design of Man-Machine System
Fatigue in industrial workers, quantitative qualitative representation and
alphanumeric displays, controls and their design criteria, control types,
relation between controls and displays, layouts of panels and machines.
Design of work places.
6.   Ergonomics and Environmental Factors   07
Introduction, ergonomics and thermal conditions, effect of noise and
vibration on human health and job performance, effect of light and
illumination on worker’s efficiency, effect of hazardous working
condition on worker’s efficiency.
Anthropometric Data Development and Application
Introduction   historical background and applications, status of
anthropometric data in India, development of anthropometric data,
mobility of human body movements around joints.
Total   45
Text Books
1. International Labour Office, Introduction to Work Study, 3rd edition, Oxford & I. B. H.
Publishing Co. Ltd, 2015.
2. R. Barnes, Motion and Time Study Design and Measurement of Work, 7th edition, Asia
Publishing, 2009.
Reference Books
1. M. S. Sanders and E.J. McCormick, Human factors in Engineering & Design, 7th Edition, Tata
McGraw Hill,2020.
2. K. Otto and K. Wood, Product Design –Techniques in Reverse Engineering and New Product
Development, 7th Edition, Pearson Education, 2020.
Laboratory Work
