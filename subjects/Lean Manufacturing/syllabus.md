## B TECH · Mechanical · AY 2026-27 · Sem 5

- Course as printed: Lean Manufacturing (code 702ME0E039)
- Source: `04_Syllabi_Archive/B TECH/3RD YEAR/MECHANICAL/SEM V/B Tech, Mechanical, 3rd Year, Sem-V, Year, 2026-27.pdf`, page 9-11

````text
Program: B Tech (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: V
Course: Lean Manufacturing   Code: 702ME0E039
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End Examinations
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Probability and Statistics
Course Objective
This course aims to appraise the students with different types of wastes and defects during manufacturing
and the remedial steps to eliminate them. It imparts knowledge associated with aligning production processes
with customer expectations and benchmark with efficient business processes.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Summarize the relationship between Work in Progress and cycle time
2. Articulate the integration of batch size, setup, manufacturing processes and waste
3. Apply lean manufacturing techniques to manufacturing, projects and service industries
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Lean Manufacturing
History of lean, fundamental purpose of lean manufacturing, comparison to other   05
methods. The seven Wastes, their causes and the effects, Implication of lean manufacturing
– traditional vs lean manufacturing.
2.   Tools of Lean Manufacturing
5S Principles, workplace organization, Process mapping / Value stream mapping, pull
Systems (Kanban and WIP systems), error proofing and set-up reduction, total quality   08
management (TQM), total productive maintenance (TPM), Toyota production systems,
Ford production systems.
3.   Value Stream Mapping (VSM)
Preparation for building a current state VSM, building a current state map (principles,
07
concepts, loops, and methodology). Application to the factory simulation scenario. Line
balancing, Poka Yoke, kanban, overall equipment effectiveness.
4.   Just - In - time Manufacturing (JIT)
Introduction, elements of JIT, uniform production rate, pull vs push method, kanban
07
system, small lot size, quick and inexpensive set-up, continuous Improvement, optimized
production technology.
5.   Designing for Quality
Introduction to concurrent engineering, quality function deployment (QFD) and failure
mode and effect analysis (FMEA), concept, methodology and application (with case
07
studies).
Quality in service sectors: Characteristics of service sectors, quality dimensions in service
sectors, measuring quality in different service sectors.

6.   Six Sigma
Meaning of six sigma, why six sigma? six sigma improvement model, DMAIC and
06
DMADV principle, building six sigma organization and culture, six sigma application, case
studies.
7.   Quality Circle
Quality circle structure, it’s operation, characteristics of quality circle, developing quality   05
circle in organization, basic problem-solving techniques.
Total   45
Text Books
1. M.K. Khanduja, Lean Manufacturing Implementation made easy, Inversio Solutions LLP, 2021.
2. Pascal Dennis, Lean Production Simplified, 3rd Edition, CRC Press, 2015.
````
