Q1
(i) Ethical Considerations in AI Decision-Making
Ethical considerations ensure AI systems are fair, transparent, and accountable. Guidelines such as fairness in
algorithms, explainability, and bias mitigation help in responsible AI development.
(ii) Ethical Issues in Smart City Data Collection
Privacy concerns in smart cities include mass surveillance, data security, and citizen consent. Balancing security
and privacy requires encryption, anonymization, and strict data governance policies.
(iii) Bias in University Admissions AI
Bias in AI admissions can lead to discrimination based on geography or demographics. Mitigation strategies
include diverse training datasets, fairness-aware models, and audit mechanisms.
(iv) Ethics in Climate Change Research Using AI
Ensuring transparency and accountability in climate data requires open data policies, independent verification,
and inclusive participation of researchers globally.
(v) GDPR and Its Global Impact
GDPR protects individual privacy by enforcing data security and user rights. Its impact on businesses includes
compliance costs, while governments must ensure lawful data handling.
Q2
(i) Ethical Frameworks in AI Recruitment
Utilitarianism promotes fairness for the majority, while deontology enforces strict rules. A hybrid approach
ensures AI hiring systems remain ethical and unbiased.
(ii) AI Explainability in Credit Approvals
Explainability ensures customers understand AI decisions. Methods to enhance transparency include SHAP,
LIME, and interpretable models like decision trees.

(i) Data Ownership, Privacy, and Anonymity in Social Media
- **Ownership**: Users should control their data.
- **Privacy**: Ensuring personal data is protected.
- **Anonymity**: Hiding identities while analyzing data.

Q4
(i) Responsible AI in Healthcare
Fairness, transparency, and accountability ensure ethical AI in healthcare. Techniques include explainability
models and human-in-the-loop approaches.
(ii) Ethical Challenges in Large-Scale Data Research
Issues include data fabrication, plagiarism, and privacy violations. Best practices include ethical review boards
and consent-based data collection.
Q5
(i) Bias in AI Loan Approval
Bias types: Data, model, and automation bias.
Mitigation: Fair dataset selection, adversarial debiasing, and fairness-aware algorithms.
(ii) GDPR vs. DPDP Bill
GDPR applies globally, while DPDP focuses on India’s data privacy framework. Multinational firms face
compliance challenges due to differing legal requirements.
Q6
(i) Algorithm Fairness in Fraud Detection
Ensuring fairness in fraud detection requires demographic parity, bias audits, and transparency in decision-
making.
(ii) Ethics in AI Content Recommendation
Challenges: Misinformation, filter bubbles, and engagement manipulation.
Solutions: Explainable AI, content verification, and ethical AI policies.
