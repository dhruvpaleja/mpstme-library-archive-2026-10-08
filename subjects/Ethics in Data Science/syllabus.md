Pre-requisite: Understanding of basic ethical principles and philosophical frameworks. Familiarity with
concepts of data privacy, ownership, and protection. Awareness of algorithmic fairness and responsible
AI practices.
Course Objective
This course aims to equip students with essential knowledge of ethics in data science, covering fairness,
privacy, data ownership and responsible AI. Students will learn to integrate ethical considerations into
AI and data science to promote fairness, transparency and accountability.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understand ethical frameworks for assessing fairness and their application in data science.
2. Demonstrate knowledge of data ownership, privacy, and anonymity, and their implications for
data science.
3. Analyze and evaluate the impact of global and national data protection policies, such as GDPR
and the PDP Bill, on data governance frameworks and practices.
4. Evaluate and apply principles of responsible and constitutional AI to ensure fairness,
transparency, and accountability in AI systems.
