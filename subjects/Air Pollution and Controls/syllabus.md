Pre-requisite: Environmental Science
Course Objective
This course aims to provide students with a scientific and technical background in air pollution monitoring,
control technologies and prevention. Students will also be introduced to the Indian legislative framework on
air quality and to international conventions.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify the major air pollutants, their sources, chemical transformations in the atmosphere and
impacts
2. Interpret meteorological data for atmospheric stability and air pollutant transport and dispersion
3. atmospheric chemical composition both drives and responds to changes in the earth system,
including climate change.
4.   Select and compare air pollution control systems in industries.
