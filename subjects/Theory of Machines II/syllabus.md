Pre-requisite: Theory Machine-I (BTIME05001)

Objectives:
e To introduce estimation of inertia forces in reciprocating parts of the engines.
e To impart knowledge of theory and working of flywheel.
e To provide the understanding about working of belt, rope, brakes and clutches.

Outcomes :
After completion of the course, students would be able to:

e Analyze the static and dynamic forces of flywheel for reciprocating prime movers
and machines.

e Draw turning moment diagram for engines and explain working of flywheel.

e Select the machine components like belt, ropes and chains for required
mechanisms.

e Distinguish between various types of clutches and brakes.

Unit | Description Duration

1 Inertia Forces in reciprocating parts: Introduction, resultant effects of 14
a system of force acting on a rigid body, D-Alembert’s principle,
Approximate analytical method for velocity and acceleration of the
piston. Angular velocity and acceleration of the connecting rod. Forces
on the reciprocating parts of an engine neglecting weight of the
connecting rod. Equivalent dynamical system.

moment diagram for a single cylinder double acting steam engine,
Turning moment diagram for a four stroke cycle internal combustion
engine, turning moment diagram for a multi-cylinder engine,
fluctuation of energy, determination of maximum fluctuation of
energy, coefficient of fluctuation of energy, coefficient of fluctuation of
speed, Energy stored in the flywheel, Dimension of flywheel rim.
