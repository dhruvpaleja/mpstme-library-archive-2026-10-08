CO: 1   firms to hedge against adverse movements in prices, interest rates, or exchange
BL:   rates. They help in transferring risk from one party to another.
Understand   Role of derivatives in risk management:
1. Hedging Price Risk
Futures and options allow firms to lock in prices of commodities,
currencies, or securities, protecting them from unfavorable price
fluctuations.
2. Managing Interest Rate and Currency Risk
Instruments such as swaps and currency futures help firms manage risks
arising from changes in interest rates or exchange rates.
3. Portfolio Protection
Investors can use options or index futures to protect their portfolios from
potential market declines.
Thus, derivatives are widely used as risk management tools to reduce uncertainty
and stabilize financial outcomes.
