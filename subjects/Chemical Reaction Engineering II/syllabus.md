Objectives
This subject motivate students to analyse and design the multiple reaction
system, to impart basic knowledge of the real reactors considering residence
time distribution in various reactors and obtaining actual design parameters to
provide necessary skills to design strategy for Heterogeneous reactions
considering different types of reactors.
Outcomes:
After successfully completion of this course, students should be able to:
1. Understand kinetics of competing reactions and their influence on product
yield and selectivity.
2. Derive batch, CSTR, and PFR performance equations from general material
balances.
3. Understand the concept of Residence Time Distribution (RTD) in various
reactors and obtain the actual design parameters to design Real Reactor
4. Find the model equation and use this model to design the reactors used for
heterogeneous non catalytic reactions.
