Pre-requisite: Material and energy balance, Chemical engineering thermodynamics
Objectives
The objective of this course is to develop the understanding of Reaction mechanism,
kinetics, and experimental analysis methodology that will provide the skills to design
the Rate for a given system along with using basic knowledge of external factors such
as heat and pressure conditions.
Course Outcomes
After successfully completion of this course, students will be able to -
1. Understand fundamentals of kinetics including definitions of rate and forms of
rate expressions and relationships between moles, concentration, extent of
reaction and conversion.
2. Analyse the effect of temperature and concentration in Rate equation.
3. Understand basic concept of catalyst and solve problems related to kinetics of
catalyst.
4. Analyse the non-catalytic heterogeneous reactions and identify the control
mechanism
