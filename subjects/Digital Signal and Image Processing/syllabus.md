Course Objective
This course aims at introducing idea of discrete time signals, systems and their classification. An
image is a discrete time signal, which is analyzed by certain algorithms for digital image
processing, to develop a foundation that provides deep insights into this field. This fact is also
emphasized in the course.

After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Apply various transforms for Discrete Time signals
2. Apply spatial domain techniques for grey and color image enhancement
3. Synthesize various morphological operations and segmentation techniques for images
4. Analyze the important parameters and their impact on filtered signals
