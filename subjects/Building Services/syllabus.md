about LT and HT. Back up power calculation, basic understanding of
DG sets, UPS, inverters, placement of DG sets, ventilation requirement
of DG sets.

3. | HVAC: Natural and artificial ventilation, Requirement as per zoning 03
rules, brief on planning and designing, Types of air conditioning
system¢, General guideline about AC equipment and space planning.
