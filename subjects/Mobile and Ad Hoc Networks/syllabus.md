Course Objective
1. To impart knowledge about ad hoc networks.
2. To learn the protocols at various layers in ad hoc networks.
3. To be familiar with the current and emerging trends in ad hoc Wireless Networks.
4. To analyse QoS and energy management in ad hoc wireless networks.

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify the design consideration of Adhoc networks .
2. Recognize the protocols for different applications.
3. Evaluate the existing network and improve its quality of service.
4. Identify security measures at different levels.
