Pre-requisite: Building Design and Drawing, Surveying, Introduction to Civil Engineering
Course Objective
This course focusses on various aspects of estimating quantities of items of works involved in
buildings, water supply and sanitary works, road works and irrigation works. The course also covers
the rate analysis, drafting of specifications for items of civil works, tenders, contracts and valuation.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Compute quantities for items of civil works and prepare estimate
2. Draft detailed technical specifications and prepare rate analysis for items of civil works
3. Prepare a tender document and valuation report
4. Analyze bar bending schedules and interpret mass haul diagrams for construction
planning
5. Conduct material survey and assess cost sensitivity for various construction materials
6. Explain different types of contracts, tendering procedures, and legal aspects involved
in project execution
