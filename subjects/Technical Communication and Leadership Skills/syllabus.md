3   2   0   4   Marks scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite
Completion of Business communication and Value Science-I & Business Communication and
Value Science-II
Course Objective
This course aims of developing an ability to engage in persuasive, technical and domain specific
communication in a clear and format driven manner. Also, the course aims at developing in the
stakeholders’ critical leadership skills and an ability to effectively engage in crises as well as
cross cultural communication.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Demonstrate the ability to utilize critical leadership skills with understanding and
empathy to communicate and to solve problems
2. Apply the conceptual insights derived through this course to efficiently engage in
workplace as well as cross cultural communications
3. Analyze a crisis situation to communicate promptly and in an impactful manner towards
effectively managing and resolving crisis
4. Create technical and scientific papers and writings to disseminate evidence based field
specific knowledge

Presenting Business analyses based Feasibility Reports using persuasive
communication strategies.
