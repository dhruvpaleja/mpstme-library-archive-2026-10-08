- Course as printed: Industrial Automation and Robotics (code 702ME0E041)

Program: B Tech (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: V

Pre-requisite: Engineering mechanics
Course Objective
This course aims to acquaint students with control of mechanical operations, sensors and their
interfacing with microcontrollers. The course offers learning of pneumatic/hydraulic systems,
electrical controls and programmable logic controllers.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify the suitable sensors and actuators for a mechatronics systems using pneumatic and
hydraulic systems and electro-pneumatic circuits
2. Apply suitable logic controls and develop ladder logic programming
3. Design an appropriate robot for given industrial applications and carryout kinematic analysis
for a robot
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Automation
Definition and fundamentals of automation, reasons for automating, basic
elements of an automated system, power, program and control system.
Introduction of mechatronics and its block diagram representation, key elements   06
of mechatronics, applications of mechatronics, domestic, industrial etc.

function for each element of mechatronic system, reduction methods and its
numerical treatment for represented block diagram.
2.   Selection of Sensors and Actuators
Criteria for selection of sensors based on requirements, principle of measurement,
sensing method, performance chart etc. Displacement, temperature, acceleration,
force, pressure based on static and dynamic characteristics. Actuators, selection

of actuators based on principle of operation, performance characteristics,
maximum loading conditions, safety etc. Principle and selection of mechano-
electrical actuators, DC motors, stepper motors, solenoid actuators, servo motors,
BLDC.
3.   Pneumatics and Hydraulics
Hydraulic and pneumatic devices, different types of valves, actuators and
auxiliary elements in pneumatics and hydraulics, their applications and use of
06
their ISO symbols, synthesis and design of circuits (up to 2 cylinders) pneumatic,
electro- pneumatics and hydraulics, electrohydraulic. Discrete control system,

Course Objective
This course aims to acquaint students with control of mechanical operations, sensors and their
interfacing with microcontrollers. The course offers learning of pneumatic/hydraulic systems,
electrical controls and programmable logic controllers.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Identify the suitable sensors and actuators for a mechatronics systems using pneumatic and
hydraulic systems and electro-pneumatic circuits
2. Apply suitable logic controls and develop ladder logic programming
3. Design an appropriate robot for given industrial applications and carryout kinematic analysis
for a robot
