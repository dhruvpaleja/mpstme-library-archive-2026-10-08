Pre-requisite: Nil
Course Objective
This course aims to provide fundamental knowledge of surveying principles and field methods
including linear, levelling, angular, and tacheometric measurements along with curve setting. It also
aims to introduce modern surveying instruments and techniques for accurate data collection and
mapping in civil engineering applications.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the fundamentals of surveying instruments with their suitability (L2)
2. Make use of levelling instruments and methods for obtaining levels (L3)
3. Perform angular and distance measurements using theodolite and tacheometry methods (L3)
4. Construct the curves using surveying instruments (L3)
5. Use modern surveying instruments and GIS technologies for data collection and analysis (L3)
