Pre-requisite: Chemistry up to standard XII level.

Objectives:

1. To identify types of reactive intermediates in chemical reactions and its applications to
various types of chemical reaction

2. To comprehend molecular orbitals theories, concepts of chemical thermodynamics and

reaction kinetics.

3. To understand the fundamentals of chemical reactions with relevance to Industrial
Applications

Outcomes:

After completion of the course, students would be able to :

1. Apply the laws of thermodynamics for calculating the changes in energy, enthalpy,
entropy& Gibbs energy.

2. Demonstrate the factors affecting reaction rates.

3. Apply the theories of bonding, metal complexes and catalysts for various chemical
processes.

4, Apply the principles of chemical reaction to arrive at mechanism of chemical reactions

applicable to industrial processes
