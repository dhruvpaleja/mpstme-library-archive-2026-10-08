Pre-requisite: Theory of structures, Concrete technology
Course Objective
This course aims to bring the students to such a level so as to enable them to take the appropriate
decision in respect of choice of prestressed section over R. C. C. as a civil engineer. Also to make the
candidate to understand the analysis of Prestressed Concrete sections and losses in prestress. This
course focuses to make the candidate able to understand and implement the guidelines of Indian
Standard code for analysis and design sections using limit state philosophy.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the concept of pre-stressing its casting techniques and applications, behaviour of the
pre-stressed structures vis-à-vis that of the RCC structure
2. Apply the concept to take the decision with respect to the choice of pre-stressed section over RCC
3. Analyse the various pre-stressed components of the structure and design the same using relevant
IS Code
