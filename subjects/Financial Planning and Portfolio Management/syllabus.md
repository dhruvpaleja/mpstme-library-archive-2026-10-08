Pre-requisite: Financial Management
Course Objectives
 To discuss financial planning process and portfolio management process.
 To analyze portfolios using different models and evaluate risk and returns of the
portfolios.
 To orient students on application of portfolio theories on stock market data.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe financial planning process and key principles in construction of personal
portfolios.
2. Estimation and Evaluation of portfolios return and risk.
3. Analyze portfolios using different theories of Portfolio Management.
4. Apply Portfolio Management theories for construction of optimum portfolios.
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Financial Planning Process   4
Personal Financial Planning process, Defining financial goals, Developing
financial plan and strategy, Implementation of financial plan and strategy,
Evaluating financial plan and strategy.

Investment avenues - Insurance Products, Retirement Products, Mutual
fund schemes, Real Assets, Alternative Investment; Investment objectives
and constraints, Strategic Asset Allocation, Investment Strategy during
uncertainty.
3   Portfolio Analysis   2
Component of risk and return, Beta of a portfolio, portfolio diversification,
Marginal productivity of Incremental Assets.
4   Markowitz Model   2
Efficiency Frontier, Evolution of Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), The

Security Market Line (SML), Application of CML and CAPM.
6   Arbitrage pricing theories (APT)   2
Arbitrage pricing Model, Arbitrage Mechanism, Comparison of CAPM and
APT, Applications of APT.
7   The Sharpe Index Model
Single Index Model, Corner Portfolio, Sharpe’s Optimal Portfolio,   4
Construction of Optimal Portfolio, Optimal Portfolio with Short sell.
8   Portfolio Revision
Need and Importance of Portfolio Revision, Pitfalls to be avoided in   2
Portfolio Revision, Portfolio Revision Techniques, Practical Problems in
Portfolio Revision.
9   Portfolio Evaluation
Measuring and Evaluating Portfolio Performance: Need for evaluation,
Meaning and Importance of Portfolio Performance.
- Measures of Return: Sharpe’s Measure, Jensen’s Differential Return
Approach, Treynor’s Approach   4
- Measuring and Evaluating Portfolio Performance: Buying the Index
Approach
-   Performance Evaluation of the Portfolio Manager Evaluating Asset
Class Managers, Decomposition of Returns
10   Equity Portfolio Management

Strategies, Types of Passive Portfolios, Active Management Styles and   2
Strategies, Combining Active and Passive Styles, Factor-Based Approach,
Equity Style Management, Book Value/Market Value Ratio.
11   Bond Portfolio Management
Active Bond Portfolio Strategies, Structured Strategies, Bond performance   2
Measurement and Evaluation
12   Portfolio management using Futures and Options
Features of Index futures contracts, Pricing of Index futures contracts, Stock   2
Index Arbitrage, Factors influencing Options prices, Elementary Investment
strategies.
