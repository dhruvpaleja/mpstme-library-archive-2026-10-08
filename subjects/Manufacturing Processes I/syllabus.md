Program: B Tech (Mechanical Engineering)   Semester: III
Course: Manufacturing Processes-I   Code: 702ME0C027
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous   Term End Examinations
(Hours   (Hours   (Hours   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   (TEE)
per week) per week)   per week)   (Marks-50)   (Marks -100)
2   0   0   2   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Workshop/Manufacturing Practice
Course Objective
This course is aimed at detailed introduction of various key manufacturing processes such as casting,
welding, forging, rolling, extrusion, drawing and machining. The course has been structured to impart the
in-depth understanding of industrial applications of various processes and equipment used in
manufacturing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Identify different types of patterns, moulding sands, and casting techniques
2. Explain the principles and applications of rolling, forging, and extrusion processes
3. Classify various welding techniques and their applications in manufacturing
4. Apply suitable metal forming and joining techniques based on material and process requirements
5. Analyze the working principles and machining operations of a lathe machine
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Metal Casting   09
Pattern Making
Types of patterns, allowances, colour coding.
Moulding sands
Types, properties, preparation and testing of sand. Core boxes, core making, types of
cores and their manufacturing; Gating system – runner and risers.
Moulding processes
Shell moulding, CO2 moulding, investment casting, die casting, centrifugal casting
and continuous casting; Study of various defects in castings.
2   Forming processes   08
Cold and hot working.
Rolling
Principle and mechanism, types of rolling mills, various rolling processes and their
applications, defects in rolling.
Forging
Classification of forging processes, basic categories and methods of forging.
Extrusion
Hot and cold extrusion, Extrusion equipment, defects in extruded parts.
Wire and tube Drawing
Metal stamping and embossing, blanking, piercing, bending, deep drawing.

Press tools
Different type of presses and their working, progressive die, compound and
combination dies.
3   Joining processes   09
Surface preparation for joining and various types of joints; classification of welding
processes, Arc welding - Types, submerged arc welding, gas metal arc welding,
shielded metal arc welding, tungsten arc welding – theory and their applications;
electron beam welding, laser beam welding, Solid state welding processes - ultrasonic
welding, friction welding, resistance welding and its types - spot, seam and projection
welding processes, welding of various metals, characteristics of good weld, weld
defects and weldability of metals; soldering, brazing and their applications; adhesives
for joining.
4   Lathe   04
Introduction, construction, working and operations performed on lathe, attachments
and accessories, cutting parameters such as spindle speed, feed and depth of cut,
Capstan and Turret lathe, automatic lathes and their construction.

Course Objective
This course is aimed at detailed introduction of various key manufacturing processes such as casting,
welding, forging, rolling, extrusion, drawing and machining. The course has been structured to impart
the in-depth understanding of industrial applications of various processes and equipment used in
manufacturing.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Recognize different metal casting processes, forming processes and joining processes along
with defects associated with them
2. Apply the knowledge, techniques, skills and tools of the manufacturing processes to broadly
defined engineering technology activities
3. Demonstrate the knowledge of construction of a lathe machine and its various operations
