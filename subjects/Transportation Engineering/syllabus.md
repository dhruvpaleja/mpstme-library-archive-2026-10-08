Course Objective
This course focusses on the principles of planning and design of highways, their geometric design
and principles of traffic engineering. It also focuses on planning of airports.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Classify the type of roads based on the loading conditions
2. Plan and design the highways
3. Illustrate various signaling processes system for highways, railway track components, and
traffic characteristics
4. Illustrate the runway patterns and basic aircraft characteristics
5. Test and interpret the highway material properties for quality control
