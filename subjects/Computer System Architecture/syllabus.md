Course Objective
To introduce fundamental components of a Computer System. To provide knowledge of
the basic principles of the organization, operation and performance of modern-day
computer systems. To make students aware of data transfer techniques, memory
organization and the working of Control unit of a digital computer. To familiarize students
with the architectures used to construct parallel computer components.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the students will be able to-
1. Discuss the functional blocks of computers and the interconnections
2. Understand the memory system.
3. Explain the components of the Central Processing Unit
4. Describe Input Output and Parallel Organization
