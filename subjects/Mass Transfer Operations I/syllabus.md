diffusion, mass transfer co-efficient and interphase mass transfer.

concept and operation of various types of gas-liquid contact equipments.

3. Determine NTU, HTU, HETP and height of packed bed used for Absorption.

4. To understand the principle of drying and design of drying equipment,
Humidification and cooling towers.
