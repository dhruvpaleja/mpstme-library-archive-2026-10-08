Course Outcomes - After completion of the course, students will be able to
1. Identify the steps in the DT process,
2. Recognize the steps in the empathize phase of DT and create Empathy Map,
3. Identify the steps required to conduct an immersion activity,
4. Create a prototype.

Pre-requisite: None
Objectives:
1. Understand the concept of Design thinking through engaging the students in
projects/ assignments that illustrate the various pillars of Design thinking
2. Apply the Process of Design thinking to achieve Innovation
3. Understand the stages of Design process that leads to Innovation
Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Develop a human centered approach to problem solving
2. Apply design thinking principles to come up with innovative solutions to the
problems, as new products, services, experiences or new Business models.
3. Develop a low fidelity prototype of the alternative Solutions to the identified
Problem

Unit   Description   Duration
Introducing the Concept of Design thinking,
Reading: HBR Article on Design Thinking
Case: ABC Nightline- IDEO Shopping Cart

Process of Design : The Phases used in a Design Process for Business

5   Step – 3: Ideate – Brainstorming/ Brain writing & other creativity   06
tools to generate ideas for solutions to the identified problem. Tools
for idea selection.
6   Step - 4: Prototype – Techniques of prototyping, examples of Jugaad,   06

Description of ICA   Marks
Test Marks   --
Term Work Marks   50
Total Marks :   50
Details of Term work:
The students would be required to do a Term long project based on application of tools &
concepts of Design Thinking.
The students projects will be evaluated as the following stage gates:-
1. Empathize Stage Deliverables- eg. Crucial observations/ identifying actionable
pain-points, Stakeholder canvas (Direct & Indirect users, Influencers, Facilitators),
User Journey Map
2. Define Stage Deliverables- eg. Affinity Diagrams based on Observations &
Insights and ‘How Might We…?’ Questions
3. Ideation Stage Deliverables- eg. Brainwriting 6-3-5 Worksheet, Brainstorming on
Clusters of solutions with Dot Voting/ Post-it Voting
4. Prototype Stage Deliverables- eg. User Journey Map (Comparison of Prevailing
Scenario with Proposed Scenario) and Lo-fidelity Prototype (Sketch/Data Flow
Charts/Screenshots)
5. Take to Market stage Deliverable- Strategy Canvas
