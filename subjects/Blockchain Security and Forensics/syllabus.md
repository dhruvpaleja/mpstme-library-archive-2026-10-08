Prerequisite: Introduction to Cryptography
Course Objective
This course introduces blockchain technology and cryptocurrency to students. Students will be able
to understand the working of blockchain and describe security issues related to blockchain. Students
will also be able to perform forensic operations on blockchain.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Explain blockchain technology and its various use cases
2. Describe security issues related to blockchain
3. Perform forensics operations on blockchain
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1   Introduction   04
Defining blockchain and   cryptocurrency, blockchain   properties,
Blockchain use cases, types of blockchain.
2   Blockchain Fundamentals   12
Cryptography (hashing, RSA algorithm and Elliptic Curve Cryptography),
Merkel tree, Basic structure of block, transactions, Mining (PoW, PoS, etc),
wallets, contracts and tokens, Blockchain Trilemma, blockchain account
model v/s UTXO model.
3   Attacks on blockchain   04
51% attack, Sybil Attack, Double spending attack, routing attacks, private
key security attack, selfish mining attack, clustering attacks and other
attacks and their countermeasures
https://www.getastra.com/blog/knowledge-base/blockchain-security-
issues/
4   Blockchain Forensics   10
Detecting the Use of Cryptocurrencies, Extracting Private and Public Keys
from Seized Computers, extracting wallets, Finding Information on a
Recovered Address, Analysing a Recovered Wallet, following transactions
and monitoring addresses, asset seizure.
Total   30
Text Books
Nick Furneaux, Investigating Cryptocurrencies: Understanding, Extracting, and Analyzing Blockchain
Evidence, Wiley, 2018.
Reference Books
1. N. Reddy, Practical Cyber Forensics, Apress, 2019.
2. Abhishek Bhattacharya, Srinivas Mahankali, and Gokul B Alex, Secure Chains, BPB Publications,
2020.
