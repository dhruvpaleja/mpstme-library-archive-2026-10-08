Course Objectives
1. This course gives basic knowledge of sensor characteristics.
2. This would be helpful in understanding the technology used in designing sensors.

Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the concept of sensors and their characteristics.
2. Understand the practical approach in design of technology based on different

sensors.
3. Describe various sensor materials and technology used in designing sensors.
