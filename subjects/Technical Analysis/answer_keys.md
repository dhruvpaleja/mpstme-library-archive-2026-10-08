2.   Answer the following question(Attempt Any 1 Question)   8
a.   8
A stock in a strong uptrend shows RSI fluctuating between 60 and 80 without falling below 50.
Apply RSI principles to explain this behavior and suggest how a trader should respond.
(4marks)
Page 2 of 5
--- page 3 ---
Normally:
Above 70 → Overbought
Below 30 → Oversold
In strong uptrends, RSI may remain between 60–80, known as a bullish range.
Interpretation: (4 marks)
RSI not falling below 50 indicates strong underlying momentum.
Staying above 60 shows buyers are in control.
Trading Response:
Traders should not immediately sell when RSI crosses 70.
Instead, look for RSI dropping below 50 as a stronger trend reversal signal.
Thus, in strong trends, RSI overbought does not always mean immediate reversal.
b. leading and lagging indicator (4 marks)   8
1.oscillation-range between 0 to 100 overbought and oversold condition
2. cross over- when indicator and price line intersects
3. divergence- bullish and bearish
4. General Advice (4 marks)
Always combine multiple indicators for confirmation (e.g., RSI + MACD)
Use stop-loss and position sizing to manage risk
Back test parameters with historical data to suit the stock’s volatility

Wave 2 retraces to ₹220, meaning a ₹40 decline.
Retracement percentage = (40/60) × 100 = 66.7%.
According to Elliott Wave guidelines: 4 marks
Wave 2 commonly retraces between 50% and 61.8% (Fibonacci levels).
It must not retrace more than 100% of Wave 1 (must not fall below ₹200).

4.   6
The crossover of the 50-day SMA below the 200-day SMA is known as a Dead Crossover,
which is a strong bearish signal. It indicates that the short-term trend has weakened
significantly compared to the long-term trend. When price trades below both moving averages,
it confirms that sellers are dominating the market and the overall trend has shifted from bullish
to bearish. The increase in volume during the decline strengthens the reliability of this bearish
signal.
3 Marks
The Stochastic Oscillator moving below 20 indicates that the stock is in the oversold zone.
However, the %K crossing below %D within this region shows continued bearish momentum
rather than immediate reversal. In a strong downtrend, stochastic can remain oversold for an
extended period. Therefore, the oscillator supports continuation of the bearish move rather than
signaling a buying opportunity at this stage.

suggests further downside potential. Traders should wait for stochastic to show bullish
crossover from oversold levels before considering any long positions.
