Prerequisite: Nil
Course Objective
This course deals with the key concern areas of self- employment and entrepreneurship
development. This course is directed to help students to develop and shape their creativity and
to understand peripheral influencing aspects.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Understand the importance of self-employment and entrepreneurship development
2. Explore the process of product development and marketing management concepts
3. Familiarize with registration process/ procedure for a new enterprise
4. Develop the skills to manage the enterprise effectively
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction to Entrepreneurship Development   07
Entrepreneurship as a career: charms, advantages, disadvantages, scope-
local and global. Traits of successful entrepreneurs: consistency, creativity,
initiative, independent decision making, assertiveness, persuasion,
persistence, information seeking, handling business communication,
commitment to work contract, calculated risk taking, learning from failure.
Types of enterprises and their features - manufacturing, service and trading.
2.   Startup Selection Process   09
Product/Service selection: Process, core competence, product/service life
cycle, new product/service development process, mortality curve, creativity
and innovation in product/service modification/development.
Process selection: Technology life cycle, forms and cost of transformation,
factors affecting process selection, location for an industry, material
handling. Market study procedures: questionnaire design, sampling, market
survey, data analysis. Use of breakeven analysis, return on investment (ROI)
and return on sales (ROS).
3.   Managing Enterprise   08
Feasibility study, Ownership, Capital, Budgeting, Matching entrepreneur
with the project, Unique Selling Proposition (U.S.P.) - Identification,
developing a marketing plan. Preparing strategies of handling business -
policymaking, negotiation, and bargaining techniques. SWOT and PESTEL
analysis. Incubation centers and accelerators.
AY 2025-26
SVKM’s NMIMS Deemed-to-be University
Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering
4.   Support System for MSME Startup   06
Concept of micro, small and medium scale enterprises, Support systems
from government agencies, Support agencies for entrepreneurship guidance,
training, registration, technical consultation, technology transfer and quality
control, marketing, and finance.
Total   30
Text Books
1. Poornima M., Charantimath, Entrepreneurship Development and Small Business Enterprises, 3rd
Edition, Pearson Education India, 2018.
2. Chitale, Avinash K, Gupta, R.C. Product Design and Manufacturing, 7th Edition, 2023.
Reference Books

2. Monica Mehta, The Entrepreneurial Instinct: How Everyone Has the Innate Ability to Start a
Successful Small Business, McGraw Hill; 1st edition, 2012.
