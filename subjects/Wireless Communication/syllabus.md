Course Objectives
1. To provide the knowledge of mobile communication systems in various aspects
and trends.
2. To understand the mobile radio propagation mechanism.
3. To understand 2G (GSM, GPRS, EDGE), 3G cellular mobile systems.
4. To understand 3GPP LTE and 4G, emerging technologies of wireless

After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand the significance of cellular concept and the capacity of wireless
communication.
2. Analyze the mobile radio propagation mechanism.
3. Evaluate application of GSM, CDMA and 3G (UMTS/ IMT 2000) mobile systems.
4. Analyze the techniques and technological advancement in LTE, 4G, 5G and
software defined networks.
