Pre-requisite: Basic Electrical Engineering (BTMA01002), Physics (BTMA01003), Digital
Electronics (BTMA03009)
Objectives:
1. To develop basic skills required for measurements of mechanical and process parameters.
2. To impart knowledge about applications and working of various metrological instruments.
3. To study different types of sensors and transducers used in industrial applications.
Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand the significance of calibration, specifications and characteristics of different
measuring instruments.
2. Analyze the impact of factors like surface finish on performance of machines.
3. Select and Apply sensors and transducers for various physical parameters.
4. Analyze the performance of different bridge circuits for measurement of Resistance,
Inductance and Capacitance.
