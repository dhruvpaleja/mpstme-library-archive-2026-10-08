Pre-requisite: Statistics and Probability, Calculus
Course Objective
Stochastic Processes and Applications refers to skills, practices and techniques used in
stochastic systems found in several real life applications. This discipline consists of an
understanding of stochastic systems and their applications in wide array of business situations
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Understand and apply the foundational concepts of probability theory, including
axiomatic probability, random variables, probability distributions, expectation,
convergence of random variables, and laws of large numbers. (L2-Understanding and
L3- Applying)
2. Utilize mathematical tools such as generating functions and transforms to analyze the
behavior of random variables and sequences. (L3- Applying and L4-Analyzing)
3. Classify and analyze different types of stochastic processes, including discrete-time
and continuous-time processes, based on their state and parameter spaces. (L2-
Understanding and L4-Analyzing)
4. Apply and analyse real-world phenomena using discrete-time Markov chains,
compute transition probabilities, classify states, and determine long-run behaviors
including stationary distributions. (L3- Applying and L4-Analyzing)
5. Apply and evaluate the problems involving continuous-time Markov chains, including
Poisson and birth-death processes, using Kolmogorov equations and infinitesimal
generators. (L3- Applying and L5- Evaluating)
