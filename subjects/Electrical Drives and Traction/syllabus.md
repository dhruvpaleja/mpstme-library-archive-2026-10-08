3. To understand the energy requirements for traction applications.

After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Understand theoretical concepts of dynamics of electric drives
2. Analyze the performance of dc motor drives and induction motor drives for various
operating conditions
3. Estimate energy consumption and decide rating of motor for traction application
