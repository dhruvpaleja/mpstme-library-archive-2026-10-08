Pre-requisite: Soil Mechanics, Transportation Engineering
Course Objective
The objective of this course is to provide the knowledge about the methods of design for flexible and
rigid highway and airport pavements. The course aims to give the knowledge of pavement condition,
low-cost roads, concrete roads and plant and machinery needed for construction of pavements.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Discuss different pavement structures and their functions
2. Estimate stresses for and design the different pavement structures
3. Identify methodology for evaluation and strengthening of pavements
4. Appraise low-cost roads, concrete roads, road making machinery
