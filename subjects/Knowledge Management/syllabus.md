Course Objectives
 To understand and analyze the nature of Knowledge Management and to propose its methods

creation of Knowledge Management systems
   Identify and the elaborate the frameworks and techniques for co-creation and management of

design of organizational structures

After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Describe the organization and structure of knowledge management solutions
2. Understand and analyze the organizational impacts of Knowledge Management and propose
Knowledge Management solution of an organization
3. Apply concepts, tools, models and frameworks of Knowledge Management for organizational
change and restructuring
4. 4. Design the key components of KM solutions through appropriate technologies, systems and
processes
