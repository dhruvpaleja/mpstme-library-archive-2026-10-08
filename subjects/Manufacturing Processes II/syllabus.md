- Course as printed: Manufacturing Processes-II (code 702ME0C037)

````text
Program: B Tech Mechanical Engineering   Semester: V
Course: Manufacturing Processes-II   Code: 702ME0C037
Teaching Scheme   Evaluation Scheme
Lecture   Practical   Tutorial   Internal Continuous Term End Examinations
(Hours per   (Hours per   (Hours per   Credit   Assessment (ICA)   (TEE)
week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   0   0   2   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Manufacturing Processes - I
Course Objective
This course aims to introduce the students to the fundamentals of material removal processes
including non-conventional machining processes. The students will be able to identify/control the
appropriate process parameters and possible defects of manufacturing processes.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Classify the metal cutting processes with tool geometry and forces of cutting
2. Choose appropriate method of machining, to produce required geometrical features in
components
3. Analyze various process parameters that affect machining processes
Detailed Syllabus
Unit Description   Duration
1.   Metal cutting   08
Tool Geometry, forces in single point cutting, tool wear and tool life, formation of
chips and types of chips, mechanics of orthogonal and oblique cutting, chip
thickness ratio, velocity relationship in orthogonal cutting, Merchant’s circle
diagram, analysis of stresses and strains and work done during metal cutting,
friction and thermal aspects of metal cutting, cutting fluids, types of cutting fluids
2.   Shaper and Planer   06
Introduction, construction, working and operations performed on Shapers, Planers
and slotters.
Milling Machines
Types of milling machines, tools and their geometry, various operations on milling
machine.
3.   Drilling Machines   06
Types of machines, operations such as drilling, boring, reaming, spot facing, counter
boring and sinking, tapping, drill speed and feeds.
Boring and Broaching Machine
Classification-horizontal and vertical boring machine, types of broaching machines,
advantages, limitations and applications of broaching.
4.   Non-conventional Machining Processes   07
Introduction, classification and overview of non-conventional machining processes,
abrasive jet machining (AJM), abrasive water jet machining (AWJM), electric
discharge machining (EDM), electro-chemical machining (ECM), laser beam
machining and allied processes
5.   Processing of Plastics   03
General aspects, methods of processing of plastics, compression moulding, transfer
moulding, injection moulding, thermoforming, Joining of thermoplastic.
Total   30
````
