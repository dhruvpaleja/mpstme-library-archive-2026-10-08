Course Objective
The course is aimed at imparting knowledge of role of the elements of automation in manufacturing
industry.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Recognize the working principle and components of an automation system
2. Select appropriate sensor for a manufacturing process
3. Comprehend the processing of sensor data
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
1.   Introduction   06
Types of Automation , automation in production systems, principles of
material handling, automated guided vehicle Systems (AGVs), conventional
and automated storage systems , types of layout, flexible manufacturing
systems (FMS).
2.   Sensor working and applications   06
Definition, application and differentiation, roles of sensors, Construction and
principle of working of sensors in automated systems for manufacturing,
types and classification of sensors, type of actuators.
3.   Principles of Selection for Manufacturing   06
Proximity Sensor, Motion Sensors, Velocity Sensor, and Pressure Sensors.
Encoder-Optical, Magnetic, Inductive, Capacitive, Accelerometer –GPS,
Bluetooth, Range Sensors, Magnetic Sensors and Hall Effect Sensors.
4.   Programmable Automation   12
Processing data from sensors, interfacing sensors with microcontroller and
simple programs for basic operations, sensor selection and justification for
industrial applications. Simple programs for sensors using microcontrollers.
Simple circuits for Electrical control of fluid power actuators.
Total   30
