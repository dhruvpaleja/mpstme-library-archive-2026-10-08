Objectives
The subject introduces students to modern biology with an emphasis on the
evolution of biology as a multi-disciplinary field, students are made aware of the
application of engineering principles in biology and engineering robust solutions
inspired by biological examples. This subject will encourage students to think
about solving biological problems with engineering tools.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. understand that all forms of life have the same building blocks, and yet the
manifestations are diverse.
2. identify DNA as genetic material in the molecular basis of information transfer.
3. classify enzymes and distinguish between different mechanisms of enzyme
action.
4. understand the application of microbiology in fermentation and bioprocessing.
