week)   week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
2   2   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Foundations of Machine Learning, Applications of Machine Learning
Course Objective
The aim of the course is to provide an iterative framework for developing real-world machine learning
systems that are deployable, reliable, and scalable. It will enable students to apply best practices and
progressive delivery techniques to maintain and monitor a continuously operating ML production system.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Explain the differences between machine learning systems in research and production, and
describe the components and needs of ML systems in real-world scenarios. (L2 – Understanding)
2. Apply fundamental concepts of data systems including data sources, formats, models, and
processing frameworks to enable machine learning workflows. (L3 – Applying)
3. Analyze the processes of data sampling, labeling, handling class imbalance, and feature
engineering in the design of ML systems. (L4 – Analyzing)
4. Evaluate deployment strategies such as batch and online prediction, and assess the trade-offs in
cloud vs. edge deployment. (L5 – Evaluating)
5. Design an end-to-end ML production pipeline including model monitoring, diagnosis of failures,
and resource management. (L6 – Creating)
