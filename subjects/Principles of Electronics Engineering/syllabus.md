1. Describe the fundamentals of semiconductors
2. Analyze characteristics of diodes, BJTs and FETs and design simple circuits using
them.
3. Determine parameters due to feedback in amplifiers.
4. Describe operational amplifier and analyse its applications.
5. Introduce to digital electronics fundamentals.
