Pre-requisite: Basic knowledge of Wireless Communication and Computer Network and Security
Course Objective
This course imparts understanding of communication technologies used for IoT. It also helps in
learning various IoT messaging, routing and networking protocols.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Relate the concept of IoT as market perspective
2. Differentiate IoT communication technologies and networking protocols
3. Compare and implement IoT messaging and routing protocols.
