Importance of Ethics in Leadership
********** 5 **********
--- page 6 ---
Ethics refers to the principles of right and wrong that guide behavior. For leaders, ethics are
essential because:
1. Builds Trust: Ethical leaders earn credibility and trust, which are the foundation for
teamwork and loyalty.
2. Sets the Tone: Employees often model their behavior on their leaders; ethical conduct
from the top fosters an ethical culture throughout the organization.
3. Ensures Long-term Success: Unethical leadership might yield short-term gains but
damages reputation and sustainability.
4. Encourages Fair Decision-Making: Ethical leaders consider the impact of their
decisions on all stakeholders — not just profits.
Example
Ratan Tata, former chairman of Tata Group. He consistently emphasized fairness,
transparency, and social responsibility — from refusing to engage in corrupt practices to
prioritizing employee welfare even during economic downturns.
His ethical leadership earned the company global respect and long-term brand loyalty,
illustrating how moral integrity strengthens leadership effectiveness.
In Summary
Leadership is not just about achieving goals but how those goals are achieved. Ethical
leadership ensures that influence is exercised responsibly, balancing organizational success
with integrity and social good.
