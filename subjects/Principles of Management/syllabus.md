• To provide better understanding and knowledge in the field of Management and its relevance for
managerial decision-making.
• To develop the basic skills in students required to pursue functions related to
Management dealing with issues at various levels in business process.
• To analyze the impact of changes in business environment and management policies for business

Course Outcomes:
After completion of the course, students would be able to:
1. Illustrate basic understanding of management and functions of management (planning, organizing,
leading, controlling) along with various management thoughts,
2. Analyze and evaluate the impact of the application of management functions in business decision-
making and its implication on the growth of business organization,
3. Understand and evaluate the management orientation towards finance, marketing, and human
resources in a competitive business environment.
