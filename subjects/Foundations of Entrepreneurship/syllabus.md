Pre-requisite: Foundations of Business.
Course Objective
This course will provide platform to students so that they can re-evaluate their views of
entrepreneurship within the framework of entrepreneurial thinking and form their own
mindset of entrepreneurship as per outcomes listed.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to -
1. Assess your personal entrepreneurial capacity
2. Differentiate between a closed mindset, a growth mindset and an entrepreneurial
mindset
3. Play with idea generation techniques to help you better create and shape ideas into
bold opportunities
4. Evaluate   entrepreneurial   opportunities using a   rigorous   feasibility and
experimentation processes
5. Develop and define a business concept to determine its commercial feasibility
6. Gain confidence to use entrepreneurial thinking and entrepreneurial action with future
opportunities
