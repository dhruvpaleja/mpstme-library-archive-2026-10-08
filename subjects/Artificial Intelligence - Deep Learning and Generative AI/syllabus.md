Pre-requisite: Basic understanding of neural networks and ML Principles & Python programming.
Knowledge on mathematical concepts, including linear algebra, calculus, and probability.
Course Objective
This course aims to provide students with a solid foundation in deep learning and generative AI models.
Students will learn neural network architectures, apply CNNs and RNNs, and explore generative
models like GANs, VAEs, and transformers. Additionally, the course addresses prompt engineering
and ethical considerations in AI, such as bias and transparency.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to –
1. Understand the fundamental concepts of neural networks, including architectures, training
algorithms, and optimization techniques
2. Analyze deep learning techniques such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs), recurrent
neural networks (RNNs)
3. Apply generative models such as generative adversarial networks (GANs), variational
autoencoders (VAEs), and transformers
4. Evaluate principles and strategies for prompt engineering, including designing effective
prompts, controlling model behavior and mitigating biases
