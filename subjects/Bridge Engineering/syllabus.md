week)   per week)   week)   (Marks - 50)   (Marks- 100)
3   0   0   3   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Theory of Structures
Course Objective
This course is designed to understand various types of bridges and also to learn IRC loading criteria for
the bridges. This course focuses on the understanding of fundamentals of Bridge design. It also aims to
understand construction systems and Planning of Bridges. It includes understanding of the principles of
long span bridge design.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to -
1. Discuss loadings on components of bridges
2. Identify the load transfer mechanism of the components of different types of Bridges
3. Identify the behaviour and suitability of various bridge types
4. Design the various components of bridges
Detailed Syllabus
Unit   Description   Duration
Introduction
1   Classification and components of bridges, historical perspective, layout and
05
planning, investigations for bridges, choice of type of the bridges, conceptual bridge
design, bridge aesthetics. Bridge appurtenances.
2   Loads on bridges   05
Loading standards for highway and railway bridges (IRC, IRS)
Analysis and design of bridge decks
3   Slab culvert bridges, slab-and-beam bridges, load distribution in slabs and beams,
15
bowstring girder bridges, behaviour of skew bridge decks, design of RC bridge
decks and introduction to PSC box girder bridge decks
4   Behaviour, analysis and design of steel bridge decks
06
Girder bridges, truss bridges, arch bridges, composite construction
Design of bearings, substructure and foundations
5   piers and abutments of different types, shallow and deep foundations – design and   06
constructional aspects
Construction practices
Modern methods of construction of concrete, steel and composite bridges, their
6   impact on analysis and design, construction stage analysis. Introduction to analysis   08
and design of long span bridges: suspension and cable stayed bridges, balanced
cantilever construction, segmental construction.
