3   2   0   4   Marks Scaled to 50   Marks Scaled to 50
Pre-requisite: Digital Electronics (BTMA03009) and Microprocessors and Microcontrollers
(BTMA04011)
Objectives:
1. To introduce 8, 16 and 32 bits advanced microcontrollers to the students.
2. To understand the assembly language and write code for applications of
microcontroller.
3. To understand the Embedded and RTOS basics.
Outcomes:
After the successful completion of this course the student will be able to
1. Understand the architecture and instruction set of advanced microcontrollers.
2. Understand the assembly language programming of advanced microcontrollers.
3. Discuss different embedded systems and concept of RTOS.
